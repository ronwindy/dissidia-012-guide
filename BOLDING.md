# Bolding rules and glossary

Applies to **part 4 and every page added after it**. Part 3 is left as hand-edited. `CLAUDE.md` points here.

## Rules

1. **Source of truth is the glossary below.** Bold only terms listed there, every occurrence, in paragraph and list text. Do not bold from memory.
2. **Longest match wins.** Match the full form first (`Chaos Piece`, never `**Chaos** Piece`). A short form is bolded only if it is listed as an alias of that entry.
3. **Numbers are italic, not bold.** Any meaningful number with its unit: `*1600 BRV*`, `*2000 HP*`, `*50%*`, `*15 seconds*`, `*25 KP*`. Spelled-out counts ("two Dragoons") stay plain unless listed under "Open decisions".
4. **References are bold.** `Chapter N`, `Gateway N`, and level/booster names such as `Level 10-19` are bold. A number inside a bold reference is not also italicised.
5. **Skip:** headings (`####`, `#####`), links, existing `*italics*`, `<Board>`, `<EnemyCard>`, `<Figure>`, `<CharacterIntro>` props, and frontmatter.
6. **Unknown capitalised phrase** that is not in the glossary: do not bold it. List it in the wrap-up as a candidate and add it here after the user rules on it.
7. **Fix, don't stack:** if a fragment is already bold (`**Chaos** Piece`), replace it with the full form.

#### Exploration

On the world map, head past Tidus and Cloud and use the Teleport Stone to head to the Land of Discord. It's just as pleasant a place as last time.

- Head to the mainland from where you appear and challenge the Transient Witch, an easy victory.
- Near it is a chest with Moogle Fur.
- Shatter the stone and grind to the island to find a BRV Charge.
- Behind the gateway is an Imaginary Champion guarding a chest with an Arcane Resin.
- Down the path beyond is a KP Gambler for the next chapter.

Finally head into the gateway.

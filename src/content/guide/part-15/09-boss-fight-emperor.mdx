<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Emperor one of the most dangerous end bosses so far. He's got a False Stalwart as Assist, it uses Round Edge and Bardiche when called, and a Land Worm summon to burn through your Bravery like kindling. For accessories, an Earring and Hyper Ring with 7 Near Opponent boosters, and they suit him better than Far from Opponent, actually.

His Thunder Crest will be deadly if he catches you in it. He has the usual moveset, but Dreary Cell is a new move I don't believe you've seen from him yet.

He creates a spark of light, similar to but distinct from his mines, and he can have up to three out at a time. When you approach that spark, it expends into a rune of energy with orbs of lightning around it, sucking you in as it does so, then explodes to do HP damage. Keep a keen eye on the field so you don't confuse the Cell sparks with his Mines.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

And on top of it all, it may just be me but the Emperor seemed to be smarter than previous fights, he kept Thunder Crests and Flares out and was good with his Light Crest to interrupt my attacks, and he was aggressive enough to charge up and use that Assist.

Try to keep to the open areas to avoid getting caught in Thunder Crest, as if you do get trapped he'll fire off a Flare to hit you in the face, and after landing an HP hit watch out for his counter Light Crest, as the projectiles are likely to bounce off the walls behind you and hit from your blind spot.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Grab EX Cores fast because you don't want the Emperor getting that EX Gauge filled, you never want an opponent in EX Mode of course but his EX Mode bonus is the same HP draining power that Firion has, and with his Flares he's liable to heal himself a significant amount, especially if he uses his Burst.

He's a tougher opponent than other end bosses, but is still no match for you. Know that Weaponsmaster can strike through walls, use that to your advantage here.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

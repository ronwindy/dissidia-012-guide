<PanelSection kind="chain">

You *could* use a Multichain at D3, but if you only have one save it for the next gateway.

Instead do a Jump Chain on either of the two purple emblems, the lower-left one is Initial BRV 0 for both you and the enemy, but I prefer the upper-right which is an Initial BRV Boost. If you lack a Jump Chain, break the orbs of the dead outside and cross your fingers.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

The KP Chance is a 90 second win.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is the lone normal enemy, Delusory Dragoon with Cyclone, Crashing Dive and Gungnir.
- Next, an Imitation Despot with Thunder Crest, Dynamite, Flare, and those EX Mode accessories. Sadly Firion has nothing that can reflect Flare here, but his Straightarrow will shoot through Flare safely, no deflection.
- Next, a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe and the same accessories.
- Last is an Idle Sky Pirate with Katana, Rifle and Windburst, again, nothing to reflect it yet. He has the EX accessories too, naturally.

The last foe, the Expert, is an Imaginary Champion with full equipment, two Earrings with 6 Far From Opponent and an After 45 Seconds (he's Sephiroth so oh no, Shadow Flare gets a slight boost sometimes), and the Magic Pot summon.

- Bravery attacks: Fervent Blow, Reaper, Shadow Flare, Oblivion and Godspeed
- HP attacks: Octaslash, Hell's Gate and Heaven's Light

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Be very careful when you're below him because Sephiroth's AI loves to use Hell's Gate whenever it can, and a single dodge on the ground won't clear the danger zone most of the time.

It's a tough fight and you probably won't make the KP Chance, but that's fine, it's just 1 KP. Save Cactuar for after Magic Pot.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Firion hit Level 8 from this fight and learned Weaponsmaster. It throws all his weapons a medium distance forward to whirl and attack for a couple seconds, and he can move at slow speed for the duration but can't turn.

It's pretty useless in the open areas, but in the small ones like Pandamonium or Kefka's Tower, it's a nightmare, the attack effective creates a cone of hitzone in front of Firion, and if you're near a wall or in some area where you can't dodge to the sides or above properly, you're gonna be in for pain.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has a Full Metal Staff, inferior to Firion's Zweihander but good for other magic users.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

On to the next gateway.

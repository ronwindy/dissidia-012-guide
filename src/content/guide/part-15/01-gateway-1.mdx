They come into battle with an Arcane Resin for a full EX Gauge - already bad, now it goes to worse - and they're got a Muscle Belt, an Earring, and 7 EX Mode boosters. That's a pretty significant damage buff, along with the EX Mode benefits of the boosted critical hit rate and Regen.

An Assist attack will knock them out of EX Mode and give you the stage bravery pool, but by the time you've charged up your Assist their EX Mode is probably done, and later in the story you have no Assist at all. Be wary if these guys.

#### Exploration

An interlude skips us ahead a bit to when the two split up, so now Firion is alone on the world map.

- Immediately turn around, there's a chest in plain sight to the right on the outcropping of land with 1000 gil.
- There's also the moogle of course, grab a few Multichains if you like, Firion's story is big on Multichains to get all enemies.

---

Now move forward to Crescent Lake, the orbs of the dead lie along the path.

- There's a Delusory Warlock, use Rope Knife's range to your advantage when engaging enemies on the world map since most other foes use melee attacks. In battle it has Nightglow, watch out for that.
- On the island is a Rosetta Stone and on the ground nearby are two Ephemeral Phantoms, the first is fond of Jecht's Blade and the second prefers Triumphant Grasp.
- And in the ruins on the hill you fought them to get to? KP Gambler GET!

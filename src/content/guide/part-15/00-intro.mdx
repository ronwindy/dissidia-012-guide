# Chapter 6

Cloud, Cecil, Tidus, one left before we turn our attention to the four.

#### The Rebel

---

<CharacterIntro name="Firion" src="drake/Dissidia_Firion.png" width={200} height={290} alt="Dissidia Firion">

Firion is just barely a step up from the Warrior of Light and Onion Knight, but in such is more a step down. Unlike them he *did* exist in his original game, but that also carries the handicap that they have less flexibility with who he is, since the other two are pretty much original characters.

And they did alright, Firion is a naive dreamer who just wants to end the war, and realizes that in comparison to his comrades, his goals are much simpler, but he'll come to realize that his dream isn't his alone.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Firion is a ground-focused character with a lousy pool of air moves, he has his spells but they aren't his niche, and he has Weaponsmaster, but Firion wants to be on the ground. So to make that a viable option, he's an anti-air fighter, he snags opponents out of the air and brings them down to him.

His other gimmick is when using his three core ground Bravery attacks, he can do one of three follow-ups:

- Press forward and Circle to knock the opponent into the air and begin a chase
- Neutral Circle to slam them far away with a punch to wall rush them
- Back and Circle to use a lance combo that slams them to the ground

He can also chain three aerial spells together, changing up which he uses with the same controller input. Combined with a decent pool of mid-ranged attacks and Firion is a fairly versatile mid-range fighter and has a lot of power, especially once he gets his HP links.

However, as opposed to most other characters thus far, you really want to keep him on the ground. This means arenas like Planet's Core and Ultimecia's Castle are his worst nightmare.

Firion starts off with Rope Knife, a mid-ranged knife toss that drags the opponent closer, and Reel Axe, which is slow to execute but throws a guard-crushing axe a fair distance.

His aerial spells are Frost and Bolt:

- Frost fires a blast of ice that begins a Chase
- Bolt creates lightning around the opponent to wall rush them into the ground

Three chained Thunders are usually enough to catch a dodging opponent on the third cast, if you chain them quickly, and even though he doesn't know Flame yet he can still throw out Fire spells as a second or third spell chained onto Frost or Bolt.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attack is Straightarrow, a fast but pretty easy to dodge arrow shot, hold the Square button to delay the firing and he'll turn to aim at opponents.

Firion is overall a pretty good character, but he won't reach his full potential until much later when he gets his other three HP attacks and learns his HP links, at which point he's a beast you don't want to get anywhere near on the ground or in the air.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

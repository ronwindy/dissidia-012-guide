<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain on the blue emblem, there's five enemies so go go KP Gambler! The emblem is sadly a Magic Critical Rate Up, not much help for Firion.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

The KP Chance is a 60 second Break, giving you lots of room after meeting it to draw out the fights and fill that EX Gauge for the boss. You'll need it.

The bonus line is 8, but even if you're over it by a level or two, take the penalty for -1 or -2 starting KP. See, if you set Firion's level to 8, even if it's just one level back, as he earns Exp his base level will rise but he'll still be stuck at level 8 and can't change in the gateway, and this is a boss where you do *not* want to be a measly level 8 because it's one of the hardest bosses in the base ten storylines.

You *need* that full EX Gauge and those few extra levels so you can have every advantage possible.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Phantasmal Girl with Fire and Blizzara.
- Next is a Fallacious Tree with Hurricane and Sword Dance, Straightarrow and Reel Axe can pierce Hurricane.
- Third is a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe.

Now the two Hard enemies:

- An Imitation Despot with Thunder Crest, Dynamite, Flare, those EX accessories and the Barbariccia summon. She activates when you use your summon and swaps your Bravery with the opponent's, too pad for Cactuar, not that you need it for these weaklings.
- Last is another Phantasmal Girl with Fire, Blizzara, and the EX Mode accessories again.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

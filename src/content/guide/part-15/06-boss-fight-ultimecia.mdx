Oh kiddies, prepare for a long, hard, frustrating battle. The problem is obvious just by considering the set-up - Firion, the slow, melee-focused ground character against Ultimecia, the annoyingly evasive, range-focused aerial character.

And oh yay, she has like, 10 levels on you, and Tidus is gone so no Assist for you. And to add more difficulty, she has the Leviathan summonstone, an Earring with six Far from Opponent and two Aerial boosters, and an Imaginary Brawler Assist.

I took the Behemoth summonstone into the fight because I wanted that Bravery boost when it counted. Ultimecia is very hard to pin down and hurt, and you're fighting in the World of Darkness, definitely not my first or even fifth choice for an arena Firion can excel in.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Ultimecia will spend most of the fight bombarding you with Knight's Axe and Knight's Arrow from range in the air, too high probably for your Bravery attacks to reach.

When she begins an HP attack, that's the time to fire off a Straightarrow or Reel Axe since she'll be defenseless. Don't be too aggressive, she can do a lot of damage, especially if she gets off those two Aerial boosters, and she can Assist Change out of any combo you hit her with.

The AI's idiocy is once again your friend, but she's still very annoying to face and very hard to get a good hit on.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Behemoth single-handedly won the day for me - I broke her and went into a Chase, and Behemoth doubled my Bravery to around 2500, and the attack was close enough to the arena border to wall rush.

No matter how slow Firion is or how pesky the dual Bravery drain from Behemoth and Leviathan is, one HP attack can lead into the EX Burst to end it, or you just wait for the drain to wear off and nail a single Straightarrow.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

#### Exploration

Another interlude tells us Firion is on his way to the enemy stronghold, but he's still feeling conflicted. On the world map on yet another familiar place, but to an unfamiliar one, the moogle has Grounded and Aerial for sale.

- Buy them if you don't already have one of each.
- Grab a Jump Chain and Multichain if you lack one of either, but do it after getting the orbs of the dead in case they give you one.
- I also bought an Icebrand, we'll be using it shortly but Firion doesn't need it, but you can use it if you like the Initial Bravery boost it gives.
- I also bought a Sakura and just some new gear for Firion, in hindsight the Mystic Mythril benefits haven't proven too useful have they? Nah.

Head into the gateway.

Cosmos appears to Firion and, as she usually does, gives a pep talk that consists of reminding the character about things they already valued. Firion laments he has no personal dream to chase like his three friends, but Cosmos reminds him of his dream of a world full of wild roses and peace, and assures him that the dream is enough on its own.

Jecht only has two boosters on his Muscle Belt, one each Near Opponent and Attacking Bravery. It's fine, he doesn't need his Bravery attacks.

Look at his summon - Bahamut. "Wait" I hear you cry out in horror, "the summon that gives him 99 Bravery every two seconds?" Yup, that's the one.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Jecht will eventually get enough Bravery to deal a one-hit knockout to Firion, hope you've gotten the hang of his attacks and how to avoid them...of course there's still Jecht Block, so you're very likely to get blocked on your Lance Combo or Rope Knife and then eat a 2000 point damage Jecht Beam in the balls.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Not much to say on this fight, you just finished taking out Jecht, this one just has lower stats but a much deadlier summon.

Fortunately, if you have Cactuar, there's a simple way to stop Bahamut in his tracks. Firion's punch follow-up does a lot of damage.

- Nail Jecht with a Rope Knife or Reel Axe and them punch him, this should do enough Bravery damage that he'll call Bahamut.
- Now snag him again and as you do the follow-up, summon Cactuar.

The faithful little cactoid will sap Jecht's Bravery to 0, just in time for you to Break him, and Bahamut's boosts suddenly don't mean a thing since the boosts don't work while Broken.

Overall if you do this, Jecht is pretty easy, wall rush him after that, then let him recovery his Bravery and use an EX Burst to blast him. Just watch for Jecht Block, he usually isn't smart enough to do it but he can use it to deflect even your Reel Axe and Straightarrow

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Let's talk about Firion's EX Mode while we're here. It's pretty lame, sadly. Firion gets Blood Weapons, so every time he deals HP damage, he heals the same amount of damage. Useless unless you're about to die, and that won't happen if you play smart.

His EX Burst is one of my favorites for badassery. Fervid Blazer is a sequence of five commands, three directional buttons and two of the command buttons, and you have to press them in the shown order within a time limit for Firion to bombard the opponent with all his weapons and magic, finishing by firing his arsenal at them with his bow. And he still heals all the damage he does with it.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

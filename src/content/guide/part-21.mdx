# Epilogue Part 2

Part two of the Epilogue - there's only four enemies left to wrap up before we get to **Chaos**!

#### A Family Matter

**Squall** is leading the troupe now. As we enter the gateway, **Tidus** wonders if **Jecht** is waiting ahead too. We then get a scene of him and the **Emperor** where the **Emperor** reveals to **Jecht** how he used to fight for **Cosmos** until the **Emperor** had his empty shell converted. Why? So that just as the other ten did, when **Jecht** proved his resolve and spirit fighting **Tidus**, a crystal was born, which the **Emperor** seized to have **Cosmos**'s power for himself so he can survive **Chaos**'s destruction and rule the world when everyone else has faded away. **Jecht** takes off, fed up with his pompous posturing, to go find a proper fight.

Ho boy, fun line-up. **Multichain** at A4 where you enter to get them all. If you lack one, go for a **Jump Chain** at C3 to get all but one of them and use the physical critical rate up emblem. The bonus line is 33, and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win.

First is a **Phantasmal Girl** with a **Hyper Ring** and three No BRV Damage boosters. She has the **Moogle** summon and uses **Meltdown**. Then comes a **Fallacious Wanderer** who has an initial **Bravery** boost, the **Cactuar** summon, and uses **Ragnarok Blade**. Then comes the Party piece, first up, a **Counterfeit Wraith** with **Fusillade Particle Beam**. Second is a **Transient Witch** with a Knight's Axe and **Great Attractor**, an unknown summon, and a **Hyper Ring** with 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value, giving it a pretty good damage buff at first so be quick to go on the offensive. The third one who you won't be fighting if you do **Round Robin** like I have - and I told you to and why are you on my walkthrough if you're going to ignore me? - is a **Fallacious Tree** with **Hurricane**, **Sword Dance**, **Delta Attack**, **Pupu**, and the same accessories as the Witch.

The Expert is a **Delusory Warlock** with the typical moveset and a new attack, Sector Ray. It sends out three attack drones that spread out and then fire continuous red lasers at you for a second, the lasers have limited range but together cover a decent arena near the orbs that are firing them. Also be aware the attack drones are solid and do not pass through obstacles, when they hit one they stop there but still fire, and in **Kefka's Tower** that can lead to some interesting and surprising firing patterns from them. The Warlock otherwise uses the **Bahamut** summon and has two **Hyper Rings** with 6 Near Loss and 2 Near Death, and a mystery Assist. I confess I couldn't get the **KP Chance** here, he's just got too much **Bravery** thanks to **Bahamut**.

The two chests have 28,000 gil and a **Crystal** trade accessory. The **Crystal** unlocks the new **Crystal** equipment in the shop, but like Mythril we need 3 pieces and that's a ways off for us. Also, my party is at **Level 30** now, save for **Squall** at 29 but the next boss should fix that, so let's finally get to that Gold stuff! Buy a Gold Bangle, Gold Helm, Gold Hairpin, and Golden Vest. Everyone except **Terra**, **Cecil** and the **Warrior** of light can use one of these three equipment pieces. When three pieces of Gold equipment are worn, you get 50% more gil from fights! This is a huge help because trust me, in the bonus storyline, equipment gets very expensive very fast. I also bought **Level 30** weapons at this point - I got the Judicer's Staff for **Terra**, and a **Crystal** Sword for everyone else - true we won't get three **Crystal** items for a while, but the raw Attack is nice for now. If you want **Terra** to have the Gold stuff too, she can get the Golden Staff. I bought her a Black Belt's Gi for **Level 30** armor since she can't equip the Golden Vest.

At the end piece, **Tidus** finds **Jecht**, who admits he's not sure what he's doing there, but since he's here, let's fight.

**Jecht** has all his attacks with the **Calcabrina** summon, and a **Muscle Belt** with three Near Opponent and three Attacking **Bravery**. He's also got the highest power of any boss yet at 85 Attack. He's also quite good at **Jecht** Block now, and your **Bravery** attacks are very likely to get blocked and punished. His **Bravery** damage output is impressive of course, and **Calcabrina** is annoying. However, I didn't have too much trouble beating him, **Jecht** Block doesn't do much to punish **Thunder Barret**, and **Squall** was charging his Assist like mad with **Fire** and **Thunder Barret** to support his melee moves. He just has a lot of HP like all the bosses do, but I didn't have any trouble. If your party leader is level 30 and decked out is new level 30 Gold stuff, which my **Squall** wasn't, you'll have an even easier time than I did thanks to the higher stat boosts. And yes, as predicted **Squall** hit level 30 from the fight, now everyone from now on has the Gold gear.

After the fight **Jecht** tosses out some really lame generic taunts at **Tidus**, which of course **Tidus** snaps back at him...I think. **Jecht**, pleased the kid isn't giving up so easily, laughs and walks away, fading away into pyreflies. I love how casual he is about it, "don't make me come out here again!"

Now the party continues on when the **Warrior** begins to fade again, but the **Crystal**'s light restores him. We're running out of time. This scene also unlocks **Report 16**.

On the overworld map, for the final gateways **Zidane** is in the driver's seat again with **Terra** and **Cloud** as co-pilots. The moogle has crossed the lava stream to come root for us, buy a **Multichain** and a **Cross Chain**, or you can break the orbs and hope to get one for free, can't hurt. **Terra**, always the sweetheart, wonders about the moogles existence in this world and thinks about fluffing the one that came to follow us. Down the left path over the bridge are a BRV 0 and an Aged Turtle Shell. Now we're finally onto the floating gateways, only three more stand between us and **Chaos**. And by process of elimination, guess who's waiting in them.

The two chests have 26,000 gil and a Sun Blade, it's a level 30 weapon with lower power than the **Crystal** Sword, but you begin fights with 20% more **Bravery**. He says to **Cecil** all he wants now is for his wish to be fulfilled, and if **Cecil** wants to find out what that is, he knows how to get it...seriously, why do these people want to fight us when they have no reason to oppose us anymore?

**Golbez** has all his attacks, a **Delusory Knight** Assist, **Ramuh** for a summon, and a full set of **Level 30** equipment to give him great stats, though a level 1 helmet so his **Bravery** is lame. His accessories are an **Earring** with one each of the timer boosters, those are gonna hurt a lot late in the fight. Not much to say here except have fun, you should know how **Golbez** fights backwards and forwards, he's just a bit smarter than previous fights against his manikins and he has stronger equipment. If you plan to use a summon use it early before **Ramuh** locks it, otherwise just beat him up.

After the fight **Golbez** says he was just testing **Cecil**'s strength and is confident they're strong enough to take out **Chaos**, so he leaves to let us go do so. He won't fight with us, since he was called by **Chaos** due to his evil heart, but **Cecil** assures him he has light in him, and **Golbez** muses he might join forces with him someday in the future before leaving again.

#### Endless Chaos

They produce their **Crystals** and the portal to **Chaos**'s world opens. On the world map open the chest for a Skull Knight Shard, and head to the final gateway.

The path in this late gateway is entirely linear, no need to fight enemies unless you want to fill your **EX Gauges** for the final battle. You're also unable to use skills. You have your choice of which purple emblem to use, or none of them - Stage **Bravery** boost, Initial **Bravery** Boost, or Initial **Bravery** 0. And now, the final conflict!

**Chaos**'s **Bravery** attacks I have to describe, the names won't mean anything to you. He's got Darkflame Fang, a basic three-hit aerial combo with fast start up and surprising range vertically and horizontally, but it can be blocked. He also has a ground combo, Darkflame Talon, and it too can be blocked, it works similarly to **Garland**'s **Round Edge** as **Chaos** slashes and spins forward. If he flips into the air, he's using Earthwalker, sending a wave of fire along the ground to slam you backwards, dodge. If he pulls his arms back in the air, he's going to use his magical spin attack, Negashock, like Machine Gun or Knight's Blade block repeatedly to block it, or be hurt and wall rushed. When he calls out "Perish!", get into the air and ready to dodge, because he's using his diving dropkick The Fall, kicking up shards of dirt into the air on impact, and it crushes guards. Finally he has Darflame Blast, spitting four fireballs that move slowly and spread out, they can be blocked.

His HP attacks start out simple. Condemn has him create a pillar of flame around him and has very strong suction on start-up, dodge away quickly. Soul of **Oblivion** has him charge an orb of flame and fire it at you to do **Bravery** damage and wall rush you, hit him before he fires, otherwise it has long range and is impossible to avoid unless there's a huge vertical gap between you. Finally, he has Divine Punishment, he summons several fire pillars around you, then blasts you with fire from below and teleports above you and throws a flaming sword to slam you into the ground. It can be very difficult to avoid and does a lot of **Bravery** damage if you're up in the air. When the attack box appears, stop moving to avoid the flame pillars, then once they appear to have stopped, dodge away to avoid the fire from below, which cannot be blocked, but can be cancelled out by some attacks like **Grand Lethal** and Braver. **Terra**'s **Tornado**, if you're using her, cancels out the entire attack. After using it **Chaos** is very vulnerable if successfully dodged, so take advantage.

**Chaos** has the best summon in the game - **Shinryu**. **Shinryu** does various things between each round and essentially copies other summon effects. He can - like **Ifrit** increase **Chaos**'s **Bravery** by half, like **Alexander** freeze his **Bravery**, like **Behemoth** double it at the cost of -60 every few seconds for some time, like **Pupu** half it then give him +60 boosts, like **Omega** half your **Bravery** every other second for a period of time, like **Ultima Weapon** charge energy to instantly **Break** you, like **Iron Giant** half your **Bravery** when he deals HP damage for a period of time, like **Tiamat** activate a timer to triple his **Bravery**, and like **Lich** activate a timer to sap your **Bravery** to 0. Phew! And for extra fun - **Shinryu** is never used up, he'll eventually recharge and the summonstone icon over **Chaos**'s HP bar will reappear for another summon.

**Chaos** has no **EX Mode** or **EX Gauge** to fill, no Assist and no accessories, and custom equipment that gives him lower HP than **Garland** and the **Emperor**, but otherwise stats equal to them. The arena is a new one, the Edge of Madness, a very small arena with little room to move about, perfect for **Chaos** of course. Be warned here, **EX Cores** appear only on the throne, and if not taken after being out, will just vanish into nothingness, taking with them all the **EX Force** they absorbed.

**Chaos** has a lot of powerful **Bravery** attacks, they come out fast and are hard to predict and avoid if you're unfamiliar with him. Fortunately aside from Divine Punishment his HP attacks are easy to avoid. Pummel him as best you can and don't be afraid to retry, he's quite powerful. Nothing else I can really say about him, his melee combos come out fast, do a lot of damage and can be blocked, but you have to know how to anticipate them. When he falls though, of course that's not it - it's time for Round 2!

Yes kiddies, **Chaos** must be beaten three times to be put down, and similar to a party battle, during that three match run anyone who takes damage or dies, they don't get healed. In his second form **Chaos** has two new attacks - a new **Bravery** attack, Onslaught, three teleporting uppercuts from portals in the ground, impossible to block but easy to dodge. And he'll use his Demonsdance HP attack, very dangerous, he erratically teleports around the arena, clawing, slashing and spinning to get you and wall rush you, dodge continuously and stay in the air, on the ground it's much harder to avoid. And if he uses **Shinryu**'s **Bravery** freeze, ho boy, you are so screwed because Demonsdance can do HP damage several times, so with frozen **Bravery** getting caught in it is a death sentence. Hope you have an **Assist Change** or **EX Revenge** ready! Otherwise it's the same as the first round.

And now comes Round 3. He has a new HP attack - Scarlet Rain. **Chaos** will summon magma on the arena floor, damaging and stunning you so you have to stay in the air. He then sends a barrage of fireballs at you, block continuously against them and then dodge the last giant one. However, while he has no other new attacks, the old ones are stronger - Negashock now has him teleport in front of you before attacking, Earthwalker is evolved into Earthcrusher which fires three times, Darkflame Blast staggers on block, Darkflame Fang and Darkflame Talon create waves of fire for more range and damage, and Divine Punishment summons larger flame pillars. And of course, he now has his Limit **Break** - Utter **Chaos**. I won't describe it because it is honestly one of the coolest limit breaks in the entire series and I won't spoil what it does. Here's the basics - it begins with a downward slash, then he attacks three more times, each time using a random one of three attacks - a forward stab that sends fire across the arena, a downward stab that sends a fire plume at you, and a swing that creates explosions. For the final blow, make sure you aren't standing on one of the glowing circles and get to safe ground quickly before they explode and you rake HP damage.

Not much else to say on this guy. He's pretty tough, fast attacks, they do a lot of damage, his HP attacks are tricky to avoid, and he has a Broken summon with variable effects. Remember to **Assist Change** and **EX Revenge** out of his attacks, and that you have five characters, if one dies in comes the next one, so don't feel pressured to have one character beat all three forms, though of course it's possible. Also note that you only get one summon per character, so make it count. Use all you've learned to take out **Chaos** and save the world.

When **Chaos** is defeated in the third round and lets out a cry of defeat, it's finally over, enjoy the ending kiddies!

# Epilogue Part 1

Part 20 - the time to end Light to All and bring this cycle to a close is upon us!

#### Preparations

In the Epilogue you can use whatever party of 5 you like, and in each gateway your Assist will be randomly chosen from the five people you left out. You can use whoever you like, I took a part of **Zidane**, **Terra**, **Cloud**, **Firion** and **Squall**. I bought all of them two more accessory slots, for a total of 8, and that **Sunrise** is finally gone, we need no more Battlegen until certain gateways in Confessions of the Creator. The line-up for them all is a **Power Ring**, **Hyper Ring**, **Muscle Belt** (**Earring** for **Terra**, obviously), HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, Summpon Unused, (Opp) **Break**, and **Level 10-19**. The **Power Ring** is back because I have no worthwhile 1.3x boosters, and a 1.2x boost to 12% is less than a flat 3% boost. I bought **Zidane** an extra accessory slot to give him the Rabbit's Foot so **Cactuar** recharges faster, just in case.

Double-check everyone's equipment, the characters from the earlier storylines may be outdated. During the storyline we'll hit **Level 30** and we'll cover the equipment I know you've all been waiting for. Also check abilities in case anyone learned new ones in the final battles of their storylines, a few did for me. When entering each gateway in this storyline, remember your Assist is random - if you get one you don't like, just exit the gateway and re-enter. Everyone save for the **Warrior of Light** is level 15 or 16, but the bonus line is 23, odd. Still no exp bonuses for us, I don't want to dilute the difficulty by being overleveled - not that the difficulty has been very high anyway, right?

#### A Light in the Darkness

With **Cosmos** dead, the ten warriors fade away one by one, **Kefka** taunting it's their fault she was killed. As they fall into darkness however, the **Crystals** light up the area and they find themselves back in **Order's Sanctuary**. They're on borrowed time, but they have to track down the **Chaos** Warriors, find out what happened to **Cosmos**, and take out **Chaos**!

As you should be used to, there are gonna be Party pieces to contend with in the epilogue. To refresh your memory, in Tournament the party leaders fight, whoever dies is replaced with the next person in line, and so on until one party is entirely beaten. Round-Robin, party leaders fight, then second in line fight, until one party has the majority win (two wins against a three-enemy party). I'll be using **Round Robin** for all my fights, but if you like Tournament you can use Tournament. The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win, and there's no way to chain all enemies here so beat the Party piece and ignore the last two, it's a waste of a chain skill to get the two of them. The blue emblem is a booster x5, always welcome.

As we enter battle, we get notified of the **Omega** arenas. All arenas in this last chapter are the **Omega** versions, I'll explain what they do as they come up. The first foe is a **Fallacious Tree** with **Sword Dance** and **Hurricane**. He's fought in **World of Darkness** Ω, here wisps of darkness appear to signal an arena shift, if you're near the opponent the arena becomes a very small closed-in room dominated by pillars, if you're far away the arena becomes a huge-wide open arena of platforms connected by grind bars. In both, the **Bravery** pool rises rapidly depending on distance, in the close arena it rises faster the closer you are, and vice-versa for the open arena.

Second is a **Delusory Warlock** with **Rising Wave**, **Gravity System**, **Nightglow**, the **Magus Sisters** summon to halve your **Bravery** when you **Break** him and a **Hyper Ring** with 8 HP = 100% 1.5x boosters, and a No BRV Damage 3x booster. Naturally priority goes to turning them off fast. Contrary to previous chapters, enemies switch up accessories throughout the journey rather than all of them using one set-up. The Warlock is fought in Lunar Subterrane Ω, pretty lame gimmick, the cliffs now boost the stage **Bravery** when destroyed. The last foe is an **Imaginary Champion** with **Godspeed**, **Shadow Flare**, **Black Materia**, **Shiva** for a summon, the **Hyper Ring** set-up, and is fought in **Empyreal Paradox** Ω. Here there are wisps of light near the **Crystal** that slowly drain the **Bravery** of nearby fighters and transfer the drained **Bravery** to the stage pool. Quite helpful in Confessions of the Creator since it helps you whittle down the **Bravery** of high-level foes.

The last two enemies, if you must fight them, are a **Counterfeit Wraith** in the **Empyreal Paradox** with **Tentacle of Spite** (large energy orb blast), **Anti-Air Particle Beam**, the **Hyper Ring** stuff, and **Ultros**. The other is an Ephmeral Phantom with **Jecht Rush**, **Jecht** Block, and is fought in **Chaos Shrine** Ω. Here, both characters are regularly drained of a percentage of their **Bravery** and have it added to the **Bravery** pool.

The chest has 28,000 gil.

As we leave, **Exdeath** confronts us and explains the truth of why **Cosmos** was killed, a truth we already know from the 12th cycle - the **Crystals** are fragments of **Cosmos**'s power, and to obtain them was to deprive her of that power. We've only survived because her power is in those **Crystals** still, but it won't last long. Now of course comes the inevitable fight. I won't do boss templates this time, you can see the boss's stats for yourself, I'll note moves and summon and such in text.

**Exdeath** is a tough cookie, **Level 26** with **Level 30** equipment, an **Earring** connected to 6 Near Opponent boosters (hello **Hurricane**, OW THAT HURTS!), and a new accessory to us, Prayer Beads, which will sap our critical hit chances. He has a **Fallacious Wanderer** Assist, **Tiamat** as a summon, and the typical tricks for his moveset, **Reverse Polarity**, **Black Hole**, **Hurricane**, **Turn Guard**, **Omni Block**, Malestrom, **Delta Attack** and **Almagest**. And we're fighting on The Rift Ω, one of the coolest **Omega** arenas. As you can see everything is topsy-turvy, turrets stacked on each other and spires floating in the air. Periodically the castle features will rearrange themselves in new ways, increasing the **Bravery** pool during the process. Be sure to grab any **EX Cores** as they appear because they may vanish during the shake-up.

Because he is so predictable and you know how to bait him, this fight isn't hard, just long, he has high defenses and a lot of **Bravery** and HP to whittle down. Attack when he isn't nearby to charge up your Assist and perform combo attacks to up the damage output, and remember to use your summon if needed. Overall it'll take time to put him down due to his power levels exceeding any prior opponent, but you'll prevail in the end.

**Exdeath** fades away for good now, laughing, and the warriors wonder why **Cosmos** would have them get the **Crystals** if it meant her destruction. They decide she must have known she was going to be destroyed and gave them her power as a last hope, so let's not waste it. Also fun note, the scenes before and after the **Exdeath** fight, along with two others, change depending on who the party leader is.

We unlock a new Report now, if you want to go watch, no fights though so I won't cover it. While I checked though I swung by Chapter 5 in the 012 story to visit the moogle who sells rare items, and bought a **KP Bonus**. If you kiddies can go buy one, it depends on your bonus day, do so. I'll be saving this for later, mwa ha ha! I also brought the **Pre-EX Mode** booster. It remains active as long as you haven't used **EX Mode** during the fight and is a nice 1.4x booster, so it replaced the **Power Ring** on my party members. There are other ones including Pre-Air Dash and **Pre-EX Revenge**, guess how they work, but they're 200 **KP** each and I could only afford one.

#### Clipping An Angel's Wings

On the world map now, we're in a new end of the **Land of Discord**, behind us is a long, empty beach. Moving ahead there's a chest with 20,000 gil, and to the right a moogle and a chest with a **BRV Charge**. The moogle sells trade accessories we don't need anymore, you found a lot of them in the storylines up to this point. Buy a **Multichain** and a **Straight Chain**. Approach the blue gateway. **Cloud** senses **Sephiroth** inside, so let's go say hello. A warning, once you enter the gateway, the only way out is through **Sephiroth**, you can exit the gateway as you can with all of them but you won't get **KP**.

**Straight Chain** at B7, of course. True there's only three but a **Straight Chain** is 8 **KP** and we'll win 9 for the three chain, so it's a slight profit, right? The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. The emblem is a magical critical rate up, so if you brought **Terra** along - and why the hell didn't you if not? - consider swapping her in to take advantage of it. Otherwise **Zidane**'s **Meo Twister**, **Tidus**'s **Energy Rain**, and everyone else's usual spells will appreciate the benefits. Don't worry about being hurt, there's a **Potion** for us before we leave and confront Sephy.

First comes an **Imaginary Champion** with the **Moogle** summon, a **Hyper Ring** and 9 Near Loss boosters, that's gonna suck when they go off. He uses **Shadow Flare**, **Fervent Blow** and **Godspeed**. Next is the Strange enemy, a **Delusory Dragoon**, it begins with a lot of **Bravery**, spams Jump, and has **Cactuar**, an easy victory. The Expert piece is a **False Stalwart** with two **Muscle Belts**, six Near Opponent, an after 45 Seconds, and the **Cactuar** summon. For his moveset he has Twist Drill, Twin Swords, Bardiche, Blaze, and **Flare**. He's fought in Dream's End Ω, the **Bravery** pool will skyrocket here as every attack, successful or not, will drive up the **Bravery** pool. He's pretty tough but you can handle him, he's oddly not as smart as I've come to expect from Expert enemies.

The chest has an Epitaph Stone, which is needed for some level 30 and 60 gear. I also became able to buy the accessories for such in the shop by trading in Elixirs, but we don't need any right yet.

A nice change between Dissidia and D012 was that in Shade Impulse, you got a special scene if your character as a villain's rival, for the other villains aside from **Exdeath**, the **Emperor** and **Garland**, you just got a couple text boxes. D012 includes all the scenes, so even if **Cloud** isn't your party leader, or not in the party at all, you'll see this scene when you leave. **Sephiroth** does what he does best - spout cryptic meaningless Ho Yay babble about how **Cloud** clings to him out of despair because he holds onto hope that's eating him like a disease. **Cloud** will just have to endure it then. And of course now it's fight time, sorry **Cloud**, you can square off but this is **Zidane**'s show.

**Sephiroth** too has level 30 equipment, he has a significantly greater attack strength than **Exdeath**, but significantly lower defenses. He's got over 4,200 HP and a **Muscle Belt** hooked up to 6 Attacking **Bravery** boosters, ouch. He has the **Alexander** summon and an **Imaginary Soldier** Assist. His moveset is the usual - Reaper, **Fervent Blow**, **Transience**, **Oblivion**, **Godspeed**, **Shadow Flare**. For HP attacks, **Hell's Gate**, **Octaslash**, Scintilla - I didn't see them but assume **Black Materia** and **Heaven's Light** are there too. The stage, **Planet's Core** Ω, is one of two stages that I consider the worst in the game. After a period of time the stage will shake and shift form. The floating rocks vanish and more tendrils of lifestream to quickmove on appear. The only platforms now aside from the large central one at the bottom are a handful of floating pillars that crumble when dashed into - and of course **EX Cores** will appear on top of them and vanish when you dash into the pillar trying to get to them. This is annoying enough, the lack of solid ground, but what makes the stage terrible is the AI *loves* to quickmove along the grindbars and up the walls endlessly for no reason, forcing you to track them down. You'll see soon enough. For a fun twist though, try going into an **EX Burst** during the stage shift, the **EX Burst** background will be lit up!

**Sephiroth** is overall a pushover, he has a heavy defense penalty compared to **Exdeath** and **Zidane** quickly sapped his HP away with his **Tempest** and **Swift Attack** HP links. True his own damage output is also pretty impressive, but you know how (not) aggressive the AI is, and it won't take advantage of this. As for damage output - **Zidane**'s **EX Burst** did 1000 damage, plus the **Bravery** pool was higher due to the stage shifting. All in all, once you get **Sephiroth** to half HP, as long as his **Bravery** isn't in **Break** status so you can re-Break him, an **EX Burst** can kill him. Beaten, **Sephiroth** stands up...somehow in front of **Cloud** again, and declares he doesn't exist...okay. **Cloud** rejects his taunts again and **Sephiroth** claims his shadow is burned into **Cloud**'s heart and that they will meet again. Did a yaoi fangirl write this dialogue?

On the world map again, go right to find a **Maser Eye** and another blue gateway with treasure. Save the **Maser Eye**, we'll have use for it later.

**Multichain** at C3 to get all four enemies. I swapped **Terra** in as party leader for this and the next gateway. The emblem is a magical critical rate up, **Terra** likes that of course. The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win, and there's another Expert enemy so let's fill that **EX Gauge**! Also note my party is outgrowing the **Level 10-19** booster, replace it with a **Power Ring** when that occurs since **Level 20-29** is only a 1.2 booster.

The first enemy is a **Counterfeit Wraith** with **Tentacle of Suffering** (aerial spin attack), she's fought in **Crystal** World Ω. Here platforms of crystal appear in the air, and the **Bravery** pool rises each time. Destroying the crystal platforms causes more to appear in new formations, in turn raising the **Bravery** pool again. The second enemy is a **Phantasmal Harlequin** with the two **Firaga** attacks, **Hyperdrive**, **Moogle** summon, and a **Hyper Ring** with 9 Near Loss. Next is an **Idle Sky Pirate** with high initial **Bravery**, the **Moogle** summon, and **Windburst**.

With the enemies beaten, take the Polished Ore from the chest and leave.

On the overworld again head to the shoreline to find a rock to break to reach an island with a Gargoyle Pebble. Now just the next gateway.

#### Cruel Truths

As we enter, the **Emperor** appears before us. The party is surprised to see him alive since they thought **Firion** beat him. How could anyone tell, the villains almost always fade away when beaten but yet they come back for more later. The **Emperor** is looking to rule this world, and declares that with **Chaos**'s victory, the **Warriors of Chaos** are free to pursue their own desires outside the war. He vanishes with a laughing, and the Warriors continue on.

There's any number of ways to get both Party pieces. A **Round Chain** or **Cross Chain** can do it at D5, missing the emblem, while a **Straight Chain** or **Multichain** at D4 can use the emblem. It depends on what you have I used a **Round Chain** since it's cheaper/easier to replace. There are six enemies here, but the **KP Gambler** should still be saved. The bonus line is 28 - HA! - and the **KP Chance** is a 60 second **Break**.

First is a **Transient Witch** with Knight's Blade, Knight's Arrow, **Great Attractor**, the **Carbuncle** summon, and the accessory gimmick of this gateway, it should seem familiar - a **Zephyr Cloak**, **Heart's Ease**, **Iifa Dew**, and 7 Near Loss, giving them a **Bravery** boost whenever they block or dodge an attack once you have enough **Bravery** to kill them. The Dew is different from the Leaf, you don't survive with 1 HP, your **Bravery** drains to 0 and you get as much HP as **Bravery** lost. It doesn't work if they're in **Break** though, keep that in mind. Next comes a **Phantasmal Harlequin** with the same accessories, **Havoc Wing**, and **Demon Wall**. The third foe, which you don't have to fight in **Round Robin**, is a **Capricious Reaper** with **Seraphic Star** and those accessories again

The second Party piece begins simply with a **Capricious Reaper** with **Seraphic Star** and nothing else. Second is a **Transient Witch** with Shockwave Pulsar. The last one, which again you don't have to fight if you use **Round Robin**, is a **False Stalwart** with Twin Swords, **Magus Sisters**, and the **Iifa Dew** set-up.

With 36 **KP** in our pocket, open up the chests for Lizardman Skin and 29,000 gil. At the end, **Terra** finds **Kefka** and demands answers from him, but **Kefka**, being **Kefka** just taunts her and laughs that at this point it doesn't matter and attacks.

**Kefka** doesn't have quite as much attack strength as **Sephiroth**, but has higher defense. He's got an **Earring** with 6 Far From Opponent, ouch, a Bomb for a summon and a **Phantasmal Girl** Assist. His moveset is the two **Thundaga** attacks, **Scatter-Spray Blizzaga**, Extra-Crispy **Firaga**, and **Meteor**, with all his HP hits. The stage is **Kefka's Tower** Ω, here every time a part of the stage is destroyed, "magitek steam" will blast out of the walls in the lower parts of the factory, damaging the **Bravery** of fighters that are hit and increasing the **Bravery** pool. This can also occur when wall rushing them in the lower parts, and can lead to a very amusing "pinball" effect of knocking them into a wall and then they bounce back and forth for a second between vents of steam.

**Kefka**'s attacks pack a serious wallop, even without those boosters activated, and are always tricky to avoid, especially **Scatter-Spray Blizzaga**. However, as you may remember from her chapter in her **EX Mode** **Terra** becomes a goddess of magical carnage, and entering battle with a full **EX Gauge** I had little trouble taking **Kefka** down with her power boost - just remember to enter Chases to keep the gauge filled! In the aftermath of the fight, **Kefka** is still yearning for destruction, and laments that hope and dreams aren't enough to make a fulfilling life, and explodes in a sad laugh as he calls out for yet more destruction. **Terra** wonders if that was why **Kefka** destroyed, and continues on.

Now we get an interlude scene of **Garland** confronting **Golbez** on his hand in the events that have unfolded, revealing that the cycles were to go on forever to temper **Chaos** into a perfect weapon of destruction. **Golbez**, stunned, asks **Garland** just who he is.

We also unlock another Report, with no battles so as I said I won't cover it.

Back on the world map, **Cloud** is gonna be the party leader for the next couple of gateways. I also swapped **Firion** and **Squall** up to the second and third slots, as they haven't been getting Exp from Party pieces and so they and **Cloud** are a few levels behind **Zidane** and **Terra**. Plus it helps keep things fresh, and while the non-fighters get exp, they don't get **AP**, so let's spread the love.

Goi around the beach to where the teleport stone used to be, the chest has a Treant Branch. Shatter the rock and grind over to find a Silver Lobo Claw. As a note, the party members here will note the lack of enemies outside the gateways, wondering if the destruction of the world has destroyed them, and note the teleport stone is gone. Approach the blue gateway and **Onion Knight** will sense an old friend inside. If you need a **Straight Chain**, go back and grab one from the moogle, and grab a **Cross Chain** while you're over there too.

The last foe is an **Ephemeral Phantom** with the same accessories, but that's all I know, I didn't have to actually fight him and neither do you.

The chest has a Wererat Claw, then approach the end piece. The **Onion Knight** approaches the **Cloud of Darkness**, and declares that even if **Cosmos** is gone, he can still fight to uphold her last hope, and of course fight time.

The **Cloud of Darkness** is actually only slightly stronger than **Exdeath**, with a lower level and attack and defense on par or worse than **Kefka**'s. Her accessories are decent, a **Pearl Necklace** and an **Attractorb** Orb with the 15, 30, 45, 60 and 90 Second boosters, one each. She has the **Land Worm** summon, all four tentacle attacks, and a few new HP attacks. Feint **Particle Beam**, only on the ground, has her vanish into a dark portal to dodge attacks then reappear somewhere else to fire an orb of darkness as you, fairly easy to avoid, and Wrath **Particle Beam** is an aerial move where she blocks and summons an orb of darkness to strike you. It's not impervious to all attacks like **Omni Block**, but can block anything with lower priority than High, which mostly means any non-HP attacks.

She's pretty simple to beat, unless you find yourself tripped up by her new HP attacks that is. All her **Bravery** attacks can be blocked and punished, and **Land Worm**'s drain is only a nuisance, as long as you stay close to her it won't do much, and you want to be close so she's more likely to do a **Bravery** attack and you can block and punish her. And since I was using **Cloud**, my **EX Mode** gave me free reign to go nuts on her since all of **Cloud**'s attacks break or stagger her on a conflict between attacks. With the **Cloud of Darkness** weakened, she tells the **Onion Knight** that there is no point to trying anymore, but the **Onion Knight** has to listen to his heart over his head. The **Cloud of Darkness** fades away into a portal of darkness, returning to her slumber.

On the overworld map again, head down the path to find a chest with an **EX Charge**, then head into the gateway.

**Cross Chain** at C1 to get all enemies here, the blue emblem is a magic critical rate up, but I'm sticking to **Cloud** for now so it'll be passed up. The bonus line is 30 and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. Also note bonus line, in all storylines, takes into account the levels of the entire party, and looks at whoever has the highest level among them to determine the starting **KP**. I used my **EX Charge**, might as well, three slots are being taken up by a **KP Gambler**, **KP Bonus** and **Maser Eye**. It'll be worth it, kiddies, be patient.

The Strange enemy is first, an **Ephemeral Summoner**. She has high starting **Bravery**, summons **Tiamat**, and spams **Thor's Hammer**. An easy win, remember to attack through **Thor's Hammer** with HP attacks like **Rough Divide**, Straightarrow, Braver, etc. Next is the Expert piece, a **Counterfeit Wraith** with all four Tentacle attacks, Anti-Air, Fusillade, Wrath and 0-Form Particle Beams, the **Ifrit** summon, and unknown Assist, and some accessories of course - a **Hyper Ring** and **Guardian Bangle** with 8 HP = 100% boosters. The stage is **Ultimecia's Castle** Ω. Here, regularly Time Compression will occur, randomizing the **Bravery** pool rapidly, even during an **EX Burst**, and reforming destroyed stage elements. The gears in the stage also spin rapidly, damaging characters and knocking them around. **Cloud**'s **EX Mode** helps a lot to deplete the Expert's high **Bravery** in time to meet the **KP Chance**.

Now it's Party time of course. First, a wimpy **Phantasmal Harlequin** with **Hyperdrive**. Next is an **Imitation Despot** with **Thunder Crest**, **Light Crest** and **Flare**. He's fought in **Pandaemonium** Ω, here the two halves of the stage (the pit and hallway separate them) will have their floors turn red and black while the other is normal, then they switch. While fighting over the red and black areas, spikes will pop out at random and stab into the air, knocking characters into the air and damaging their **Bravery** and adding it to the pool. Spikes can also hit and lock Assists so be cautious calling them in such areas. The grey floors are safe, fortunately. The last enemy I avoided is a **False Stalwart** with a **Hyper Ring** hooked up to 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value, and he uses **Cyclone**.

The chest has a Scorpion, a level 30 weapon we thus cannot use yet.

As we reach the end we come upon **Kuja**, still spouting off his poetry. **Zidane** still refuses to give up, and of course there's only one thing to do - let another party member beat the shit out of him.

**Kuja**'s offense is only equal to the **Cloud** of Darknesses, but he's got the highest defense yet, and as with all previous bosses a few hundred more HP over the last one. His boosters are pretty pathetic though, a **Pearl Necklace**, **Attractorb** and **Earring** with three Aerial boosters, that's only a 3.3x booster, we have more than that! Give him at least five or so. For moves he's got **Ring Holy**, Remote **Flare**, **Burst Energy**, **Strike Energy**, **Snatch Shot**, **Flare Star**, **Seraphic Star**, **Force Symphony**, and **Ultima** - basically, all the moves he's ever had. His summon is **Magic Pot** and he's got a **Capricious Thief** Assist. Also, they got it right this time and use the proper boss music they should have had in Chapter 8.

I decided not to use **Cloud**'s **EX Mode** and fought normally, and since I had no HP links yet, it was a rough ride. **Kuja**'s attacks are always annoying to avoid, especially his HP attacks, and he was good about charging and using his **Capricious Thief** Assist. **Rising Fang** was my main offense since **Cloud** has trouble striking from above and being in front of **Kuja** is about the worst place you can be. True, being below him is never a good idea...god I can write innuendo so well ;p, but **Rising Fang** is a decently quick response, even striking **Kuja** during his **Force Symphony** conducting. So overall, he's pretty tough without an **EX Gauge** to help you out. With one? Crush him, **Cloud**'s **EX Mode** bonus will blast his **Bravery** into oblivion and can clinch the match by knocking off half his HP with ease.

As **Kuja** lies dying, he asks **Zidane** to finish him off, as his inability to understand hope means life itself has defeated him. And yet, **Zidane** still tries to help him. **Kuja** cannot understand why and pushes him away, fading away with dignity.

Afterward we see a scene of the **Emperor** and **Jecht**. The **Emperor** tells **Jecht** that with **Cosmos** dead, soon **Chaos**'s warriors too will fade, and when **Jecht** protests, the **Emperor** tells him he isn't one of **Chaos**'s minions. We also unlock **Report 15**, with fights this time.

On the overworld map again, it's **Firion**'s turn to lead the posse. Note **Bartz** has a very intriguing thing to say here if you chat him up. Go along the path to a blue gateway, on the left is a chest with an **Assist Charge** and further down the path is a chest with a Zu Feather. Now we're heading into the gateway of course, we need a **Cross Chain**, go all the way back and buy one, and to save a trip get a **Multichain** too. As we approach the gateway, **Squall** senses a particular someone inside.

**Cross Chain** at E3 to get all five, yup, all five Strange enemies. And they all use different gimmicks. This is gonna be a treat! The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win, absolute piece of cake, take your time filling up that **EX Gauge** kiddies.

First is a **Capricious Thief**, he has an **Iifa Leaf** and barely enough HP to use it as always, an initial **Bravery** boost, an **Arcane Resin**, the **Mandragora** summon to keep his **Bravery** regen rate up to insane levels, and he spams **Grand Lethal**. Keep your distance from him to avoid it and get him as he recovers. Second is an **Ephemeral Vision** with an **Arcane Resin**, a **Muscle Belt**, **Hyper Ring**, and 5 Near Opponent boosters, with the **Moogle** summon for random fun, and it likes Wither Shot. Third is an **Imaginary Brawler** with a **Bravery** boost, **Cactuar**, and it likes **Blizzara**. The fourth foe is an **Imaginary Soldier** with a *huge* **Bravery** boost, **Odin**, and it likes using **Slashing Blow** to Omnislash Version 5 HP link. The last enemy is a **Transient Lion** who likes Fusillade and **Beat Fang**, he comes into the fight with 0 **Bravery** and a **Guardian Bangle** connected to three HP = 1 boosters, and of course, it uses the **Carbuncle** summon at the start of the fight.

The chest has a Cockatrice Feather.

As we arrive at **Ultimecia**'s lair, **Squall** asks her what she wants. **Ultimecia** wants a world of compressed time where **Squall** will worship her as a god. **Squall** tells her in no uncertain terms "get over yourself", and **Ultimecia** says he's just the same as hers, trying to create his vision of an ideal world. **Squall** agrees, and says he has a promise to keep. They then exchange the obligatory taunts before fighting...for...some reason. Well, **Squall**'s entire story was full of mixed messages and confusing morals, why break tradition?

**Ultimecia** naturally has all previous moves you've dealt with, and a new one, Hell's Judgment, it creates a crest of magic below you and blasts you up into the air. For accessories she's got an **Earring** with 2 HP = 1, 2 Near Death, 3 Near Loss, and an **Iifa Leaf**, giving her an impressive boost to damage on the bring of death. She also uses the **Marilith** summon, has a **Transient Lion** Assist, and has the high attack power of **Kefka** and **Sephiroth** with the high defense of **Kuja**, giving her the best stats yet.

However, she is, as always, an idiot. At one point with **Firion** I just spammed Straightarrow - yes she dodged it sometimes, but it also hit far too often, because she would respond by using an attack of her own, making her unable to dodge. I also had great ease catching her with Reel Axe to **Bravery** pain, and in general she was just really bad at avoiding **Firion**'s attacks. And you've used him, you know it is not difficult to avoid **Firion**'s attacks, *especially* Straightarrow. Even that aside, I could just camp and use Shield Bash over and over, all of her uncharged attacks - which are mostly all I found her using - trigger it. Also have fun throwing a Reel Axe at her during a charged Knight's Blade, the Axe has higher damage priority and will deflect the blades as it flies towards her. The only complications were **Marilith** and the **Iifa Leaf**, but they only work once and are just nuisances.

As **Ultimecia** stands weakened and vanishing, she philosophizes that time will always slip through your grasp and the happiness of the moment is fleeting - all she wants is to take hold of that. **Squall** then strikes her down before she can continue, and says he can't let anyone take his time from him. So once again, the game portrays **Squall** as a callous, self-righteous prick.

On the world map, head to the next gateway, collecting the orbs of the dead and a Shockwave from the chest, a new skill that reduces enemies to half their normal levels in chains.

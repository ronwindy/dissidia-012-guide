<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Warrior has mostly attacks you've seen from his Manikins:

- Dayflash is a quick ground combo
- Blue Fang summons ice shards to shoot at you from above
- Ascension is a short-range shield throw into the air
- Crossover is basically the same in the air
- Shield Strike is an aerial downward shield throw
- Rising Buckler is an upward one

His HP attacks are where this gets deadly.

- Ultimate Shield has him pause for a moment, then throw his shield at you to a surprising range, and drags you closer to combo you.
- Shining Wave sends a pillar of light at you, tracking you into the air
- Bitter End and Rune Saber are a new type of trick - HP Links.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Some Bravery attacks can link right into HP attacks without pause, and the Warrior has four of them here, Ascension, Crossover, Shield Strike and Rising Buckler. This makes avoiding his Bravery attacks more important than previous enemies, taking Bravery hits has been fine before since they can't actually damage HP, here, he can link them to attacks that can. Fortunately all four of those attacks are a bit slow on the start-up so you can block them.

And if he Breaks you, well you're in for a real rough time of it because he has Alexander, which freezes his Bravery at its current value, preventing him from increasing it yes, but also preventing you from damaging it, and if he lands an HP hit, guess what, he doesn't reset to 0. Do not let him Break you whatever you do.

And to wrap this all up, he's got six boosters and a Power Ring to give him a decent buff when he attacks with a Bravery attack. Be thankful it's not a Hyper Ring or Muscle Belt.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Similar to the Emperor, the Warrior has a pretty good set-up. What saves us and dooms him is once again the AI's incompetence, he can't use his moveset effective. He has HP Links mostly in the hypothetical sense, the AI doesn't much use them when it should be using them every chance it has because it has.

Smack the Warrior around with EX Mode Lightning's simpler but more effective Smite and Launch to Break him, and I had Ifrit here to boost me up to 1600 Bravery or so. Then I let him recover his Bravery and whipped out my EX Burst, Breaking him again and killing him. Not a very difficult battle, especially compared to the Ephemeral Phantom and even then he wasn't too hard.

</PanelSection>

---

Cosmos breaks up the brawl and the Warrior confesses to what's going on. Kain told him that the wars are a cycle that will be repeated, and if they can incapacitate their allies and keep them safe from the manikins until the next cycle begins, they'll be able to survive the manikin horde and awaken to fight again. However, if they keep fighting now the manikins will eventually overrun them through sheer numbers.

Thus Kain set out to find the others before the manikins do, put them to sleep, and hide them away to save them. Lightning is promptly pissed at this plan, and sees the pretty obvious flaw of it - who's to say when they wake up next time the manikins won't still be around, and what's it matter either way if the war is indeed endless?

---

Laguna, Vaan and Yuna arrive in time to hear what's going on, and when the Warrior tells them that there's nothing to doubt accept defeat and let the cycle run its course, Lightning is again unimpressed and says that before the current cycle ends they might as well go down fighting and killing as many as they can.

However, Laguna has a third option to let us know about and tells them about the portal they're coming from. Well, there's our path then. The four set out with the Warrior staying to guard Cosmos.

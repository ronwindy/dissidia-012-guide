# Chapter 5

Chapter 5, the plot thickens!

#### The Fabled Gun Appears

We're back with Lightning now, on her way to Sanctuary. As we suit up, you'll learn there's new equipment in the shop, better stuff in general, including an Altair! We can finally get the Capella we've been dreaming of! Well I have very nerdy dreams, excuse me.

There's just for one problem, the Altair is 3800 gil and the Capella is another another 15000 on top of that. Well, fortunately we have something expensive to sell off. Yup, we're gonna ditch the Pink Tails. You always knew they'd come in handy some day, didn't you? I know you did, I told you so, this is just a different use than what I said back then.

The six tails net us 10000 gil, getting us halfway there about. To get the rest of the way I sold off the Chocobo Wings I had from my Play Plan, that Slasher axe, and the "Soul" breaking accessories.

---

As for other new goodies:

- The Earring is a very nice accessory to boost magic damage, but I haven't got the goods for it, it needs 3 Sephira, 3 Girl's Dream and 3 Summoner's Dream, so if my memory serves you *could* have these items, and if so buy it if you have it, it's a great accessory.
- The Muscle Belt boosts physical damage but we won't be getting it for a long time because the arena associated with the Promyvion Shard is not encountered until *way* later, as for storyline purposes it has a special usage.

With our glorious new weapon equipped, begin the storyline.

<PanelSection>

Immediately turn to the right and you'll see a blue gateway, head over naturally. There's a chest near it with a BRV Charge skill. The enemies inside aren't too tough, go ahead and use the BRV Charge if you like.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

As for the enemy types....sigh, they're back. Delusory Dragoon, Fleeting Flash, Idle Sky Pirate, and they haven't changed a bit.

- The Strange Battle Piece is the Sky Pirate, he's got a huge Bravery boost and Torrent, along with the Cactuar summon.
- The False Hero at A2 is new-ish, it has Ascension, a short-range shield throw that reaches into the air and knocks you into the sky to begin a chase, and Crossover, essentially a midair version of Ascension. Both have high power.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

At Level 9 Lightning learns Reverse Paradigm, which lets her cycle through them in reverse order. But we're not using the Medic role so she only has two, and thus the ability is useless.

At Level 11 she gets Lightning Strike, an effective mid-range HP attack that is a welcome addition to our ground arsenal when the alternative has been Razor Gale.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chests have an Iifa Leaf and a Soul of Patience, and then a Zombie Rag. It unlocks a single equipment piece, the Bard's Tunic, which is way out of our price range.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

---

Back outside follow the main path, grabbing two orbs of the dead along the way, to a ruined castle with a moogle and our next gateway. Turn right to another area with a Capricious Reaper, he won't last too long with three Cursed Rings on. Behind it is a chest with 1500 gil.

---

Approach the ruined castle and the moogle calls out to us as the "Thunder Lady" (I love that line, moogles are so adorable). Apparently he's the same moogle who greeted Lightning back in Chapter 1 and followed us around that little region, and now he's got his own shop, but not much for inventory.

All he has is Pebbles. They're useless as bought, but if you equip them, after a fight they have a random chance to break and become either another Pebble, or a Growth Seed, which is one of several trade accessories needed for the Growth Egg, along with some Chocobo accessories from the Play Plan. We'll worry about this later, no point giving up accessory slots for stuff we can't use yet.

<PanelSection>

In the gateway, open the chest for 2000 gil.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The two enemies you can skip but you won't, because you're not a wimp, are an Ephemeral Summoner and a False Hero. The Summoner has a new Bravery attack, Aerospark, a ground-based mid-range charge attack. Given that both are pushovers, draw out the fights to charge your EX Gauge.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As I fought Lightning learned Ground Dash. Ground Dash only works when you're on the ground and won't let you dash into the air, but when dashing you count as being on the ground, letting you quickly stop and use ground attacks.

Take the summonstone and Lightning unites with an old friend, Odin. He summons himself automatically when the opponent has enough Bravery to kill you in one hit, and tries to Break them.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Now, time for the last enemy. Warning - he's the most powerful enemy in the pre-Epilogue storyline.

The Ephemeral Phantom is Level 23 so he has a huge boost to his starting Bravery, and has Bahamut to boot. He has all of Jecht's attacks except for Ultimate Jecht Shot - that includes Jecht Block which again can block almost anything.

And as the highest level of battle piece possible, he's very good with them, and will chain his combos into HP attacks if he gets in a hit on you. And when he gets weak his boosters trigger for a 25x multiplier to his two Hyper Rings, giving him an insanely high damage buff.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

You need to be in top form to beat this guy. Begin by entering EX Mode and pummeling him what-good, he'll activate Bahamut. You've got a few choices for a summon of your own, I took Cactuar but I imagine Carbuncle is a better choice really, land an HP attack after Bahamut wears off to sap him of all his hard-boosted Bravery and Break him.

He's not *too* bad really, but he is easily the most competent opponent yet easily above the bosses, and if you're a new player he'll be quite a challenge I imagine.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The rewards for beating him are of course the two chests, which have a Blazefire Saber and a piece of Mythril. The Saber has power nowhere near that of the Capella, while the Mythril unlocks numerous items in the ship requiring it, none of which we'll buy because the ones I think worth buying, like the armor or the hand armor, either require two pieces or are too expensive.

It's okay kiddies, Mythril equipment is only worth using in threes, I'll explain why and cover buying it when we can get three of them.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Now chug the Potion to top off your HP and EX gauge after the fight, and approach the Cosmos piece. Lightning tells the Warrior of Light and Cosmos about the hell out there with the manikins running amock, Kain being a traitor and the rest of the warriors scattered to who-knows what fate.

Cosmos then senses Jecht's defeat and insists she can sense the rest of the group is alive in spite of what Lightning thinks of Kain's actions, but Lightning cuts her off to keep bitching. The Warrior decides he's had enough and raises his sword.

The line-up is the Party piece, consisting of a **Level 89** **Counterfeit Wraith** with **Atomos** and a **Level 121** **Imaginary Brawler**. Then comes a **Level 91** **Transient Witch** with **Deathgaze**, and a **Level 90** **Imaginary Champion** with **Bahamut** and **Imaginary Soldier**. I miss being able to go more in-depth on the enemies kiddies, but now they all have the same movesets and accessories, mostly, even between different types, so what can I do?

The chest has a Titan's Tear, a unique trade accessory. It unlocks the Giant's equipment - Giant's Axe, Giant's Gloves, Giant's Helm, and Giant's Harness. This set *isn't* entirely useless and in fact is quite use*ful*. When three pieces are worn together they trigger the Pride of the Titans combination, giving you an extra 100 base **Bravery** and 750 more HP. Though the pieces are only level 90, this boost is more than what you'd get if you traded out the Helm and Harness for **Level 100** gear. Thus the only penalty you get for opting for Giant's gear over **Level 100** stuff is slightly lower defenses. And as a three-piece combination, you only need the three armor pieces and can still use a **Level 100** weapon for no power loss.

Grab the **Phoenix Down** if anyone has died and head down. <br />

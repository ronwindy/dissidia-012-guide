# Chapter 5

Chapter 5, almost half-done the storyline!

#### The Ace

---

<CharacterIntro name="Tidus" src="drake/Dissidia_Tidus.png" width={150} height={230} alt="Dissidia Tidus">

**Tidus** has a heavily simplified story in Dissidia, but it's not his fault nor is it a bad thing. **Tidus** is consumed in his hatred for **Jecht** and wishes to defeat him, but his true reasons for this are buried. As we know from the last cycle, **Tidus** can't remember anything of his home yet except for **Jecht**, and his hatred for his father is all he has to drive him forward. And...that's it. Not much more to say there. **Tidus**'s story could have done with a bit more, for example with D012 expanding the story arc with the three FFX characters, maybe he begins to remember stuff and feels remorseful over being so malicious about **Jecht**? They could have done something more, the storyline works, but it's very two-dimensional.

**Tidus** is #4 of the characters I don't use. The reason is simple - his fighting style is to be a total pussy. **Tidus** has six attacks in air and ground, **Hop Step**, a blitzball throw, and a slow charging attack. His gimmick lies in the other three attacks - **Tidus** dodges and counterattacks, either leaping in the air to come down spinning, flipping backwards and stabbing forward, or dodging to the side and throwing his sword. I dislike this really cowardly, wimpy way to fight, and honestly it doesn't work as well as you might think reading how they work here, many times the opponent will recover and dodge away by the time **Tidus** attacks after dodging. And tt's impossible to be aggressive as **Tidus** because aside from **Hop Step** all his attacks come out ridiculously slow, and even when they do come out they got a huge damage nerf for D012. His HP attacks are equally slow and easy to avoid once you get the timing down. The only advantages **Tidus** really has going for him are high movement speed, and his attacks generate a lot of **EX Force**.

**Tidus** begins with **Sonic Buster**, a slow charging attack on the ground that drives the opponent down and away, and Dart \& Weave, where he dodges forward over the opponent's head and comes down spinning his sword into them. **Hop Step** is a quick but weak back-off aerial attack, and it's the only thing that makes **Tidus** less than totally unplayable since he could try some mind games, dash up to them and do Full Slide but they block early expecting **Hop Step** and now they're eating a Full Slide for being surprised. Wither Shot sucks, **Tidus** easily has the worst obligatory projectile attacks of the melee fighters. For HP attacks he has **Spiral Cut**, a series of flips across the ground that is ridiculously easy to avoid by getting into the air, and **Energy Rain**, which is a kick at the opponent and a leap back to send energy blasts at them.

Much like **Cecil** **Tidus** is capable in his own story, since his slow attacks are meaningless against the dipshit AI, but really, he's pretty terrible. Ironically given his movement speed and **EX Mode**, his main weakness are slow attacks and in my opinion they aren't very strong either.

</CharacterIntro>

The difficulty of this battle is directly proportional to if you charged up your **EX Gauge** or not before coming here. The **Emperor** has all the same tricks as before, but this time you're in his domain, the wide-open spaces of Dream's End and the **Empyreal Paradox** are gone. Here in **Pandaemonium** his spells take up half the hallways, his projectiles bounce like mad and interrupt your attacks, and in general the tight confines make it hard to avoid his traps. He's got five Blue Gems boosting his Base **Bravery** to 550 by default, and factor in he's probably got at *least* a dozen levels on you, maybe more, and he can blast you into hell with only a few attacks while also being very hard to **Break**. Remember to use **Energy Rain** to deflect **Flare** when you can, but avoid **Spiral Cut** since it has you move on start-up so if you mistime it **Tidus** will jump right into the flame and get hit. His summon **Atomos** sucks your **Bravery** out and gives it to him when you land an HP hit, but he seemed picky about using it and didn't do so until late in the fight when it didn't make much difference.

The reason the fight's difficulty depends on how much you built up your **EX Gauge** is because of **Tidus**'s **EX Mode** bonus. It's the same as **Cloud**'s bonus, he wields the Caladbolg and gets its benefit of doing up to twice as much damage as usual depending on his HP, along with the critical hit rate going up. Without **EX Mode** you're going to have to whittle the **Emperor** down, barring your **Cactuar** summon doing it for you one time, and he will do a lot of damage to you with ease. But with **EX Mode** a single Full Slide or **Sonic Buster** will **Break** him, and you can quickly rack up the **Bravery** needed to finish him off. As for **Tidus**'s other **EX Mode** benefits, let's take care of them while on the topic, he runs faster and deflects low-priority projectiles while running, and his dodging attacks make him invincible longer while dodging. His **EX Burst** isn't too hard, stop the cursor as close to the center as you can, you have enough time to let it make a couple passes while you pin down the timing of the cursor movement. Don't underestimate its damage just because the first few hits are weak, my **Level 5** **Tidus** did 600 **Bravery** damage on the last hit alone, the Burst's power is all in that final hit so it is crucial you nail it right.

As the fight ends, the **Emperor** blasts **Tidus** with a shock of electricity and vanishes with a laugh, mocking that hatred won't get him his **Crystal**.

**Jecht** has a full-move set of all his attacks, and **Jecht** Block in both ground and aerial flavors. Fortunately the AI is too dumb to use it correctly, if it was then **Jecht** would be nearly untouchable thanks to **Tidus**'s slow attacks. However, it will do it occasionally and you'll eat a **Jecht** Combo to the face. **Jecht** prefers his combos to his HP attacks in my experience, and he's got 3 Near Opponent boosters and 2 Attacking **Bravery**, giving him around a 2.4x booster to his **Muscle Belt**. If he goes to charge up a combo, jump away and use Slice \& Dice to smack him while he's helpless. His **Calcabrina** summon will remove your **Break** when he inflicts it, sapping you to 0 **Bravery** and a sitting duck, so land an HP hit to restore it. His Assist is an **Ephemeral Summoner**, aka **Yuna**.

**Jecht**, sadly, is a total pushover. An opening **Sonic Buster** followed by **Hop Step** Broke him on my game and the HP attack wall rushed him, and the follow-up **EX Burst** did 1800 damage with a second **Break**. As I said, he's just not much stronger than the **Emperor** and is less powerful comparatively to us than the **Emperor** was at that time.

As **Jecht** fades, **Tidus** makes a heartfelt confession to him, and his **Crystal** appears.

You also unlock **Report 10**, but it has no fights so I won't cover it.

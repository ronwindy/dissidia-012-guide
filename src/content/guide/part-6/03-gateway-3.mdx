<PanelSection kind="chain">

A Jump Chain at C2 or a Multichain at C3 will chain all enemies here. I had neither so I killed the enemy at D2 and then moved there and used a Round Chain.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies here are:

- a Delusory Dragoon with Demon Wall as a summon
- Fallacious Tree with Typhon
- Fleeting Flash with Odin
- another Fleeting Flash with nothing
- and the Expert Battle Piece, an Imitation Despot

The Tree is a new enemy type, an Exdeath manikin.

- It uses Sword Dance, pausing for a second or two before sweeping the sword, and Hurricane, surrounding itself in a whirlwind.
- Its HP attack is Delta Attack, where it creates a field of energy and fires a long-range blast of light from the center. The crest deflects attacks like a block so wait for the blast to fire and die down before countering.

The Tree will be encountered from now on of course, and it's Battlegen items can be used to trade for the Heart's Ease, which boosts your Bravery a little every time you block an attack, and isn't much worth it.

---

The Imitation Despot is a tough one. He has a Hyper Ring, Guardian Ring, and boosters that all trigger at full HP, so land an HP attack first thing to deactivate them and rob him of his considerable power boost.

He has:

- Landmine
- Mine
- Bombard
- Light Crest
- Thunder Crest
- the ground and aerial versions of Flare
- Starfall
- good equipment
- and the Carbuncle Summon

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

I recommend using your Maser Eye to knock off half his HP, which is around the 2500 area, give or take a couple hundred.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The summonstone is Shiva, who has an amusing scene of teasing Firion when she senses him. She appears when the opponent's Bravery hits 0 and locks it so it can't increase.

The auto version is useless for the moment, the only time an opponent's Bravery will hit 0 is if they're in the middle of getting attacked and are gonna get Broken, and Shiva doesn't stop the Bravery regen, or they just landed an HP attack, and that shouldn't be happening since enemies are still dumb as a post so far.

The chest has a Black Dragon Spear, a powerful exclusive weapon for Kain.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Also at this point, Kain hit Level 8 and learned Sky Rave. He moves forward a bit and dives down, drilling the opponent into the ground.

It and its cousin move, Rising Drive, are meant to be used for combos because Sky Rave does a huge amount of Bravery damage the longer Kain drives them into the ground, so you want an Assist to get the opponent stunned at the top of the arena. But if you want to try it out, run up a pillar in the World of Darkness, block the opponent when they dash towards you, then let loose and watch the fun.

</PanelSection>

#### The Tree's Pursuit

On the overworld map now, make a left and open the chest for a Pink Tail.

- On the shoreline to the left is a Fallacious Tree, it has Delta Attack again but is otherwise unremarkable. The Trees are easy to get the drop on on the overworld because their attack to initiate battle is Sword Dance, which is amazingly slow and easy to avoid.
- The chest beyond him has a KP Gambler, we're going to save it for the next chapter because we want to use it to chain as many enemies as possible and Kain can't get any more than four in the remaining gateways.
- The chest on the island has the Jump skill, which lets us leap over an enemy piece in a gateway to land behind them. We'll have a use for it too.
- The Counterfeit Wraith here has a new move, Long-Range Particle Beam, which creates a line of energy orbs that run along the ground firing lasers high into the air, fairly simple to dodge.
- On the hill behind it with the ruined castle is a chest with 1000 gil.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies here are at first unremarkable, but they do have a new trick. The line-up is a Fleeting Flash, Idle Sky Pirate, Transient Gunner and Fallacious Tree.

All of them except the Gunner have an accessory line-up of an Attractorb, Dragonfly Orb, Pearl Necklace, and six Break boosters. Thus once you break them, they get more than an 11x booster, greatly increasing the range at which they absorb EX Force and how much they get from it. As a result their gauge will charge surprisingly fast.

They also have summons, Odin for the Flash and Sky Pirate, Typhon for the Tree.

The Gunner is the weakest but has a midair Pummel that goes off pretty quick, so be ready to block it, it won't hurt much but on principle, not to mention it negates the AP chance. Also it may just be me, but the Idle Sky Pirate was a bit smarter than I expected, got in a few hits on me.

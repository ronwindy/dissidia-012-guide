I love Kain's one-liner. "So, you offer your life in exchange for theirs?" "Of course not. I offer yours."

Exdeath is an opponent entirely different from any other up until now due to a radically different fighting style, even the Fallacious Trees haven't tapped into his true power yet.

First, he has the smartest accessory set-up of any opponent so far.

- He has Pre-Assist and Assist Gauge Empty, and since he doesn't have an Assist at all they'll be active the entire match for an instant 2.1 boost.
- Then, he has After Summon, which will tack on another 1.3 once he calls his summon for 2.7 during the rest of the match.
- And finally he has a booster that triggers when his Bravery is its base value or lower for another 1.3, leading to a potential 3.5x booster.

And what is getting boosted? Attractorb and Pearl Necklace to help him charge up the EX Gauge, and unlike the Manikins, he's going to live long enough to potentially do it. And this time of course, no Firion Assist anymore to help you stop him.

His summon, Hecatoncheir, is usually called when he's at low Bravery, it'll sap the Bravery pool to 0 so you get no bonus for Breaking him. Annoying, but it only works once, after the first time Break is inflicted the Bravery pool resets like normal.

---

Now, Exdeath's manikins have had slow, easy to avoid or block attacks. This is because Exdeath's true power isn't in offense, it's defense. When he blocks an attack using a Guard or Block move, he can instantly use Sword Dance, Vacuum Wave or Hurricane, and they execute faster, and have a higher critical hit rate since you got blocked while he attacked.

- Turn Guard is a glow red and orange barrier where Exdeath turns to keep defending, you need to use a guard-crushing attack, fortunately all of Kain's HP attacks are such.
- Omni Block blocks *anything*, everything you throw at him, and it's a momentary instant-activation block, fortunately the AI sucks at using it.
- Reverse Polarity sends out a circle of energy that Exdeath can instantly teleport to when he chooses, and if he teleports on top of you you're damaged, *and* he can use an attack immediately, so you're likely to get knocked into the air and come back down into the blast from Delta Attack, or get caught in a Hurricane.

And his new HP attacks:

- Almagest has him move towards you in a field of energy and explode, and he can stop it in the middle of execution to block your attack.
- Grand Cross summons orbs of darkness to spin and converge on you.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

All in all, Exdeath is surprisingly the strongest boss yet, especially if you're a new player and don't know the quirks of his playstyle and how to counter him.

- There is no way to stop his Omni Block, it comes out way too fast for you to know he's even preparing it, and when he lands it you are going to feel pain.
- If he uses Almgaest, he can block you if you try to attack him and you will feel pain.
- If he hits you with Reverse Polarity, you are *probably* going to feel pain.

The main thing to watch for is Reverse Polarity, it always fires straight at you, do a neutral dodge and Exdeath will teleport right in front of you, you'll avoid damage and can attack him before he recovers from the teleportation. And if he does hit you with it, use Aerial Recovery quickly so you don't fall back down into his next attack.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

The best opening you can ask for besides Reverse Polarity is Hurricane, make sure you have enough distance from him to get it off safety and chuck a Gungnir in his face. He *might* stop Hurricane by Omni Blocking and pain is soon to follow, but it's a risk you'll have to take because there's not much you can do to stop him.

You can also try to hit as he fires out Reverse Polarity, but likely he'll teleport out of the way and laugh in your face. What stops Exdeath from just curb-stomping you is his AI again, the AI is good at using him, but not *too* good.

- Fake him out by attacking when he's nowhere near you and this will bait him into Reverse Polarity, dodge and counter as described.
- And if he Omni Blocks but mistimes it, the ending lag of it is a perfect opening.

Overall just don't be too aggressive or he'll make you pay for it, be cautious and wait for your chance to strike when he gives you an opening.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

I never talked about Kain's EX Mode but I like finishing off bosses with EX Bursts, so this is the first time I've gotten into EX Mode actually.

Kain gets a special attack called Lancet by pressing R and Square, it has a second's long charge time and causes a giant blade of energy to impale the opponent from below. The attack will not only do HP damage, but Kain is healed the HP he inflicted. Furthermore, Lancet will recharge to base Bravery very quickly after it hits, and you can press Triangle to dash at the opponent like Kain's melee moves.

His EX Burst has you rapidly tap a button to build up a power meter, and the button turns over and changes twice as he jumps, very easy to get right. Appropriately Exdeath is very vulnerable to Lancet when using Delta Attack or Hurricane.

</PanelSection>

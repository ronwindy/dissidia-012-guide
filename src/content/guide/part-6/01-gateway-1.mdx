The line-up is a Transient Gunner, an Imitation Despot, a Fleeting Flash and a Delusory Dragoon, no surprises here save for one thing.

The Flash has a summon, Typhon, when called it randomly blows away a digit of your Bravery, so say, 774 Bravery will become 770, or 074 (aka 74), and so forth. I use this example because I retried three times just to check, and every time Typhon blasted the hundreds digit. The AI likes using Typhon after you Break her, keep that in mind.

</PanelSection>

With the opposition dead open the chests for yet another Pink Tail, and a Javelin, a new weapon for Kain.

With enough gil you can trade it with your Mythril for a Mythril Spear, but don't for the reasons I've already explained.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As we leave, Kain has a flashback to when he met with Golbez, who told him about the cycles of war and the proof of such that lies in their memories - Kain and Golbez remember their home clearly, but Cecil, who is newly summoned, does not, because he hasn't had time to fight and remember yet.

Kain doesn't remember this truth because while memories of home return and remain, when you are felled in battle and revived in the next cycle, you lose your memories of the previous cycle. This leaves a gaping question to be answered - who on Team Chaos was so badass last cycle that they beat Kain? - but we'll never find out, alas.

Cecil shows up and Kain confirms he has few memories, and thus realizes Golbez told the truth.

#### Exploration

On the world map, behold our map theme, Suspicion, very appropriate for the story all things considered.

- Immediately turn around and go back through the forest. There's an Imitation Despot here to deal with and a chest with 500 gil in the mountains beyond him.
- The Despot is fought in a new arena, Ultimecia's Castle, with a new Battlegen to go with it, the Time Warp, though it isn't needed for anything good.

---

Back at the start point, the island has a Maser Eye, a new skill that saps all enemies in a chain to 50% HP. To the right is a path with an Assist Charge in a chest.

Continue along and ignore the bridge and the person waiting for us, there's a path up to the Gulg volcano ring with the frozen lake, and a False Stalwart to fight. Yay, we need to fight these guys, we need their Battlegen for that coveted Hyper Ring! Remember to watch for the gold star icon when you Break him and keep Breaking him until you get it so you know you'll get the Stalwart's Hopes when you win.

Hidden behind the grass near the sealed gateway up here is a Rosetta Stone, you know what to do.

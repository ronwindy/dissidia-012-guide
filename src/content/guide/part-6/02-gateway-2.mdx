#### A Naive Ally

Back across the bridge, we meet up with Firion, who joins us as our Assist for the rest of the story. The area beyond him is small, shatter the remaining orbs to get your new chain skill and enter the blue gateway.

Chain the lot of them with a Cross Chain at B3, a Jump Chain at C3, or a Multi Chain at D2 or B3.

<PanelSection kind="assist">

- In the air he tosses our three Fire spells, weak slow-moving projectiles that are good for some other characters that could use distractions or stunning attacks, but Kain has no use for it.
- What he *does* have a use for is Firion's ground Assist, Rope Knife. He tosses out daggers to drag the opponent close, then slams them with a superpowered punch to knock them away.

It does a lot of damage and wall rushes to set up for further blows, yeah it can be a problem if Firion knocks the opponent too far, but a charged up Jump, which you can charge as he attacks, can get you the distance you need a lot of the time. And awesomely, Crashing Dive's downward attack slams the opponent into the ground, a perfect set-up for Firion to come in.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up is a bit more varied now, an Imitation Despot, Capricious Reaper, Fleeting Flash and Phantasmal Girl.

- The Despot has Thunder Crest and the tracking ground Flare, use Kain's Jump to reflect Flare.
- There's also some summons to look out for, the Despot has Ifrit, the Sky Pirate has Demon Wall, and the Girl has Giant of Babil.

As you've noticed the game is slowly ramping up the difficulty, making opponents of higher levels more common and giving them summons and accessories. It still isn't very challenging yet but it is making a stronger effort.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

The Strange Battle Piece is a Phantasmal Girl and she has a new, potentially deadly trick.

Holy Combo is a Bravery attack that shoots five slow-moving orbs of light, but if one of them hits she can follow it up by firing four Flare fireballs at us, each fireball she turns to track us if we move. And if one of the Flares hit, she can use the HP Link Ultima, creating a sphere of magic around us to explode and damage our HP.

Fortunately Holy Combo is slow and pretty easy to block. Be thankful this isn't the real Terra, her EX Mode Holy Combo is a lot harder to avoid and is one of the most powerful combos in the game.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

During this round of fights Kain hit Level 6 and picked up Jump Boost, giving his jumps greater height.

Many fights here take place in the Crystal World, another new arena. The Battlegen is the Star's Core, which unlocks three new items, two of them are the Dragonfly Orb and Bravery Orb.

- The latter is largely useless but the Dragonfly Orb increases EX Core absorption, giving us more EX from the cores. It's not as good as the Pearl Necklace and Attractorb in my opinion though, I'd prefer to absorb the EX Force as it appears rather than let a core absorb it all and an opponent get to it first.
- The third item is the Iifa Leaf, which can be traded for one each Blackcrystal Sliver, Sealed Darkness, and Star's Core. Given how much we've fought in the Chaos Shrine and World of Darkness, the first two should be in abundance.

The chests have 500 gil and a Soul of Valor.

</PanelSection>

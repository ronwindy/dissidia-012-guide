#### Exploration

On the overworld now, shatter the stone and grind to the island. Depending on where it is when you land the Fallacious Tree may attack as soon as you land so be ready to dodge if so.

The arena is new, Planet's Core, we can Battlegen the Materia here. The Materia is the last component, along with Reaper's Dreams and Gunner's Dreams, for the Zephyr Cloak, we don't need it but something of note. Now just gather the orbs of the dead and approach the moogle.

---

- The summons can mostly be passed up, though if you must buy one go for Carbuncle.
- For weapons, we can still afford the big 200 KP ones. I would say buy the Snowscepter, the character in the next storyline can't use weapons most of the rest can but she can use Staves, so the Snowscepter will do well for her, and continue to be handy in later storylines for spellcasters that can't use guns or swords like the rest of the cast.
- The Grounded and Aerial boosters are good, they can be annoying since you have to stay on the ground or in the air, but they have their uses. I'll pass them up though since we don't really need boosters urgently yet.

I also bought several more Rosetta Stones, six, leaving me with 57 KP. I only used one of them for Kain though. Now just buy a Multichain and head into the gateway now.

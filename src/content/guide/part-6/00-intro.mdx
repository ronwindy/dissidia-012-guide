# Chapter 6

Part 6, with revelations in hand let's get the full story of the other side...

#### The Dragoon's Path

Well, new chapter means new character, but not just any character!

---

<CharacterIntro name="Kain" src="drake/Kain_(Dissidia_012).png" width={183} height={200} alt="Kain (Dissidia 012)">

KAIN IS HERE, KIDDIES! A strong contender for the one most popular and famous characters of the series, not to mention one of the most badass. As we now know, Kain isn't really a traitor, he's just going to extremes to ensure the survival of his comrades, but he is a good guy.

Alone as he searches for more friends to get to safety, it's not an easy path with the chance of someone following him and finding out where the others are, but there's no one more qualified to walk it. Turn up the volume if you've had your PSP muted. Liam O'Brien's voice has been reported to induce orgasm in people from just a few seconds exposure, even straight males.

Kain has a comparatively small moveset, but it's an effective one with a cool gimmick, though very difficult to get right.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Kain has, aside from his Cyclone which is just the token projectile all characters need, two ground Bravery attacks and three aerial ones.

When he attacks he can press Triangle after knocking the opponent back to instantly dash after them, letting you attack again before they recover. As I said though, very hard to get right, your timing has to be precise.

You can also tilt the analog stick up, down, or leave it neutral when doing the second part of his aerial combos, letting him knock the opponent in a different direction and changing if the attack does wall rush or Chase.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attacks consist of jumps and dives effective at many altitudes, and some mid-range spear throws.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Despite his limited moveset, like Yuna Kain has the moves he needs to be effective, unlike Yuna he's still fun to play as and like Lightning is rewarding when you learn his quirks.

- I must confess I never got the hang of any of his combos except for Lance Burst to Lance Barrage, but if you can, good for you.
- Jump is awesome, learn to use it effectively and you can dodge attacks and land nasty hits.
- Finally, Gungnir is pure win, the opponent *will* be driven across the arena by it.

A fun combo, though I'm sure it can be avoided with Aerial Recovery but the AI is too dumb to do that, is to use Cyclone to blast them up into the air and towards Kain, then Jump and hit them as they're staggered. Kain is overall a blast to play as and a potent character, use him well.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

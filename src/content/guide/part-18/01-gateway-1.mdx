The Moogle summon randomly copies the effect of any other summon in the game, so who knows what'll happen, it could be useless, could work to your benefit, could swing the entire flow of the match around. The Summoner also has the accessory gimmick of the chapter, and it's a pretty good one. They have a Force to Courage with a Pearl Necklace, Attractorb, and Near Loss.

Thus when you have enough Bravery to kill them, they get a 17x booster to absorb EX Force in a wide area and get more energy from it, and Force to Courage then converts the absorbed EX into Bravery. So be careful when they're in the throws of death because suddenly they'll rapidly be gaining Bravery until you finish them off. On the up side it means they won't be able to use EX Mode, but their absorption range of EX Force means you probably won't be getting any for yourself

#### Exploration

On the world map turn around and go back to find an Imaginary Champion. Bartz's map attack is Hazard Raid, which as I said is like Cloud's Climhazzard but with Tifa's Beat Rush three-hit combo after the charge. Thus like Firion and Zidane before him Bartz can exploit his range to get the drop on enemies easier than other character.

On the island past it is a Blind skill, in battle it limits the actions enemies can take, but I've never used it because how often do enemies act anyway, really? Play this game and take a shot for every time they stand there and let you hit them with the battle-ending HP strike. Now grind down to the mainland and go past the ruined town to find a Rafflesia Vine.

---

Now, let's have a talk about Chain skills. As I'm sure you've noticed, those random skills you get from the orbs of the dead more often than not are Jump Chains and Round Chains. I'm low on Chain skills myself so we're gonna go get some.

The fastest way to handle this is to just launch The prologue of Treachery of the Gods, there are four orbs to shatter on the overworld, do so, get your skill, quit the story and start it over. But this may take too long for your tastes. Well, if we're gonna "cheat" by getting skills from other stories, let's do it right.

To get good skills, launch Laguna's story and fight two enemies to leave his first gateway, then proceed over the overworld map to the next gateway where a moogle awaits. I was low on KP so I bought a Cross Chain and used my last KP Gambler in the next gateway to win 106 KP. Just be sure to adjust Laguna to level 6 so your winnings aren't penalized - but even if you don't, he's at Level 24 in my story, so we just only get 80 KP, still plenty when it comes to buying skills.

Meeting the 90 second victory KP Chance is easy with all the powered-up equipment you have now, not to mention summons. When the gateway is done, buy two Multichains, then save and leave the story. If you need skills later in Bartz's story or in the next one, you can always exit their story, come back here, buy what you need then dive back in.

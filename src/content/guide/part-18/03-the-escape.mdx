On the world map go left to get an EX Charge, then follow the path to a blue gateway. Down the right path is a Delusory Warlock with Rising Wave and Gravity System. Then head inside the blue gateway.

<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 90 second win.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain at C3 to get all six enemies, the Party piece being three. I used an EX Charge here, we have three, why not? And the EX Revenge ability helped a lot with the Party enemies, they're tricky ones.

If you somehow have a KP Gambler I don't, this is the place to use it.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Fallacious Tree with Vacuum Wave and Almagest.
- Second comes a Delusory Warlock with Rising Wave and Genesis Rock.

Be careful, Genesis Rock is intensely hard to avoid in Pandaemonium.

- Third is a Phantasmal Girl with Blizzara, Flood, Moogle summon, and Force to Courage gear.

Now of course it's the Party piece. They're all Strange, and use the same set-up as the Delusory Knife with a little less than 1 HP, an Iifa Leaf, Mandragora and a full EX Gauge.

- The first one is an Imaginary Soldier with Slashing Blow and Omnislash Version 5, with the EX Mode critical rate it will hurt a lot so watch it carefully reading to block.
- Next is an Imitation Liegeman with Weaponsmaster.
- The third one is a Delusory Knight with Dark Flame and Paladin Force.

With my EX Gauge charged up, I didn't use EX Mode, rather I saved it to EX Revenge and halt their attacks if needed.

</PanelSection>

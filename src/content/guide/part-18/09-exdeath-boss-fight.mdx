As always, Exdeath could be one of the most challenging opponents yet, save for one trait. He's got a great accessory line-up, an Attractorb, Earring and Guardian Bangle pumped up by 7 Near Opponent boosters. Thus his Hurricane has a big boost in damage, and every time you attack him with a melee move - which of course is most of yours - he's going to get a decent defence boost.

He has that Brynhildr summon again, and a Fleeting Flash Assist - contrary to the prior cutscene, Squall is still hanging around to be yours. And since last time in Cecil's story he was just a mid-boss, he's a lot tougher than last time we tangoed with him.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Not much I can say about him otherwise, you've fought him three times by this point, you know all the annoying tricks he has, you know that his biggest weakness is the poor AI. Exploit his Reverse Polarity spam as last time, attack while he's on equal footing to bait him into using it, neutral dodge and he'll appear in front of you, a sitting duck. He's got Turn Guard on the ground, Reel Axe can crush it but Hazard Raid is asking for him to block it, and in the air it's Omni Block.

Overall, you can handle him, his blocks are annoying but you know how they work for yourself. His only new trick is Malestrom, an HP attack that has him charge for a long time, then summon a whirlwind around you wherever you are in the arena. And he can cancel it into a block too.

He usually doesn't though so easy to stop.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

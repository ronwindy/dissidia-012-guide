#### Exploration

Go to the Moogle and buy a Jump skill and Multichain, if you don't have one. Head back through the teleport to the Land of Discord, suddenly there are four orbs here to get a chain skill from. There's also a Level 20 Imaginary Champion to deal with.

Unfortunately there are no new chests in the region, so gather the orbs and head into the newly active gateway nearby.

# Chapter 9

Chapter 9, the penultimate quest, and I'm sure you know who's turn it is.

#### The Mouse Trapped

Say hi to our latest hero.

---

<CharacterIntro name="Bartz" src="drake/DissidiaBartz.png" width={175} height={322}>

Ah, Bartz. FF5 is an interesting game, the only things people know it for are Gilgamesh and the awesome Job system. But it never had anything really resembling a coherent three-act plot with any sort of even flow to events, or anything resembling deep and memorable characters.

The Japanese developers apparently agree because Bartz's story is completely bland, he's a happy-go-lucky guy who loves adventure, but he's a bit of a dummy and a little cowardly at times. The moral lessons he learns from his story is to not run from your enemies...even if it is you against innumerable odds. At least that's the only lesson I ever saw him learn, and it makes no sense, but why break a streak of nonsensical aesops?

Bartz's fighting style is pretty lame too. He's a Mime, so he doesn't use any attacks of his own, he takes attacks from other characters and combines them to make his own, like charging forward with Cloud's Climhazzard, then punching the opponent away like Tifa's Beat Rush. In that regard though his moves kinda suck, he has a handful of close-range moves and a few slow long-range moves, and his HP attacks are likewise, he has four, melee and long-range.

So, much like Yuna, Bartz suffers from an extreme lack of reliable moves that makes him, to me, rather un-fun to play as. He's not weak, his moves do pack a good punch to them comparable to Squall or Cloud, but he's just boring to play as, for me at least. His moves have the unique property of possessing support skills, in the end-game he'll learn stronger versions of those abilities for himself, or can have equipment to provide those boosts, but they're still nice add-ons, and some stack with the innate ability for a higher boost than normal characters can get.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Bartz begins with Blade Crash, a two-hit sweep with Lightning's Gunblade, stepping forward slightly, then he slashes past the opponent like Vaan's Katana and begins a Chase. His Reel Impulse throws an axe like Firion's Reel Axe, but then he jumps up and attacks to wall rush them with Zidane's Mage Mashers. For aerial moves he has Rush Impact, where he slashes twice with Kain's lance as in Lance Barrage, then slams the opponent down like Lightning's Smite.

His other aerial starting move is Holy, which works just like Terra's but sucks. No, really, it has weaker homing and speed.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attacks are Yuna's Hellfire and Laguna's Ragnarok Blade, which work just like the originals.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As I've said so often, Bartz is fine for his own story but I dislike using him in later stories due to his limited moveset and lame non-melee moves.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

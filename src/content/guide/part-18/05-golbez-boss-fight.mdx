Golbez has one of the dumbest builds of any boss since Ultimecia and her trio of Cursed Rings. He has a Hyper Ring with seven HP = 100% boosters, and thus his build will drop to useless the second he takes a single point of HP damage. He's also got Odin to strike us with Break at a 50% success rate, but it's the Auto version so it only appears once he's at low HP, and since we'll probably be ending this fight with an EX Burst as per tradition, Breaking him in the process, Odin is useless because summons can't be triggered during a Burst.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

As a result of the above, Golbez is a pretty lame boss. They tried to make him tough, but it fails. He has the same attacks as always so the same strategies as always will carry you through to victory, he seems a little smarter than usual, but is still an idiot.

The problem is that unless the extra damage from completing the attack will Break me, or if you've got an Assist to combo with, I don't get why you would never not want to use HP links, doing HP damage is always better than doing Bravery damage, and if you're inflicted with Break an HP attack will cure it. The AI never got this concept and thus Golbez doesn't abuse his four HP links like he should.

And Gravity System, well, the AI never got the idea that it's ridiculously easy to counter, either.

</PanelSection>

Well, gotta fill up this empty space next to that big boss template, let's chat about Bartz's EX Mode. He masters his Mime job, gaining a longer red cape and three golden stars over his head as in FF5. His EX Mode bonus is disappointing though, it's the Goblin Punch attack.

Pressing R and Square will use it, it's a combo of hits to do Bravery damage and then launch the opponent away, it has low range but very fast execution. The reason it's lame is because the Bravery damage it does is pathetic normally. The true power of Goblin Punch is that when used on an enemy with the same level as Bartz, its Bravery damage increases eightfold, and suddenly it does as much Bravery damage as Bartz's Bravery combos.

Awesome yes, but outside of player-versus-player matches, how often will Bartz and his opponent be at the same level? Even in the end game when he's Level 100, enemies go past that. Bartz's EX Burst is a little tricky and needs good reflexes, you have to press three pairs of directional pad commands as they appear to have him bombard opponents with eight magic-empowered slashes, before he combines his weapons into his own Brave Blade and delivers the final strike.

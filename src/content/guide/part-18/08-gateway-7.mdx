<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 90 second win, prepare the appropriate summon(s) for the Party and Expert foes before using the chain, and the bonus line is 16.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Jump over the first enemy to get to D4, and Multichain to get them all. Not only is there an Expert enemy to deal with, but the Party enemies are higher level too, not Expert levels but a bit tougher than the Hard ones up to this point. Great time to use that last EX Charge.

Notice most characters seem to end their stories at Level 16 or 15? Or maybe it's just me.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First two are Normal enemies and thus easy, an Imaginary Champion with Sudden Cruelty and Heaven's Light, and a Delusory Warlock with Rising Wave and Genesis Rock.

Now come the Party enemies.

- First, a Delusory Warlock with Rising Wave, Float System, Genesis Rock, Moogle, Phantasmal Girl Assist, and Force to Courage.
- Next comes a False Stalwart with Chain Cast, Moogle, an Idle Sky Pirate Assist, and Force to Courage.
- Now the Expert enters.

The Level 28 False Stalwart has what you'll come to recognize as the typical moveset for them - Lance Charge, Round Edge, Highbringer, Bardiche, Chain Cast, Twist Drill, and all of Garland's EX attacks. He's got the Alexander summon, a False Hero Assist, and two Muscle Belts powered up by 6 Near Opponent boosters and an After 45 Seconds. He's very powerful, has a lot of HP to burn through, and the Crystal World arena isn't conducive to Assist combos with Squall.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Use your summon and EX Mode wisely, the AI tends to spam Chain Cast so block it, dash up and use Rush Impact to smash him. If you're lucky to slam him into the ground, call your Assist and dash down to Rush Impact him while Squall attacks, then do the Assist chase and strike with Ragnarok Blade. He's tough, but you can win.

Don't worry about losing EX Mode or taking HP damage, there's a Potion to heal us up for the boss.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Bartz hit Level 16 from this fight and learned Solid Ascension, it starts out like Squall's Solid Barrel then ends with the Warrior of Light's Ascension to stab the opponent into the air, beginning a chase or wall rushing them, if they hit a wall. It's also Bartz's strongest move in terms of pure power.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Open the chest for a Mu Fu, then approach the end piece.

Both of them push their cutscene power to the max, Exdeath having his sword move with magic to fight Bartz and Bartz dual-wielding the Buster Sword and Gunblade, then settle down for a proper fight.

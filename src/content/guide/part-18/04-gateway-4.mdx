Does this place look familiar? It should. Jump Chain at C3 or Multichain at D3 to get all enemies here.

I didn't have either so I quickly swung over to Laguna's story and got a Jump Chain. All the two options affect is which emblem you use, the Initial BRV Boost one that affects both, or the Booster x5 emblem. It's up to you.

Also note some of the Hard enemies around this time begin using Level 30 weapons again. You should also aim for that EX Gauge filling up for the boss, or you can just use an EX Charge, it's up to you.

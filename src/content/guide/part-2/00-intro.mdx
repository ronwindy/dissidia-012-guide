I fucking hate Vaan. Yeah I know, everyone does, but I actually didn't mind him in FF12, he was a supporting player and he was fine in that capacity, he was a bit of a goof but he had his moments of insight and wisdom and was ultimately tolerable. Here though he's an outright clueless idiot, incapable of remembering simple *names*, and even the other party members joke about how stupid and naive he is.

My outlook on this shift of character is accompanied by the problem of the two characters his story focuses on aside from him. We're supposed to believe he's Terra's protector? My lovely Esperkin could roast him alive with a snap of the fingers. And for that matter so could Kefka. Sorry kiddies, I'm getting this all out of my system now so this part of the walkthrough isn't just a rant.

<PanelSection title="Fighting style">

As for his fighting style, there are four characters with annoying fighting gimmicks that I decided I didn't care enough to master - Vaan is one of them. Yes I suck with Vaan, I admit that, sue me. I suck by choice though.

His gimmick is that when he attacks using a different Bravery attack than the one he just used, he switches weapons, triggering a Switch attack that has different effects. So using Spear when he just used Axe \& Shield will have a different effect than the second use of Spear after the first. HP attacks disarm him entirely. I never got the hang of this myself, but if you can, good for you.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Attacks">

Vaan's starting attacks are good enough.

- Spear is a close-range spin attack that does high damage.
- Greatsword is a charging attack you can charge up for range.
- He'll get Crossbow at Level 2, which fires a barrage of shots as he turns to track enemies.

Vaan will learn aerial variants of his attacks a few levels after the ground version. I find these three base attacks enough for Vaan's story and never use him beyond that.

His HP attacks are great though. Cataclysm has a good reach into the air and turns on start-up, and Inferno is a basic and simple charging attack. At Level 8 Vaan gets Torrent, which calls a flurry of watery orbs to drive the opponent into the ground from above, and combos well with the Assist that joins him later.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

Start off equipping Vaan, we should have gear for him already, but the shop has new weapons and I recommend getting him an Spear for more power than what we already have. Give him the same Power Ring-Level 1-9-HP 100% accessories we've been using before. I won't say this from now on but this is going to be my starting accessory set for all characters, it works well enough for what we have so do the same.

---

Begin by heading onto the island to get EX Charge from the chest, then shatter the two orbs. On the peniunsula on the right ahead is a chest with a BRV Charge skill, it gives you 5x your base Bravery when you begin the fight.

The Manikins ahead are Phantasmal Harlequins, their world map attacks are slow and you can easily strike them before they hit you. Both of them just spam Havoc Wing to try and stab you, but its long start-up makes it easy to dodge and you should be able to Break and kill them pretty quickly.

---

The path to the right, where Lightning's story took us, is empty so go through the empty gateway into the mountains. The other two orbs are here, and in the grass on the right is a chest with 200 gil.

The Blue Gateway here is a new sight, these gateways are optional and can be exited any time or skipped entirely, and can be re-entered if you like. For purposes of this walkthrough they are non-optional.

Inside the Onion Knight is waiting for us, he's only in this gateway but he'll be our Assist for now. He's a good one too, his ground Assist begins a Chase while the midair one wall rushes the opponent up into the air.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

If you have a Jump Chain use it at C3, or use a Multi Chain at D3. You can use that BRV Charge if you want to make the battles easier. Note you can only have five Skills at one time and have to discard one when you find a sixth, so no point hoarding them.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The opponents are nothing new, the same three enemy types Lightning faced and another Phantasmal Harlequin. They aren't too hard to beat up.

- The Harlequin's offense is Meteor, which calls five fireballs to hit the ground around you and bounce toward you
- Hyperdrive, sending a wave of explosions along the ground.

Both have long start-up and are easy to dodge. However, you'll notice the last enemy in the right corner is a new type.

---

Strange Battle Pieces are always risky to face. They begin battle with low HP and can probably be killed in one hit, but they use Summons to sap your Bravery or increase their own, then spam HP attacks.

This one is a Fleeting Flash that has Bahamut, giving it regular boosts of 99 Bravery for a period of time, and then it'll use Crushing Blow repeatedly. Keep your distance to avoid the blast and hit it on the recovery. If you're having trouble getting in the HP hit to kill it, Wall Rush it and call your Assist, giving you a clear shot.

</PanelSection>

#### Character Derailment FTL

So as you'll soon realize, Vaan is just the viewpoint character for a chapter that focuses on Terra changing sides and escaping Kefka, much like his original game. We head down into the Elven Snowfields now, my favorite part of the D012 world map.

The path out of the mountains and onto the field is linear, grab two orbs of the dead as you head to the blue gateway. Before you head in get the chest on the island for a Rosetta Stone, you know what to do.

---

I recommend adding in the Red Drop or Blue Drop. The Near Opponent booster you'll have for completing Chapter 1 is tempting, but it's only a 1.2 booster, which raises our 2.2 boost from our other Boosters to a 2.7 boost. Do the math, that's a 50% boost to an accessory that raises damage by 3%...or you can just use the Red Drop which has a full 3% boost.

Now enter the blue gateway.

<PanelSection kind="assist">

Terra will be our assist for the rest of the storyline.

- When you're on the ground she'll use Graviga, charging up for a couple seconds to slam an orb of darkness on the opponent's head, it does a lot of damage but is hard to hit with. The easiest way if you're determined is to block an attack, call her out, then attack the opponent with Spear and Wall Rush them as she charges.
- Her aerial Assist is Blizzara, which is easier to use, Wall Rush an opponent then call out Terra as you drop to the ground to use Torrent or dash closer to use Inferno.

If you lack the CP to use Torrent, de-equip one of Vaan's ground attacks (I recommend Spear) to make room.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

The easiest way to chain enemies here is a Jump Chain at B3. If you lack one there's a moogle right outside that will sell one for 6 KP. But we'll win 16 KP for chaining all four enemies so why not.

I had 55 KP before buying the Jump Chain, so we'll come back to the Moogle after the gateway is done because there's stuff to buy. The KP Chance is once again to win within 90 seconds.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies are two Idle Sky Pirates, a Fleeting Flash and a Phantasmal Harlequin. I know, I hate being so brief about this but D012 reuses a lot of enemy equipment and move set-ups over the course of a story, so if you've seen one Idle Sky Pirate odds are you've seen all of them for the next chapter or two.

These ones are a few levels higher but are just as incompetent as their predecessors, and now you've got a bigger accessory pool, Ifrit, and Terra. Just watch out for the Flash's Watera, it'll deflect your Crossbow shots but otherwise can be blocked or avoided.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As a note, it was at this point Vaan mastered several support skills. Depending on how your AP Chances have gone you may have mastered them before or will soon. This should free up enough CP to use all of his Bravery attacks and Torrent without having to give up other skills. And I also had enough left over to give him Speed Boost, yay!

Vaan is at Level 10 now on my game so the Level 1-9 Booster is now useless, I replaced it with a Blue Drop for a minor Bravery boost.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chests have an Iifa Leaf and a Delicious Fish Scale.

- The Leaf gives us the Second Chance ability - when you're hit with an HP attack that would kill you, you live with 1 HP left over. We'll save it for later because there *is* a powerful enemy in a future storyline we could use it for.
- The scale unlocks two new Shop items, the Linen Cuirass and the Capella. The Cuirass can be passed up, you *could* get it but it needs a lot of gil to trade.

The Capella we want, it's one of the best weapons we'll be able to access for the time being, but we need an Altair to trade for it along with almost 15,000 gil, so we'll have to buy it later.

Back outside we can fight a Phantasmal Harlequin to get access to a chest with a second Pink Tail. The fight got me to Level 11 and Vaan learned the ground variant of Katana, it's a charge attack past the opponent to chop then into the air and begin a chase.

It's pretty good with damage to rival Spear and generates a lot of EX Force, and its switch attack is two chops. I would say get rid of Greatsword for it if you want it, so you've got melee, mid-range and long-range all covered.

---

Now, I've got 65 KP so there's some stuff we can buy at the moogle. Pass up the equipment, the 200 ones are where it's at.

- The boosters are of questionable usage since they rely on the opponent's HP.
- For summons, Mandragora is limited in usefulness and Tonberry can be passed up.
- Cactuar is pricey but worth it, buy it. When called it instantly does 1000 Bravery damage to the opponent. However it only has two charges. Call it whenever you like by pressing R and Circle in battle.

Set your reserves now since you have two summons, Ifrit first than Cactuar second. Now whenever a summon is depleted, the next highest-numbered summon is automatically equipped in its place. Get the last two orbs on the world map and head to the gateway for the end of the story.

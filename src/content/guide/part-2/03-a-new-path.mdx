<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Guess the four enemies here. If you guessed "Dragoon, Sky Pirate, Flash, Harlequin", have an e-cookie.

- The Dragoon has Dragon's Fang
- the Harlequin has Havoc Wing
- the Flash has Lightning Strike
- the Sky Pirate has Windburst

As with Lightning's Crushing Blow, use Vaan's Inferno to deflect Windburst.

---

The new foe at B4 is a lot more dangerous though, a Capricious Reaper at a higher level of power. He's level 15, has the Demon Wall summon to protect his Bravery from damage, and has a massive boost to his attack and defense while he's at full HP.

Focus on landing an HP attack quickly to drop him of his buffs, then fight him, you should know how to dodge the Reaper's attacks by now.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Use a Straight Chain at D4 with an EX Charge to give yourself an advantage when fighting the Reaper.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Vaan's EX Mode effect is quite good, he gets variably special effects to each attack as described on his page, Spear for example spins twice, and of his three base Bravery attacks so far that's the best benefit. He can also put away his weapon with R and Square, letting him prepare for a Switch attack without having to use another attack.

The KP Chance this time isn't just to win, but to win in 90 seconds, so retry if you run out of time. Focus on HP attacks as Demon Wall will halt your attempts to Break him and wastes your time if you keep trying such.

Chain all five enemies for 25 KP.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Inspect the red orb to get our first summon, Ifrit. Equip him in the summons menu. Ifrit will automatically trigger when you Break an opponent and gives you a 50% boost to Bravery. However, he only has three charges, after which he has to recharge by sitting out for three fights.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Open the chest for a powerful new weapon, the Slasher. Equip it to Vaan now, it has the highest power boost of any weapon thus far and gives a slight boost to the Wall Rush HP damage he inflicts.

The shop also has an assortment of new inventory for us.

- The Wakizashi is an upgraded Kunai, giving a higher defense benefit.
- You can also grab an Iron Shield for higher defense but no attack boost, or the Power Armlet for a slight HP boost, take what you like.
- The helmets are similar, the Plumed Hat has a higher BRV benefit, Iron Helm boosts defense and Headband boosts HP.
- Armor is more clear, the Chainmail is where it's at.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

While we're here, let's talk about accessories and Battlegen, since you're probably curious about all those greyed-out accessories you can't buy. A lot of shop items cost more than gil, you also need to trade other equipment pieces or trade accessories for them, and Battlegen trade accessories are won from enemies, specific enemies or arenas rewarding different items.

- If you have three Flash's Desire you can buy another Power Ring, but you can only use one at a time.
- The superior Hyper Ring needs three Blackcrystal Slivers and three Stalwart's Hopes, and we get neither yet.
- The Guard Ring needs three Dragoon's Desires, we *could* get it but we don't need it really.

Everything else we can't get yet because we don't meet enemies that leave the Battlegen items we need for trade, so I'll mention what's new as we progress from now on.

</PanelSection>

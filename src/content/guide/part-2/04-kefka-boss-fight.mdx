At the Chaos Piece, Vaan sees Kefka mocking Terra, sporting her new alt outfit for this game which rather gratuitously gives an unobstructed view of the bottom part of her leotard, front *and* back. There's your fanservice, Terra fans!

Kefka tortures her into submission when she refuses to fight, and Vaan intervenes to stop him. My money is on Kefka, but since we're fighting on Terra's behalf I guess I might as well root for Vaan.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

You know how several of these attacks work already. The two Firagas are new.

- One of them throws out a single fireball that weaves through the air, stunning you and doing nasty damage when it hits.
- The other one is telegraphed by Kefka making some hand motions in the air, then he shoots out three fireballs that zigzag towards you, easy to anticipate and block.

His HP attack Trine has him tap the air to create a spark of light, then three triangles of energy surround you and quickly converge, watch for the flash of light on Kefka's hand to anticipate and dodge them.

This stage also has a new feature, Banish Traps. The pits in many stages will suck Bravery from a character if they fall into one, or are knocked in, and teleports them back to solid ground with a 20% Bravery loss. If you're having trouble wearing Kefka down, use your normal Spear attack (not Switch) to knock him down into the traps and let them help you out.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

If you've got a full or nearly full EX Gauge, the battle is a snap, bombard Kefka with Crossbow from range and use your double Switch Spear when close. With Ifrit on your side, Breaking Kefka will put you at 1300 Bravery or so, a Wall Rush later he'll be weak enough to kill with your EX Burst.

His Firagas and Scatter-Spray Blizzaga are tricky if you're a new player, but if you played the first game you know how to handle Kefka well enough and shouldn't have any trouble. You may be caught off-guard a few times, but that's what Kefka does, in the end you'll prevail.

</PanelSection>

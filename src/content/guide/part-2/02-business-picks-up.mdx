<PanelSection kind="chain">

If you have a Multichain, activate it at D3 to chain all five enemies for a cool 25 KP. Otherwise the enemies in the upper-right can be avoided, it's the same trio of Dragoon-Flash-Sky Pirate Manikins we've seen before, I won't cover them because I won't be fighting them and because you should know how to handle them by now.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The two enemies we need to fight to get to the end piece are another Phantasmal Harlequin and a new foe, the Phantasmal Girl.

- The Harlequin has Meteor and Hyperdrive and a new move called Ultima, it's a little harder to anticipate at close-range, and if the start-up hits us it'll blow up in our face.
- The girl is a tricky one, in the air she has Blizzara, a long-range but medium speed ice projectile we can block, and on the ground she has Blizzard Combo, a fast-executing but very low range combo attack. Her HP attack is Tornado, it lasts several seconds and protects her from attacks, so when she uses it keep your distance and hit her as she recovers.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Open the chest for an Orange Drop and approach the end piece, use EX Charge if you want the benefit. Vaan is suddenly ambushed by a mystery opponent!

I won't give this boss a template because it's fairly simple, she spams Tornado, which you likely just saw, but can use it twice in a row. That's all she does, Tornado spam. Let her, then swat her with a Crossbow or charged Greatsword as she recovers. Don't expect her EX Gauge to deplete, a unique accessory all but ensures she'll be in EX Mode the entire fight.

This is as good a time as any to talk about Vaan's EX Burst. Press R to shift commands and use White Whorl, then shift to use Pyroclasm. Odds are you'll only have enough time to get the command to come up once so try not to shuffle past it.

With Vaan's opponent deciding he isn't worth killing (because I doubt he actually beat her, I mean really) she runs off and Vaan continues on. He should be Level 4 now and will learn the aerial variant of Crossbow.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

---

We have two paths now, the plains to the right or the mountain to the left. Shatter the orb and go right first.

The chest on the island has a Cure command for us. The Phantasmal Harlequin up ahead has two new Bravery attacks, a bouncing Blizzaga attack on the ground and in the air, a Blizzaga that shoots forward, stops, then shatters and spreads out. The shattering Blizzaga comes out fast and can deflect Crossbow blasts so be aware of that. Beyond it are two chests with a Leather Chestplate and a Green Drop.

---

Return to the mountain entrance and head up, there's a chest here with another EX Charge, and the other orbs we need for a new chain skill.

There's a new enemy two, a False Stalwart, dodging its attack is difficult to be aggressive for the pre-emptive strike. In battle it uses Earthquake, creating spikes of earth in an area in front of it, but its only usable on the ground and has little vertical reach, so stay in the air to crush it with ease.

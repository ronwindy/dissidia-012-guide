As we enter we see the worst cutscene of the game, where in a moment of weakness, Terra asks Vaan to kill her, and he has to talk her down, assuring her the side she's on doesn't matter, he saved her because it was right. Now it's time for the gateway.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The opposition consists of a Phantasmal Girl, Phantasmal Harlequin, two Idle Sky Pirates, and the Strange Battle Piece, the Fleeting Flash.

This time around the Flash has Iron Giant, which will cleave your Bravery in half every time she lands an HP attack. Not a huge problem though since she has no Bravery attacks to take advantage of your weakness. Enemies are Level 15, a bit tougher than before, but not a huge problem.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

A Cross Chain at C4 will chain all five of them for 25 KP.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The two chests here hold a Soul of Destruction and our third Pink Tail.

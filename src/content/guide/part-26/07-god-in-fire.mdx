#### God in Fire

Kiddies, this part of the walkthrough, I won't be playing through the game as I walkthrough it here. The reason is, frankly, I've done this part of the game so often I could play through it in my sleep.

Welcome to the final gateway, it's five floors but don't worry, we're just going to skip to the end fighting as few enemies as possible, after all we want to conserve our strength. Feel free to mix up party members and draw out fights to charge up EX Gauges.

---

Floor by floor:

- The first enemy in our path is a Level 120 Delusory Dragoon with Ultima Weapon, defeat him and continue to the next floor. The chest has a Gold Angel, if you want it the enemy guarding it is a Level 120 Fleeting Flash.
- On the second floor, the enemy is a Level 121 Transient Gunner with Ultima Weapon and a Transient Witch, beat him and continue on, the chests only have Friendship and Valor Resin, if you want them then go to a moogle and don't be cheap.
- On the third floor, the enemy is a Level 122 Imitation Liegeman with Omega, the blue emblem before it restores your HP before the fight.
- On the fourth floor there's two enemies, a Level 127 Imaginary Champion with Ultima Weapon and an Imaginary Soldier, and a Level 125 False Stalwart with Ultima Weapon and a False Hero. Fight your Assist after if you want the Potion and just flee to make them vanish.

---

On the fifth floor, as you enter your Assist is chosen at random. I recommend Gabranth, Kuja, Jecht or Gilgamesh, preferably Jecht.

To get this, use the home button on your PSP to quit the game, when you reload you'll load the game's auto-save to appear back on the fourth floor next to the end piece, and get a new random Assist when you proceed to the fifth, rinse and repeat until you like who they give you.

---

The mured moogle restores our HP, and the path to the boss is clear. On all characters, equip:

- Level Gap ≥ 30
- Large Gap in HP
- (Opp) HP ≥ 10000
- (Opp) Summon Unused
- and whatever other boosters you like

Also consider an Iifa Leaf for everyone. For summons I would recommend Alexander, Bahamut, Omega, Demon Wall, and even Tiamat, Kraken or Carbuncle. Then approach the piece.

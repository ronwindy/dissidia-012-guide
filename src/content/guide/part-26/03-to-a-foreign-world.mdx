Beyond the gateway is a dead end with a Moogle. He sells the Adamantite and Samurai Spirit accessories, needed to trade for the top-grade Level 100 Adamant and Genji sets. The two sets are both three-set combinations, though they have pieces in all four equipment types.

- Adamant Chains boosts your Bravery every time you dodge an attack and increases the distance you move when you dodge in midair, making it easier to dodge attacks.
- The Genji set's Blessing of Yamato gives you +3 luck, +20% HP Regen, converts extra HP from any regeneration into Bravery, and raises the rate at which EX Cores appear.

The moogle also sells the four pieces of the Snowpetal set, there's no weapon of the set but two hand pieces, its combination doubles the power of deflected magic attacks and also gives you a nice boost to your Bravery when you block an attack, equal to 30% of your base Bravery. All three are excellent equipment pieces, some of the best in the game, go nuts if you're tired of the Lufenian stuff.

The moogle also sells the Omega, Hecatoncheir and Ultima Weapon summons, buy all three.

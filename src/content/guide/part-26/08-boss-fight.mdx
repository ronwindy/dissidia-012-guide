Enjoy the *amazing* boss music, Cantata Mortis \& God in Fire. This is the ultimate challenge, Feral Chaos. He's Level 130 with over 120,000 HP, and due to his level innately has a base Bravery of over 3000. Let's run down his attacks.

His Bravery attacks:

- Vicious has him leap in place for a moment before lunging forward, it crushes guards.
- Spew has him slam the ground to summon columns of flame below you, it can be blocked or dodged easily.
- Erupt is an upwards charge attack straight up that has him spin when he decides to stop, not seen too much, I find Feral Chaos prefers to jump up to my level.
- Destroy is his basic aerial Bravery attack and is his main offense with Vicious, a quick combo attack with a spinning tail horizontally, it can be blocked.
- Splinter launches four large waves of fire at you, each once staggers you and tracks individually.
- Raid is the opposite of Erupt, he rushes down to spin when he stops or to slam into the ground and create a shockwave on impact, for this reason stay out from under him if he's high in the air.

For HP attacks:

- Quo Vadis has him pause for a moment then warp towards you and uppercut into the air, then he performs a series of attacks and slams you into a wall.
- Deus Iratus has him block and summon a shockwave around him.
- Via Dolorosa has him send out two waves of fire, but he can delay the firing of the second wave, it's arguably his worst attack because he is *very* patient and will wait for you to dodge and sends it out to catch you on the recovery, or he waits for you to land from a jump and sends it out during the brief moment you're recovering and can't dodge.
- In the air he has Ventus Ire, he angles up or down at you and flaps his wings to generation wind at short-range.
- Lux Magnus has him fire a pillar of fire straight down in the ground and he can move to chase you with it.
- Flagro Maximus has him charge up a large fireball he launches forward.

Also, all of Feral Chaos's attacks Wall Rush, and his Bravery ones all begin Chase as well, if you don't hit a wall first.

---

Now to round out that arsenal with an Assist and summon. Garland is his Assist, attacking with Round Edge or Bardiche. Round Edge can only be blocked on the first swing, remember.

Garland is one of the two factors that makes this battle so annoying, Feral Chaos is *very* good at coordinating attacks with him, comboing a wall rush with an Assist and an HP attack as we often have, and when he calls out Garland while charging Via Dolorosa, well guess what, 9 times out of ten you're going to get hit, even if you dodge Garland, here comes the fire wave.

Next, Shinryu Verus, his summon, is drastically depowered from the original. He copies the effects of the Four Fiend summons - 20 second timer to a Bravery swap, triple Bravery, freezing your Bravery, or sapping it to 0. Pretty easy to handle:

- Bravery swap is easily to manipulate, let Feral Chaos beat you up then land an HP attack to swipe his Bravery and leave him at 0
- triple Bravery, whittle him down
- Bravery freeze, rack up as much as you can and spam HP attacks
- sap to 0, land an HP attack just before it goes off and its effect is wasted

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Here's where things go from bad to worse - Feral Chaos has three unique accessories, one to just boost his HP. Endless Despair gives him a 400% damage boost in EX Mode, and has his Assist charge up 50% faster. and Ruthless Atrocity...any time he lands an HP attack, even if it does no damage, your Assist and EX Gauges are completely drained.

Yeah, that is by far the most annoying part of this fight, for this purpose Assist Change and EX Revenge out of an HP hit if you can, you'll lose them anyway so you might as well avoid the damage part.

BUT, there is a mercy! Feral Chaos has piddling Level 1 equipment. As a result, while his attack is only slighter lower than the Level 120s we've fought so far, his defense is only 167, significantly lower. Thus, with those four boosters I mentioned which will constantly be giving you a 3.8x boost, with a few other boosters you'll actually do quite a lot of Bravery damage, much more than the 120s took.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

And well kiddies, good luck. The best advice I have now is, honestly, video footage - [this playlist](http://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL91CDF0A8E5603350&feature=plcp) shows all 43 fights I've done against Feral Chaos using one character. Don't mistake me, hundreds, possibly even thousands of retries went into them, *especially* the No Damage ones, but they will help you out.

Watch them, take note of his attack patterns, how his moves work, when he likes using them, how to anticipate them and deal with them, when's a safe time to counter-attack, etc. And Feral Chaos *is* highly predictable, he can fake you out decently enough when he tries but largely he is very predictable, and once you get the hang of his attacks it's just a matter of dodging and countering as you whittle down that monstrous HP.

As my vids demonstrate Alexander is awesome and almost single-handedly breaks the difficulty of this fight in two. HP wall rush the opponent, call Assist (Jecht), HP attack in the middle of the Assist attack, they're wall rushed by Jecht, third HP attack, knocks off a large chunk of HP.

Exp to Assist is my recommended Exp ability of choice, the Assist gauge is a critical lifeline, letting you get in guaranteed hits and avoid a fatal HP attack. You probably *will* have to use the entire party for this fight, I did on my first time.

Feral Chaos is tough, but with my vids for help, your own experience and proper preparation, you can handle him and take him out. Just remember to use Assist Change and EX Revenge to avoid damage, and really, watch the vids, you'll see how Feral Chaos fights and will know what to expect even after just a couple of them, and can also see how I use each character to fight him as I use all but Tifa, Vaan, Tidus and Shantotto. Good luck kiddies, you'll need it, but you can do it!

With Feral Chaos vanquished, five chests appear. Four contain the equipment he was wearing, now yours because defeating him unlocks Feral Chaos for purchase in the PP Catalog as a playable character. I won't give him a profile because Feral Chaos is crippled as a character by very high CP costs, you can only equip three or four attacks with him and he's really a novelty than a character.

The four equipment pieces in turn are needed to trade for his exclusive Level 100 equipment. The fifth chest will appear on subsequent trips through the gateway after you beat Feral Chaos on said revisits, and contains a Megalixir.

As we exit, Chaos, having regained his senses, calls out to us - he tells us some intriguing info, and thanks us for saving the mured moogle, and if you didn't know who he was before, well this should make it obvious. The final 20th Report is unlocked, containing the game's secret ending. Go watch it and enjoy it, you've earned it.

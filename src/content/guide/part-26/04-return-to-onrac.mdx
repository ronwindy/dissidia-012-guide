---

What makes this gateway so significant is that this is *the* best one for earning KP. Come here with a Level 1 character with no equipment, and for accessories the four "No equipment" boosters, Level 1-9, Level Gap ≥ 90, and any 1.4 or 1.5 booster you like, Pre-EX Mode will do nicely. Then equip the Smiting Soul accessory.

You'll get 99 KP for going under the bonus line, and in battle all opponents will enter with 9999 Bravery, until you hit them and Iai Strike has a 99.9% of instantly Breaking them, transferring that 9999 Bravery to you and making the battle go quite quickly. Clear out the enemies one by one without chain skills, and grab the moogle chalice for 240 total KP.

Now, this isn't much more than the Frozen Continent gateway. However, because you can get to 240 KP without any skills, you can bring in 5 KP Bonuses with you, increasing that 240 KP to 7,680. Strictly speaking one of the Multifloor gateways gives much more, into the tens of thousands, head into the Forsaken Land to find out.

In the long run, you'll find this is the best gateway for KP even without KP Bonuses, since Frozen Continent takes longer to clear, bring along Multichains and a KP Gambler for even more winnings, include a KP Switch if you think the KP Chance is too difficult.

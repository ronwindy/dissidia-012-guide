# Land of Discord and Feral Chaos

The time has come kiddies! Land of Discord and the end of the game's storyline and this walkthrough are imminent!

#### Return to Where It Began

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Land_of_Discord.png" width={266} height={227} alt="World B Land of Discord" />

You don't have to worry about Chain skills if you don't want to for the next few gateways, I'll still note how to chain most enemies but earning KP from them isn't a concern.

First, you should already have several thousand, and second, by the time you need more you'll have access to *the* most efficient gateway for earning KP. Besides that, the KP Chance is hard to meet, because in this final region, *all* enemies are Level 120+. Good luck getting that 60 second win/Break every single time. Far easier (and less madening) to just not worry about KP for these gateways.

---

Also, if you don't yet have Level 100 equipment for everyone, go farm some Lufenian items at an earlier gateway. Try and use matching pieces if you can, since you probably can't access Level 100 equipment in the shop on a major basis yet, you're going to have to make due with Lufenian stuff, so you might as well access their combination skills.

For me:

- Cloud and Lightning are using Excalibur
- Prishe has the Level 100 Ehrgeiz grappling weapon
- Zidane and Terra have their exclusive Level 100 weapons

If you have the trade accessory needed for a Level 100 weapon, you should have several Electrums, while Scarletites for the Level 30s are sold by the moogle near the Gulg Volcano, accessible by the Pravoka or Elven Castle teleport stones. Easy to spot and get to, if you're unsure it's a white ring on your map.

---

Head into the Land of Discord from the Citadel of Trials teleport stone and make a white. You'll come to the Inherited Memories gateway.

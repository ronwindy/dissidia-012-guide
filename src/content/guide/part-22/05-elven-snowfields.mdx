Beyond the gateway is a moogle, this is the second awesome thing, that gateway not only has 9 enemies to chain, but it's about two feet from a moogle who sells Multichains and KP Gamblers, so KP grinding is quick and easy! Sure you get a penalty if you're over level 60, but if you do the math, you're getting 240 KP a run, so even at level 100 you're just losing 40 KP, but the level advantage makes the fights go much quicker, so it evens out.

The moogle sells the Warlord and Heike equipment:

- The Warlord stuff is level 30 armor with a Combiation, but unlike the Mythril and Gold stuff you need all four pieces to activate it, lamely the effect is just faster Bravery recovery.
- The Heike equipment is level 60 and gives you a full EX Gauge at the start of each fight, but in return your EX Mode only lasts half as long.

Quite useful I imagine for someone like Cloud or Terra, with EX Mode damage buffs so awesome they don't *need* a lot of time in EX Mode to batter the opponent and end the fight.

---

Near the moogle is a Level 88 Imaginary Champion to play with, he has the normal Bahamut. Beyond it is a chest with a KP Bonus.

I bolded that because if you don't need KP Bonuses, or have no room for them in the skill list, leave the chests with them alone. The only way to get more aside from the chests is the Cornelia moogle, which only sells one a day on a specific two days or so, so leave the chests be and come back for them later when you need them, just scroll up and down the page looking for the bolded KP Bonus word to find them when you need them.

Further down the main path is a Diamond, and further down is the Gateway of Melting Sorrows.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

You'll be revisiting this gateway a lot in the future, as it's the best place to grind for AP. Doing Arcade Mode will reward Diamond Studs, Diamond Rings and Diamond Necklaces that boost the AP won, but have a 100% break rate. The blue emblem at C5 stops accessories from breaking, and a Straight Chain there with a Moogle in close proximity to buy more lets you fight five enemies with a huge AP boost.

As a result from now on, whenever you want to AP grind, do it here, best place. Multichain at C4 where you enter to get all enemies, the KP Chance is a 30 second Break again, and the bonus line is 50.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Level 42 Fallacious Wanderer.
- Second is a Level 41 Counterfeit Youth.
- Third is a Level 50 Counterfeit Wraith with Hecatoncheir.
- Then comes a Level 50 Imitation Despot with Brynhildr.
- Fourth, because we love them so, a Level 51 Fallacious Tree with Asura.
- Last is a Level 52 Transient Witch, she has Deathgaze.
- The seventh foe the chain misses is a Level 52 Ephemeral Phantom with the Chocobo summon.

Of note of something you may notice, now with the 013 story complete, the Battlegen for several arenas has been unlocked - Pandaemonium Top Floor, Crystal Tower, Phantom Train, and Prima Vista.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest randomly has a Tome of Memories or a Tome of Mysteries, both needed to trade for many special accessories that affect how you absorb EX Force, some allowing you to absorb EX Force while attacking or being attacked, and others affecting how much EX Force you absorb, or how long EX Mode and EX Revenge last, and more. Check what's for sale and feel free to re-enter this gateway and farm a couple Tomes. I won't be, but you can if you see something you like.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain where you come in at A5 to get them all, or Jump Chain at C4 to get them all with the Initial BRV 0 purple emblem.

Strictly speaking you can just get to the exit and unseal the gateway just by fighting your Assist. They'll come in at level 100 with full equipment and accessories and an Assist of their own, while you have none. You don't actually have to beat your Assist though, just challenge it and then flee and it will vanish and leave the way open. But you can try to beat it if you want a challenge.

You might as well just do this to unseal the gateway, with only five enemies there's no point chaining them. The KP Chance is a 30 second win and the bonus line is 60.

</PanelSection>

Beyond this gateway, there's no teleport stone, but there is a moogle! He sells many, though not all, of the trade components in the game, including Mythril, Gold, Crystal, and Diamond.

I bought four Diamonds so I could use the Diamond gear and begin AP grinding. Be warned though, unlike the Gold and Mythril sets, there is no Diamond Staff, and with only Armor and Vest for body armor, mages won't be able to use the set. Well, there *is* a way, but it costs 500,000 gil, quite a bit out of our price range at the moment.

The moogle also sells attack skills, like EX, BRV and Assist Charge, Soundwave, Maser Eye, etc.

---

I also traded in my two Hi-Elixirs from the Cornelia moogle to get a Darksteel Ingot, allowing me to get the powerful Save the Queen sword. The equivalent Rod, sadly, needed a Moore Log which also needed two Hi-Elixirs, but I bought a Macalania Timber from the Moogle to trade up to the almost as good Staff of the Magi, giving Terra a Level 60 weapon. I also bought from him a Chimera Hide to get Prishe the Burning Fist, since the Cat Claws too, needed two Hi-Elixirs to get a Behemoth Leather for trade.

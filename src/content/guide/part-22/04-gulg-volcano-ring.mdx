#### Gulg Volcano Ring

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Volcano_Ring_Gulg.png" width={216} height={181} alt="Ancient Volcano Ring - Gulg" title="Ancient Volcano Ring - Gulg" />

This region also covers the Crescent Lake area down to that island where the teleport stone was in the 013 story. There's only one gateway in this region, but lots of treasures and our first map enemies, as you can see from the map.

Follow the path to the waterfront, the gateway to the left is sealed so smash the rock and grind over. The chest has a Diamond, which naturally trades for Diamond equipment, but it's Level 60 gear and a few levels out of our range now. Go along the path to a split, you can go up into the volcano, or to a gateway.

---

Up in the volcano is a chest with a KP Bonus, and our first map enemy, a Delusory Warlock Level 88, ouch. He has Demon Wall, a Delusory Knight Assist, Level 60 equipment with Level 100 Armor, and a Hyper Ring with 9 Near Loss bostters.

He is easily the most challenging opponent in the game so far, have fun. Remember and feel free to use the entire party to fight him, fortunately when fighting on the overworld, party members are automatically healed after a fight, so it's five-on-one with no penalties.

---

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain at D4 to get all enemies. The enemies here and in the next area are about 10 levels above previous enemies, fortunately you're more than 10 levels stronger by now. The bonus line is 60 and the KP Chance is a 30 second Break.

The blue emblem you're on refills your HP before each battle so feel free to get beaten up a bit.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is an Level 63 Ephemeral Vision.
- Then comes the first Party piece, Level 52 Transient Witch with Deathgaze, a Level 52 Capricious Reaper with an unknown summon, and a Level 52 Ephemeral Phantom with Chocobo.
- The second one is a Level 51 Fallacious Tree with Asura, 51 Phantasmal Harlequin with Ultros, and 51 Imaginary Champion with Bahamut auto.

Yes, even now gateways reuse enemy set-ups - not that they aren't working, right?

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The two chests each randomly have a Tome of the Farplane and a Tome of Lies. They give access to the Dismay Shock and Battle Hammer, basic accessories that sap a bit of the opponent's EX or Assist Gauge when you deal HP damage. They allow access to more accessories:

- like Aggressor and Avenger, which increase the damage done during EX Mode or EX Revenge but with a penalty
- the largely useless Great Gospel to boost Regen rate and Bravery recovery rate
- or the awesome Beckoning Cat, which doubles the AP won in battle
- the decent Sturm und Drang and Soul of Thamasa, which boost physical or magic damage with benefits to wall rush and chase damage
- and finally the pathetic Battle Ring, which gives you a 5% chance to not lose the charge when you call an Assist

---

On the other side, the moogle sells trade accessories, specific ones:

- There's Pink Tails
- the Gear Collection, needed for the Machine equipment which has high stats but needs an ability to be used
- and Scarletite, which as I said is needed for exclusive weapons

Nothing we need yet, but if you're short on Pink Tails you'll want to come back here later.

The Ribbon is level 60 armor and well out of our range, but it gives a -20% chance for breakable accessories to break, letting us use the Exp-boosting Chocobo accessories with only a 10% break rate, and of course at level 100 we can trade it for the Super Ribbon with a -30% chance. But that's not for yet some time, just remember this place for future reference.

Grab a Multichain and KP Gambler for the road. Follow the path into the mountains, there's a moogle to chat with and a Crystal in a chest, but it's otherwise a dead end.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

---

Return to the volcano and descend into the Elvan Snowfields. Watch out on the journey, the Warlock may have respawned.

Fun discovery too - if you get a sneak attack on them, the 0 initial Bravery carries between battles! Send out your first party member, easy Break and HP damage, then fight as normal and if you lose and go to the next person, they still have 0 Bravery and are easy pickings for the next person in line. Times fight party members and yeah, slightly unfair.

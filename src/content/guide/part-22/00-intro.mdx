# Southern Areas

Part 22 kiddies, time to dive into a living nightmare!

#### Beyond Discord

Before launching Scenario 000, let's cover a few things. First, the world is entirely wide-open, you can go anywhere you want in the southern regions and can go right for the first objective.

This is a *bad* idea though, since the enemies in the objective gateway are quite high level, 90 in fact, and you're likely no higher than 50 after the main storyline, if even that. So I'm going to go around the world visiting other gateways and powering up the party before we get to the main mission.

000 lets you use a party of *any* five people you like, I will be using Zidane, Terra, Cloud, Lightning and Prishe. So, let's do it!

---

<CharacterIntro name="Prishe" src="drake/Prishe.png" width={100} height={324}>

Prishe is here, where's the grub? I've never played FF11, but as I understand it in her original game she's an immortal due to a crystal embedded in her chest that marks her as the mortal vessel for the day the twilight god Promathia will descend to the world, but the god was vanquished and Prishe made mortal. Naturally none of this comes up in D012.

Prishe is a foul-mouthed, rowdy tomboy who loves a good brawl, and as seen in report flashbacks was one of the earlier fighters in the war with Shantotto. I quite like Prishe, she's a spunky lass and rather funny to listen to, especially once she hits Level 100 and you can unlock her hidden voice clips to randomly appear in battle. I won't spoil the fun, kiddies, level her up and find them for yourselves.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Prishe has a unique fighting style. She has eight Bravery combo attacks aside from the obligatory ranged ones.

Each Bravery attack though can set one of the eight, including itself, as a follow-up attack, and much like an HP link, or more specifically Onion Knight's Bravery links, Prishe can then use two attacks in a row to pile up more damage. Combining certain skills like this together can create a Skillchain for even more damage.

Prishe's attacks are:

- Combo, a three-hit combo attack
- Dragon Kick, a leg sweep that can be charged to crush guards and fire a projectile into the air
- Howling Fist, a wind-up punch that crushes guard when charged
- Should Tackle, a leaping shoulder charge
- Spinning Attack, an aerial uppercut
- Backhand Blow, a drop-down punch attack
- Raging Fists, a five-hit combo
- and One-inch Punch, a wind-elemental punch that crushes guards when charged

Phew!

---

Prishe's Skillchains do pretty massive damage, and she has quite a bit of tactical diversity with them too. As she levels up and learns them I'll be using the set-up I preferred in another file on this game:

- Combo-Combo
- Howling Fist-Shoulder Tackle
- Dragon Kick-Backhand Blow
- One-Inch Punch-Howling Fist
- Backhand Blow-One-Inch Punch
- and Spinning Attack-Spinning Attack

You can use whichever set-ups you like. In her EX Mode, Prishe gets a movement speed boost and performs her second attacks twice in a row to do even more damage. Her EX Burst is pretty simple, a button command appears and then you quickly press them in sequence clockwise starting at that button.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

I know I'm going in-depth on Prishe's Bravery game, and it's because that's her real strength - her HP attacks *suck*, oh how they suck!

They aren't *bad* compared to each other:

- Auroral Uppercut is well, an uppercut
- Banishga sends out a shockwave around her
- Asuran Fists is a rapidfire punch barrage that reaches forward and into the air
- and Nullifying Dropkick is a long-range homing dropkick

The problem with them is they all have massive start-up, making them ridiculously easy to avoid since the opponents in 000 are competent now, and forcing Prishe to rely on blocking and punishing, Assists, or Chases to deal HP damage.

However, in spite of her flaws Prishe is still one of my favorite D012 characters, her awesome Bravery game makes her a blast to play as, and I like using my Assists anyway so relying on them isn't too big a concern for me.

</PanelSection>

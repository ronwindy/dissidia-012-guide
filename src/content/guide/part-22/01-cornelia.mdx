#### Cornelia

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Cornelian_Plains.png" width={316} height={196} alt="World B Cornelian Plains" />

As we begin, a voice calls out to us and asks us to come rescue it near Melmond, in the Chasm in the Rotting Land. We wake up on the beach near Cornelia, and a moogle is surprised to see us! Buy some Pebbles from it.

The accessory line-up is up to you, I recommend a Hyper Ring with a Muscle Belt or Earring and whatever boosters you like, the Sunrise is not needed. Also as a note, at any time if you save and quit the storyline, when you re-launch it you can change your party freely.

---

Buy whatever limited items the moogle has for sale and go north and left, Final Fantasy fans will recognize this place as the ruins of the Chaos Shrine. The wiki has maps of this scenario so I'll provide them for you. Take the chest, chat with the moogle if you like, then return to Cornelia and enter the Gateway to Departure.

As we enter, spirits of fallen warriors call to us and offer their assistance, but warn they're not here to help you in your quest, they'll stay within the gateways and will attack if you approach them.

Be warned - in all gateways, talking to your Assist will trigger a fight with their character, and they will always be level 100 with full equipment and strong accessories. Furthermore, flee or victory, they will vanish and you'll have to complete the gateway without an Assist. It's best to just avoid them unless they deliberately block your path, and I'll mention such circumstances.

Multichain at B3 to get all enemies with the physical critical rate up emblem. The golden area is a locked area, we can't pass it until later.

From now on I'll only cover the enemy types in so much as listing who they are, what level they are, and what summon and Assist they have. I won't list movesets because honestly from this point forward most enemy types use the same moveset, so you'll learn from experience what they have. When we come to the handful of *new* enemies, then we'll cover movesets.

As a general note, enemies are not to be underestimated, even the Normal ones have accessories, equipment and good movesets, critical hit rate boosting abilities, and are pretty smart. This gateway should serve as a warning of what's to come, because it's all uphill from here. Remember to use your summons, kiddies! The bonus line is 50 and the KP Chance is a 30 second Break, not too hard with the emblem helping out.

When the gateway is exited, you'll notice it doesn't shatter like normal but turns into a blue gateway instead, letting us come and go freely and pass through it.

Go north through the mountains, on the left behind the grass is another chest with a Crystal, then visit the moogle. He has a lot of boosters for sale, which ones you buy depends on your fighting style, I found none of them to be usable myself. The Level Gap ones are useful later though, you can buy them now if you like. Otherwise buy two Multichains and two KP Gamblers - yup, the moogles sell them now. Then head to the Gateway of Good and Evil.

#### Melmond Fens

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Melmond_Fens.png" width={338} height={262} alt="Melmond Fens" title="Melmond Fens" />

There's actually very little to do here, no gateways to unlock as you can see save for two, and they're special ones. Go out and to the right to find a Level 89 Transient Witch with Kraken to fight, and she's got an Iifa Dew, yay. The island has a Diamond on it.

On the other path you can see two distant gateways over the see, the moogle says that one of them is the Chasm in the Rotting Land, where the mured moogle is sealed, but the only way there is by teleport stone. Head further into the region and grind over to an island with a Crystal.

---

After heading through an empty gateway in the mountains, turn left, it will begin to rain here. Soon you're come to a teleport stone in a ruined town, smash the rock and grind over to a chest with a Diamond. Cross the swamplands and you'll find another Transient Witch to make friends with. On the beach behind it is a KP Bonus.

Return to the previously mentioned gateway and go right this time. Go to the right this time and smash the rock, the island has yet another Crystal. The gateway here leads back to the Cornelia region.

---

Well, we've explore the entire world now. Go visit the various moogles and buy whatever needed, grab some of the listed KP Bonuses and grind for KP as I told you if you're poor. We can head to the mured moogle's resting place, but first, there's two optional gateways to explore. Grab a couple Multichains any maybe a KP Gambler, then head to a teleport stone.

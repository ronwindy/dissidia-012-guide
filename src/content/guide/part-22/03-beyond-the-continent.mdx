On the world map with this gateway unsealed, head forward to the rock and grind to the island to find a Crystal. You probably have enough to trade for more Crystal equipment so I'll cover over that.

Like the Gold and Mythril stuff, wearing three pieces of Crystal equipment activates the Blessed Crystal combination, giving you +20% from EX Force and EX Cores. Pretty helpful and the Crystal stuff has better stats than the Gold stuff, it's up to you. I'm sticking with the Gold stuff just for extra cash, but if you're someone hwo likes abusing EX Mode, go ahead with the Crystal stuff, and throw in two Cyan Drops too.

---

Beyond that Crystal though, this area has little. There's a teleport stone here, but the one path is barred by a closed gateway. Head through the open path to a new region.

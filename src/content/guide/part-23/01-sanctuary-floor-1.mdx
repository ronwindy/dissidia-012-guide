#### Sanctuary

You can do either of these two gateways in any order, but I'm going to do this one first since the other has a higher bonus line, so the game seems to say this one should be first. These two gateways are good warm-ups for the Chasm in the Rotting Land.

Teleport to the Cavern of Earth teleport stone, this is where the Chasm in the Rotting Land is, but we're bypassing it. Follow the beach to a chest with a Crystal, around the other end we can see our destination gateway. Shatter the rock and grind to another Crystal, and to the left and behind the gateway are yet more Crystals. Finally, head inside.

As we enter we meet Shantotto, that bossy little mage we've met in reports. She says we have no business here, but since we can't find the way out, she invites us to fight our way to find the path.

This is another hold-over from the original Dissidia, when this gateway was its own storyline. The Official Quests also explain this gateway is a path to another dimension, and despite the fact we can leave any time, in the story, we're stuck in this alternate dimension now.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain at D2 to chain all enemies, letting us voice the annoying red emblems on the two pieces guarding that chest. This gateway has five floors, actually four when it comes to exploration and chaining, so even only two Multichains is plenty to get us through the way.

The bonus line is 75, and the KP Chance on all floors is a 90 second win. The blue emblem is an accessories protected, but I don't use resins and was already at a pretty high level and needed no help from the Chocobo stuff. so this was meaningless to me.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Level 60 Lady of Antiquity, the Shantotto manikin which you've probably fought in a Report, you're perhaps a bit unfamiliar with them but the huge level advantage will take care of any disadvantage that might give you.
- Second comes a Level 59 Idle Sky Pirate.
- Third, a Level 59 Delusory Dragoon.
- Last, a Level 60 Ephemeral Summoner.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Shantotto's main problem should be obvious right off the bat - Level 70. As a result you're going to benefit from a pretty large Bravery boost. Of course, she has the same - she's got nine Blue Gems equipped, which is why she has that whopping 1,391 base Bravery, and thus her HP attacks are going to hurt a lot.

She's also got the Bonecrusher to give her another Bravery boost when near death. She has the Ultima Weapon summon and a Horror of Antiquity Assist.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

This fight was over before Ultima Weapon fired, in spite of Shantotto calling it right at the start of the fight. The reason is simple - I had the Bahamut summon, the perfect summon for Order's Sanctuary Ω. When I broke Shantotto the stage gimmick doubled my Bravery, which was over 4000 between the damage I was doing and Bahamut's boosts. Then it was just the formality of an EX Burst finish and the Chainspeller was felled.

---

Even if you don't use this strategy, Shantotto won't be too much trouble, her massive level disadvantage will work against her, as long as you got the hang of her attack patterns when facing her manikins, you'll be fine. Just watch out for Stun and remember to block her ground staff combo, you can block her aerial staff throw but it'll stagger you and you might get hit by something else.

</PanelSection>

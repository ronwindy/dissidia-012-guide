As we reach the final level, Gabranth realizes the world above holds meaning for us, if we're fighting so hard to get back.

The last two enemies are:

- a Level 79 Warrior of Antiquity with Zalera and an Idle Sky Pirate
- and a Level 80 Horror of Antiquity with a Lady of Antiquity and Hecatoncheir

The red chest is another enemy in disguise, a Level 97 Idle Sky Pirate with Cactuar and a Warrior of Antiquity Assist.

---

You now have an important dilemma to deal with, you can get those two chests with Level 90 trade accessories, but you have to fight your Assist to do it. The choice is yours.

The normal chest has a random piece of the Elemental Archfiend equipment, a four-piece set that grants a boost to your Bravery during block, dodge, quickmove and stage destruction. It also sucks, largely due to its level. Square-Enix, if you're going to make an equipment set that forces us to run through a dungeon of Level 80 enemies at least four times to get, we're probably past the point that Level 60 equipment is useful.

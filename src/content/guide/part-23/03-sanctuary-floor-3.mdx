<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First foe on the chopping block, a Level 62 Fallacious Wanderer.
- Next is a Level 61 Horror of Antiquity, naturally from the level it's much weaker than the Expert one from before.
- Then comes the Party - Level 63 Transient Lion, Level 63 Capricious Thief, Level 63 Ephemeral Vision, and finally a Level 79 Lady of Antiquity.

---

Then comes one of the Expert enemies, a new one, a Fallacious Giant, Gilgamesh's Manikin. He has:

- Cross Slash, a low-ranged chop attack on the ground
- Whirlwind Slash, a slow long-ranged dive forward
- Wind Slash, which fires a blast of wind
- Double Trouble, a very quick but very short-ranged attack
- Tsubamegeshi, a diving chop forward
- and Electrocute, which summons a lightning bolt around you

His HP attacks are:

- Death Claw, a two-hit charge attack forward similar to Cloud's Cross-Slash but with one less hit and more horizontal range
- Jump, which is just like Kain's but with longer lag on impact and no charging
- Hurricane, a close-range aerial spin attack
- and Missile, which fires a large homing missile at you that explodes

His summon, appropriately, is Gilgamesh, which randomly triples his Bravery or reduces it to 1. His Assist is a Fallacious Tree. He's not too dangerous as long as you don't get close, aside from Double Trouble and Hurricane his attacks are easy to anticipate and dodge or block, once you get the hang of him of course, feel free to retry.

---

The last enemy is a Level 89 Counterfeit Wraith, not sure what her summon is, she never used it.

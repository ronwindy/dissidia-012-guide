As we reach the exit, Gabranth understands we still wish to return, so now there's one final test. Time for the Judge Magister to do what he does best - dispense judgement upon us and carry out sentencing.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Gabranth is a *much* more competent opponent than Shantotto. You've probably got a level advantage on him, but a much weaker one than Shantotto, since Gabranth is Level 90 to Shantotto's 70.

And they gave him what is, honestly, one of the most brilliant accessory builds of any enemy - a Puppeteer's Wheel. Yes, the same one Terra had in her boss fights that let her stay in EX Mode the entire fight. Ho boy. Gabranth also has an Attractorb and Hyper Ring hooked up to five EX Mode boosters, giving him a 3.7x booster as long as he's in EX Mode, and after 30 seconds pass in the fight, off go two more boosters, taking him to 8.3x.

And to finish up, he's got an Idle Sky Pirate Assist and the Omega Summon. About the only way he could be worse is if they gave him an Iifa Dew or Leaf, or a Muscle Belt instead of the Hyper Ring. Fortunately he doesn't have EX Critical Boost or else most all of his attacks would be critical hits.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

I hope you came into the fight with a full EX Gauge, because with that pumped up Attractorb he won't be leaving any EX Force for you. Between that and the Puppeteer's Wheel, Gabranth is going to be in EX Mode the entire fight unless you knock him out of it with an Assist Break...unless you killed your Assist to get access to those chests. Oops.

As well, the fight is in Edge of Madness Ω, where the Bravery pool is constantly in flux from several thousand to a few hundred Bravery. Time your Break well or else you'll have nothing to show for your effort in wearing him down.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

You know how Gabranth's attacks work from his manikins, he's just a much bigger, beefier version of them, and is fought in a very small arena so you have nowhere to run or hide from him. His accessories are going to give him a big damage buff so I hope you can block on a whim because if he connects he's going to hurt, and know which of his attacks can't be blocked. And of course, don't forget he's going to be recovering HP the entire fight thanks to Regen.

He's a very powerful, very *smart* opponent, and he can thrash you easily. If you didn't kill your Assist, charge it up and get in an Assist attack to EX Break him and end his EX Mode, then don't let him sit still long enough to charge it back up. If not, then it's just a battle of pure brawn. Good luck kiddies!

</PanelSection>

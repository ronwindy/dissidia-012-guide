#### Highway to Hell

Restock your chain skills and *definitely* bring a Multichain and KP Gambler this time. Otherwise, bring a Jump Chain or two. Use the teleport stone to teleport to the Marsh Cave, this is the gateway down in the snowfields we couldn't reach before. Head down, open the chest for a Crystal, then dive into the gateway. This time Lightning will lead the charge.

As we enter, we find ourselves approached by Gabranth. He leaves quickly, telling us to choose our path and head down it, not thaty it matters. This is another alternate dimension like the one Shantotto dwells in, but this one is where those who refuse to fight in the cycle are banished until they submit. Gabranth was one such warrior and now guards this place in case anyone stumbles into it, like we have.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Jump Chain at D3 to get all enemies, the purple emblem is a KP +1. You could also do it at B3, but that purple emblem is an Initial BRV 0 so we win less KP. Also only do this if you have more than one Jump Chain, if not hold onto it.

The bonus line is 80, and the KP Chance is a 90 second win. Enemies here are much stronger than in Shantotto's gateway, the weakest you'll see is Level 70s, most though reach into the 80s and 90s.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First up is a Level 79 Lady of Antiquity with Carbuncle.
- Second is a Level 80 Horror of Antiquity with Hecatoncheir.
- Third, a Level 89 False Stalwart with Behemoth and a False Hero Assist.
- The last is a Warrior of Antiquity Level 79, Gabranth's Manikin.

Don't be deceived by how the fight in the Reports against Gabranth went, these guys are equipped for war. As I covered in his profile, Gabranth's gimmick is to charge that EX Gauge and stay in EX Mode where he's a powerhouse of mayhem. Well, the Warrior has an Arcane Resin to begin with a full EX Gauge, then he has a Pearl Necklace, Dragonfly Orb, Attractorb, and 6 Break boosters. I'd have given him EX Mode boosters, but that is a good build.

---

He's armed with:

- Enrage, a long and powerful ground combo that has him dash forward on start-up
- Relentless Lunge, a series of three sharp dashes forward
- Aero, which creates a blockable tornado
- Rupture, a dive up or down at you to slam you in the same direction
- Dual Rend, a quick two-hit blast backwards
- and Vortex of Judgment, a trio of spin attacks

His HP attacks are:

- Execution, a charge forward to consume you in an explosion
- Innocence, sending four beams of energy in an arc downwards
- and Hatred, creating a sphere of energy around him that explodes

Have fun fighting the Judge Magister in his most powerful form and seeing just how strong he is, and get used to it because the real thing is even worse as I'll cover when the time comes.

</PanelSection>

Shantotto needs more proof of our power, let's give it to her. The best way to chain enemies is a Cross Chain at F4 to get four, but our Assist is in the way, fight it if you want to clear it out.

- The enemy on H2 above is a Level 60 Transient Gunner.
- Third, a Level 62 Fallacious Wanderer.
- The last Hard enemy is a Level 63 Capricious Thief.
- The Expert piece is a Level 50 Horror of Antiquity, Prishe's manikin. She's pretty tough, a Hyper Ring with No BRV Damage and 8 HP = 100% boosters, and the Hecatoncheir summon.

Once you turn off those boosters though she isn't too hard, though Prishe's fast attacks are annoying, her slow HP attacks and the Horror's lack of an Assist make it easy to avoid HP damage. Remember to use the crystal of the arena to help drain the enemy's Bravery. Defeating the Expert reveals the tile at B1 so we can reach the third chest.

On the final floor, the layout is simple and not needing a map. Shantotto thanks us for our help, in spite of how much of a loser we are, and invites us to come receive our reward. There's a final enemy to face, as you can see, a Level 62 Horror of Antiquity.

The blue and gold chest has more level 90 trade stuff, the normal chest has a random piece of the "Allure of Honey" set - the Sexy Cologne, Member's Card, Blonde Wig and Silk Dress. When all four are worn, the Battlegen and item drop rates increase by 50%. It's pretty useless though, Mognet gives us an endless supply of Superslick and Mog's Amulet for those effects, and for Battlegen there's the Sunrise. Not to mention the equipment is a lame Level 30 set.

Now, you can approach the end piece, or you can fight your Assist and get them out of the way to get access to the chests their guarding, three more trade accessories. The choice is yours, but I say do it.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

There's a lot of enemies here but don't be intimidated, we can handle them.

- First fight the Imitation Despot at D4, then move there and use a Multichain to chain six enemies and win a lot of KP.
- Feel free to use that EX Charge and take damage in the fights, that Potion there will let us fill our HP and EX to full before we face the boss.

---

Speaking of EX Mode, time to chat about that I guess.

- Yuna's EX Mode increases her damage output to insane levels, when she uses a Bravery attack two Aeons appear to attack at once.
- Her EX Burst has commands appear as rings of light close around them, press the shown command just as the ring closes to be just around the command icon, repeat several times.

During this round of enemies Yuna got to Level 11 and learned a new HP attack along the way up, Hellfire.

It creates three columns of flame in front of her with a pretty high reach, it's effective at close-range and can deflect those attacks like Flare and Windburst on start-up, but otherwise stick with Energy Ray.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies are two Ephemeral Phantoms, two Ephemeral Visions, two False Heroes, an Imitation Despot, and an Ephemeral Summoner, all enemies you've seen before and can handle.

- The Phantoms have Triumphant Grasp, a downward-arcing charge HP attack that can easily be avoided by just getting above them.
- The Visions have Energy Rain. One of them is the Strange Battle Piece and has Odin along with 3000 starting Bravery. Cactuar is good if you have charges for it.
- The False Heroes have an HP attack called Radiant Sword, it creates six swords of light that fire outwards and spread out, then turn and rush you to impale you. It can't be blocked and the swords will deflect your projectiles, so you'll have to dodge them. They have quite a bit of range on them too.

As I said, if you take an HP hit or two, it's fine.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chests have a Pink Tail (that's six) and a Soul of Valor.

</PanelSection>

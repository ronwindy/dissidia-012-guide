<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up this time is two **Idle Sky Pirates**, an **Ephemeral Phantom**, a **Counterfeit Wraith**, and a **Fleeting Flash**.

One of the **Idle Sky Pirates** is perhaps our most dangerous foe yet:

- He begins with 3000 **Bravery**.
- Uses the **Tiamat** summon to triple it up to 9000.
- And then spams **Windburst**, and **Yuna** has nothing that can reflect it yet.

I would say this is the first battle so far where **Carbuncle** is useful, except that as a **Strange Battle Piece** he has low HP so once your **Bravery** hits 0 from the HP attack he'll be dead.

Instead try **Cactuar**, wear him down as much as you can before **Tiamat** finishes its countdown to activation and then summon **Cactuar** if you get him to say, 1100 or lower, leaving him with a few hundred once **Tiamat** goes off. If you can't get him that weak then don't use **Cactuar**, save the charges.

---

# Chapter 8

Part 17! Hey, my birthday was August 17th! Cosmic...no, not really actually.

#### The Thief Steals The Show

Another new character, we're running out of them eh?

---

<CharacterIntro name="Zidane" src="drake/Dissidia_Zidane.png" width={200} height={219} alt="Dissidia Zidane">

The monkey-boy thief is here! **Zidane** is my favorite hero in the series, just barely outranking **Terra**. While other heroes are bland, annoying and/or angsty, **Zidane** is cool, friendly, laid-back and witty, not to mention being a rampant flirt. He does have his hesitations and insecurities, but he deals with them better than so many other character, overcompensating for them instead of being emotionally crippled. Overall he's just a really cool guy, characters like **Cloud** and **Squall** are ones you might enjoy doing a thesis on for a psychology class. **Zidane** is the character you'd enjoy going to a party with and just hanging out. Apparently his English voice actor gets some flak, but I like it, totally different from the Japanese VA but I think it works. He sounds better in battle than in cutscenes, he sounds too deep in the latter, but then battles take up the majority of time you listen to the voice actors anyway. And hey, if you want to wield a badass weapon, it doesn't get much more awesome than reverse-gripping two swords that can connect into a dual-bladed sword - just ask **Gabranth** and Xehanort.

**Zidane** is, in my humble opinion but this is my walkthrough so I'm right, the best character in the game. He excels at aerial combat, having a decent projectile attack, a diving attack, rising attack, forward spin attack, and a quick close-range keep away attack. His ground moves are limited, but they're so effective they're all he needs, His HP attacks are likewise awesome, he's got a mid-range charge attack, a close-range explosion of energy, and a long-range entrapment attack of lightning and water. He also has HP links to boot. **Zidane**'s "weakness" is that he doesn't do a lot of **Bravery** damage, though this is a bit of an informed attribute to me, he does plenty good damage to kick ass, and when you've got some HP attacks including links, who cares if the **Bravery** damage is lame, HP damage is what wins battles. Sure he's no **Squall** or **Cloud** when it comes to his **Bravery** game, but he isn't as pathetic as **Onion Knight** so don't write his **Bravery** game off as weak - his **Booster** 8 to Storm **Vortex** hits just as hard as **Squall**'s **Solid Barrel**.

**Zidane** starts out with **Rumble Rush** on the ground, a quick rolling kick foward that launches the opponent into a chase, **Swift Attack** which is a quick two-hit close-range move that begins a chase, and **Scoop Art**, which fires out three slow-moving energy bolts that track the opponent. Don't worry, he'll learn **Tempest** and **Vortex** before **Level 10**, giving him more options in the air. His HP attacks are Tidal Flame, which sends a ring of fireballs spinning along the ground to explode in a fairly wide radius, **Free Energy**, a very fast but very short range blast of energy, and **Shift Break**, creating a tempest of lightning strikes around the opponent before blasting them with water, very good for opponents who try to charge up attacks at range, like **Starfall**, **Meltdown**, **Black Materia**, etc.

As I said, I consider **Zidane** one of the game's best characters, he's my main, and he'll be back for the Epilogue and in Confessions of the Creator, so learn to love him. And you will, he may seem weak now, but once he learns his higher level attacks and gets his HP links, you'll be raining furry Genome death on the opposition.

</CharacterIntro>

As we open, **Zidane** and **Bartz** decide to turn the **Crystal** search into a treasure hunt to see who can get theirs first.

**Straight Chain** on the purple emblem at C3, naturally. It's a **KP** +1 emblem, score! The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. Before approaching the end piece, suit up with the **Empty Assist Gauge** booster. As the introductory screen and the end of **Squall**'s story spoiled for us, someone is about to get captured. **Bartz** and **Zidane** find what appears to be a **Crystal**. But when he runs off to grab it, there's a flash of light and he vanishes. **Kefka** pops out, musing how that wasn't the plan, but he can still have fun with **Zidane**. **Zidane** just wants to know what he's done, and he's prepared to beat the answers out of him.

This is a pitifully easy battle, **Kefka**'s got a **Cursed Ring** that really cripples him. He's got the typical attacks and the **Lich** summon to begin a 15 second countdown to sapping your **Bravery**. The catch is that the AI only summons **Lich** once you **Break** them, and you can easily land the HP strike to avoid losing your boost. Time it so you land the HP hit with 4 or 3 seconds on the countdown, then when it goes off and drains you, you're already in the process of regenerating your **Bravery** and **Lich** will effectively do nothing.

Because the fight always begins in the same location, here's a step-by-step process to how to fight. As the battle starts, **Kefka** will usually, but not always, hop onto the railing on your right and grind past you. Move right and block him to stagger him, then enter **EX Mode** and hit him with **Tempest**. This will **Break** **Kefka** and put you close enough to a wall that the Chase HP attack will **Wall Rush** him, putting him at less than 1000 HP. Now just land another **Bravery** combo on him or let **Zidane**'s **Bravery** regenerate, then him him against and **EX Burst**. My **Level 5** **Zidane** (that first HP strike put him up another level) did over 800 damage with his Burst, so once you recover his base **Bravery** or use another combo as I said, **Kefka** shouldn't have enough HP to survive.

So let's cover **Zidane**'s **EX Burst**. As awesome heroes are want to do (see also: **Terra**), **Zidane** enters Trance to transform into a pink and purple superpowered being. He gains the ability to jump an additional 10 times, on top of his normal jumps and any boosts to how many jumps he can do based on his abilities. He also becomes invincible while jumping, and after dodging his jumps "recharge", since Dissidia vets probably know or have realized that once a character dodges in D012, they can't jump anymore until they land or use a Quickmove indicator. Admittedly **Zidane**'s is one of the lamest EX Modes, his ability isn't useless, but it isn't really a stand-out help, the large open arenas where it should be awesome like **Planet's Core**, The Rift, etc have Quickmove options for quickly moving around the stage faster than jumping, and in other stages it isn't much needed. **Zidane**'s **EX Burst** is pretty easy, just tap Circle over and over as he bombards the opponent with attacks, the power bar is segmented but I'm not sure why, it stops for a moment at each increment but you have to keep tapping Circle so ignore this. **Zidane** has one of the weakest **EX Bursts**, **Squall** is the worst but above him it's the **Onion Knight**, then **Kuja**, and then **Zidane**.

**Zidane** against demands answers, and **Kefka** informs him that **Bartz** is SMACK...dabinthemiddle of the enemy territory. He vanishes, leaving **Zidane** to try and find him.

To leave this gateway with a lot of **KP**, the secret is...nothing! The purple emblem at E4 is a **KP** +1 emblem, giving us 18 **KP** alone for chaining the three enemies without any chain skills, compare to a potential 16 **KP** for using chain skills in a few configurations, plus 4 **KP** for chaining the last two enemies without skills. First up at the purple emblem, an **Imitation Despot** with **Flare**. The aerial version, good thing too since **Zidane** can't reflect the ground variant yet. Next is a **Capricious Thief** with **Storm Impulse** and **Stellar Circle** 5. **Zidane** mastered **Swift Attack** during these fights and learned **Meo Twister**, the HP link. After he sends the opponent flying, **Zidane** teleports in front of them and blasts them with lasers from his swords. It doesn't so much damage on itself but the damage stacks on the damage of the **Bravery** move, so it works out in the end. As a note, in some arenas with weird walls and ceilings like the **Chaos Shrine**, **Pandaemonium** or **Kefka's Tower**, **Zidane** is liable to throw his daggers in such a way that an obstacle blocks one of them them, reducing the **Bravery** damage done and, I presume if both get blocked though it's never happened to me, not doing HP damage. Not a major problem but you're likely to see it happen a couple times.

As we reach the end we come upon a familiar scene. **Zidane** spies **Squall** facing **Garland** and **Ultimecia** and tackles **Garland** aside to take him on himself.

**Garland** is here, been a long time eh? Haven't fought him since the end of Treachery of the Gods. He's got all the attacks you remember from the Stalwarts up to now, and the **Iron Giant** summon to cleave your **Bravery** in to every time he gets an HP attack off. His accessories are completely useless, he has a **Guard Ring** with six Near Opponent boosters. The **Guard Ring** reduces the wall rush **Bravery** damage **Garland** takes...except none of **Zidane**'s current attacks inflict wall rush save for **Vortex** (I don't have it but you may) and his Chase attacks, and by nature of **Ultimecia's Castle** as explained further down, those attacks probably won't be able to wall rush him anyway. Not to mention that most wall rush attacks tend to knock the opponent far away anyway so he's probably not going to be close enough to get those boosters off by the time he smacks the wall. Once again I wonder if the devs accidentally gave him the wrong equipment, maybe they meant the **Guardian Bangle**, the all-purpose defense bonus accessory? We'll never know.

**Garland** isn't really a strong enemy, and the terrain is on your side here. The problem is that here in **Ultimecia's Castle**, you start out the fight on the balcony, and all of **Zidane**'s attacks outside of **Scoop Art** drive the opponent up and backwards, likely knocking them into a banish trap and preventing you from going into a Chase with them. However, the banish trap cannot stop **Meo Twister** - smack **Garland** with **Swift Attack** and press Square to use **Meo Twister** like normal, **Zidane** will automatically teleport down to where **Garland** reappears to zap him. The banish trap will furthermore sap **Garland**'s **Bravery**, so while he has a nice level advantage on you his **Bravery** won't last long. The only real difficulty spike compared to **Kefka** is slightly higher HP and, unless you lure **Garland** to the lower levels, you probably can't use a Chase HP for wall rush. Otherwise just pummel him with **Swift Attack** - **Meo Twister** until he falls to around 800 HP, then let loose an **EX Burst**.

**Zidane** hit **Level 8** from this fight and learned **Vortex**. It's an aerial attack that has **Zidane** spin upwards, then perform a combo to knock the opponent back, doing wall rush damage. Sadly, no HP link. **Vortex** is alright, it has the same power as **Tempest** and good vertical lift, the problem is that its horizontal is non-existent. Even **Cloud**'s **Rising Fang** has better horizontal range, and **Cloud**'s **Rising Fang** does not have horizontal range. But it'll do to replace **Scoop Art** for a while until **Zidane** gets **Storm Impulse** much later.

**Garland** vanishes with a laugh and **Zidane** lets **Squall** in on what happened to **Bartz**, and **Squall** joins the search. We've got an Assist again, begone, **Empty Assist Gauge** booster!

**Kuja** has a pretty good set-up, two **Earrings** and an **Attractorb** with an Aerial booster and 5 Far From Opponent. He's got the **Calcabrina** summon to halt your **Bravery** regen if he Breaks you, a full set of attacks, and a Capriacious Thief Assist. And if there's one character who can challenge **Zidane**'s prowess over combat in the skies, it's the Graceful Glider. True he may not have any HP links, but he has a wider pool of **Bravery** attacks more effective over range, and his HP attacks are good just on their own. But then you already know how tricky **Kuja** can be.

Fittingly for a duel of aerial masters, the tide of the battle here can be kept in your favor by holding the high ground - metaphorically speaking - over **Kuja**. His attacks are most effective on enemies in front and below him, and even with **Vortex**, you do not want to be below **Kuja**, and if you forget why here's a **Force Symphony** to remind you. If you can stay over his head then many of his attacks can't hit you, though there's still **Strike Energy** and **Flare Star** to watch for. **Kuja** tends to favor **Snatch Blow**, where he fires out his two **Flare** orbs, easily avoided if you have an altitude variation, then swoop down to strike with **Tempest** or spin up with **Vortex**. And that, as usually, is his downfall, the dumb AI will keep using **Snatch Blow** when in the air even if you're out of its reach, and thus you can easily pummel **Kuja** to a bloody pulp. Go go **EX Burst** finish!

With **Zidane** proving his badassery, he obtains his **Crystal**, which is a better reason to get it than whatever half-assed backwards moral the game's story insists on feeding us. **Kuja** fades away as the two have some more painful banter, and through the power of deus ex machina, **Zidane** is teleported to **Bartz** and **Squall**.

Final note, **Zidane** hit **Level 16** from this fight and learned **Booster** 8. It's an awesome move and **Zidane**'s second worthwhile ground attack. **Zidane** takes a moment to coil his feet before launching forward and into the air. It can't be blocked for one thing, the opponent will stagger instead of taking damage, but they're a sitting duck because of what **Booster** 8 is meant for - combos. After hitting, much like **Squall**'s **Thunder Barret**, **Zidane** can quickly combo into another attack. **Booster** 8 into **Free Energy** for quick HP damage, **Booster** 8 into **Swift Attack** for a Chase or **Meo Twister**, **Booster** 8 into **Storm Impulse** for massive **Bravery** pain and wall rush. It has some recovery time to handle, but with proper usage it effectively lets **Zidane** combo any aerial attack from a ground attack and tack on extra damage and range.

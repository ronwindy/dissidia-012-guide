The four red chests are all Level 120 enemies, but they yield the trade accessories for the Level 100 weapons. As you get each such accessory the weapon it's needed for will appear in the shop, unless you have a Level 90 exclusive weapon in which case the next one up is already there and you know exactly what accessory you need for it.

This link lists the exclusive weapons, if you're unsure which ones are exclusive to which characters. Once you get the level 100 trade accessory for a weapon though, it will appear in the shop and you can just check them who uses it.

With your spoils, considering getting the Level 100 weapons of anyone you're using now and were fortunate enough to find the proper trade items for.

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

When you're done, grab the KP Chalice to triple your winnings, the five chests for three more Level 100 trade accessories, a Friendship Resin, and a Dragon's Tear.

The Tear is part of the Whisper of the Wyrm combination, when three pieces are worn together they give you a 10% bonus to physical and magical defense. The pieces are all Level 60 except for the Wyvern Lance, which is Level 90.

That 10% bonus to defense may make you wonder about the lost defense in Level 60 armor, but let's do some quick math on that - my level 100 Zidane, with no armor, has 110 defense. With the Dragon Armlet he gets 170 defense, and a 10% boost of that is an additional 17 point, whereas the top-level hand equipment in the same category as the Armlet gives 72 defense.

</PanelSection>

So the math, if I understand Dissidia's stat system properly, says go ahead. Sure you'll have a slight penalty to Bravery and HP, but you're perfectly functional regardless of both, if you play properly HP is no concern, and base Bravery, meh.

The only catch is of course, doing the dungeon over to get two more Tears. Up to you kiddies, but me, I'm done. Let's blow this popsicle stand and get to the final objective - the Land of Discord, where the true final boss of the game awaits.

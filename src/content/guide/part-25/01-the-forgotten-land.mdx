<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has a Titan's Tear, a unique trade accessory. It unlocks the Giant's equipment - Giant's Axe, Giant's Gloves, Giant's Helm, and Giant's Harness.

This set *isn't* entirely useless and in fact is quite use*ful*. When three pieces are worn together they trigger the Pride of the Titans combination, giving you an extra 100 base Bravery and 750 more HP.

Though the pieces are only level 90, this boost is more than what you'd get if you traded out the Helm and Harness for Level 100 gear. Thus the only penalty you get for opting for Giant's gear over Level 100 stuff is slightly lower defenses. And as a three-piece combination, you only need the three armor pieces and can still use a Level 100 weapon for no power loss.

Grab the Phoenix Down if anyone has died and head down.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

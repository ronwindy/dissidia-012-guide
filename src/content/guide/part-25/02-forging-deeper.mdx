I'm not going to post the map for the 9th floor because it's a simple layout, the end piece is guarded by a Party pieces, consisting of:

- a Level 122 False Hero with Ultima Weapon and a False Stalwart
- a Level 122 Imitation Liegeman with Omega

However, there's also a long path of chests leading around the outside of the board to approach the end piece from the other side. The path has several Ultimate pieces on it though, and more appear as you head down to block your way back.

Going around the board:

- We face a Level 120 Fleeting Flash with Ultima Weapon and a Delusory Warlock, and get access to our first three chests, each with 3 gil. That's not a typo. 3 gil.
- Then comes a Level 120 Idle Sky Pirate with Omega and a Warrior of Antiquity. The next three chests have 30 gil, then a random skill in the fourth.
- The next four chests have 300 gil, but as we advance a Level 121 Transient Gunner appears behind us, he has Ultima Weapon and a Transient Witch Assist.
- The next enemy on the path is a Level 120 Delusory Dragoon with Ultima Weapon and Fleeting Flash. The next four chests have 3000 gil three times, and an Assist Charge.
- The third and second-to-last each have 30,000 gil, but an enemy appears again, a Level 121 Ephemeral Summoner with Omega and an Ephemeral Vision.
- The last chest has a chain skill, but is blocked by a Level 121 Imaginary Brawler with Omega and an Imaginary Soldier.

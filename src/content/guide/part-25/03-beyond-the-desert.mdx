#### Beyond the Desert

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Bahamut_Isles.png" width={287} height={156} alt="Bahamut Isles" title="Bahamut Isles" />

Zidane is party leader for the next while. Don't worry about restocking your map skills, we don't need any yet.

Teleport back to the desert, the exit to the Bahamut Isles is nearby. Head forward past the empty gateway to find a new playmate, a Level 123 Imitation Despot with Omega and an Imitation Liegeman Assist. And he's got level 100 equipment too, what joy. Behind it is a chest with a KP Bonus.

---

Moving forward, shatter the rock and cross to the opposite island, to the right here is a chest with a Diamond, to the left is a second Imitation Despot. At the end beyond it is a chest with a Gold Angel.

Back on the mainland there's another rock to shatter, it leads to an Elixir.

Through the open gateway we'll encounter Lightning, who unsurprisingly has some pretty cynical words for us, specifically for our companion. Who is this "mured moogle" anyway?

After her is the Citadel of Trials teleport stone, with the associated ruins nearby. And beyond that our next gateway, Conquered Trials.

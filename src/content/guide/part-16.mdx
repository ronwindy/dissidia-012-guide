# Chapter 7

Chapter 7, we're finally done with the group of six and take a look at a trio now.

#### The Lion, the Mouse, and the Monkey

Well, new character time as always!

---

<CharacterIntro name="Squall" src="drake/Dissidia_Squall.png" width={175} height={261} alt="Dissidia Squall">

**Squall** is back from FF8, and as always is cold, quiet and professional. Unfortunately his story is one they didn't carry over well, **Squall**'s character arc in FF8 was him being torn between his duties as a mercenary and what he knew was right which conflicted with his duties, and his coping mechanism for dealing with the world, that is complete emotional detachment, broke down as his personal universe tore at the seams, forcing him to re-evaluate his life. Here, he's cold and impersonal for no given reason, but he still has a good heart. He goes off alone and leaves his friends behind, but declares they still fight together. He abandons them and doesn't go back to save them when he knows they're in trouble, but says he still cares about them and considers them friends... Yeah. The morals of the next three chapters are all a bit confusing, I'll discuss this as we proceed.

**Squall** is another melee-focused fighter, but he has a lot of spell attacks, in fact he has five spells to four sword combos. Yup, **Squall** only has two melee attacks on ground or in the air. However, they're all he needs. **Squall** is an absolute *beast* in **Bravery** damage, **Solid Barrel** and **Beat Fang** do incredible damage, and **Upper Blues** and **Heel Crush** give him a bit of versatility to save him from being completely predictable. His spell attacks, contrary to the lines of **Cloud** and **Tidus** who have ranged moves solely so they have ranged moves, are as vital a part of his arsenal as his melee blows, setting opponents up for them and doing a fair bit of damage in their own right. However, **Squall** has a crushing weakness...his HP attacks are *slow* as can be, and very easy to avoid. You have to rely on Assists or his spells to set up for them, not a problem against the dumb AI in this chapter but later, it's a big hassle. Still, his potent **Bravery** attacks make him so powerful you won't need to land many HP hits to finish off the enemy. And in his **EX Mode**, oh god...but we'll get to that when I cover his **EX Mode** during the chapter.

**Squall** starts off with **Upper Blues**, an uppercut that has him dash forward a short distance, then he leaps up and performs a few more attacks to slam the opponent into the ground, and **Blizzard** Barret, a basic ice shot at the opponent. His aerial moves are **Beat Fang**, a fast-hitting combo attack that slams the opponent into the ground, and **Heel Crush**, a spinning attack forward to strike once and, you guessed it, make the opponent kiss the dirt. His HP attacks are **Rough Divide**, where he pauses then dashes at the opponent, rising or descending on an angle to strike, and Aerial Circle, where he swings to create explosive orbs in a vertical ring around him, then detonates them. He also moves towards the opponent during the swing and always creates the ring in a way to hit the opponent based on their current position. It also has Magic Block, preventing projectiles from stopping you during the execution.

**Squall** is overall a very powerful character with strengths far outweighing his flaws in my opinion, but he *is* flawed and you have to learn how to make up for them. Use him well.

</CharacterIntro>

For accessories the customary **Hyper Ring**, **Muscle Belt** and **Sunrise**, but for boosters I opted for HP = 100%, **Empty Assist Gauge** and **Level 1-9**, as **Squall** never has an Assist in his tale. Guess what Chain to use and where! **Straight Chain** at A3 where you begin you say? Good boy, have an e-cookie! The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. The blue emblem is a Physical Critical Rate Up, if you lack a **Straight Chain** use a **Jump Chain** on it to get three enemies.

First is a **Transient Lion** with **Upper Blues**, **Heel Crush**, and Aerial Circle. Next comes a **Transient Witch** with Knight's Axe. Now comes the hard enemy, a **Transient Gunner** with Richochet Shot, **Homing Bazooka** and **Satellite Laser**, and the **Typhon** summon. It also has the accessory gimmick of the story, 8 BRV ≥ Base Value boosters with a **White Cape** or a **Protect Stud**, giving them a an 8x booster level for a total 24% boost to magic or physical defense when they have higher **Bravery** than their base. It sounds impressive, but the second you land a **Bravery** attack doing even 1 damage those boosters turn off, so it's pretty lame. It also doesn't make sense to give some enemies White Capes when the *really* powerful moves **Squall** uses are physical blows, and that build only uses 9 slots anyway so why not use the Cape and Stud together? The last foe is a **Capricious Thief** with **Scoop Art**, Solution 9, **Shift Break**, the **Giant of Babil** summon, and the **White Cape** and booster accessory build.

The chest has a useless Arcane Incense for us.

As we leave and appear on the world map, **Zidane** and **Bartz** are still running around. Turn around and approach the rock on the shoreline. An annoying little problem **Squall** has is that his world map attack, **Solid Barrel**, has him take a step back, and the rock doesn't shatter until he does the last part of the attack, so if you aren't right up next the rock, **Squall** will attack twice, then step back and the third strike will miss and the rock remains intact. Get closer and try again, ugh. The island the beam connects to has a chest with 1000 gil, and despite being large has nothing else on the two ends. Back on the mainland approach the sealed gateway in view for another 1000 gil. Go through the mountains to find **Zidane** near a rock, shatter it to create a long grindbar to an island with 2000 gil. Also **Zidane** has a funny line if you chat with him. Go back to where you appeared and head to the shoreline to find a Capricous Reaper to take out, it's level 17 but a first strike and its three **Cursed Rings** will sap its level advantage.

**Squall** hit **Level 3** from this fight and learned **Solid Barrel**. It has much less range than **Upper Blues** but does more than twice as much damage, and begins a Chase, the only one of **Squall**'s melee blows to do so, so when the time comes to charge up that **EX Gauge** later on, guess what move I hope you know how to use well. At the far end of the beach is a **Maser Eye**. Now just go to the next gateway, not hard to find, just watch for the dark smoke rising over the mountains. Near it is a chest with another Arcane Incense.

Sadly all I had were two **Jump Chains** so I used one at A3 to get two, yeah it technically loses **KP** given the cost of a **Jump Chain**, but meh, I have two so why not? The summon is **Alexander**, when called he freezes your **Bravery** in place, preventing it from increasing or being damaged, and letting you spam HP attacks at full power. He's one of many ways to break the game's superboss, as anyone who has seen my YouTube account can attest, but on his own on normal enemies, not too awesome, he usually won't last long enough for you to get more than two uses out of him.

As we leave, **Cosmos** appears to **Squall**, who tells him his choice to go it alone without **Zidane** and **Bartz** was a wise decision, as he has to face **Ultimecia** alone. However, some rather unsubtle foreshadowing shows not all is as it seems.

Welcome to the desert, a big place. The ruined tower hides a chest with 1000 gil, and near the inactive gateway is a chest with **Iifa Dew**. There's also one of two Fallacious Trees here, an easy win. Far to the right of the inactivate gateway at the edge of the desert is a **Rosetta Stone**. Head to the next Tree, hard to miss given it's bright blue, and in an alcove nearby is a chest with 1999 gil. You can also head to an outcropping over to the sea to snag a **KP Gambler**, can never have too many of those...well you can, but it's still good to have more than one. Head back into the desert and go to the gateway, there's a chest on your right on the path with a **Valor Resin**. Buy a **Multichain** from the moogle if you lack one. He also has some new boosters and summons for sale, the boosters aren't much use, but the summons, **Bahamut** and **Leviathan** should be your main purchases if you're looking to spend.

**Kuja** is pretty well-equipped, he's a few levels weaker than the mid-chapter bosses before, but is fought a bit earlier. He has full equipment and an **Earring** with 3 Far from Opponent and an Aerial, Near Opponent would have been a far better choice for **Kuja**. The **Tiamat** summon will give him a pretty significant boost if it goes off, so either **Break** him or use **Cactuar** or **Leviathan** to sap his **Bravery** so **Tiamat** does nothing.

It's been a long time since you've fought **Kuja**, the last time was way back in Chapter 1 with **Lightning**. I guess he just has a knack for pissing off Gunblade users. He's quite a bit smarter and more aggressive than back then of course. Despite their appearances not all of his moves count as magic in terms of damage priority, so sadly **Heel Crush** and Aerial Circle are not be-all answers to him. **Snatch Blow** and **Burst Energy** come out fast, Remote **Flare** is just a nuisance, and **Snatch Shot** can be annoying to dodge at longer range. Ultimately though, **Kuja**, as bosses often are, is entirely impotent against the power of an **EX Mode** **Squall**. Speaking off, let's cover him.

**Squall**'s **EX Mode** swaps out the Revolver for the Lionheart Gunblade, giving him a boost to the range of all his melee attacks. His true power however is that when using **Solid Barrel**, **Upper Blues** or **Beat Fang**, he pulls the trigger of the Gunblade with each strike, hitting an extra time. This almost *doubles* the base power of the three attacks, in addition to the benefit of more critical hits. An **EX Mode** **Beat Fang** or **Solid Barrel** can quickly drain the **Bravery** of even the toughest foes, simple but effective. His **EX Burst** is Renzokuken, you have to tap R as blue bars flash by a power meter, timing the button press as they enter a hit zone. **Squall** has the embarrassing distinction of having the weakest **EX Burst** in the entire game, save for **Cloud** and **Tidus** with 1 HP and **Tifa** with an empty **EX Gauge**, as those conditions negate their damage bonuses.

With **Kuja** beaten down, he opts out of using **Squall** and heads off to find someone else to use. **Squall** thinks back on the two and wonders if he should go back to help them now that he knows **Kuja** is after them, but decides to continue on alone.

**Squall** learned **Thunder Barret** from these fights. It's his key spell, **Squall** creates a line of lightning in front of him, dragging opponents in front of him and out of the air. If it hits though the end lag is cancelled out, letting **Squall** quickly combo into another move while the opponent is helpless before him. **Thunder Barret** to **Solid Barrel** is a brutal combo that helps compensate for the latter's crap range, and while the longer start-up makes the timing tighter, you can also combo **Thunder Barret** into the HP attacks **Fated Circle** (which you'll learn soon) or Blasting Zone (ditto).

As we reach the end, **Squall** runs across the **Warrior of Light**. The **Warrior** questions his choice to travel alone, and **Squall** aptly points out the **Warrior** himself is alone, declaring he won't be dissuaded. So, now they have to fight. Why? I don't know.

The **Warrior** comes into battle with a Flametongue, giving him an initial **Bravery** boost on top of his level advantage. He's got a pretty nice accessory build, 3 Attacking **Bravery** and 4 Near Opponent pumping up a **Power Ring**. I'd have opted for a **Hyper Ring** or a **Muscle Belt**, but hey, it's decent. He's got HP links for all his aerial attacks and his ground attack **Ascension**, Radiant Sword to send six swords of light homing in on you, **Shield of Light** to block and punish your **Bravery** attacks, and **Dayflash** as a quick ground attack to get you out of his face. His summon is...I'm not really sure, he never used it.

The problem is, as always...yup, **EX Mode**. Use that new **Thunder Barret** to drag him in and slam him with an **EX Mode** **Solid Barrel** to **Break** him, then enter chase and wall rush him to knock off 2000 HP within the first ten seconds. Now just land another **Solid Barrel**, HP attack and **EX Burst** to end it. The **Warrior** is decently aggressive and has a good set-up for moves and equipment, he just can't stand up to the brute power of **Squall**'s **EX Mode** bonus.

**Squall** got to **Level 12** from this fight and learned **Fated Circle**, a ground version of Aerial Circle that creates orbs horizontally. It combos well with **Thunder Barret** as I said.

The **Warrior** is impressed with **Squall**'s power, and wonders why he doesn't use that power to help protect his friends. Good question, since **Squall** knows other villains are actively pursuing them. **Squall** responds by saying that even if they're apart, they can support each other, and shows him **Bartz**'s chocobo feather, claiming that they support him in their own way and he's returning the favor. That...really doesn't answer the question, and is a weak argument in itself. The **Warrior** sees he has misjudged **Squall**, thinking he preferred to be alone because he didn't trust his allies...which was never the case, and **Squall** never said so. The **Warrior** departs, reminding **Squall** he isn't alone, something **Squall** clearly already believes, no matter how bullshit an argument he formulated to express this. Why didn't they just have this chat before the fight? I dunno, like I said kiddies, this chapter's morals are all screwed up.

Remember kiddies, if your friends are being pursued by an androgynous mage intent on trapping them, it's best to ignore the danger to them and just have faith that they'll be okay, because they have faith in you. That's the *true* meaning of friendship. And now because I know you were all thinking it - "**Squall**, don't believe in yourself, believe in them! Believe in the friends that believe in you!"...ya know it makes a lot more sense phrased as Kamina said it. I guess the shades just have that effect.

At the end of the stage, **Squall** confronts **Ultimecia**, who rolls out **Garland** to tag team **Squall**. **Squall** spews some more confusing double talk about how he can fight alone because he's not alone, he has friends who support him by not being here! **Squall** postures that he'll just beat both of them, then **Zidane** shows up to steal his thunder, tackling **Garland** to the side. Well, that leaves a bit of one-on-one for **Squall** then. A pity for **Ultimecia** her power over time only works in cutscenes or else this fight would be over literally before it started.

**Ultimecia** came well equipped with a full moveset, and a **False Stalwart** for an Assist. She's back **Brynhildr**, though it'll probably help you more than her, and has a good accessory set. She's got on a **Hyper Ring** with four Far from Opponent, two Aerial, a Near Loss, and an **Iifa Leaf**. The Leaf, if you never used one yourself, gives her the **Last Chance** ability - the attack that will normally kill her, one time will just sap her to 1 HP, even an **EX Burst**. It also doesn't matter how much HP she has before hand, as long as she has more than 1 HP without using **Last Chance** already, it'll save her.

Even without **EX Mode**, **Ultimecia** is, once again, an anti-climax boss. She's pretty stupid and just flat weak. She tends to spam Knight's Axe the entire fight, which is very easy to block uncharged and easy to dodge when charged. Aside from a Knight's Spear counter when you wall rush her, she has no real signs of intelligence. If she used Knight's Blade or Knight's Arrow more maybe, but as is she's pathetic. Her HP attacks are perfect chances to smash into her with a Rouge Divide - she has the ability to move during Apocalypse but the AI has her hold in place, a fatal mistake. And for her normal blows, remember Aerial Circle and **Fated Circle** block magic attacks, and the only attacks of **Ultimecia**'s that can pierce that are a charged Axe or Spear, and the Circle will detonate before she can get them off. The only complication is one of nuisance, **Last Chance**, all it means is that you have to sap her to 1 HP first if you plan to do the typical **EX Burst** finishing blow.

With the time witch faded away, **Squall** receives his **Crystal** and joins up with **Zidane** to go look for **Bartz**, who has apparently been captured.

As a final footnote, **Squall** hit **Level 16** from the fight and got Blasting Zone. He extends a column of energy up into the air and then slams it down, dragging aerial opponents down and doing a bit of **Bravery** damage. He also turns on start-up to track the opponent, and its good reach and fast chop make it pretty effective as an anti-air attack.

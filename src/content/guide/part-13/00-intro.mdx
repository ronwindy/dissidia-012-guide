# Chapter 4

Part 13, time for Chapter 4 where we get back to the friends Cloud left behind.

#### Twilight Knights

Well, guess who now?

---

<CharacterIntro name="Cecil" src="drake/DissidiaCecilArt.png" width={200} height={247}>

Cecil has the unfortunately distinction of being the most dry and bland of the core 10 heroes. Sure, he wasn't exactly an interesting guy back in IV, but they did more for the Warrior of Light and Onion Knight than they did for Cecil, and they didn't exist at all until this game.

Cecil's character arc in the 13th cycle largely focuses on him wondering if he can trust Golbez or not, since he's Cecil's brother but also an enemy. I do appreciate that they explore their relationship deeper than The After Years did, but mostly it boils down to Cecil running after Golbez crying "Brother!" and then angsting when Golbez talks down to him. Beyond that, not much character to talk about here.

<PanelSection title="Forms">

Cecil fights somewhat similar to Lightning, he switches forms automatically between Dark Knight and Paladin when attacking.

- The Dark Knight specializes in ground attacks and aerial moves that bring himself or his opponent to the ground.
- The Paladin relies on aerial combo attacks and ground moves that take the fight to the skies.

Cecil can only change form now by using an HP attack, shifting into Dark Knight mode for ground HP attacks and Paladin for aerial ones. He can change freely in his EX Mode, which I'll explain later, and he'll get Bravery attacks that let him shift forms later at around Level 30.

Cecil has reliable moves, his projectile attacks are good and his melee combos have a good amount of range on them, but I find that aside from his Searchlight set-ups, he isn't really a stand-out character. There could be a lot of potential for creative combos if they gave him more freedom over his form shifting, but they don't, aside from relying on a sustained EX Mode build.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Cecil's Dark Knight begins with:

- Valiant Blow, a quick three-hit combo with shocking range to it
- Dark Cannon which sends out orbs of darkness that spiral back to him and drag the opponent closer
- Gravity Ball which in the air sends an orb of darkness out to drag the opponent into the dirt

Dark Cannon to Valiant Blow or Soul Eater will be our bread-and-butter combo.

As a Paladin he has:

- Slash, which has him spin forward and up to knock the opponent away
- in the air Radiant Wings, a spinning combo forward that then has him slam the opponent down
- Sacred Cross, where he dashes under the opponent and spins up into the air to begin a Chase

A good combo is to use Slash and then immediately follow it up with Radiant Wings, but this only works if you're near a wall, otherwise the opponent flies too far away form Slash and Radiant Wings won't connect.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attacks are:

- Soul Eater, a forward stab that does Bravery damage before slamming the opponent backwards
- Saint's Fall, a rising swoop that has him rise up and them slam the opponent into the ground

</PanelSection>

Cecil is perfectly capable in his own story, but he's not one of my favs.

</CharacterIntro>

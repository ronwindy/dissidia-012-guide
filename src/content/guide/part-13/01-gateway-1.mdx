<PanelSection kind="enemies">

From this storyline on, it may be my imaginary or faulty memory, but there is a slight difficulty spike, you're going to be seeing several Expert Battle Pieces in this story, and for the next five Expert and Strange enemies seem more common. Or maybe it's just me.

Enemy line-up:

- First up, a Delusory Knight with Dark Lance, Slash, Searchlight, which sends a disc of light at you to stop and fire projectiles, and Saint's Fall as his HP hitter.
- Next is a Transient Gunner with Homing Bazooka and Satellite Laser for the HP attack.
- Now it's the two Hard enemies, the Capricious Thief has Scoop Art, Storm Impulse, an aerial spinning attack with decent range but easy to block, and for the HP attack, Tidal Flame, which either of Cecil's HP attacks can reflect, the build is wrapped up with the Pupu summon.
- The last foe is a Fleeting Flash with the same accessories, Smite, Aerora, Lightning Strike, and Pupu.

The Thief also has the accessory gimmick for Cecil's story, a Zephyr Cloak and Heart's Ease with 8 Near Loss boosters, giving them a pretty impressive boost to their Bravery every time they block or dodge an attack. Of course, this assumes the enemy is smart enough to block or dodge your attacks in the first place, and most of the time they aren't.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest, when you're done, has a Dark Sword.

This is one of Cecil's four exclusive equipment pieces found in his story, when three are equipped at once he gets a powerful Combination, the same way as Mythril gives you an ability when three pieces are worn, but the Dark equipment pieces have a different effect. The Dark Sword is pretty potent so use it if you like, but as with Mythril we need three pieces to get real use of them, so I kept Cecil with a Nodachi.

#### A Divergent Path

On the world map immediately turn around and attack the Fallacious Tree. As a nice nod to continuity, the trio is in the same region they were checking out when Cloud went off alone.

- Go to the sealed gateway behind the Tree when you've chopped it into kindling to find a BRV Charge.
- The Moogle has nothing you desperately need, but if you have 200 KP and want a new weapon, I leave it to you.
- Beyond it at the other sealed gate is a chest with Mythril, save it, we now want to to get Mythril Armor or Mythril Vest.
- The stone on the outcropping opposite leads to a chest with 1991 gil.

---

Now returning to the main path (your companions are visible to help you figure out the way), there's another Fallacious Tree on the shoreline to chop down, on the island beyond it is the Dark Helm, and by the gateway now there's an EX Charge. Remember this gateway, it's important much, much later. Now only the next gateway awaits.

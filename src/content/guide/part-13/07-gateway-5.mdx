As we enter Golbez appears and zaps Cecil what-good when he goes on a friendship speech again. He departs, lecturing him and leaving Cecil angsty again.

The KP Chance is a 60 second Break, and the bonus line is 15.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Now, the way to get the most KP here is a Jump Chain on C3, the emblem doubles the KP won for 32 total, and don't use the KP Gambler yet, we're saving it for the next story.

I lacked a Jump Chain so I used the Round Chain the orbs of the dead outside got me, and used it at D2, fighting the Expert piece and the straggler Normal enemy after.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Charge up your EX Gauge during the initial fight for the Expert enemy.

Enemy line-up:

- First is a Delusory Warlock with Attack System, Rising Wave and Gravity Force.
- Next is an Imaginary Soldier with Sonic Break and Rising Fang.
- Third is a Delusory Dragoon, he has Cyclone, Crashing Dive, Gungnir, the dodge and block accessory line-up, and the Asura summon.
- The other Hard enemy is an Imaginary Champion with the same accessories and Asura, Fervent Blow, Oblivion, and Hell's Gate.

The expert enemy is the Capricious Reaper, the Hyper Ring-Guardian Bangle combo, all six of Kuja's Bravery attacks between ground and air, Seraphic Star, Force Symphony, a Phantasmal Harlequin Assist, and the Carbuncle summon.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

As always hit that HP attack first, then enter EX Mode and spam Sacred Cross to Break it and a Chase HP to Wall Rush. After that you just have to whittle him down, I ended with an EX Burst of course.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has Dark Armor, now suit Cecil up with the Dark Sword, Dark Helm and Dark Armor. This activates the Power of Darkness combination, boosting all Wall Rush damage Cecil inflicts by 20%. Admittedly not as awesome as it was in Dissidia, since Wall Rush got sliced from 50% damage to 25%, but still good.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As we leave Cosmos appears, and Cecil tells her he's conflicted between his faith that he wants to work for his friends and Golbez's words he needs to look for it alone. Cosmos tells her Golbez is absolutely right, he can't depend on others, but the answers Cecil seeks are in his heart. Depending on others and working with others are not the same thing.

Cecil, his faith restored in...exactly what he believed in before Golbez showed up, sort of making his character arc a bit pointless and shallow, runs off to find Golbez.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

#### Exploration

Shatter the stone before you to find a Bangaa Tooth. I used it to grab a Zweihander from the shop.

- Nearby you can see an Ephemeral Phantom, go deal with it.
- There's a Cure skill in the passage behind it.
- Head along the path now, grabbing the orbs of the dead along the way as you head into the volcano.
- There's a chest opposite the gateway with 2000 gil before you go inside.

The next red gateway, there's a couple way to get three enemies here, no way to get all four.

- If you have a Jump Chain use it at C3 to get the Initial BRV 0 for both.
- If you don't, you can either buy one from the moogle, or buy a Jump skill and use it to leap over the enemy at C4 and get the trio there without chain skills.

Jump and Jump Chain both cost 6 KP so it only affects if you want the emblem or not.

---

I used Cecil's EX Burst here so let's cover that now.

Cecil's EX Mode swaps out his generic spear-type swords for the Mythgraven Blade and Deathbringer. He does 150% normal Bravery damage, in addition to the critical hit rate bonus, and by pressing R and Square. It's mostly a convenience, like say you want to use Valiant Blow without having to do Soul Eater to swing or vice-versa with a Paladin attack.

Now, it could potentially open up combo possibilities, like dragging them close with Dark Cannon, switching to Paladin and hitting them with Slash, but I find that the change takes too long to reliably set up those types of moves, maybe against a dumber AI who won't dodge away but not against the smarter AI.

Cecil's EX Burst has you input two sets of commands, you must press the indicated directional button at the same time as the indicated X-Circle-Triangle-Square button, three times. It's pretty simple once you try it once or twice.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The summon is the Magus Sisters, they're automatically called when you get Broken and instantly sap half the opponent's Bravery, also halving their base Bravery for a while. They're useless, there are a lot of summons in Dissidia to sap Bravery more reliably, right now we have Cactuar, and later there's Leviathan, Lich, Omega, even Hecatoncheir will sap the Bravery pool and so sap your opponent's boost if they're about to Break you.

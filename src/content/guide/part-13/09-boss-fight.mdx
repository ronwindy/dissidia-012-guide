Something quick I want to note I miss, in the original Dissidia the storyline fights had the characters begin the fight in the same locations as they were in the preceding cutscene, in this game they begin in the same starting positions as normal fights. I just miss that little touch, helped blend the cutscenes and fights together.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Anyway, Golbez time. He has all four Cosmic Ray chains, so aside from Attack Force and Gravity Force, all his Bravery attacks chain into HP attacks, and he has Nightglow and Genesis rock.

He's got good equipment and an Earring with four Far From Opponent. Not sure why because Golbez is a mid-range to melee fighter, Close to Opponent would be far better. He's also got a Delusory Dragoon as an Assist. I like that he has Kain as an Assist and Giant of Babil as a summon, so fitting for Golbez, yes?

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

He's not a very complex opponent, or really all that difficult. Exdeath was a far more challenging opponent to me based solely on the vast level difference, Golbez has only three levels on him, but you've gained far more than that by this point so the power gap between Cecil and Golbez is much smaller. As well, Golbez has lower Defense than Exdeath, so overall you'll easily do a lot of hurt to him.

Attack Force is useless, Gravity Force is easy to count, and his other attacks, just don't get close to him, or do if you know what cues to watch for and know how to counter or block them. A Break and Wall Rush sapped half his HP for me, and then an EX Burst to Break him again finished him off.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

As Golbez kneels, weakened, the Crystal appears to Cecil as proof he kept his faith in the things he already had faith in despite his brother challenging this faith.

I get that the Crystals appear when they prove their will and determination, but for a lot of these guys, it isn't until the villain shows up that those traits are in doubt, or the revelation they reveal at the end of their storyline is just a reaffirmation of what they already believed. Just underscores how poor the original Dissidia's story was. Anyway, Golbez departs, leaving Cecil to return to his friends.

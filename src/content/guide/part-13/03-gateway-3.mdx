<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up:

- The first foe is a False Stalwart, easy prey.
- Next is a harder Phantasmal Harlequin, it has Pupu, those Bravery accessories, and uses Ultima, Meteor and Hyperdrive.
- Now comes an Imaginary Champion, he has Fervent Blow (the ground sword beam), Oblivion, Hell's Gate, and those Bravery accessories.
- And the last one is the Ephemeral Phantom, he has full equipment, a Hyper Ring and Guardian Bangle with 8 HP = 100% boosters, and the Phoenix Summon.

Breaking the Harlequin in 60 seconds is hard with Pupu. A few ways to do it:

- Build up EX Force against the Stalwart and go EX Mode
- Let the Harlequin block and then hit after because when an enemy mistimes a block the next attack is all critical hits
- Or just be all-out aggressive, if you go Valiant Blow on him over and over, as long as he doesn't dodge too often, you can just do more damage than Pupu gives and you can get him after several seconds of battering down his Bravery

---

Phoenix grants him Reraise, so for a period of time, whenever you reduce his Bravery enough to Break him, his Bravery restores to its base value and he isn't Broken.

And he has pretty much all of Jecht's attacks, here's Jecht's page to read up on them since we haven't fought him or his manikins lately, and he has Jecht Block both on the ground and in the air so he can block anything you through at him, and is smart enough to do it his share of times.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Base line, keep your distance until you're ready to attack since his Bravery combos come out fast, and wait for him to let loose if you see him charge an attack and get him as he attacks and misses. Remember, both his Bravery attacks combo right into HP attacks, so it is vital you not get hit.

To get the KP Chance get him to use Phoenix before the 30 second mark, then wear him down to where a combo will Break him, and wait for Phoenix to wear off a few seconds before time is up. And of course as always, HP attack first thing to get rid of those nasty boosters.

This is one of the toughest fights I've had, he got a lot of HP hits in, even Broke me. I used Cecil's EX Burst to finish the fight, which I'll cover in the next gateway.

</PanelSection>

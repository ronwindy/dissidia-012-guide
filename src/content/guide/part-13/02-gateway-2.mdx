<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up:

- First is a Phantasmal Girl, she uses Blizzard Combo on the ground, Blizzara in the air, Meltdown for HP, has those Near Loss accessories, and the Asura summon.
- Next is a Delusory Knight with Shadow Lance, Nightfall, Slash and Searchlight for Bravery attacks, Nightfall is a quick downward slam attack that creates a short burst of darkness on impact and begins a Chase, be careful if he's above you in Dark Knight form. Otherwise, those accessories, Asura, and Saint's Fall as an HP attack.
- Now it's the Counterfeit Youth's turn, he has the melee Bravery attacks, Blade Torrent, and those same accessories.

Asura does one of three random effects when called, she either shatters your summonstone to prevent you from using it, forces it to activate, or seals it like Ramuh. Not a problem, we don't much use our summons for much except bosses anymore. Well I don't, I just stick with Cactuar now.

---

Last but not least, it's Strange time. The enemy is an Imaginary Soldier with Climhazzard, Rising Fang, and Braver. He's got a Hyper Ring and Muscle Belt with 5 Near Opponent boosters, the enemy 2x versions, and an Arcane Resin for a full EX Gauge. And finally, the Giant of Babil summon.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

If he lands Climhazzard, you *will* feel great pain, he's got a big damage buff and a good chance to score critical hits since he'll likely be in EX Mode. Be sure to listen and watch for when Climhazzard begins so you can block him, and don't get above him, stay on the ground so Rising Fang can't be used properly.

He can do a lot of damage very fast to be careful, he's a surprisingly tough opponent. His summon is pretty worthless fortunately, the Giant takes away a percentage of your Bravery, but even with EX Mode's Regen he's going to be weak enough to kill in one blow.

</PanelSection>

#### Exploration

On the world map, head right to find a Cure skill in the chest.

The moogle has only one summon of note really, Brynhildr, to double the Bravery pool and your base Bravery. Pricey, but what else are we saving up for much anymore, the moogle equipment is now being equalled by our shop equipment right? So I bought Brynhildr, and if you need one get a Multichain.

---

Go the blue gateway on the left, there's a Pink Tail nearby, and then head inside. Fun note, this area is where Lightning met the moogles, that moogle is the first moogle shop you ever visited way back in Chapter 1 of the 012 story.

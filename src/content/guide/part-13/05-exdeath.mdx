<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Exdeath is back from Kain's story, and he's got all the same tricks as before. In fact he's almost the same opponent as last time, he's the same level, has a few hundred more HP, mostly the same attacks.

The major difference? In Kain's story Exdeath had several good boosters buffing up an Attractorb and Pearl Necklace. This Exdeath has...a Cursed Ring. Right there, his good arm is crippled. He also swapped out Hecatoncheir for Brynhildr, bad move on his part, because that summon so easily backfires if your opponent gets the Break.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Ultimately though, aside from your attacks doing more Bravery damage, Exdeath is a very similar opponent to before. He's got the same sneaky tricks like Almagest and then cancelling into an Omni Block, he's still got the Reverse Polarity to Hurricane combo.

Do *not* use Gravity Ball on him, it moves way too slowly and is just an invitation for him to Omni Block it and fire off a counterattack. You could try Dark Cannon, the AI I found tries to block it on the initial firing and not the return trip, but it's Planet's Core so good luck lining it up to get Dark Cannon to hit.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Sacred Cross is a good move to spam here, since Radiant Wings is a bit slower and easier for him to block, Sacred Cross is harder to predict I find, though it's mostly due to Radiant Wing's spinning motion taking longer. It's Planet's Core so ground attacks are out.

As before, fake out Exdeath by attacking and baiting him into using Reverse Polarity, neutral dodge to have him teleport in front of him, and smash Cecil's spear into his face.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

Exdeath vanishes, leaving some rather meaningless yin-yang-esque mocking words in Cecil's ear. Golbez is confused though, he fought Exdeath alone to defend what he believes in and won, but the Crystal didn't appear. He thought Cecil would be strong enough to get it.

Cecil asks Golbez to come with him to tell his friends about his advice, and then they can look for the Crystal together. Golbez refuses, unsuiting to accompany him, and is disappointed in Cecil's attitude. He leaves, and Cecil is alone again on the world map.

<PanelSection kind="assist">

**Squall** is here and he'll be our _Assist_.

- He is an _excellent_ combo to call after landing a **Satellite Beam** or when the opponent is tumbling down from **Machine Gun**, he'll use **Solid Barrel** which hurts _a lot_ - for direct comparison, a full-blast from **Machine Gun** has **32** power, while **Solid Barrel** has **55**.
- And for more fun it begins a **Chase** teleport so we can follow up with a **Shotgun** blast or **Ragnarok Blade**.
- His aerial **Assist**...well **Squall** used so much awesome for his ground version he has little left for the aerial one, he uses **Heel Crush**, which is slow to execute and does about a third the damage **Solid Barrel** will do.
- So yeah, _stick to the ground Assist_, doesn't much matter though since he's only in one gateway.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemies are another **Gunner** and **Vision** with a **Fleeting Flash** and a **Delusory Dragoon**, no new tricks here except for the **Dragoon**, being a **Strange Battle Piece** and all. He begins the fight with a big **Bravery** boost and uses **Bahamut** to get more, then spams **Jump** the whole fight.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

**Jump** ironically leaves him wide open for **Squall**'s **assist**, just be sure to call **Squall** _after_ the landing so you don't get **Assist Locked** - that is, if you're unsure, when your **Assist** gets hit or blocked, and your gauge is locked and can't be used or charged for a period of time.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The treasures here are a **Fish Scale** and a **Soul of Valor**, hey, I spoke too soon! Go pick up a new **Valiant**, if you're lacking in gil for it sell off those _"Soul"_ accessories, or some of the **Chocobo** stuff your playthrough might have given you. If you really need a lot, go ahead and sell the **Pink Tails**.

</PanelSection>

#### Exploration

- Outside again, go back to where we began and follow the path onward to find **Vaan**, who will be our **Assist** for the rest of the storyline.
- On the island beyond the breakable stone is an **Assist Charge**, it'll fill our **Assist Gauge** in a chain.

---

There's a **Capricious Reaper** around here, as you may have noticed earlier **Laguna**'s **Machine Gun** is a great attack to use to get the drop on enemies in the field, long range and duration for the win.

---

Continue on to a mountain area with a **Moogle**:

- If you don't have one buy a **Cross Chain**.
- And a few **Rosetta Stones** if you like, there's another one in the chest nearby.

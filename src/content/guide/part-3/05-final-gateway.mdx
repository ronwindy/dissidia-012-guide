<PanelSection kind="chain">

Use a **Cross Chain** at C3 to get all the enemies, good time for that **EX Charge**. Let's chat about **Laguna**'s **EX Mode** while I bring it up. His special bonus is to instantly cancel out the lag from a **Bravery** attack by using another one, letting him launch even more firepower than ever.

The **Cross Chain** at C3 will miss the **Sky Pirate** at E4, you'll have to fight him after. As I fought **Laguna** got to **Level 15** and learned **Pummel** and **Sticky Bomb**.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

His **EX Burst** is actually pretty challenging to get right, you have to hold R and move the cursor to center the opponent between a crosshair as they move around, and if you land enough hits to fill up the power bar **Laguna** summons his hyperspace arsenal together to charge power and fire a giant laser cannon...which he rides.

Badass? Very. And it comes complete with the best attack call of any video game ever. I'll let you hear for yourself so as to not ruin the fun.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemy line-up is two each **Sky Pirates**, **Wraiths** and **Gunners**. This time around the **Wraith** is the **Strange Battle Piece** and is packing **Mandragora**. Her HP attack is pretty nasty, **0-Form Particle Beam**, she charges for a second and then fires a big laser beam a short distance, turning to track you if you dodge.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

It's very difficult to avoid at close-range, when she begins to charge it up run away and then dodge backwards when it fires. You can slam her with an HP attack as she recovers, so it's not hard to win. Otherwise, nothing new here to watch for.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

**Pummel** is an effective two-hit keep-away attack and is handy in his aerial arsenal when he learns the midair version, but for now his current line-up of ground attacks is fine.

**Sticky Bomb** throws an explosive that sticks to the opponent and explodes several seconds later, great for comboing with other attacks but it's easy to block so it's hard to use, but you might as well if you've got the **CP**.

The new Summonstone is **Carbuncle**, it summons when our **Bravery** hits 0 - probably after landing an HP hit - and mirrors the opponent's **Bravery** to 0. For now it's useless, oh it'll be effective down the road, but at this point by the time you're going for an HP hit, it's probably after the opponent is already **Broken** so **Carbuncle** will do nothing, and we also have **Cactuar** which is just as effective for sapping the opponent's power, and can be called whenever we like.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The **Cloud of Darkness** has all four tentacle attacks, whose effects, to recap, are - ground variants which create dark electricity around you, send a large orb of darkness forward, spin attack, or just lash out to strike you, and aerial variants which fire two dark energy balls, fire a swarm of smaller ones, whirl to attack in a sphere, or lash out.

She has two new HP attacks. **Wide-Angle Particle Beam** has a second of charge before a wave of dark energy appears with a wide horizontal range and sweeps forward quickly, not too hard to dodge if you see it coming.

**Aura Ball** creates several large spheres of darkness one by one that spread out and move towards you, then explode when she wills it. Overall a pretty versatile pool of HP attacks to trip you up with, and her tentacles are hard to predict too.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

What breaks this fight through and stops a potential 2 or even 3 on the scale is one problem for her - **Ifrit**. I **Broke** her with a single **Ricochet Shot**, and **Ifrit** then boosted me to 1600 **Bravery**, **Wall Rush** her and a good 2000 HP of hers vanishes in the first 15 seconds of the fight.

After that mopping her up was pretty simple, and while she can be a difficult fight if you're a beginner, the ability to take off 2000 HP so easily will swing the fight your way regardless of skill level, if you've made it this far there's no reason you can't handle her.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

In the aftermath of the scene, **Laguna** suddenly notices the **Cloud of Darkness** is hot, and suddenly gets a leg cramp. The **Cloud of Darkness**, understandably dumbfounded by this, has a chat with him when he mentions he's fighting not for **Cosmos** but just to go home.

She tells him where the **Manikins** came from and how to stop them from increasing their numbers, by closing the portal they're using to emerge from the **Rift**. And why tell him this?

Because she wants to see how he handles it, it would be a suicide mission to seek out the portal, so what will **Laguna** do? She overestimated his dumb heroicness.

**Laguna** heads off and that ends the chapter. Unlike the last two, no Reports this time.

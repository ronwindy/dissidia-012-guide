Let's chat about **Laguna**'s **EX Mode** while I bring it up.

His special bonus is to instantly cancel out the lag from a **Bravery** attack by using another one, letting him launch even more firepower than ever.

The **Cross Chain** at **C3** will miss the **Sky Pirate** at _E4_, you'll have to fight him after.

As I fought **Laguna** got to **Level 15** and learned **Pummel** and **Sticky Bomb**.

His **EX Burst** is actually pretty challenging to get right, you have to hold R and move the cursor to center the opponent between a crosshair as they move around, and if you land enough hits to fill up the power bar **Laguna** summons his hyperspace arsenal together to charge power and fire a giant laser cannon...which he rides.

Badass? Very. And it comes complete with the best attack call of any video game ever. I'll let you hear for yourself so as to not ruin the fun.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">
The enemy line-up is two each **Sky Pirates**, **Wraiths** and **Gunners**.

This time around the **Wraith** is the **Strange Battle Piece** and is packing **Mandragora**.

Her HP attack is pretty nasty, **0-Form Particle Beam**, she charges for a second and then fires a big laser beam a short distance, turning to track you if you dodge.

It's very difficult to avoid at close-range, when she begins to charge it up run away and then dodge backwards when it fires.

You can slam her with an HP attack as she recovers, so it's not hard to win. Otherwise, nothing new here to watch for.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">
**Pummel** is an effective two-hit keep-away attack and is handy in his aerial arsenal when he learns the midair version, but for now his current line-up of ground attacks is fine.

**Sticky Bomb** throws an explosive that sticks to the opponent and explodes several seconds later, great for comboing with other attacks but it's easy to block so it's hard to use, but you might as well if you've got the **CP**.

The new Summonstone is **Carbuncle**, it summons when our **Bravery** hits 0 - probably after landing an HP hit - and mirrors the opponent's **Bravery** to 0.

For now it's useless, oh it'll be effective down the road, but at this point by the time you're going for an HP hit, it's probably after the opponent is already **Broken** so **Carbuncle** will do nothing, and we also have **Cactuar** which is just as effective for sapping the opponent's power, and can be called whenever we like.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Use a **Jump Chain** right where you spawn to chain the foursome here.

The line-up is a **Fleeting Flash**, two **Delusory Dragoons**, one Strange, and a **Transient Witch**. The **Witch** has a new attack, **Knight's Arrow**, she either sends up a shower of magic projectiles into the air that rain down on you, or creates a few that hang in the air for a long time before firing at you one by one.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The **Strange Dragoon** has **Odin** this time, when called it has a 50% chance to instantly **Break** you, not as much of a hassle as the summons the previous ones have had. Use the **BRV 0** skill if you want an advantage, but save **EX Charge** for the next gateway.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The enemies are easy to wipe out, and you'll find a **Gargoyle Pebble** in the chest here, it unlocks new shop items but nothing we can use.

On to the next gateway, last in the story now. If you lack a **Cross Chain**, head back to the **Moogle** and buy one.

</PanelSection>

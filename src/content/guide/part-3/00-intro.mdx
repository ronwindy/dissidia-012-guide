# Chapter 3

Welcome to **Chapter 3** - finally some plot development for the overarching story! _Read on..._

#### The Greatest Attack Call Ever

New chapter means new character of course!

---

<CharacterIntro name="Laguna" src="drake/Laguna012.PNG" width={84} height={250}>

Ah, **Laguna**. I wasn't too big on him in **FF8**, he was a goof and a half-insane loon, like most of the cast of that game come to think of it. _Dissidia_ however has made him one hell of a badass, giving him an unrivaled arsenal of weaponry and tweaking his quirks to be genuinely funny. **Laguna** is a well-meaning do-gooder who just has a habit of misusing words and getting confused when it comes to directions.

Also give a big round of applause for his **VA**, **A.V. Kennedy**, who does an awesome job, especially since it's his first time with SE voicing a character who has never been voiced by anyone before. He turns in what I consider one of the more memorable and character-fitting performances alongside **Kefka**, **Kain** and **The Emperor**.

<PanelSection title="About Laguna">

**Laguna** is far and away the hardest of the new six to use, relying on tactical fighting and pressuring the opponent to catch them when they slip or trap them between two attacks to inflict pain.

- His **Bravery** attacks hurt a lot but are easy to block, but that's where the trickery on your part comes in.
- **Laguna's HP attacks** are slow to use but combo well with his **Bravery** attacks.

  - For example, throw a **Sticky Bomb** at the opponent, then call down a **Satellite Beam** as it explodes so they can't dodge, or fire out a **Homing Bazooka** missile and smack the opponent in the face with **Ragnarok Blade** as they try to dodge the missile.
  - Or, flip it around. You get the point. This makes **Laguna** very hard to use and he'll require practice, fortunately you have an **Assist** the entire storyline as a crutch if his slow _HP attacks_ are giving you trouble.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Skills">

He starts off with some of his key attacks, **Machine Gun** and **Missile Barrage**.

- **Machine Gun** is just a solid attack option that does a lot of damage
- **Missile Barrage** fires out six missiles that turn and weave to track enemies, and should be used to combo with **Satellite Beam** or **Ragnarok Blade** as mentioned above.
- He'll quickly learn the **Ricochet Shot** attack, letting him shoot a bullet off of obstacles to bounce the shrapnel into an enemy, and he picks up **Ragnarok Blaster** as an _HP_ attack, blasting the enemy on the ground at close-range.
- Once you master him **Laguna** is quite effective and hurts a lot, but it will require effort. And of course, his **EX Burst** makes it worthwhile too.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

<Callout type="tip">

As you suit **Laguna** up before beginning the story, I did some quick math and found out the **Power Ring** with a _1.5 booster_ and a **Red Drop** is stronger than the **Ring** with two boosters, so yeah, let's go with that build from now on.

He won't have a weapon unless you wanna buy him one, but don't, he'll be tough enough to handle the enemies we meet as we begin and before we exit the starting gateway we'll find a weapon for him for free. And alas, while the shop may have new wares, none of them are the **Altair**. The **Capella** remains out of our reach for a while longer...

</Callout>

#### Exploration

And now outside again, go forward to find a **Counterfeit Wraith**, deal with it and take the **EX Charge** behind it.

Go to the left to a marsh area, there's a **Soul of Unrivaled Valor** in the chest, and a **False Stalwart** to fight.

We're in a new arena for this fight, break the Esper capsules and hope you **Battlegen** a **Magicite Shard**.

<Callout type="warning">
The **Stalwart** can be tough if you're unfamiliar with them from the first game:

- It has **Chain Cast** for a long-range aerial attack. **Round Edge** for a spinning ground attack.
- And its HP Attack **Cyclone** will deflect all of **Laguna**'s current **Bravery** attacks and stays out for several seconds, use **Ragnarok Blade** or **Buster** to disperse the winds.

 </Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Now, that **Magicite Shard** **Battlegen** is needed for the **Sunrise** accessory, which gives a **50%** boost to **Battlegen** rate, helpful since we need them to trade for so much.

**Kefka's Tower** will be encountered more in the next gateway, aim for two more **Shards**.

All we need from there is three each **Dragoon's Desire** and **Harlequin's Desire**, and we've fought a lot of those two enemies so far so you may have them, if so get the **Sunrise** and equip it over your **Booster**.

If you lack them, and I did sadly, continue on for now.

</Callout>

Beyond the **Stalwart**'s position is yet another **Pink Tail**.

Back on the main path there's a **BRV Zero** on an island over a grind bar from the stone.

Now head into the blue gateway.

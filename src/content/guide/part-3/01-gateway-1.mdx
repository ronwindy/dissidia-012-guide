<PanelSection>

We'll quickly see that as goofy as he acts, **Laguna** knows a strategy when it hits him in the face.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

The first chest gives us a **Cross Chain**, and **Laguna** lets us know precisely where to use it - _C4_, of course, chains all five enemies for a cool **25 KP**.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies of this stage are _NEW!_ Yes, gone are the **Idle Sky Pirates** and **Delusory Dragoons**, my comrades! We instead have two **Fleeting Flashes**, a **Counterfeit Wraith**, a **Transient Gunner**, and an **Ephemeral Vision**.

The **Wraith** has an **Anti-Air Particle Beam** attack that fires four lasers high in the air in a square formation around her, and she moves towards you on start-up, it has weak horizontal range so a back-dodge will be enough to avoid it.

- She also has two **Tentacle Moves** with varying effects, the ground one has her use a spin attack you can't block, and at full charge fires a large energy orb you can reflect to damage her.
- The aerial tentacle blow fires several energy orbs at us that bounce along the ground and are easily avoided by being airborne.

The **Gunner** has the same moves you have and will be fought last so you'll know how to handle it by that point.

The **Vision** has **Hop Step** on the ground, a quick and hard-to-anticipate move, fortunately it has low range, and in the air uses **Dart \& Weave**, where he dodges our attack and jumps up to come down spinning.

- Fortunately if he uses it when we aren't attacking it's _ridiculously_ easy to block and punish.
- The HP attack is **Energy Rain**, when you see the notice appear run away because he'll bounce off you, even stopping attacks, and bombards you with magic blasts.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Being that the enemies are dumb, don't worry about comboing **Laguna**'s moves too much, just **Machine Gun** or **Shotgun** them and then slam them with a **Ragnarok Blade** or **Satellite Beam**.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Open the chest when you're done mopping up the opposition to find a **Tanegashima**, a gun-type weapon that of course **Laguna** can use, so let him.

It also unlocks the **Valiant** in the shop, not as badass as the **Capella** but still nice, but we lack a **Fish Scale** and won't have one for a while so too bad.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

<Callout type="note">
That's the main problem with **D012**'s shop, by the time you can access trade accessories consistently the equipment you need them to trade for are underpowered compared to the point in the story you'll be at, but you have to take them anyway to unlock higher-level equipment, which you'll also lack the trade accessories to get, and so on until you get to the top **Level 100** equipment.

Yup, you'll find that a good half or so of the cost of most equipment pieces you'll be buying is actually spent paying for lower-level equipment pieces you need so you can trade up to what you actually wanted in the first place - like how we want an **Altair** right now just so we can get the **Capella** 🥴.

</Callout>

#### Exploration

**Laguna** is on the world map now, the music is a remix of **Eyes on Me**, insufferable in _FF8_ but here as a world map theme with no lyrics, actually quite nice.

_Immediately turn around_ to explore the region behind us, there's a **blue gateway** between a ring of grass, and a chest by the closed gateway near it with a **KP Gambler**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Save this item, it is very useful later and will make big bucks for us.
</Callout>

- The **Phantasmal Harlequin** outside the gateway has no new tricks to watch for.
- To the left of the gateway is a stone that produces a grind bar leading to an island with our fourth **Pink Tail**.

Head for the gateway now.

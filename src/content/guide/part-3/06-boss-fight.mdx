<PanelSection kind="enemies">
The **Cloud of Darkness** has all four tentacle attacks, whose effects, to recap, are - ground variants which create dark electricity around you, send a large orb of darkness forward, spin attack, or just lash out to strike you, and aerial variants which fire two dark energy balls, fire a swarm of smaller ones, whirl to attack in a sphere, or lash out.

She has two new HP attacks:

- **Wide-Angle Particle Beam** has a second of charge before a wave of dark energy appears with a wide horizontal range and sweeps forward quickly, not too hard to dodge if you see it coming.
- **Aura Ball** creates several large spheres of darkness one by one that spread out and move towards you, then explode when she wills it.

Overall a pretty versatile pool of HP attacks to trip you up with, and her tentacles are hard to predict too.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">
What breaks this fight through and stops a potential 2 or even 3 on the scale is one problem for her - **Ifrit**.

I **Broke** her with a single **Ricochet Shot**, and **Ifrit** then boosted me to _1600_ **Bravery**, **Wall Rush** her and a good _2000 HP_ of hers vanishes in the first _15_ seconds of the fight.

After that mopping her up was pretty simple, and while she can be a difficult fight if you're a beginner, the ability to take off 2000 HP so easily will swing the fight your way regardless of skill level, if you've made it this far there's no reason you can't handle her.

</PanelSection>

In the aftermath of the scene, **Laguna** suddenly notices the **Cloud of Darkness** is hot, and suddenly gets a leg cramp. The **Cloud of Darkness**, understandably dumbfounded by this, has a chat with him when he mentions he's fighting not for **Cosmos** but just to go home.

She tells him where the **Manikins** came from and how to stop them from increasing their numbers, by closing the portal they're using to emerge from the **Rift**. And why tell him this?

Because she wants to see how he handles it, it would be a suicide mission to seek out the portal, so what will **Laguna** do? She overestimated his dumb heroicness.

**Laguna** heads off and that ends the chapter. Unlike the last two, no Reports this time.

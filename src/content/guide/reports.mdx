# The Reports

As you play through the game, you'll receive notification of new Reports being unlocked. Reports provide backstory for the world of Dissidia and also contain scenes of other characters our heroes don't see. But there's also fights to be done too. You can do the Reports as you unlock them or put them off, or not do them at all. I'll assume you did them as soon as you unlocked them because that's what I did, and if you're not going to do what I tell you then why are you here? I am your lord and master, you will play as i decree it! KNEEEEEL BEFORE DRAKE!

#### Report 01

First, go to the main menu and check the **PP Catalog**, and purchase **Kuja** for usage.

View the scenes of the first report, showing us **Kuja** decided to betray **Chaos** and help **Zidane** and his friends get to the god to fight him, but **Kefka** caught wind of the scheme and derailed it. **Kuja** has to pretend he was just putting on an act, and engages the trio of heroes. We also get a glimpse of one of my all-time favorite characters in the series and of all fiction, but we'll wait to talk about her in detail next time. Before you view the scene leading up to the fight, be sure to equip **Kuja** to make it easier, buy him at a Rod and a Bronze Bangle at the shop (you'll have head and body armor for him already), and give him the **Power Ring**, **Level 1** - 9 and HP = 100% for accessories. The menu recommends he be **Level 5**, I'll be doing it at **Level 1**. If you feel like grinding a bit, go ahead and fight a bit in Quick Battle to boost him up and familiarize yourself with his attacks, he can be tricky to master. Note that you need to purchase **Kuja** from the **PP Catalog** to be allowed to access these fights, if you didn't go do it now.

First up is **Bartz**, with a **Squall** Assist. **Squall**'s ground Assist hurts a lot, but his aerial Assist is slow and easily avoided. He starts off with a full **EX Gauge** and will be absorbing **EX Force** at a higher rate as long as he stays in **EX Mode**. Don't get too close when he's in **EX Mode** because **Goblin Punch** is near impossible to anticipate and will hurt a lot since **Kuja** and **Bartz** are likely both at **Level 1** (GP gets a damage buff when used on an opponent the same level as **Bartz**). When in the air he'll spam **Holy**, easily reflected. His HP attacks are **Hellfire** and **Ragnarok Blade**, **Hellfire** has low horizontal range and **Ragnarok Blade** has long start-up, so avoiding HP damage isn't _too_ hard. The attacks you want to see often are **Hazard Raid** and Slide Shooter, two charging attacks that are easy to block and punish. His summon **Kraken** begins a 15 second countdown to the two of you swapping **Bravery**, land an HP attack as the counter gets to 2 to steal **Bartz**'s **Bravery** and leave him with 0.

Next up is **Squall**, his accessories will give him a big boost to physical defense when he's close to dying, a pity for him all of **Kuja**'s attacks are magical. He's got **Zidane** for an Assist. **Squall**'s **Solid Barrel** and **Beat Fang** hurt a lot but otherwise his attacks are mostly magical and none of them are too dangerous, though **Mystic Flurry** can be a nuisance. He's harder to avoid hits from compared to **Bartz** but fortunately there's no **EX Mode** shannanigans to worry about, and ultimately I found him easier to handle.

Finally, it's **Zidane**, with **Bartz** as his Assist and the build **Squall** wished he had, giving a massive boost to magic defense when he's low on HP. **Zidane** is flat one of the best characters in the game and one of my mains, and I say this for reasons this fight may demonstrate if the AI is feeling competent - he's got fast and versatile **Bravery** attacks that do quite a bit of damage, and many of them link to his **Meo Twister** HP attack. He's otherwise got very flexible HP attacks with **Stellar Circle** 5 to pull you in on the ground, **Free Energy** to explode in your face at close range, **Shift Break** to create a blast of water right under your feet, and **Grand Lethal** to charge at you from mid-range. His summon is Bomb, which will reduce **Zidane**'s **Bravery** to 0 when it explodes and damages your **Bravery** by the same amount, try to build up more **Bravery** than **Zidane** has or you'll get Broken.

The best way to deal with **Zidane** is to play defensively, let him use one of his attacks and as long as it isn't **Booster** 8, his charging ground attack (attack cue "Stings huh?"), you can block it and punish him. If you've gotten your **EX Gauge** charged up, feel free to use it to activate **EX Revenge** and stop a heavy HP attack he catches you in. Otherwise try to finish him with an **EX Burst** to avoid the defensive gain he gets from his boosters as you assault him near-death.

With **Zidane** dead, **Kuja** is left to contemplate his failure to betray **Chaos**, while **Kefka** plots how to punish him for the attempt. And now you know why **Kefka** brought him to fight **Lightning**.

#### Report 02

This time the story is a lot shorter and the fights simpler. **Terra** is still hiding out at the Dimensional Castle when she sees **Manikins** heading to **Order's Sanctuary**, and she fights them to protect **Cosmos**. Equip her first, you should have plenty of stuff for her to use. The first villain is a **Imaginary Soldier**, he uses **Sonic Break** and **Rising Fang**, the former is a mid-range ground combo and the latter is a midair leaping attack. His HP attack is Braver, it has short range and high charge-up so it's simple to avoid. **Terra** starts out with a good moveset, **Blizzara** and **Thundara** for midair and **Blizzard Combo** and **Fire** for ground, and **Tornado** as her HP attack. She can easily mop up these foes with that moveset, she's easy to get the hang of too to boot.

Next up, a **Imitation Despot**. He has **Thunder Crest**, which creates a field of lightning on the ground to trap and stun you for several seconds, and Dynamite in midair, which fires an orb of energy that sticks to walls and floors, and will also trap and stun you before exploding, it _really_ hurts so don't get caught. His HP attack is the aerial **Flare**, fires out an orange fireball to float in place, when he uses it a second time the first **Flare** vanishes. **Flare** will deflect your attacks but otherwise just avoid the traps and stay away from walls to make it easier to do so.

Third, the **Transient Witch**. She has Knight's Blade, which either fires a quick barrage of bolts as she turns to track you, or a slow storm of more bolts with slower turning to track you. Her HP attack is **Great Attractor**, which has a long charge-up time before she fires a planet at you, when you see her begin to charge it use **Thundara** to hit her and stop her.

Last up, our strongest foe is fittingly a **Phantasmal Harlequin**. He has the same tricks you've come to expect from them, particularly after the real **Kefka** showed up, but a nasty accessory set. He's got a **Hyper Ring** for +5% damage, and a series of timed **Boosters** to give him escalating boosts as the fight goes on longer. At 30 seconds five of them trigger for a total multiplier higher than 9x, giving him almost a 50% damage buff, and at 45 seconds that doubles, then doubles with an extra .5 at 60 seconds, and at 90 seconds, that _triples_. So yeah, try to end this quick or you're in for a world of agony when those boosters start firing and his damage output is more than doubled.

For real fun, spend the three fights up to this one charging **Terra**'s **EX Gauge**. Her **EX Mode** is her Trance form and lets her use Dualcast to cast all her spells twice in a row, except for **Blizzard Combo** which just deals more hits. Crush the image of **Kefka** with her epic **EX Burst** Riot Blade, it's easy to input. Also enjoy the fun of "Smurf **Terra**", on the Wiki IRC we joked about **Terra** turning blue for her **EX Mode** when we saw her third outfit in trailers for 012, then we turned out to be right.

Now with the **Manikins** vanquished, **Terra** stumbles to **Order's Sanctuary** and collapses, weakened. In spite of her alignment, **Cosmos** senses her pure and good heart and imbues her with her power to awaken as one of her warriors in the next cycle.

#### Report 05

We have a recommended level of 5 for this, **Sephiroth** was **Level 4** due to my using him for some Quick Battles previously. Of all characters to have a chat, here's **Kefka** and Sephy! **Kefka** tells **Sephiroth** that if two warriors from the same world fight, their memories return faster, and he wants to get **Cloud** off the bench and into the game, so to speak. So what's to do? There's a particularly girl on Team **Cosmos** **Cloud** knows from their world, if **Sephiroth** were to attack her, **Cloud** would run to the rescue and **Sephiroth** could punish him for betrayal. So Sephy does just that and tracks down **Tifa**.

**Tifa** has a very interesting accessory set-up, don't know where she lost all them when we got to her story. She has an **Iifa Dew**, so when she hits 0 HP, her **Bravery** is converted into HP and she lives. However, if she's Broken when she hits 0 HP, it won't trigger. And then she has **Zephyr Cloak** and **Heart's Ease**, giving her small boosts to **Bravery** when she dodges or blocks an attack, and seven Near Loss boosters, for a total 17x multiplier, increasing the boosts from those accessories to +34% of her base **Bravery**. **Tifa** is armed with a good attack set-up, Beat Rush, **Blizzard** and **Blizzara** on the ground and Falcon's Dive and Dolphin Blow in the air, with an attack we didn't learn yet, **Moonsault Kick**, a charging kick through the air. She has all the HP attacks you picked up in her story, and she's also got decent timing on her feints. What ruins **Tifa**'s difficulty in this fight is that she has nothing but a Leather Hat for equipment, no weapon or armor and so her HP, defense and attack suck. You should have no trouble doing massive damage to her with **Sephiroth** and can wipe out her piddling 1300 HP easily. Just make sure to **Break** her before dealing the final blow.

Now we cut to Part 2 of the Report. **Kefka** tracks down **Cloud** and lets him know what's going on. Suit up **Cloud** and launch the scene. **Sephiroth** prepares to kill **Tifa** when **Cloud** arrives, and **Sephiroth** of course does what **Kefka** suggested. Somehow he's gained four levels from fighting **Tifa** because he's **Level 8** now. **Sephiroth** has a whopping 99.9x booster here with a **Hyper Ring**...except his boosters only trigger when he's far enough away from **Cloud**, and even with his sword beam attacks he won't be far enough when attacking, so much like **Sephiroth** usually is, he's all show and no substance, his accessories are useless. He does have equipment though, missing a shield but having armor, a helmet and a sword, so he's more durable than **Tifa**. His attacks are easier than hers to block though, especially his ranged ones, so dodge or block them and punish him with **Slashing Blow** or **Climhazzard**, **Cloud**'s **Bravery** attacks are ridiculously powerful. **Sephiroth** won't take long to put down.

In the aftermath, **Sephiroth** takes off and **Cloud** tells **Tifa** even though he saved her, he's one of the **Chaos** warriors, he just felt like saving her. **Tifa** recognizes her name, but **Cloud** denies that they know each other. We then get a few mythology gags, and **Tifa** muses that she and **Cloud** will have to fight each other eventually, and **Cloud** tells her to head back to the others before he has a change of heart. **Tifa** runs off and **Cloud** thinks she'd lose the will to fight when her memories return, so he has to do something before that happens.

#### Report 06

Time to find out what happened to **Kain** when we left him behind. I bought him accessory slots for a total of 6 and gave him a **Power Ring**, **Hyper Ring**, **Pearl Necklace**, HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, and **Level 10-19**. The game says its recommended level if 16, I was 15 so hey, pretty spot on. **Exdeath** unleashes a swarm of manikins against us we must fight off! In a neat touch of irony, they're manikins of the people we've seen **Kain** attack.

First up is a **Fallacious Wanderer**, incredibly pathetic, I stood there and he only ever used two attacks, crush him. Next, an **Imitation Liegeman**. He's a new foe and has Rope Knife, a mid-range attack that will pull you towards him for one of three follow-up attacks, watch his movements and block it when you see him make his move. Rope Knife can snag you on an angle, so he's liable to pull you out of the air and ensnare you if you're on a lower ledge. In the air he has Swordslash, a very quick but very short range sword blow that slams you down. He's more dangerous than the Wanderer by virtue of actually trying to hurt you, but still won't put up much resistance.

Third, a **Capricious Thief**, a Strange enemy. He's got Soul of the Craven for 0 starting **Bravery**, and three HP = 1 boosters with a **Guardian Bangle** for a pretty impressive defense bonus. He uses the **Carbuncle** summon to share his 0 **Bravery** with us, then spams Solution 9, firing a barrage of energy runes at us. It's actually an impressive set-up they gave him. Solution 9 has to be blocked several times since of course it hits several times, and attempting to dodge just has us get hit by the others he's firing. He's also got **Scoop Art**, firing three slow energy blasts we can reflect back at him. You might be inclined to just attack and **Break** him, but of course he's got that **Guardian Bangle** and a 64x booster. It's a bit hard to **Break** him even if he is at 0 **Bravery** when our **Bravery** attacks do 0 damage. Go ahead, combo the little sucker and see how well it works out for you. I even hit him with **Spiral Blow**, with 20 power the strongest single hit **Kain** can deal, no dice. The only way to beat him is to land an HP attack to get your **Bravery** back to normal, then deal the proper finishing blow. Jump is the perfect move here since he kinda can't move when using either of his attacks, and you'll move too fast for him to hit you mid-attack.

Next a Fleeing Flash, she actually has a powerful weapon and a **Hyper Ring** backed up by 8 HP = 100% boosters. Why is it these manikins are better equipped than the bosses we killed in the Epilogue? She's got **Watera**, **Ruin**, Ruinga, Launch, and an Assist - an **Imaginary Brawler**. She can be a mild challenge, deal an HP hit early on to deactivate her boosters and you won't have much trouble with her, as always the AI's incompetence ruins her. Now suddenly, the manikins stop attacking! But only a **Warrior** of **Chaos** can control them...**Golbez**! **Kain** gets past them and confronts the tree directly.

Contrary to that moment of treachery, **Golbez** is on **Exdeath**'s side as his Assist here. The tree has two **Hyper Rings** and _eight_ boosters to trigger when he's about to die, so watch out for a huge power boost when that comes. He's also got the **Iron Giant** summon and good equipment all-around, making him much more formidable than the five bosses the rest of the 012 crew faced down. He's got the same **Omni Block**, **Reverse Polarity** and **Turn Guard** tricks as before, but is comparatively not much more difficult, he's a few levels higher, but then **Kain** likely is to, and you've got better equipment for him this time. To avoid his power surge on the brink of death, either weaken him and blast him with an **EX Burst** to finish him, or build up enough **Bravery** to knock off the last chunk in one swoop without him entering near death status. **Exdeath** fades away and we get an excellent scene between **Kain** and **Golbez** I won't spoiler. It just serves to confirm for you doubters that **Kain** is a genuine hero.

#### Report 07

This is a quick one and takes place after the **Cloud**-Sephiroth-Tifa confrontation above. **Cloud** seeks to end the war so **Tifa** doesn't risk being slain. Well, only one way to do that, and so we open on him speaking to **Chaos**. And so the battle begins. **Chaos** has no equipment, is **Level 1**, and has three Cursed Ringed. Even with his strong, fast attacks, he is helpless before you, barely doing any damage and taken heavy damage in turn. Defeat him and watch as we learn the battle was a formality - **Chaos** strikes down **Cloud** with a blast of darkness and leaves him to sink into oblivion. As he lays dying, **Cloud** calls out to **Cosmos** to save **Tifa**, and she hears him and saves his spirit, vowing that for the goodness in his heart, he will be returned in the next cycle.

Remember I said **Tifa** and **Vaan** are two of the four characters I dislike and don't use? **Shantotto** is #3. **Shantotto** is really fun to watch in cutscenes and has a good vocal performance, and I like her as a character. She's just a horrible fighter. Her gimmick is creative but it doesn't work out. In the original Dissidia **Shantotto** was one of two secret characters unlocked after completing the story mode for her, she had little to do with the main story. Here it's much the same, though they are least do properly tie her story into the main plot by explaining how she ended up outside the cycles as she did.

**Shantotto**'s **Bravery** attacks are supportive, she only has two proper attacks that do damage, and they're good for what they are. Otherwise she uses Bind, creative a binding ring of energy that holds the opponent in place, but they can still dodge and attack, just can't move. Stun interrupts the opponent's current action, and if it hits can chain into other attacks like **Exdeath**'s **Reverse Polarity**. And finally Bio tosses out a cloud of poisons that tracks the opponent, and deals damage over time when it hits.

**Shantotto**'s true strength is her HP attacks. She "levels" them up at 2000 and 4000 **Bravery**, and they hit over larger areas and do more damage, etc. Furthermore she can chain three HP attacks together, piling up more **Bravery** damage until the third hits for HP damage. It's a creative gimmick like I said, the problem comes in well...think about it. Against a competent, well-equipped opponent, how do you expect to build up 4000 **Bravery**? Even 2000? So that means most of the time you'll be using the **Level 1** versions of her attacks, which are alright on their own but are still not that awesome once you figure out how to avoid them.

Maybe I just suck with her, maybe I just lack the patience to use her, but I dislike **Shantotto** honestly. She's annoying to fight as an AI, they're always Stunning you and them smashing her staff into your face, but annoying does not equal powerful. As a footnote they didn't do her any favors by nerfing her **EX Mode** - in Dissidia she could spam HP attacks freely because her **EX Mode** ability didn't lower her **Bravery** back to 0 after dealing HP damage, which also meant she could use an **EX Burst** and not only do extra damage because she kept the **Bravery** from the initializing HP attack, but she would still keep the **Bravery** she racked up from the Burst after it ended. In D012, it just restores her **Bravery** to its base value quickly after landing HP damage...basically it's just the **Mandragora** summon in that aspect, and by the way, the **Mandragora** summon sucks.

**Gabranth** is the other secret character from the original Dissidia, and like **Shantotto** he's an outsider to the cycles, he participated in them in the past but escaped somehow. For both of them, the how is explained in the Official Quests if you'd like to read.

Unlike **Shantotto**, **Gabranth** is 100% pure, unfiltered **_badass_**. His **Bravery** attacks suck, they're slow and weak. But that's fine, because his HP attack is unique - it does no damage, it just charges his **EX Gauge**. Once **Gabranth** fills that meter up, he enters **EX Mode**, the helmet goes on, the swords separate, and the carnage begins. **Gabranth**'s moveset changes entirely in his **EX Mode** and he is insanely powerful, his **Bravery** attacks do massive damage, execute fast, have surprising range to them for melee combos, and a lot do **Wall Rush** for more pain. He also gets a big movement speed boost, and he gets the normal **EX Mode** benefits like Regen and a critical hit rate increase. And of course, an **EX Burst**. **Gabranth**'s **EX Burst** is one of the coolests of the game. His HP attacks are mediocre, the only one really awesome is the aerial version of Innocence, sending a rain of giant energy blades crashing down over the arena. Hatred has its uses too though, and Execution...well I guess it doesn't hurt to round out the arsenal.

**Gabranth** is awesomely strong, he has three glaring weaknesses though. The first is, obviously, filling up that **EX Gauge** and keeping it active while you slice the opponent into red paste. His exclusive equipment, the Chaosbringer and Highway Star, help by increasing the duration and giving him more EX when he absorbs **EX Force**, and he can of course use accessories to power up both traits further. But ultimately, unless you rely on Exp to EX, you're gonna run out of power sooner or later, and **Gabranth** goes back to normal and you have to dodge the opponent using **EX Charge** when you get a break until you're back at full. The second is Assists, **EX Charge** slightly kinda very makes **Gabranth** a sitting duck, and if he gets hit with an Assist in **EX Mode**, then his gauge depletes. Fortunately the opponent gets no **Bravery** thanks to a unique ability of **Gabranth**, but still, no more **EX Mode**. Third and last, he has pretty much no ranged attacks, he has Gaia Breach which doesn't _suck_, but still isn't all that great and is just there to give him a ranged option. **Gabranth** isn't that good competitively when you fight without equipment and the opponent is a human who knows to just avoid the Judge until his **EX Gauge** depletes, but I still love him, he's a blast to play with. You know how in say, Kingdom Hearts Birth By Sleep, you entered a Command Style and just started pressing X to unleash hell until the Unversed stopped twitching? That's **Gabranth**'s **EX Mode**. Go forth, ye players, and deliver judgement unto the hapless AIs.

#### Report 09

In the 13th cycle, **Terra** awakens alongside the **Onion Knight** and the two are quickly attacked! The first enemy is naturally a **Counterfeit Wraith**, it uses the spin attack tentacle attack on the ground and the energy orb barrage attack in the air, and has **Anti-Air Particle Beam**. Nothing even vaguely resembling a threat here. Next is a **Phantasmal Harlequin**, it as a **Hyper Ring** and **Guardian Bangle** with 8 HP = 100% boosters, full equipment, and uses **Ultima**, the three-blast **Firaga**, **Scatter-Spray Blizzaga**, **Lickity-Split Thundaga**, and for HP has **Havoc Wing**, **Trine** and **Hyperdrive**. Just land an HP attack early to sap its boosters and it isn't a problem.

After the fight **Terra** thanks the Knight for protecting her and begins to head off on her journey, but the **Onion Knight** knows she dislikes fighting and volunteers to come with her and protect her. **Terra** doesn't want to be a burden because of her hesitation, but he has none of it and tells her to just lay low if a fight comes. **Terra** muses the words sound familiar but doesn't remember why.

This unlocks the next Report, where in the 12th cycle, **Vaan** acts not only like an idiot, but an asshole, to **Onion Knight**, telling him since he's young he shouldn't work as hard as the "adults" and thinking it must be tough being treated like a kid yet still being called to fight. **Onion Knight** of course _isn't_ treated like a kid by the other warriors, yes he's young but he's still strong enough to be called by **Cosmos** and he's perfectly capable in battle - not to mention that as he says, **Vaan** is a kid himself not that much older. **Vaan** of course ignores him and appoints himself the Knight's "big bro". We then get a couple scenes of **Vaan** trying to act like his guardian brother while the **Onion Knight** just gets annoyed, **Vaan** telling him he doesn't need to train so hard and yelling at the **Warrior of Light** for not going easy on **Onion Knight** when sparring. **Vaan** _finally_ notices he's in a bad mood and asks what's the cause, assuming he's annoyed over getting hurt during that sparring match, but **Onion Knight** just sits there looking at him like he's a fucking idiot, which he is. However, he confides to **Squall** he's getting used to having someone look out for him...which **Vaan** isn't, he's being a condescending douche who thinks **Onion Knight** can't take care of himself.

So yeah, in the original Dissidia **Onion Knight** just wanted to protect **Terra** and their friendship was a neat throwback to **Terra**'s kids at Mobliz. **Dissidia 012**, he's just **Vaan**'s little brother and replaces him as **Terra**'s guardian.

God how I wish **Vaan** wasn't in this game. It's a mighty feat when he manages to mangle _two_ character arcs and still not have one of his own.

#### Report 12

Recommended Level is 30? I laugh at your recommended level with my **Level 16** and 17 **Tidus** and **Bartz**!

Now suddenly **Tidus** and **Bartz** are travelling together...how? This must take place post-Bartz chapter because we've been with **Tidus**'s crew since the start of the game, odd the game would throw this scene at us without any basis for time. **Bartz** confesses to **Tidus** he can't remember much about his home, save for that feather. **Tidus** wonders why that doesn't bug him, when suddenly from off-screen, the manikins arrive! **Bartz** goes first in this fight, if he dies **Tidus** is in, and neither has any Assist.

First foe is a **Level 28** **Transient Witch**, she uses Knight's Axe, **Great Attractor**, and for accessories she has an **Iifa Leaf**, **Heart's Ease**, **Zephyr Cloak** and seven Near Loss boosters, letting her avoid death and get a nice buff to **Bravery** for blocking and dodging attacks when you have enough **Bravery** to kill her. An easy win, she has no equipment aside from a helmet so no defense or attack benefits.

Next is a **Level 33** **Imitation Despot**, no equipment but a **Hyper Ring** with 6 Far From Opponent, the Ai-exclusive 3x onces, for a total 99.9x multiplier pumping up his **Hyper Ring**, OUCH! He uses **Light Crest**, **Thunder Crest** and **Flare**, slightly higher **Bravery** and HP but otherwise still easily taken down. Use **Ragnarok Blade** or **Energy Rain**'s kick to deflect **Flare**. Fortunately the AI is too stupid to stay away from us when we approach his traps so his boosters go off.

Last is a **False Stalwart**, he enters with a full **EX Gauge** and has a **Muscle Belt**, **Earring**, **Attractorb**, **Pearl Necklace**, and five **EX Mode** boosters, so once he enters **EX Mode** he probably is going to stay in **EX Mode**. He has the **Carbuncle** summon and **Counterfeit Youth** Assist, but fortunately, still no equipment. The moveset is the typical **Garland** build as I covered in Chapter 9 before you unlocked this Report. Still pretty dumb, and still pretty easy to take out. If you kept **Bartz** alive up to now it's a cakewalk, even if **Bartz** is taken out at this point **Tidus** will come in at full HP, while the Stalwart keeps however much HP it had when it won on **Bartz**.

**Bartz** now finishes his thought that someone advised him not to worry about stuff he can't control, so even though he doesn't remember who said it, just some old guy, he lives up to that advice.

#### Report 15

This is pretty easy, you get **Zidane** and **Onion Knight** fighting off some **Manikins**. First is a **Capricious Thief** with **Storm Impulse**, he's got a **Hyper Ring** with a No BRV Damage and 8 HP = 100% boosters, easy pickings. Second, a **Phantasmal Girl** with **Blizzara**, **Meltdown**, and a **Hyper Ring** and 9 Near Loss boosters, a little stronger but still not a threat. Last is a moderate threat, a **Counterfeit Youth** with an **Attractorb**, **Pearl Necklace**, **Dragonfly Orb**, **Arcane Resin**, 6 **Break** boosters, and just about every attack the **Onion Knight** has. What really makes him tricky is his **Bahamut** summon, those HP links are going to hurt like a bitch. Fortunately you have two party members to go through, two on one should be able to handle him fine.

With them gone the **Onion Knight** wonders if they're all just manikins, just more advanced versions given fake memories. The idea doesn't bother **Zidane** though, it's what you do with life that's important, not how you were created, and wondering about the potential secrets won't do anything. He then makes a crack about **Terra** thinking of him like a "annoying little brother", and laughs he has to cheer up the kids when that gets **Onion Knight** riled up. I can only presume if the scene continued beyond what was shown in the game the next line of dialogue would be **Zidane** asking OK if he's got a shot at **Terra**, because no one can resist her charms, not to mention...well, **Zidane**.

#### Report 16

For this one the recommended level is 34, I went in guns blazing with my level 19 **Warrior of Light**, let's rock! First is a level 30 **Imaginary Brawler** with Waterkick, **Moonsault Kick**, **Burning Arrow**, and 8 Near Death boosters with a **Protect Stud**. She was easily wiped out. Second is a **Level 34** **Ephemeral Summoner**, she has **Aerospark**, Impulse, **Energy Ray**, a **White Cape** with 8 Near Death boosters, and the Barbarricia summon. That is one of the worst in the game kiddies because it is easy to turn back - Barbara appears when you call your own summon, and swaps your **Bravery** with the summoner's. Though with the **Cactuar** summon it won't do much, with other summons it can cripple the summoner and leave us supercharged. Anyway, the **Summoner** is also quickly dispatched.

The last foe is an **Idle Sky Pirate**, **Level 38**. He's got Axe \& Shield, Katana, Greatsword, Staff, Rifle, **Torrent** and **Inferno**. His summon is **Magic Pot**, a **Fleeting Flash** is his Assist, and for accessories he's got a **Hyper Ring** with 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value. He's the toughest foe, but only because he has a hand equipment piece to give him a defense bonus, otherwise, still easy to kill, even if he does have 15 or more levels on the **Warrior**. Crossover - Rune Saber for the win!

After the fights there's a short scene of **Terra** thanking the **Warrior** for protecting her, and then we unlock the next part of the Report. Now we take control of the **Onion Knight** and **Cecil**. The recommended level is 35, yeah and how did that go just a minute ago? Anyway, **Cecil** makes a crack that the **Onion Knight** has fallen for **Terra**, and the **Warrior** tells him if he's vowed to protect her he must stick with it like a true knight.

These three fights take place in one of the worst arenas in the game, Sky Fortress **Bahamut** Ω. Here rushing winds constantly push you back towards the end of the stage, where the entire wall is a banish trap back to the center area. This makes aerial combat virtually impossible since characters are constantly getting blown around. You'll see what I mean. It isn't a huge problem for the **Onion Knight** or **Cecil** really, but characters like **Kuja**, **Zidane**, even **Terra** and **Kain**, hate this arena. The **Bravery** pool rises when the characters keep their feet on the ground, if they're both grounded it rises faster.

First is a **Level 32** **Transient Gunner**, it has an **Iifa Dew** with Force to Courage, **Pearl Necklace**, **Attractorb** and 6 Near Loss boosters. As a result when near death it'll absorb a lot of **EX Force** and get **Bravery** from it, then get that **Bravery** back as HP when you deal the would-be final blow. It's pretty clever, but once that occurs he's stuck with 0 **Bravery** and that extra HP boost won't go very far. It uses Machine Gun, Pummel and Ragnarok Buster. Next is a **Delusory Dragoon** with a **Hyper Ring**, and some timer boosters, 15 seconds, 5 30 seconds, 45 seconds, 60 seconds, and 90 seconds, and an unknown summon and **Ephemeral Summoner** Assist. He uses **Spiral Blow**, **Lance Barrage** and **Dragon's Fang**.

The last one is a **Level 40** **Fleeting Flash** with all of **Lightning**'s attacks, an **Idle Sky Pirate** Assist, **Alexander** as a summon, and a **Hyper Ring** and **Guardian Bangle** with 8 HP = 100% boosters. She's pretty tough, use the stage to your advantage, whittling her down via the Banish traps while using HP links, and by the time you can **Break** her the stage **Bravery** should be fairly high thanks to the gimmick of the stage, and she'll be weak enough to kill normally afterwards.

With the manikins gone the group takes a short rest, and **Onion Knight** wonders how the **Warrior of Light** can be so calm with all they've learned about **Cosmos** and the war, not to mention her death after how long he's fought for her. **Cecil** explains the **Crystals** and the warriors that wield them are **Cosmos**'s legacy, and he has to protect that now before he can express the conflicting emotions the **Warrior** must be feeling.

#### Report 17

As we open up, the game wastes no time pitting us against enemies with the **Warrior of Light** and **Bartz**. First is a **False Stalwart** with the usual moveset, as well as all his HP attacks, a **Hyper Ring** with 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value, **Lich** as a summon, and a Fallacious Giant, **Gilgamesh**, as an Assist.

Second is a **Lady of Antiquity**, a **Shantotto** manikin and thus a new foe. She has a three-hit ground staff combo as she steps forward, a boomeranging staff throw in the air, Stun which interrupts your actions at close-range and then lets her attack you while you staggered, and a variety of HP attacks, on the ground she shoots fireballs at you, creates lightning above you and summons rocks from below, in the air she shoots a shard of ice at you and summons wind and water to surround you. She summons **Iron Giant**, uses an unknown Assist, and has the Force to Courage stuff enemies in previous Reports and the Epilogue use. She has long start-up to her HP attacks though, get in close and use an HP link, rinse and repeat, she's pretty tricky, especially if you're new to Dissidia and don't know how she works.

Last is a foe I _know_ is new to you, a Horror of Antiquity - **Prishe**. She has a Fallacious Giant Assist and **Tiamat** for a summon, and a **Hyper Ring** with a familiar set of timed boosters. She has a lot of really quick but short-ranged attacks and and her HP attacks are very slow. Her **Bravery** attacks really hurt if she connects with them, especially because she has support skills to increase her critical hit rate. Watch out for Backhand Blow and Spinning Attack, an aerial dropdown punch and a spinning uppercut respectively, my best comparison to give you an idea of how they work and where they hit is to say they're like **Tempest** and **Vortex**, roughly. On the ground if she pauses and is hunched down, she's charging up Dragon Kick, when she lets it go she'll charge forward and do a leg sweep that sends a projectile into the air, it has Mid priority when charged so it can't be blocked. She has a lot of **Bravery** too, stay in the middle of the arena and let the stage help you whittle her down. Remember the **Warrior** is with **Bartz**, so if you die it's okay, as long as you're good enough with **Bartz** to finish her off.

After the fight, **Bartz** says he's seen the warrior at this place before and thinks he likes it here...um, when was he ever shown here? **Bartz** asks why but the **Warrior** changes the subject, so **Bartz** asks if he's remembered his name yet. He suggests they come up with a name for him, but the **Warrior** refuses - he has a memory, somewhere deep down, of someone calling him by name, and he wants to hold onto it instead of a new name burying it.

#### Report 18

Frankly this Report's difficulty setting is just silly. You take command of **Prishe** with a recommended level of 50, but since you unlock this after the Chasm in the Rotting Land, either you used **Prishe** in 000 and she's going to be far higher than level 50, or you didn't use her and she's at level 1. I'm just saying, it's kinda weird either way.

The scene opens of **Prishe** exploring, bored with the world because she's explored everyone, but suddenly she comes across something new - a glowing figure floating in the air. It's the **Warrior of Light**, without a helmet and looking quite dead-eyed. He's got a personality to match too. **Prishe** thinks she's never seen him before and doesn't think he's a warrior for either god, when **Garland** comes upon him. He recognizes the **Warrior** somehow, and warns **Prishe** she doesn't understand what she's found and should back off. **Prishe**, of course, accepts the challenge.

So, with our **Level 100** **Prishe** because sportsmanship is overrated in this game, and you'll agree if you knew what's coming up in 000 after the Chasm in the Rotting Land, we'll have no trouble trouncing the mere **Level 50** **Garland**. Finders keepers, losers bleeders!

Once the Stalwart runs off with his tail between his legs nursing his bruises, **Prishe** heads back to **Order's Sanctuary** with the **Warrior**. **Prishe** suggests **Cosmos** imbue him with her power, and **Shantotto**, thinking his appearance intriguing, agrees. **Cosmos** is hesitant, and the **Warrior** just continues to stand there looking stoned or hung over or whatever sort of incapacitation you think is most amusing. Suddenly, **Cosmos** recognizes him too somehow, and accepts him into the fold. Imbued with her light, the **Warrior** finally speaks a few proper sentences.

Later, **Prishe** asks how the **Warrior** has no memory, and the **Warrior** says it's no concern to him, since he's only there to fight. **Prishe** assures him he's not defined just by the fighting, and just because he has no memories doesn't mean he isn't a person who has a life to live. Then for an odd reason we get a short CG scene of **Prishe** coming up with the perfect name...which we never hear. Way to cocktease, Square-Enix.

#### Report 19

Savor this kiddies, the last Report fight! The recommended level is 50 for our hero, **Gilgamesh**. He arrives in the Rift to confront **Bartz** like we saw earlier, but **Bartz** curiously reacts a bit differently then we saw. **Gilgamesh** wants his promised rematched, and **Bartz** is happy to give it to him.

**Bartz** is **Level 50** with good equipment and a pretty cool **EX Mode** build, watch out once you **Break** him. He has **Hazard Raid**, **Solid Ascension**, Slide Shooter, **Holy**, and **Rush Impact**, and of course all his HP attacks. Keep away once he enters **EX Mode**, that lightning-fast **Goblin Punch** can catch you off-guard, and then it's **EX Burst** time. Otherwise this fight is surprisingly easy, or it was for me. I gave **Gilgamesh** a **Hyper Ring**, **Muscle Belt**, Pre-Assist, **Empty Assist Gauge**, After **Summon**, **Pre-EX Mode**, BRV ≥ Base Value, and HP = 100%. This gave me a nice 7.4x multiplier once I began the battle by activating my summon - **Tiamat**. Then I broke **Bartz** with one attack, hung back and let **Tiamat** triple me to over 6000 **Bravery**, and fired off a Missile to end the fight.

As **Gilgamesh** stands in triumph and **Bartz** congratulates him, the scene ends. It turns out this was all just one of **Gilgamesh**'s day dreams while stuck in the Rift, when suddenly he senses **Bartz** and heads off to investigate.

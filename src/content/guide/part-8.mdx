# Epilogue

Epilogue time!

#### Launch

For the entirety of this chapter, you'll command a party of five characters, **Kain** will be our Assist with the other five being the party. I used the party in order of **Lightning**-Yuna-Laguna-Vaan-Tifa. When fighting in a party, at any time when fighting a foe if the first person is defeated, you can go to the next person in line to continue the fight with them, though the first member is still KO'd.

Before we depart, let's buy some new equipment. I bought a **Silken Shirt**, as all five can use it, then sold off my **Pink Tails**, the Metal Knuckles, a **Chocobo** Wing, and a few of the Battlegen items I had more than 10 of. I then bought a **Mythril Bangle** and a Wizard's Hat. Finally I bought two more accessory slots for **Lightning**, **Yuna** and **Laguna**. I put them first because at no time during this will you need to use more than three people until the end. I gave **Lightning** a **Capella**, **Mythril Bangle**, Wizard's Hat and **Silken Shirt**, and for accessories a **Power Ring**, **Hyper Ring**, **Sunrise**, HP = 100%, BRV \> Base Value, and **Level 10-19** for boosters, and the **Cyan Drop**. Sadly the **Level 10-19** will quickly become useless. **Yuna**, **Laguna** and **Vaan** got the same set-up, **Tifa** doesn't matter because we won't be using her until the end as I said. Now launch the chapter.

**Lightning** and crew are lost, thanks to **Laguna**, of course. **Tifa** and **Kain** show up and the four explain the situation. They know it's probably a suicide mission, but they have to try. **Tifa** and **Kain** naturally sign up to help, though **Lightning**, being **Lightning**, isn't enthusiastic about it.

There's a new type of enemy here, the Party Battle Piece. You fight them in one of two ways - Tournament or **Round Robin**, by pressing L to choose before you fight them. In Tournament, the two party leaders fight, whoever loses is replaced by the next member in-line and the winner fights them with however much HP they had left from the first fight. The winner fights the next person in line on the loser's team, and so forth until one team is out of members. **Round Robin**, the first two people fight, then second people in line fight, and so forth. Whoever wins enough fight for a majority win, for example 2 victories against a gang of 3 opponents, wins the overall conflict, and the remaining enemies on the losing team are beaten without a fight and **KP** rewarded for them instantly. In both ways, you get a **KP** multiplier for enemy parties as though you chained them like normal enemies, and they in turn count towards the chain count when chained with normal enemies.

Now, naturally and obviously Tournament and **Round Robin** have their share of problems, soloing an entire enemy party with one person versus splitting the load among the entire group and possibly being given a poor match-up. However, I tend to go with Tournament for a simple reason - when you fight in **Round Robin**, only the people who are going to fight get exp. Tournament, all five members get exp for the fight. **Round Robin** where you fight two opponents here for example, only the first two people get exp. And in addition, soling all the enemy parties in this tale with **Lightning**, or whoever you're using up front, isn't difficult at all, in later storylines (read: Confessions of the Creator) the decision isn't so cut and dry, but here it's just more practical, especially since at the end of this tale you have to use all five people to fight and don't want them underleveled because you used **Round Robin**.

**Straight Chain** at B2 to get them all, of course, and snag 25 **KP**. As for the actual opponents, the lone enemy is an **Imaginary Brawler**, while the two Parties are a **Delusory Dragoon** and **False Stalwart**, and a **Fleeting Flash** and a Stalwart. The Stalwarts have a **Hyper Ring** and a series of timed boosters to give them more and more power as the fight wears off, so try and defeat them quickly. For summons, one has **Tiamat**, which triples its **Bravery** after 15 seconds, and the other has **Lich**, which saps your **Bravery** to 0 after 15 seconds. None of these guys have any new surprises, but the enemy level in general here is a bit higher than in previous storylines, in the level 13-19 range with a few going higher. Still not very dangerous but be aware they aren't as big pushovers as you've gotten used to.

The chest has 500 gil for us. We then get a scene of **Lightning** and **Kain**, she's still kinda pissed over that whole thing back in Chapter 1, where **Kain** kinda tried to put his lance through her in the non-innuendo way, and **Kain** accepts that. But at least he did save the others and is here helping now, which she's grateful for.

#### The Final Path

On the world map, you'll quickly notice the **Land of Discord** is not as pretty to look at as the southern regions. Immediately backtrack to another area, a narrow land strip. There's an orb of the dead here and a grindbar to an island with another piece of Mythril. Sadly still not enough for the three Mythril equipment pieces we need to unlock their true power, but we're getting there. Head back to the **Land of Discord** and go left to find a chest with a **Maser Eye**, and a nearby moogle. He has several summons available but the best, if you want one, is **Tiamat**, **Kraken** and **Marilith** are useless and **Lich** is good but at this point in the game **Cactuar** is superior. He also sells some minor trade accessories, if you want to buy any then grab the Fish Scale, Zombie Rag or Ghost Rag to unlock various shop equipment. The boosters he sells are very expensive and not worth the price for their limited usefulness at this point of the game. Also buy one each of a **Straight Chain**, **Multichain** and **Cross Chain**, if you already have one of them then of course don't buy. The gateway on the bridge is locked but we can see a chain of floating islands beyond leading to a gateway set into a volcano. Hmmmm....head back to the entrance of the **Land of Discord** and go the other way to the next gateway.

As we enter the party wonders why their **Crystals** haven't appeared, and think maybe it's because they got sidetracked with the manikins instead of fighting like **Cosmos** wished. **Vaan** decides then that they're just doing what's more important at the moment, the heroes that are left can get the **Crystals** once the manikins are stopped. See, this is the **Vaan** I would have liked to see more of, the dopey but insightful one.

**Straight Chain** at C4, of course. The feather is a new sight, the **Phoenix Down**. If a party member dies in a gateway, the **Phoenix Down** will revive them. But if your characters are dying, you're doing it wrong. The three lone enemies are an **Imaginary Brawler** and two Phantasmal Harlequins, one of which with the timed accessories set-up and the **Lich** summon. The party piece is an **Idle Sky Pirate**, **Phantasmal Harlequin** and **Delusory Dragoon**, the latter two with the mentioned accessories, the Harlequin with Liche and the **Dragoon** with **Kraken**. **Kraken** is a fun summon to counter, it triggers a countdown at the end of which you and your opponent swap **Bravery**, assuming neither of you has **Break**. Inflict an HP attack just before the counter hits 0 to steal your opponent's **Bravery** and leave them sitting at 0. The **KP Chance** is to win in 90 seconds remember, use a skill (I had a **BRV Zero**) to help if you think you'll have trouble.

This comes to one problem I have with party battles, though it only matters in terms of wiki-work like this - they don't display a new move or ability when learned as a party member, they just mention you learned *an* ability, so if you chain enemies and you gain three or so levels at a time and learn more than one ability during that, well how do you tell what level you learned what at and record it on a page? Anyway. **Lightning** learned **Aerora** at **Level 18** for me, it's a **Ravager** skill that acts similar to **Kain**'s **Cyclone**, firing a blast of wind that pops the enemy into the air. She also got Jump Boost. **Yuna** picked up Sonic Wings, a useful aerial **Bravery** attack that brings out Valefor to spin the enemy into the air to begin a Chase, it has decent range horizontally *and* vertically and is a good move. **Vaan** got Axe \& Shield, which...I don't know what it does, I told you I don't use **Vaan**. No one else has picked up anything aside from the typical support skills like Boost abilities, new Dashes, etc.

As we exit, **Kain** admits he doesn't know for sure if there's another cycle, but he took the gamble rather than fight as a pawn to be defeated. Continuing on the world map, the island up ahead has 500 gil on it. Around the other side of the land surrounding it you'll see another chest, it has a **BRV Charge**. Next gateway.

**Cross Chain** at E4. This gateway has a new **KP Chance**, **Break** the enemy within 60 seconds. It's pretty simple, but if you're having trouble then use **Carbuncle** or **Cactuar** to lower their **Bravery**, or if you still have a BRV 0 like I do, even better. Enemies are the lone **Transient Witch** and **Fleeting Flash**, the latter has those timer accessories and **Lich**, and the Party Piece consists of a **Counterfeit Wraith** with **Fusillade Particle Beam**, a **False Stalwart**, and an **Imitation Despot** with **Thunder Crest**, Dynamite, **Flare**, those accessories, and the **Marilith** summon. **Marilith** enacts a 5 second timer to freezing your **Bravery**, build up as much as you can before then so you can spam HP attacks without losing your **Bravery**.

The enemies with the summons and accessories have another new trick - Assists of their own now. The Flash has an **Idle Sky Pirate** and the Despot has the **Transient Gunner**. The Wraith uses a long-range tentacle thrust for its ground Assist and its whirling tentacle attack for its aerial Assist. The Gunner's aerial Assist is Shotgun and easy to avoid, but the ground Assist is Machine Gun, and if you get stuck on a **Thunder Crest** or Dynamite it will hurt a lot. Remember to block the Wraith's attacks rather than dodge them if you can, as this will Assist Lock the opponent and you'll instantly absorb all **EX Force** in the arena. The Gunner's attacks are ranged though and don't lock it if blocked, so dodge and attack it directly, press L to switch target to the Assist.

There's an old arena here you've been to, but only for the **Kefka** boss in Chapter 2 so it's sort of new, the Sky Fortress **Bahamut**. The Battlegen for it, the Sephira, unlocks the **Earring** in the shop with **Summoner** and Girl Battlegens. The chest has 2000 gil. At **Level 21**, which of course I hit at this point, **Lightning** and I believe the others as well will learn EX Critical Boost. This is an ability that you'll learn many of, it costs a lot of **CP** to equip but when used, it further increases the chances of landing critical hits in **EX Mode**. You'll learn many attacks like this as characters continue to gain in level, including Sneak Attack (boost when attacking from behind), Riposte (boost when attacking staggering opponent), Counterattack (boost when attacking just after opponent has attacked) and Gambler's Spirit (boost when attacking while in **Break**), just to name a few. You can check this page to note the abilities and the boost to critical chance they give. With **Lightning** outgrown from the **Level 10-19** booster,

As the party talks for a moment when we exit, and agree that if they think about looking forward to seeing what the portal looks like, it doesn't seem so scary and helps them stay positive. Suddenly a booming laugh alerts us to Incoming Ham, and **Exdeath** appears. **Golbez** joins him and tells them that there's a reason the opposition isn't as strong as you might expect given this is where the manikins are coming from. The horde of them are nearing their destination - **Order's Sanctuary**, and **Cosmos**, and only the **Warrior of Light** stands between her and them. There's not enough time to make it back to protect her, so **Kain** sends the five ahead to continue on while he stays to fend off **Exdeath** and **Golbez**. **Exdeath** is concerned with **Golbez** telling them what's going on, but **Golbez** brushes off it doesn't matter since it'll be over for them soon. We unlock a Report now which you can play after you finish this story, it makes a nice sort of epilogue.

#### The Last Stand

Back on the world map, for the rest of the story we'll be without an Assist. If you bought it before, equip the **Empty Assist Gauge** **Booster** on your characters over **Level 19** and thus can't use **Level 10** - 19 anymore. I didn't have it though so I had to make due with the lousy 1.2 booster Attacking **Bravery**. Also ditch the **Cyan Drop** for a **Pearl Necklace**. During that last gateway **Lightning** picked up Ruinga, a decent projectile on the ground that explodes and is pretty good as far as mandatory projectile attacks go. **Laguna** got Electroshield, a good attack that creates a spherical forcefield, it can damage opponents on contact and deflect normal projectiles. Know the **Emperor**'s aerial **Flare**? Think that, but as a **Bravery** attack. Don't bother equipping EX Critical Boost with anyone, not worth the **CP** for the limited use you'll get at the moment.

Head right to the island which has an **EX Charge** for us. There's two blue gateways to hit, I hit Recurring Tragedy first, the one shown to the right. A **Cross Chain** at D5 will get four of them, a **Multichain** at B7 will get four of them too, but a **Multichain** is more difficult to replace. As you can see all the enemies here are Strange, which means fun hijinks will ensue, being able to kill them all in one hit but the possibility of it happening to you being present. There's an **Ephemeral Summoner**, **Transient Gunner**, **Phantasmal Girl**, **Idle Sky Pirate**, and **Delusory Dragoon**.

The Gunner has the **Tiamat** summon and a huge initial **Bravery** boost, and it uses Machine Gun and **Ragnarok Blade**. The **Dragoon** has the same **Bravery** boost with Jump and **Bahamut**. The **Phantasmal Girl** also has that **Bravery** set-up with **Holy Combo**, **Ultima**, and **Cactuar**. The Ephemeral **Summon** has those accessories for **Bravery** along with an **Arcane Resin** to begin with a full **EX Gauge**, an **Iifa Leaf**, and uses **Thor's Hammer** with the **Mandragora** summon. Be thankful manikins can't use **EX Bursts**. The **Idle Sky Pirate** has the same accessories as the first three with **Odin** for a summon, and uses **Windburst**, remember what can reflect it - **Crushing Blow**, **Ragnarok Blade**, **Inferno**, Rolling Blaze or **Meteor Crusher**. **Yuna** is still unable to reflect it except if it's close to the ground, in which case **Hellfire** can work, but it's too risky.

**Lightning** got **Thundaga** during this round of levelling, it creates a field of lightning bolts for several seconds and begins a Chase if one hits. The chests have a Mage's Staff and a **Soul of Destruction**. The Staff is way useless.

Well that's all for this one, let's hit the other gateway, Gateway of Lost Innocence. <br />

Enemies here are a **False Stalwart** with **Lich**, **Phantasmal Harlequin** with **Kraken**, a **Transient Witch** with **Lich**, and an **Imaginary Champion** with **Iron Giant**. They also have the same series of timer booster accessories except for the Champion, who has two **Earrings**, six Far From Opponent, and an After 45 Seconds. I once again wonder about this choice since this is *Sephiroth*, the melee physical fighter, but meh. Also note of course the Champion is an Expert Piece and is at **Level 35** with good equipment and almost 4000 HP, while the rest are around your range, that being **Level 19-23**-ish. He has a new attack too, **Heaven's Light**, he pauses for a moment then does a quick slicing uppercut sort-of attack. Remember the **KP Chance** here is a 60 second **Break**, so use the Banish Traps in the arena to help wear him down quicker.

As for chaining them, Multi chain at C3 or E3. I lacked one so I did a **Cross Chain** at D1 to get three of them. **Lightning** hit **Level 26** and learned Controlled Recovery, a helpful ability that lets you move during Aerial Recovery. **Yuna** got to **Level 20** and learned Diamond Dust, it's similar to **Thor's Hammer** but executes quicker and has lower range. **Laguna** got to **Level 22** and picked up the midair Pummel, I replaced Shotgun for it. **Tifa** got to **Level 23** and got the ground version of Rolling Blaze, rather pointless to me since she has the aerial version already. The chests here have 5000 gil and **Chocobo** Powder. The Powder of course unlocks new equipment in the shop, the Chakram, a good throwing weapon but way expensive, and the Sakura, a Parrying weapon that's good and no as expensive but not cheap. There's also new higher-level stuff available, nothing vital though, just stronger equipment.

Back on the world map, approach the final gateway. The moogle has followed us here and hopes we come back safe. So do we, friend, so do we. If **Lightning** is under **Level 28** in your game, buy **Level Gap** ≥ 10 from him, and if she's somehow **Level 19** or lower, buy **Level Gap** ≥ 20 instead. Summons are pretty meaningless still, you may want to buy a few **Rosetta Stones** and give the party at least 7 accessory slots each, but aim for more, even 10 if you can. Then equip everyone with the following: **Power Ring**, **Hyper Ring**, **Pearl Necklace**, **Sunrise**, BRV ≥ Base Value, After **Summon**, and After 30 Seconds if you didn't buy **Empty Assist Gauge** back when you could, if you did buy it use that. For **Lightning**, use **Level Gap** ≥ 10 instead of After **Summon**, or even better, get her an extra slot and use both. If people have more slots, use Attacking **Bravery** and **Bravery** ≤ Base Value, but don't feel you have to overdo it, save some Stones and **KP** for the next story. Equip **Lightning** with **Cactuar**, then the others with either **Carbuncle** or **Ifrit**, one other person can use **Cactuar** but not two, as it only has two charges and **Lightning** will need it. If you want you can also give everyone except **Lightning** a **White Cape**, if you have the extra slot. If you want a little bit of insurance, buy a **Cure** skill.

I won't provide a map for this last gateway, the path is entirely linear and there's only four weak enemies, don't bother using any skills. There's also small conversations along the way, I'll let you enjoy them rather than sum them up. The chest has 1000 gil for us. The first enemy up is a **Phantasmal Harlequin**, he has a **Hyper Ring** and 8 HP = 100% boosters, so user an HP attack early to turn them off. He also has the **Bahamut** summon and **Idle Sky Pirate** Assist. The **Phantasmal Girl** is next, she has the same accessories and calls **Hecatoncheir**, but has no Assist. On the next floor is another **Idle Sky Pirate** with the same boosters, fought with **Brynhildr** and a **Counterfeit Wraith** Assist, and then finally a **Counterfeit Wraith** with a **Transient Gunner** Assist and the **Leviathan** summon. **Lightning** hit **Level 27** here and learned Army of One, a **Ravager** attack with long start-up but very flashy and cool graphics, and a good range once it goes off. As we move on there's a short scene where **Vaan** thinks back about **Terra** and how he never got her like he promised. Except he ***can't remember her friggin' name and wonders if she's Tidus or Tifa before he gets it right***. God I want this kid to die.

Final line-up as we head to the third floor: **Lightning** Lv 27, **Yuna** Lv 20, **Laguna** Lv 23, **Vaan** Lv 21, and **Tifa** Lv 24. Your party is automatically fully healed when you get here. The path behind us vanishes, and now only the **Chaos** piece is here. I used an **EX Charge** before starting this, you can use whatever skills you have, they'll be active in all of these last battles. The group has at last found the portal, but suddenly, the **Warriors of Chaos** appear! They're here to stop us of course, but the group isn't going to give up. We're here to save the world and our friends, and we're not turning back! We're going to take them down and keep going! For **Cosmos**, our friends, and the world, let the battle begin! Heart of the Cards, guide us!

You have to use everyone in turn to fight their designated opponent, and despite the similarities this isn't a party battle, you have to win all five fights. You'll notice everyone has a **Level 60** armor piece on for an extra 2000 HP over you, save for **Garland** who is **Level 39** and has that much without high-level armor because he's got 15 or so levels on us. Throughout these matches be sure to shatter the **Crystals** around the arena, as the item Battlegened from this arena is what we need for the **Muscle Belt** we want to use in Cycle 013. If you need a refresher course on everyone's attacks, their name in the boss box links to their article so you can read about their attacks. You won't have too much trouble with the first four, which is disappointing because you can tell the developers thought outside the box in order to try and make them a proper challenge, they just didn't think hard enough.

**Kefka** is up first, facing **Vaan**. He's got a **Dragonfly Orb**, **Attractorb**, **Pearl Necklace**, and 4 After 90 Seconds boosters for a whopping 81x multiplier when that time passes, allowing him to enter **EX Mode** and stay in it for the rest of the round. Thus it's vital you don't let the fight last that long. He's got all his old tricks and Forsaken Null, which creates a field of energy that follows you around the arena, dropping exploding orbs on you. Dodge them and dash up to him because he's highly vulnerable at this point. Aside from his higher HP and smarter AI, this **Kefka** is no different than previous ones so he shouldn't last that minute and a half he needs. If he does, well, you kinda deserve the intense pain his incredibly powerful **EX Mode** is going to inflict on you. By this time you kiddies should be good enough to win quicker. He has the **Tiamat** summon and if he Breaks you he'll use it, so don't let him.

Next, **Ultimecia** and **Tifa**. She's a lot stronger this time around, though I'm not sure if she could be weaker than the first time. She has an **Earring** and a **Muscle Belt** with four Far From Opponent for a 81x booster when they're active, and for some reason held on to a **Cursed Ring**. She's got the **Giant of Babil** summon. The problem is as always, the AI screws her over, it won't use those boosters well and will stay too close for them to activate, good thing too because her damage output is obscene when they're on. Use the same tricks as last time, that is Beat Rush to **Break** and Chase HP for the **Wall Rush** to open the fight, then wear her down little by little. Just remember her charged Knight's Blade can't be blocked, so if you hear here call out "flash" and see her curl up and form a dark portal, get above her because it can't angle up to hit you. She's the weakest of this final round.

Third, the **Emperor** is back. He's got the same **EX Mode** accessories as **Kefka**, and if you're careless around his traps he may live long enough to get them off. His summon is **Brynhildr**, it'll help you more than him by boosting the **Bravery** pool. His only new move is Dreary Cell, it creates a spark of light in the air, and when you get close it expands into a field of mines and explodes. Don't confuse it with his normal Mines. Otherwise as always just watch out for his traps, keep two steps ahead of **Flare** and be careful. If he gets into **EX Mode**, *do not let him hit you*, his **EX Mode** bonus heals him every time he damages you, making killing him a lot harder.

Fourth opponent, the **Cloud of Darkness**. She has **Hecatoncheir** and will use it to drain the **Bravery** pool and deny you a boost. She has a similar set-up to **Ultimecia** except with Close to Opponent instead of Far, and it's far more effective and gives her attacks ridiculous power. This in tandem with the wide variety of attacks at her disposal probably makes the most difficult opponent of the run, though that **Cursed Ring** still cripples her. Use **Laguna**'s Satellite beam to take advantage of the fact she's not able to dodge or move at proper speed when using an attack to blast her into the ground when she's defenseless, though be aware the giant crystal in the center of the arena will block the beam if she's beneath it. Remember to toss out Sticky Bombs and Missile Barrages to distract her as you go for the HP hits, and watch for the tentacle whips as warning of a **Bravery** attack coming so you can block it and counter. Pummel in midair is a quick hitting move and a good option if she charges up 0-Form **Particle Beam** and you're too close to get away, though you still have to be close enough to hit.

And now the final boss, **Garland**. He's got a lot of tricks you haven't seen before, his high level gives him a ridiculous **Bravery** bonus on you, and in tandem with his innately high skills in spite of his lousy equipment, he is a worthy opponent. However, the devs could have made him a lot more difficult, he *is* the final boss after all, give him a weapon and a real shield. Without a weapon and with the Cracked Shield, **Garland**'s attack and defense are only about equal to the first four enemies, so the only real thing to set him apart from them in terms of difficulty is his high base **Bravery** due to his level advantage. After 90 seconds pass he gets a 27x booster, raising the **Wall Rush** **Bravery** damage he inflicts by about 10 times its normal amount. And unfortunately, a lot of **Garland**'s attacks do **Wall Rush** damage. And in spite of his slow movement speed, he has a lot of charging attacks where he suddenly moves quick, so be ready to dodge them. Blocking will only work on **Round Edge** if you're close, if he spins the second time he'll crush your guard, and Lance Charge can't be blocked at all. His summon is **Ultima Weapon**. He'll probably use it once one of you is Broken, and it'll charge energy and 60 seconds later inflicts **Break** on you instantly. Nothing you can do to stop it and it's about impossible to tell when it'll fire.

Overall though, **Garland** is pretty simple to take down, as I said his only advantage is higher **Bravery**, which in practice means it takes another **Smite** or two to **Break** him. I told you to give **Lightning** **Cactuar** and it will sap 1000 **Bravery** from him, draining him to 0 no matter how high his boost is, unless he's Broken you. Pummel him and use the **EX Burst** finish to knock **Garland** down and end this final gauntlet of disappointing battles.

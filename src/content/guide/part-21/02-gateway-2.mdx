Inside the actual gateway now, Multichain at D4 to get them all. The bonus line is 36 and the KP Chance is a 60 second Break, be sure your summons are ready to help you out with that.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The first foe to fall is a Capricious Reaper with Ring Holy, Snatch Blow and Seraphic Star.
- Next comes an Ephemeral Phantom with Jecht Rush, all of his HP attacks, and Deathaze. For accessories he uses an Iifa Dew, Force to Courage, Pearl Necklace, Attractorb, and 6 Near Loss, if you're doing the Reports as you unlock them you know how this works - when near death he'll absorb mass amounts of EX Force and convert it into Bravery, then get that Bravery back as HP when you try to kill him. But this leaves him at 0 Bravery, so that extra HP won't go too far.
- The first Strange enemy is a Counterfeit Youth who begins will many thousand Bravery, summons Odin at the start of the fight, and uses Firaga.
- The second is a Delusory Knight, he begins with a couple thousand Bravery, he summons Tiamat and then uses Searchlight and Saint's Fall.

---

The Expert enemy is a Fallacious Tree, shit.

- He has the usual Exdeath moves including Reverse Polarity, Turn Guard, Omni Block and Delta Attack, two Hyper Rings with 6 Near Loss and 2 Near Death, an Iron Giant summon, Delusory Warlock Assist, and level 30 equipment.
- He's very tough and very good with Exdeath's blocks, Breaking him for KP isn't too hard since I had the Leviathan summon, but good luck getting out of this fight without taking HP damage. It eventually did it, but it was a lot of luck on my part.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The two chests have 26,000 gil and a Sun Blade, it's a level 30 weapon with lower power than the Crystal Sword, but you begin fights with 20% more Bravery. I'd stick with the Crystal Sword.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As we reach the end piece, Golbez appears before us with his usual greeting, a blast of lightning. He says to Cecil all he wants now is for his wish to be fulfilled, and if Cecil wants to find out what that is, he knows how to get it...seriously, why do these people want to fight us when they have no reason to oppose us anymore?

Golbez has all his attacks, a Delusory Knight Assist, Ramuh for a summon, and a full set of Level 30 equipment to give him great stats, though a level 1 helmet so his Bravery is lame. His accessories are an Earring with one each of the timer boosters, those are gonna hurt a lot late in the fight.

Not much to say here except have fun, you should know how Golbez fights backwards and forwards, he's just a bit smarter than previous fights against his manikins and he has stronger equipment. If you plan to use a summon use it early before Ramuh locks it, otherwise just beat him up.

</PanelSection>

#### Endless Chaos

The ten warriors, still fading now, hold onto their will and think about how Chaos must be despairing with the loss of Cosmos, but they can't let him just destroy the world to end his pain. They produce their Crystals and the portal to Chaos's world opens.

On the world map open the chest for a Skull Knight Shard, and head to the final gateway.

---

The path in this late gateway is entirely linear, no need to fight enemies unless you want to fill your EX Gauges for the final battle. You're also unable to use skills.

- Proceed down to the third floor, as we enter Chaos's lost memories return to him, causing him great anguish, but Shinryu tells him that that he knows the truth now, and lends him its power.
- At the third floor grab the Potion for an EX Gauge top-up, and approach the end piece.

You have your choice of which purple emblem to use, or none of them - Stage Bravery boost, Initial Bravery Boost, or Initial Bravery 0. And now, the final conflict!

Chaos's Bravery attacks I have to describe, the names won't mean anything to you:

- He's got Darkflame Fang, a basic three-hit aerial combo with fast start up and surprising range vertically and horizontally, but it can be blocked.
- He also has a ground combo, Darkflame Talon, and it too can be blocked, it works similarly to Garland's Round Edge as Chaos slashes and spins forward.
- If he flips into the air, he's using Earthwalker, sending a wave of fire along the ground to slam you backwards, dodge.
- If he pulls his arms back in the air, he's going to use his magical spin attack, Negashock, like Machine Gun or Knight's Blade block repeatedly to block it, or be hurt and wall rushed.
- When he calls out "Perish!", get into the air and ready to dodge, because he's using his diving dropkick The Fall, kicking up shards of dirt into the air on impact, and it crushes guards.
- Finally he has Darflame Blast, spitting four fireballs that move slowly and spread out, they can be blocked.

---

His HP attacks start out simple:

- Condemn has him create a pillar of flame around him and has very strong suction on start-up, dodge away quickly.
- Soul of Oblivion has him charge an orb of flame and fire it at you to do Bravery damage and wall rush you, hit him before he fires, otherwise it has long range and is impossible to avoid unless there's a huge vertical gap between you.
- Finally, he has Divine Punishment, he summons several fire pillars around you, then blasts you with fire from below and teleports above you and throws a flaming sword to slam you into the ground. It can be very difficult to avoid and does a lot of Bravery damage if you're up in the air.

When the attack box appears, stop moving to avoid the flame pillars, then once they appear to have stopped, dodge away to avoid the fire from below, which cannot be blocked, but can be cancelled out by some attacks like Grand Lethal and Braver. Terra's Tornado, if you're using her, cancels out the entire attack. After using it Chaos is very vulnerable if successfully dodged, so take advantage.

---

Chaos has the best summon in the game - Shinryu. Shinryu does various things between each round and essentially copies other summon effects. He can:

- like Ifrit increase Chaos's Bravery by half
- like Alexander freeze his Bravery
- like Behemoth double it at the cost of -60 every few seconds for some time
- like Pupu half it then give him +60 boosts
- like Omega half your Bravery every other second for a period of time
- like Ultima Weapon charge energy to instantly Break you
- like Iron Giant half your Bravery when he deals HP damage for a period of time
- like Tiamat activate a timer to triple his Bravery
- and like Lich activate a timer to sap your Bravery to 0

Phew! And for extra fun - Shinryu is never used up, he'll eventually recharge and the summonstone icon over Chaos's HP bar will reappear for another summon.

---

Chaos has no EX Mode or EX Gauge to fill, no Assist and no accessories, and custom equipment that gives him lower HP than Garland and the Emperor, but otherwise stats equal to them. The arena is a new one, the Edge of Madness, a very small arena with little room to move about, perfect for Chaos of course.

Be warned here, EX Cores appear only on the throne, and if not taken after being out, will just vanish into nothingness, taking with them all the EX Force they absorbed.

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Chaos has a lot of powerful Bravery attacks, they come out fast and are hard to predict and avoid if you're unfamiliar with him. Fortunately aside from Divine Punishment his HP attacks are easy to avoid.

Pummel him as best you can and don't be afraid to retry, he's quite powerful. Nothing else I can really say about him, his melee combos come out fast, do a lot of damage and can be blocked, but you have to know how to anticipate them. When he falls though, of course that's not it - it's time for Round 2!

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Yes kiddies, Chaos must be beaten three times to be put down, and similar to a party battle, during that three match run anyone who takes damage or dies, they don't get healed.

In his second form Chaos has two new attacks:

- A new Bravery attack, Onslaught, three teleporting uppercuts from portals in the ground, impossible to block but easy to dodge.
- And he'll use his Demonsdance HP attack, very dangerous, he erratically teleports around the arena, clawing, slashing and spinning to get you and wall rush you, dodge continuously and stay in the air, on the ground it's much harder to avoid.

And if he uses Shinryu's Bravery freeze, ho boy, you are so screwed because Demonsdance can do HP damage several times, so with frozen Bravery getting caught in it is a death sentence. Hope you have an Assist Change or EX Revenge ready! Otherwise it's the same as the first round.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

And now comes Round 3. He has a new HP attack - Scarlet Rain. Chaos will summon magma on the arena floor, damaging and stunning you so you have to stay in the air. He then sends a barrage of fireballs at you, block continuously against them and then dodge the last giant one.

However, while he has no other new attacks, the old ones are stronger:

- Negashock now has him teleport in front of you before attacking
- Earthwalker is evolved into Earthcrusher which fires three times
- Darkflame Blast staggers on block
- Darkflame Fang and Darkflame Talon create waves of fire for more range and damage
- and Divine Punishment summons larger flame pillars

---

And of course, he now has his Limit Break - Utter Chaos. I won't describe it because it is honestly one of the coolest limit breaks in the entire series and I won't spoil what it does. Here's the basics - it begins with a downward slash, then he attacks three more times, each time using a random one of three attacks:

- a forward stab that sends fire across the arena
- a downward stab that sends a fire plume at you
- and a swing that creates explosions

For the final blow, make sure you aren't standing on one of the glowing circles and get to safe ground quickly before they explode and you rake HP damage.

---

Not much else to say on this guy. He's pretty tough, fast attacks, they do a lot of damage, his HP attacks are tricky to avoid, and he has a Broken summon with variable effects.

Remember to Assist Change and EX Revenge out of his attacks, and that you have five characters, if one dies in comes the next one, so don't feel pressured to have one character beat all three forms, though of course it's possible. Also note that you only get one summon per character, so make it count. Use all you've learned to take out Chaos and save the world.

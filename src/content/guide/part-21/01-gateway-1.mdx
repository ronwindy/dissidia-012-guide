#### A Family Matter

Squall is leading the troupe now. As we enter the gateway, Tidus wonders if Jecht is waiting ahead too.

We then get a scene of him and the Emperor where the Emperor reveals to Jecht how he used to fight for Cosmos until the Emperor had his empty shell converted. Why? So that just as the other ten did, when Jecht proved his resolve and spirit fighting Tidus, a crystal was born, which the Emperor seized to have Cosmos's power for himself so he can survive Chaos's destruction and rule the world when everyone else has faded away.

Jecht takes off, fed up with his pompous posturing, to go find a proper fight.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Ho boy, fun line-up. Multichain at A4 where you enter to get them all.

If you lack one, go for a Jump Chain at C3 to get all but one of them and use the physical critical rate up emblem. The bonus line is 33, and the KP Chance is a 90 second win.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Phantasmal Girl with a Hyper Ring and three No BRV Damage boosters. She has the Moogle summon and uses Meltdown.
- Then comes a Fallacious Wanderer who has an initial Bravery boost, the Cactuar summon, and uses Ragnarok Blade.
- Then comes the Party piece, first up, a Counterfeit Wraith with Fusillade Particle Beam.
- Second is a Transient Witch with a Knight's Axe and Great Attractor, an unknown summon, and a Hyper Ring with 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value, giving it a pretty good damage buff at first so be quick to go on the offensive.
- The third one who you won't be fighting if you do Round Robin like I have - and I told you to and why are you on my walkthrough if you're going to ignore me? - is a Fallacious Tree with Hurricane, Sword Dance, Delta Attack, Pupu, and the same accessories as the Witch.

---

The Expert is a Delusory Warlock with the typical moveset and a new attack, Sector Ray.

- It sends out three attack drones that spread out and then fire continuous red lasers at you for a second, the lasers have limited range but together cover a decent arena near the orbs that are firing them.
- Also be aware the attack drones are solid and do not pass through obstacles, when they hit one they stop there but still fire, and in Kefka's Tower that can lead to some interesting and surprising firing patterns from them.

The Warlock otherwise uses the Bahamut summon and has two Hyper Rings with 6 Near Loss and 2 Near Death, and a mystery Assist. I confess I couldn't get the KP Chance here, he's just got too much Bravery thanks to Bahamut.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The two chests have 28,000 gil and a Crystal trade accessory. The Crystal unlocks the new Crystal equipment in the shop, but like Mythril we need 3 pieces and that's a ways off for us.

Also, my party is at Level 30 now, save for Squall at 29 but the next boss should fix that, so let's finally get to that Gold stuff! Buy:

- a Gold Bangle
- Gold Helm
- Gold Hairpin
- and Golden Vest

Everyone except Terra, Cecil and the Warrior of light can use one of these three equipment pieces. When three pieces of Gold equipment are worn, you get 50% more gil from fights! This is a huge help because trust me, in the bonus storyline, equipment gets very expensive very fast.

---

I also bought Level 30 weapons at this point:

- I got the Judicer's Staff for Terra
- and a Crystal Sword for everyone else

True we won't get three Crystal items for a while, but the raw Attack is nice for now.

If you want Terra to have the Gold stuff too, she can get the Golden Staff. I bought her a Black Belt's Gi for Level 30 armor since she can't equip the Golden Vest.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

At the end piece, Tidus finds Jecht, who admits he's not sure what he's doing there, but since he's here, let's fight.

Jecht has all his attacks with the Calcabrina summon, and a Muscle Belt with three Near Opponent and three Attacking Bravery. He's also got the highest power of any boss yet at 85 Attack.

He's also quite good at Jecht Block now, and your Bravery attacks are very likely to get blocked and punished. His Bravery damage output is impressive of course, and Calcabrina is annoying.

However, I didn't have too much trouble beating him, Jecht Block doesn't do much to punish Thunder Barret, and Squall was charging his Assist like mad with Fire and Thunder Barret to support his melee moves. He just has a lot of HP like all the bosses do, but I didn't have any trouble.

If your party leader is level 30 and decked out is new level 30 Gold stuff, which my Squall wasn't, you'll have an even easier time than I did thanks to the higher stat boosts. And yes, as predicted Squall hit level 30 from the fight, now everyone from now on has the Gold gear.

</PanelSection>

After the fight Jecht tosses out some really lame generic taunts at Tidus, which of course Tidus snaps back at him...I think. Jecht, pleased the kid isn't giving up so easily, laughs and walks away, fading away into pyreflies. I love how casual he is about it, "don't make me come out here again!"

Now the party continues on when the Warrior begins to fade again, but the Crystal's light restores him. We're running out of time. This scene also unlocks Report 16.

---

On the overworld map, for the final gateways Zidane is in the driver's seat again with Terra and Cloud as co-pilots.

- The moogle has crossed the lava stream to come root for us, buy a Multichain and a Cross Chain, or you can break the orbs and hope to get one for free, can't hurt.
- Terra, always the sweetheart, wonders about the moogles existence in this world and thinks about fluffing the one that came to follow us.
- Down the left path over the bridge are a BRV 0 and an Aged Turtle Shell.

Now we're finally onto the floating gateways, only three more stand between us and Chaos. And by process of elimination, guess who's waiting in them.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is one of the Strange enemies, an Imitation Liegeman, he enters with a full EX Gauge, the Mandragora summon, and uses Weaponsmaster. And he has an Iifa Leaf too, always annoying.
- Second is the other Strange foe, a False Hero. It has 0 Bravery and naturally uses Carbuncle, attacks with Dayflash and Shield of Light, and has a Guardian Bangle with 3 HP = 1 boosters. You've dealt with this build before and know how to handle it.
- Third is the Expert, a Phantasmal Harlequin with Lickity-Split Thundaga, Extra-Crispy Firaga, Meteor, Ultima, and both his Blizzaga moves, and of course all his HP attacks. He has full level 30 equipment, two Earrings with six Near Opponent and an After 45 Seconds, an Imitation Despot Assist, and Titan as a summon. He's actually quite easy to beat thanks to that blue emblem pumping up your attacks, and well, I'm using Zidane.

So let's go check the shop.

- The Sun Blade, with Narshe Ore (we get one in about 2 minutes) gets the Ancient Sword, even stronger than the Crystal Sword and with a +30% initial Bravery! Get it now!
- The exclusive weapons, you can get whichever one you like, I passed them up because I didn't need them, but the choice is yours.

Only some of them are really anything awesome compared to the other weapons available - I for example would rather give Zidane an Assassin's Dagger for +10% initial EX Force, as opposed to his Sargantas which has the same stats but a lame +2 Bravery boost when he dodges an attack.

<PanelSection>

As we reach the end, we confront the Emperor again. He reveals the Warriors of Chaos challenged the morals and resolve of the heroes on purpose, to manifest their Crystals precisely because they knew it would weaken Cosmos. All the challenges they've overcome were put in place *to* be overcome, as part of his plan. The warriors are of course outraged, and prepare to finally put the Emperor in his place.

The Emperor is at quite a higher level than previous bosses, and accordingly has much higher stats. He has all level 30 equipment, and some nice effects on them even. His accessories are an Earring and an Iifa Leaf with two HP = 1, two Near Death, and three Near Loss. He has all the moves you've come to hate, an Imitation Liegeman Assist, and the Atomos summon.

---

And I hate to say it kiddies, but I just had no trouble with him because Zidane is awesome and the AI is stupid. I kept him in the air thanks to Zidane's HP links and thus all he did for most of the fight was spam Light Crest, dodge towards him, Swift Attack.

A nice Assist combo I got here came thanks to Onion Knight - as Zidane is zapping him with Meo Twister, call the Onion Knight, he'll attack even as Meo Twister blasts the Emperor away, and the follow-up Bravery link will hit regardless of this, wall rushing the Emperor and leaving him open for another attack. Combine with how he'll Light Crest so often after Meo Twister and dodging and countering triggers an instant charge of the Assist gauge...yeah, he's not long for this world.

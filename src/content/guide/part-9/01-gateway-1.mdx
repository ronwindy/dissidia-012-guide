#### Gateway 1

Approach the gateway and you'll be informed of one of D012's dumbest mechanics, the Bonus Line. All gateways in all stories now have a bonus line, if you're over it you get a KP penalty at the start of negative KP (you don't lose KP for exiting with a negative penalty though). By contrast, lower your level to below the line to get a bonus to KP without fighting.

The dumb part comes in with the fact this is only here to encourage players who import their Dissidia data with level 100 characters to lower their levels to the bonus line to be challenged...except you don't *have* to, and later in the game you can earn thousands of KP at a time, so players may not even care.

On the other hand the bonus line applies regardless of data import, so if you're playing on a bonus day with an Exp bonus or are just good at level grinding and say, bring your Level 11 Firion to a Level 8 gateway, you're encouraged to set his level to 8...except while you lower your level this way you don't *increase* in level while fighting, so your Firion may gain enough Exp to get to Level 14, but he'll remain at Level 8.

And no, you can't change level in a gateway, and when you finish it you have to manually set your level back to normal, it's really tedious and annoying. I would have just added in an option so when you enter a gateway it asks "Change Level To Bonus Line? Yes/No", and it automatically changes your level or not to the bonus line, and then you include the third option to manually set level, and the level is changed only for that gateway. So much simpler and convenient.

There's another new sight here, though it doesn't carry over to replays of the 012 stories - Emblems. Emblems come in Red, Blue and Purple varieties and active when you fight while standing on them (enemies do not get their effects if they're positioned on an emblem).

- Blue emblems grant you various boosts, like stopping breakable accessories from breaking, boosting critical hit rate or restoring your HP before the fight.
- Red emblems inflict penalties, like negating your equipment, making certain attacks do no damage, or starting you with 0 Bravery.
- Purple emblems are "neutral" and will affect both or affect neither and do something else, like increasing the KP you win for the fight, raising the stage Bravery, or raising the initial Bravery of both characters.

# Chapter 5

Chapter 5, almost half-done the storyline!

#### The Ace

---

<CharacterIntro name="Tidus" src="drake/Dissidia_Tidus.png" width={150} height={230} alt="Dissidia Tidus">

Tidus has a heavily simplified story in Dissidia, but it's not his fault nor is it a bad thing. Tidus is consumed in his hatred for Jecht and wishes to defeat him, but his true reasons for this are buried. As we know from the last cycle, Tidus can't remember anything of his home yet except for Jecht, and his hatred for his father is all he has to drive him forward. And...that's it. Not much more to say there.

Tidus's story could have done with a bit more, for example with D012 expanding the story arc with the three FFX characters, maybe he begins to remember stuff and feels remorseful over being so malicious about Jecht? They could have done something more, the storyline works, but it's very two-dimensional.

Tidus is #4 of the characters I don't use. The reason is simple - his fighting style is to be a total pussy.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Tidus has six attacks in air and ground, Hop Step, a blitzball throw, and a slow charging attack. His gimmick lies in the other three attacks - Tidus dodges and counterattacks:

- leaping in the air to come down spinning
- flipping backwards and stabbing forward
- or dodging to the side and throwing his sword

I dislike this really cowardly, wimpy way to fight, and honestly it doesn't work as well as you might think reading how they work here, many times the opponent will recover and dodge away by the time Tidus attacks after dodging. And tt's impossible to be aggressive as Tidus because aside from Hop Step all his attacks come out ridiculously slow, and even when they do come out they got a huge damage nerf for D012.

Tidus begins with:

- Sonic Buster, a slow charging attack on the ground that drives the opponent down and away
- Dart \& Weave, where he dodges forward over the opponent's head and comes down spinning his sword into them
- Hop Step is a quick but weak back-off aerial attack, and it's the only thing that makes Tidus less than totally unplayable since he could try some mind games, dash up to them and do Full Slide but they block early expecting Hop Step and now they're eating a Full Slide for being surprised
- Wither Shot sucks, Tidus easily has the worst obligatory projectile attacks of the melee fighters

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attacks are equally slow and easy to avoid once you get the timing down. The only advantages Tidus really has going for him are high movement speed, and his attacks generate a lot of EX Force.

For HP attacks he has:

- Spiral Cut, a series of flips across the ground that is ridiculously easy to avoid by getting into the air
- Energy Rain, which is a kick at the opponent and a leap back to send energy blasts at them

</PanelSection>

Much like Cecil Tidus is capable in his own story, since his slow attacks are meaningless against the dipshit AI, but really, he's pretty terrible. Ironically given his movement speed and EX Mode, his main weakness are slow attacks and in my opinion they aren't very strong either.

</CharacterIntro>

The bonus line is 5 and the KP Chance is a 90 second win, especially easy if you use Soundwave. There's a boss at the end to try and get that EX Gauge up.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Ah, the perfect place for that KP Gambler we got in the last chapter! Multichain at D3 with the KP Gambler to chain all five enemies and get a nice 75 KP. Use that Soundwave skill if you like, might as well, it's just gonna take up space in your skill menu better used for a Charge skill.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up:

- First up is a Fleeting Flash with Launch, Smite and Aerora.
- Next a Capricious Thief with Scoop Art and Strom Impulse, the aerial forward spin attack.
- Now the three Hard enemies, a Capricious Thief with Scoop Art and Solution 9, the Hyper Ring set-up, and Cagnazzo.
- Next, an Ephemeral Phantom with Jecht's ground combo, aerial Jecht Block, the Hyper Ring, and Scarmiglione.
- Last is an Ephemeral Vision with Sphere Shot, Hop Step, that Hyper Ring, and Cagnazzo.

Soundwave makes already simple and easy fights even simpler and easier here.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The summonstone is the auto version of Leviathan, as you know from seeing enemies use it, it summons automatically when the opponent Breaks you and saps their Bravery by increments of 50 every other second or so for a period of time. It's useless, the only time opponents will be Breaking you is in Confessions of the Creator, by which time we can get the manual version of Leviathan which drains more Bravery than the auto version.

</PanelSection>

As we approach the end piece, Tidus tells Firion to go on ahead, for some really dumb reason. So of course we're without an Assist now, change boosters to Empty Assist Gauge appropriately.

This is a continuity snarl from the original Dissidia, where they actually split up before this gateway, so here it made sense for Tidus to call out for Firion. Jecht arrives to fight, but the Emperor appears and tells Jecht that it's too early for the final boss now, so Jecht takes off while the Emperor gives Tidus a way to vent in liu of the old man.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Normal enemies are pansies as always, but the Hard enemies continue the trend of being a little more difficult to deal with. They seem smarter to me, dodging and countering swiftly now and then.

Enemy line-up:

- First, an Ephemeral Phantom, it has the ground combo Jecht Rush and the midair Jecht Block, but is pretty easy.
- Second is an Ephemeral Vision, it has Sonic Buster and Wither Shot.
- The third enemy is also an Ephemeral Vision with the same moveset, but he has the Scarmiglione summon, it saps your Bravery to 0 instantly, but only activates when you call a summon of your own. He also has the accessory gimmick of this storyline, a Hyper Ring and 6 Near Opponent boosters, simple but effective.
- The last opponent is a Counterfeit Youth with Blizzard, Blizzaga, Thunder and Thundaga.

</PanelSection>

#### Exploration

As we exit onto the world map, turn around and fight the Fallacious Tree right behind you, easy pickings.

- To the left near the sealed gate is a Rosetta Stone, and there's that same moogle again.
- There's two more chests, near the other sealed gate beyond the moogle and opposite in on the island with the stone grind beam, the two have 2000 gil and 2001 gil.
- Back on the main path as last time there's another Tree on the shore to take out, and beyond it another grind bar to the island with a Patience Incense.
- Back down it is a sealed gateway with a new skill, Soundwave, it halves the levels of enemies in the chain, pretty good.

Buy a Multichain from the moogle and head to the next gateway.

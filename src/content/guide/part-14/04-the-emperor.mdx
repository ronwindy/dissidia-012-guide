<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The difficulty of this battle is directly proportional to if you charged up your EX Gauge or not before coming here.

The Emperor has all the same tricks as before, but this time you're in his domain, the wide-open spaces of Dream's End and the Empyreal Paradox are gone. Here in Pandaemonium his spells take up half the hallways, his projectiles bounce like mad and interrupt your attacks, and in general the tight confines make it hard to avoid his traps.

He's got five Blue Gems boosting his Base Bravery to 550 by default, and factor in he's probably got at *least* a dozen levels on you, maybe more, and he can blast you into hell with only a few attacks while also being very hard to Break.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Remember to use Energy Rain to deflect Flare when you can, but avoid Spiral Cut since it has you move on start-up so if you mistime it Tidus will jump right into the flame and get hit.

His summon Atomos sucks your Bravery out and gives it to him when you land an HP hit, but he seemed picky about using it and didn't do so until late in the fight when it didn't make much difference.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

The reason the fight's difficulty depends on how much you built up your EX Gauge is because of Tidus's EX Mode bonus. It's the same as Cloud's bonus, he wields the Caladbolg and gets its benefit of doing up to twice as much damage as usual depending on his HP, along with the critical hit rate going up.

Without EX Mode you're going to have to whittle the Emperor down, barring your Cactuar summon doing it for you one time, and he will do a lot of damage to you with ease. But with EX Mode a single Full Slide or Sonic Buster will Break him, and you can quickly rack up the Bravery needed to finish him off.

---

As for Tidus's other EX Mode benefits, let's take care of them while on the topic, he runs faster and deflects low-priority projectiles while running, and his dodging attacks make him invincible longer while dodging.

His EX Burst isn't too hard, stop the cursor as close to the center as you can, you have enough time to let it make a couple passes while you pin down the timing of the cursor movement. Don't underestimate its damage just because the first few hits are weak, my Level 5 Tidus did 600 Bravery damage on the last hit alone, the Burst's power is all in that final hit so it is crucial you nail it right.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

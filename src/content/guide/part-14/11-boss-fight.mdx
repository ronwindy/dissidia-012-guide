Jecht suffers the same problem as Golbez did in the last chapter - compared to the midboss, he isn't at a much higher level or have much better gear, while you are at least half a dozen levels stronger. As a result he has a few hundred more HP but a few hundred less Bravery, and at this point at least kiddies, Bravery is the more dangerous stat for an opponent to load up on.

Jecht has a full-move set of all his attacks, and Jecht Block in both ground and aerial flavors. Fortunately the AI is too dumb to use it correctly, if it was then Jecht would be nearly untouchable thanks to Tidus's slow attacks. However, it will do it occasionally and you'll eat a Jecht Combo to the face.

Jecht prefers his combos to his HP attacks in my experience, and he's got 3 Near Opponent boosters and 2 Attacking Bravery, giving him around a 2.4x booster to his Muscle Belt. His Calcabrina summon will remove your Break when he inflicts it, sapping you to 0 Bravery and a sitting duck, so land an HP hit to restore it. His Assist is an Ephemeral Summoner, aka Yuna.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

If he goes to charge up a combo, jump away and use Slice \& Dice to smack him while he's helpless.

Jecht, sadly, is a total pushover. An opening Sonic Buster followed by Hop Step Broke him on my game and the HP attack wall rushed him, and the follow-up EX Burst did 1800 damage with a second Break. As I said, he's just not much stronger than the Emperor and is less powerful comparatively to us than the Emperor was at that time.

</PanelSection>

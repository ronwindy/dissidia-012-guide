<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain at D4 to get them all, or you can just take the Counterfeit Wraith at B6 if you're ready to just get it over with. You could also tackle the Strange enemy at D2, but then you can't reach the chest, the game sticks Firion in the way. Get used to that, later maps *especially* will stick your Assist in a specific tile to deny you access to areas, and in Confessions of the Creator that blockade takes on new meaning.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Strange enemy is a Capricious Thief with a bit of an odd set-up. It comes in with several thousand Bravery and uses Tidal Flame, but its summon is Leviathan, which is odd because you would think they'd give him a set-up to Break you if they were going for Leviathan, but whatever. You know how to deflect Tidal Flame by now I'm sure, do it and watch him die as his own fireballs explode in his face.

The two normal enemies are:

- an Ephemeral Phantom with Jecht Rush and Jecht Blade
- an Ephemeral Summoner with Energy Ray

The Expert enemy is a Counterfeit Wraith with the typical Cloud of Darkness moveset you should recognize by now. She's got the same pair of Hyper Rings with the boosters the last Expert had, 6 Near Loss and 2 Dear Death, and has Alexander.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

As you should be used to by now, charge up your EX Gauge before facing her and pummel her with Tidus's pumped up damage as much as you can before going for the Burst. Firion's aerial Assist, which tosses out three Fires, is a good compliment for Energy Rain, time it so you kick as she blocks the Fire.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

At Level 12 Tidus learns Stick \& Move, another dodge attack where he dodges to the side and throws his sword forward.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

By the way, with all the Ephemeral Phantoms in this story, you can get what you need for a Silver Hourglass, if you want one. The three core EX Accessories - Attractorb, Pearl Necklace, Silver Hourglass - you can get the Necklace and Hourglass, the Orb sadly needs a Battlegen item associated with the new arenas in the 13th cycle like the Phantom Train and Prima Vista, and we can't get those yet.

And I don't count the Dragonfly Orb as one of the core EX Accessories because you don't want to be waiting for an EX Core to get usage out of it, go with the Necklace.

</PanelSection>

#### Dream's End

Conveniently a moogle has followed us here, grab a Straight Chain, and a Jump Chain and Multichain for the road.

- Head to the right and shatter the rock to reach the island and grab a Pink Tail.
- There's a False Stalwart to handle nearby, Round Edge is always a bitch to dodge on the overworld map but it doesn't matter, anything with three Cursed Rings on isn't long for this world any way you slice it.
- Grab the Cure skill beyond it, then go back and head up into the Volcano for the third time in five chapters.

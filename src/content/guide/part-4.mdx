# Chapter 4

**Chapter 4** - And now for a more personal story arc.

#### Stay Away From The Summoner!

Another new character.

---

<CharacterIntro name="Yuna" src="drake/Yuna.png" width={72} height={250}>

Howdy **Yuna**, what's shaking?. **Yuna** isn't my favorite character, but she's certainly not on my list of dislikes.

- She's got a good character arc in her base game, isn't too annoying (though that may be because **Wakka** raised the bar for shithead-ery), and is a **Summoner**, and Summoners are up there as one of my fav character classes.
- Sure, she's no Rydia or Garnet, but them's some tough competition for anyone.
- In this game among the six heroes, **Yuna** is pretty bland though, between **Laguna** and **Vaan** being comic relief and **Kain** and **Lightning** being stoic badasses, **Yuna** is just sort of there.

In this chapter she gets a much more personal quest than the rest of the group, and in terms of the singular stories of the chapters, I would say it has the best one, and connects very nicely to the 13th cycle. But that's for another part in the future.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

In battle, **Yuna** doesn't fight herself, she calls **Aeons** to attack for her. And to my great disappointment, it is nowhere near as badass as I'd expect from a **Summoner**.

**Yuna** has only six **Bravery** attacks, and they're divided up the same way for ground and air:

- close-range melee attack
- charging mid-range attack
- long-range projectile attack

I find thus that she's very boring to play as, her moves are good for sure, they pack a fair punch, especially in **EX Mode**, but when there's only four worth using since the projectile attacks suck, it doesn't make for engaging and fun combat.

And since she's just starting out she won't learn those charging attacks for several levels, making fighting as her even more boring because she now has basically only two attacks.

She does have one combo using the **Holy** glitch:

- Fire off a charge **Impulse**, and if she recovers before it hits,
- Time your usage of **Heavenly Strike** as the opponent is hit by **Impulse**,
- And even though you didn't hit with **Heavenly Strike**, the game will let you combo it due to the glitch since **Impulse** hit.

You can practice this throughout the story if you like.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

Her HP attacks are better varied, **Yuna** is an AI Breaker.

- The computer opponents even in the hardest areas of the game seem incapable of dodging **Energy Ray**.
- **Thor's Hammer** and **Hellfire** are nice close-range moves with good radius.
- Eventually **Yuna** gets Mega **Flare** which breaks the AI even harder.

And...that's it. **Yuna** only has two moves worth using until she gets **Sonic Wings** at **Level 12**, so run up to an enemy, slam them with **Meteor Strike** or **Heavenly Strike** until you can kill them, then fire off an **Energy Ray** and do so.

Nothing else to say, she really can't do anything else yet, and even later there's not much more to it. I have no idea why they made her so limited, **FFX** had plenty of **Aeons** and that means **Yuna** has plenty of potential attacks since she uses the **Aeon**'s moves, so where are Yojimbo and Anima?

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

<PanelSection kind="assist">

**Jecht** is our Assist and he's one of the best Assists in the game.

- His aerial Assist does high damage and slams the opponent into the ground, and it's long enough that if you're close-enough to the opponent when he starts it up, you can hit with **Heavenly Strike** and recover in time to use **Thor's Hammer** on the enemy before they recover.
- His ground Assist is less useful since it slams the opponent up into the air, but a well-timed **Heavenly Strike** will drive them back down,and depending on the arena they may not be so high as to be unreachable.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Also, for most of this storyline we have a lot of new opponents! Let's do a rundown.

- The **Ephemeral Phantom** is **Jecht**'s **Manikin**, it uses **Jecht**'s ground combo attack **Jecht Rush**, the midair **Jecht** Blade which is a charging series of sword swings, and **Jecht** Block which deflects all moves. **Yuna** will stagger if he deflects **Meteor Strike**, but her other moves either cause no stagger or stagger him too, so rely on aerial strikes to fight him.
- There's an **Imitation Despot** which you'll recognize from a **Report**, they have **Land Mine** which puts so mines on the ground, **Meteor Strike** will dispel them, and **Light Crest** which creates an energy field that fires projectiles that bounce off obstacles when you get close. HP is the aerial **Flare**.
- The **Ephemeral Summoner** has the same **Meteor Strike** and **Heavenly Strike** as you do, but a **Diamond Dust** HP attack in the air which has shorter range than **Thor's Hammer** but executes a lot faster and is hard to anticipate, so be on your toes or only strike when she's grounded.
- And then there's a **Delusory** Dragon....you know them by now I'm sure.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

- If you got lucky enough in Chapter 2 to get some **Girl's Dreams**, the **Silver Hourglass** which increases **EX Mode** Duration by 5% needs three **Girl's Dreams**, three **Phantom's Dreams**, and three **Blackcrystal Slivers**, we've got plenty of the last one, since it seems every other battle so far has been in the **Chaos Shrine**.

There's nothing else of note though, still a lot of items needing **Battlegen** stuff we can't get yet.

The chests have:

- The **Cotton Robes** and **Hairband** for equipment

#### The Pilgrimage

On the world map we start on a beach (nice mythology gag) and the path forward out into the marshlands is linear.

- As you come out and grab the second orb of the dead, make a left to a chest with an **EX Charge** and a **Counterfeit Wraith** to fight, she has **Anti-Air Particle Beam**.
- Continue across the marsh to a ruined castle with a **Moogle** shop.

---

On my game I had 195 **KP**, *almost* enough to get my hands on those coveted 200 **KP** items! If you have enough now, maybe it's your **Bonus Day** and you've got a discount or had a **Chain Skill** I lacked and didn't have to buy one, if you have 200 **KP** and feel the natural urge to splurge, buy the **Icebrand**, as Swords are one the most versatile equipment types, usable by a good portion of the hero characters, and the **Icebrand** is the most powerful sword we can use until we get to **Level 30**.

**Vaan**, **Laguna**, **Lightning** and **Kain** use Swords, so this weapon will see more than its share of usable throughout the rest of the game, probably going to end up being used by all my characters throughout the 013 story except for **Terra** since she can't use swords (I don't know why, she could in FF6, but that's not something we need to get into here).

---

For the other items available, none of them are must-buy items, but **Empty Assist Gauge** would be the best if you must, since the other four have fluctuating activation requirements, EAG, you'll always know when you have no Assist and thus the gauge will always be empty during that time.

As an edit note, while writing this walkthrough I did not buy EAG and later wished I had, so yes, buy it, learn from my mistakes.

- Summons are all a waste of **KP** at this point.
- Buy a **Multichain** for down the road, we may get one from the orbs of the dead but it's unlikely.
- The stone produces a grind bar leading to a **Rosetta Stone**.

This gateway is one of the precious few times a **Round Chain** has any use, and I happened to get one from the orbs on my way here.

You can use a **Multichain** at C4 if you want, you'll make 16 **KP** for the chain and can go but another **Multichain** when you're done, but I was cheap and decided the **Round Chain** would lead to larger net winnings, being that it was free and didn't need replacing.

I used it at C4 to chain the three, then manually chained the last two.

<PanelSection kind="assist">

**Zidane** is our Assist.

- His ground attack is **Rumble Rush** and is a combo that launches the opponent into the air.
- His aerial attack is **Vortex** which much like **Jecht**'s ground assist slams the opponent up and probably out of reach.

**Heavenly Strike** slams the opponent down so stick to the ground Assist for **Zidane**.

You'll also find a new arena here, **Dream's End**, which **FFX** fan will recognize easily I should hope. It's a big wide-open arena with a banish trap pit on most of it, you can fight on the central large platform or the outlying grandstands, and railings along the outside let you quickmove between the two.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up, the **Ephemeral Phantom**, **Ephemeral Summoner** and **Fleeting Flash** are familiar.

The **Imitation Despot** is a problem though, he's got **Thunder Crest** and Dyanmite, remember them from the **Report**? Except his HP attack this time is the ground **Flare**, which sends out a large fireball that slowly tracks us around the stage for a long long time, making it a constant danger, and if you get caught in one of his **Bravery** attacks that stuns you, there's a good chance **Flare** will come around and explode in your face as you writhe trapped. Don't let this happen.

Other characters can deflect it to track and hurt him like **Laguna**'s **Ragnarok Blade** or **Lightning**'s **Crushing Blow**. **Yuna** has no such attacks to do this yet, sadly.

There's another new enemy, the **False Hero**:

- He has **Dayflash** on the ground, a quick three-hit combo.
- And **Rising Buckler** in the air which is an upwards-swooping attack that hurts a lot, listen for his cry to block it if you're above him.
- His HP attack is **Shield of Light**, he blocks and fires a blast of light at short-range, but our HP attacks will crush it and **Heavenly Strike** won't stagger us for getting blocked.

The only trick here is the enemies have summons.

- The Flash has **Odin**, summoned when you **Break** her, and it might **Break** you back.
- The Despot has **Bahamut** for periodic boosts of 75 **Bravery**.
- And the Phantom has **Demon Wall** to protect his **Bravery** from damage when he gets low on it, fortunately you'll have no trouble building up **Bravery** to kill him without needing to **Break** him.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

With the first round of enemies dead, the chest has an **Oak Staff**, useless for **Yuna** with the **Capella** on her belt, and the **Ramuh** summonstone.

**Ramuh** summons himself automatically when we get Broken (and if you're getting Broken at this stage of the game, you're doing it wrong), and seals the opponent's summonstone (not that enemies at this stage of the game really use them too often, and again, if the ones that do are Breaking you, you're doing it wrong). So yeah he's pretty useless.

All automatic summons have manual versions, and the difference is usually the automatic ones have more charges and recharge faster while the manual ones let you decide when to use them, and which of the two is stronger depends on the exact summon, but **Ramuh** is one of the ones that you never want the automatic version for.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Well as the choice of boss music will remind us, **Yuna** has experience taking out smug fashion victim mages who won't stay dead.

You've already seen most of the **Emperor**'s moves before with the **Imitation Despots**, but he's smarter about using them - if he snares you in a **Thunder Crest**, he will take advantage of your vulnerability. To refresh your memory further:

- **Dynamite** fires an orb that sticks to obstacles and draws you in to stun you.
- **Thunder Crest** creates a lightning field on the ground to do the same.
- **Landmine** and **Mine** toss out exploding orbs of lightning that can be dispelled by dashing through them.
- **Light Crest** creates a field that shoots projectiles when you get close.
- His new **Bravery** attack **Bombard** is a close-range staff smack that knocks you back, but you should never be close enough when he's not being hit himself that it can hit you.

---

He has both versions of **Flare**, the ground one that follows you and the midair one that stays in place. There's a way to deal with the blue **Flare** now, lure it into tracking you through a floor or wall, like the giant sword in the middle of the stage or the railings along the edges, and it'll explode when it hits the obstacle. The explosion can still hit you though so stand clear.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

His new HP move is **Starfall**.

When he begins to charge it up it has one of the largest charge times in the game, several seconds, but when it goes off you're screwed, a giant meteor is going to slam down on your head preceded by fireballs, and unless you can quickmove away, you are not going to be avoiding it.

When charging he's immune to projectile attacks, and unfortunately, **Heavenly Strike**, strictly speaking, has the same damage priority as a projectile, so the only way to break through his defenses and stop him when he begins **Starfall** is to use **Thor's Hammer** or an **EX Mode** **Heavenly Strike**, which also calls **Ifrit** to do a different type of damage that will hit fine.

And to wrap up this description:

- He has the **Giant of Babil** summon, which saps 30% of your **Bravery** and his and adds it to the stage pool.
- And he has accessories for a 10% buff to his magic damage when he's far enough away from you.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

With his high defense (for this point), your **Bravery** attacks won't do as much damage as you're use to, so use **Yuna**'s **EX Mode** buffs, or **Heavenly Strike** to knock him down in the banish trap over and over and whittle him down.

He can be surprisingly tough, with that HP he can endure two **Break**-boosted HP attacks and keep coming, and his high level means he'll have a base **Bravery** boost to over 400 points, so **Yuna**'s 2000 or so HP will melt off pretty quick, especially since his aerial **Flare** inflicts wall rush.

His weakness is fortunately he isn't *that* smart about using his traps, just smarter than the **Manikins**, if he played like a human player - that is, **Thunder Crest** and **Flare** the place up constantly to keep you from getting close to him - he'd be a real challenge. He still is, for a new player at least, but only because his higher level gives him a power bonus.

You can win as long as you aren't stupid and don't blunder into his traps too much.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

#### Kefka

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Kefka doesn't have quite as much attack strength as Sephiroth, but has higher defense. He's got an Earring with 6 Far From Opponent, ouch, a Bomb for a summon and a Phantasmal Girl Assist. His moveset is the two Thundaga attacks, Scatter-Spray Blizzaga, Extra-Crispy Firaga, and Meteor, with all his HP hits.

The stage is Kefka's Tower Ω, here every time a part of the stage is destroyed, "magitek steam" will blast out of the walls in the lower parts of the factory, damaging the Bravery of fighters that are hit and increasing the Bravery pool. This can also occur when wall rushing them in the lower parts, and can lead to a very amusing "pinball" effect of knocking them into a wall and then they bounce back and forth for a second between vents of steam.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Kefka's attacks pack a serious wallop, even without those boosters activated, and are always tricky to avoid, especially Scatter-Spray Blizzaga. However, as you may remember from her chapter in her EX Mode Terra becomes a goddess of magical carnage, and entering battle with a full EX Gauge I had little trouble taking Kefka down with her power boost - just remember to enter Chases to keep the gauge filled!

In the aftermath of the fight, Kefka is still yearning for destruction, and laments that hope and dreams aren't enough to make a fulfilling life, and explodes in a sad laugh as he calls out for yet more destruction. Terra wonders if that was why Kefka destroyed, and continues on.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

#### Kuja

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Kuja's offense is only equal to the Cloud of Darknesses, but he's got the highest defense yet, and as with all previous bosses a few hundred more HP over the last one. His boosters are pretty pathetic though, a Pearl Necklace, Attractorb and Earring with three Aerial boosters, that's only a 3.3x booster, we have more than that! Give him at least five or so.

For moves he's got Ring Holy, Remote Flare, Burst Energy, Strike Energy, Snatch Shot, Flare Star, Seraphic Star, Force Symphony, and Ultima - basically, all the moves he's ever had. His summon is Magic Pot and he's got a Capricious Thief Assist. Also, they got it right this time and use the proper boss music they should have had in Chapter 8.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

I decided not to use Cloud's EX Mode and fought normally, and since I had no HP links yet, it was a rough ride. Kuja's attacks are always annoying to avoid, especially his HP attacks, and he was good about charging and using his Capricious Thief Assist. Rising Fang was my main offense since Cloud has trouble striking from above and being in front of Kuja is about the worst place you can be.

True, being below him is never a good idea...god I can write innuendo so well ;p, but Rising Fang is a decently quick response, even striking Kuja during his Force Symphony conducting. So overall, he's pretty tough without an EX Gauge to help you out. With one?

Crush him, Cloud's EX Mode bonus will blast his Bravery into oblivion and can clinch the match by knocking off half his HP with ease.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

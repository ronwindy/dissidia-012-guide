#### Ultimecia

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Ultimecia naturally has all previous moves you've dealt with, and a new one, Hell's Judgment, it creates a crest of magic below you and blasts you up into the air. For accessories she's got an Earring with 2 HP = 1, 2 Near Death, 3 Near Loss, and an Iifa Leaf, giving her an impressive boost to damage on the bring of death. She also uses the Marilith summon, has a Transient Lion Assist, and has the high attack power of Kefka and Sephiroth with the high defense of Kuja, giving her the best stats yet.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

However, she is, as always, an idiot. At one point with Firion I just spammed Straightarrow - yes she dodged it sometimes, but it also hit far too often, because she would respond by using an attack of her own, making her unable to dodge. I also had great ease catching her with Reel Axe to Bravery pain, and in general she was just really bad at avoiding Firion's attacks.

And you've used him, you know it is not difficult to avoid Firion's attacks, *especially* Straightarrow. Even that aside, I could just camp and use Shield Bash over and over, all of her uncharged attacks - which are mostly all I found her using - trigger it. Also have fun throwing a Reel Axe at her during a charged Knight's Blade, the Axe has higher damage priority and will deflect the blades as it flies towards her.

The only complications were Marilith and the Iifa Leaf, but they only work once and are just nuisances.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

As Ultimecia stands weakened and vanishing, she philosophizes that time will always slip through your grasp and the happiness of the moment is fleeting - all she wants is to take hold of that. Squall then strikes her down before she can continue, and says he can't let anyone take his time from him. So once again, the game portrays Squall as a callous, self-righteous prick.

---

On the world map, head to the next gateway, collecting the orbs of the dead and a Shockwave from the chest, a new skill that reduces enemies to half their normal levels in chains.

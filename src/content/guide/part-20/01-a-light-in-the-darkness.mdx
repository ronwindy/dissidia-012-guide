#### A Light in the Darkness

With Cosmos dead, the ten warriors fade away one by one, Kefka taunting it's their fault she was killed. As they fall into darkness however, the Crystals light up the area and they find themselves back in Order's Sanctuary. They're on borrowed time, but they have to track down the Chaos Warriors, find out what happened to Cosmos, and take out Chaos!

As you should be used to, there are gonna be Party pieces to contend with in the epilogue. To refresh your memory, in Tournament the party leaders fight, whoever dies is replaced with the next person in line, and so on until one party is entirely beaten. Round-Robin, party leaders fight, then second in line fight, until one party has the majority win (two wins against a three-enemy party).

I'll be using Round Robin for all my fights, but if you like Tournament you can use Tournament.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

As we enter battle, we get notified of the Omega arenas. All arenas in this last chapter are the Omega versions, I'll explain what they do as they come up. The first foe is a Fallacious Tree with Sword Dance and Hurricane.

He's fought in World of Darkness Ω, here wisps of darkness appear to signal an arena shift, if you're near the opponent the arena becomes a very small closed-in room dominated by pillars, if you're far away the arena becomes a huge-wide open arena of platforms connected by grind bars. In both, the Bravery pool rises rapidly depending on distance, in the close arena it rises faster the closer you are, and vice-versa for the open arena.

- Second is a Delusory Warlock with Rising Wave, Gravity System, Nightglow, the Magus Sisters summon to halve your Bravery when you Break him and a Hyper Ring with 8 HP = 100% 1.5x boosters, and a No BRV Damage 3x booster.

Naturally priority goes to turning them off fast. Contrary to previous chapters, enemies switch up accessories throughout the journey rather than all of them using one set-up. The Warlock is fought in Lunar Subterrane Ω, pretty lame gimmick, the cliffs now boost the stage Bravery when destroyed.

- The last foe is an Imaginary Champion with Godspeed, Shadow Flare, Black Materia, Shiva for a summon, the Hyper Ring set-up, and is fought in Empyreal Paradox Ω.

Here there are wisps of light near the Crystal that slowly drain the Bravery of nearby fighters and transfer the drained Bravery to the stage pool. Quite helpful in Confessions of the Creator since it helps you whittle down the Bravery of high-level foes.

The last two enemies, if you must fight them, are a Counterfeit Wraith in the Empyreal Paradox with Tentacle of Spite (large energy orb blast), Anti-Air Particle Beam, the Hyper Ring stuff, and Ultros. The other is an Ephmeral Phantom with Jecht Rush, Jecht Block, and is fought in Chaos Shrine Ω. Here, both characters are regularly drained of a percentage of their Bravery and have it added to the Bravery pool.

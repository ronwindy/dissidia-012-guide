#### Exploration

Back on the world map, Cloud is gonna be the party leader for the next couple of gateways. I also swapped Firion and Squall up to the second and third slots, as they haven't been getting Exp from Party pieces and so they and Cloud are a few levels behind Zidane and Terra. Plus it helps keep things fresh, and while the non-fighters get exp, they don't get AP, so let's spread the love.

---

Goi around the beach to where the teleport stone used to be, the chest has a Treant Branch. Shatter the rock and grind over to find a Silver Lobo Claw. As a note, the party members here will note the lack of enemies outside the gateways, wondering if the destruction of the world has destroyed them, and note the teleport stone is gone.

Approach the blue gateway and Onion Knight will sense an old friend inside. If you need a Straight Chain, go back and grab one from the moogle, and grab a Cross Chain while you're over there too.

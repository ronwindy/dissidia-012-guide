#### Cloud of Darkness

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Cloud of Darkness is actually only slightly stronger than Exdeath, with a lower level and attack and defense on par or worse than Kefka's. Her accessories are decent, a Pearl Necklace and an Attractorb Orb with the 15, 30, 45, 60 and 90 Second boosters, one each. She has the Land Worm summon, all four tentacle attacks, and a few new HP attacks.

Feint Particle Beam, only on the ground, has her vanish into a dark portal to dodge attacks then reappear somewhere else to fire an orb of darkness as you, fairly easy to avoid, and Wrath Particle Beam is an aerial move where she blocks and summons an orb of darkness to strike you. It's not impervious to all attacks like Omni Block, but can block anything with lower priority than High, which mostly means any non-HP attacks.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

She's pretty simple to beat, unless you find yourself tripped up by her new HP attacks that is. All her Bravery attacks can be blocked and punished, and Land Worm's drain is only a nuisance, as long as you stay close to her it won't do much, and you want to be close so she's more likely to do a Bravery attack and you can block and punish her. And since I was using Cloud, my EX Mode gave me free reign to go nuts on her since all of Cloud's attacks break or stagger her on a conflict between attacks.

With the Cloud of Darkness weakened, she tells the Onion Knight that there is no point to trying anymore, but the Onion Knight has to listen to his heart over his head. The Cloud of Darkness fades away into a portal of darkness, returning to her slumber.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

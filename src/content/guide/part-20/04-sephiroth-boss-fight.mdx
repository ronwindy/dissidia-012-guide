#### Sephiroth

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Sephiroth too has level 30 equipment, he has a significantly greater attack strength than Exdeath, but significantly lower defenses. He's got over 4,200 HP and a Muscle Belt hooked up to 6 Attacking Bravery boosters, ouch. He has the Alexander summon and an Imaginary Soldier Assist.

His moveset is the usual - Reaper, Fervent Blow, Transience, Oblivion, Godspeed, Shadow Flare. For HP attacks, Hell's Gate, Octaslash, Scintilla - I didn't see them but assume Black Materia and Heaven's Light are there too. The stage, Planet's Core Ω, is one of two stages that I consider the worst in the game.

After a period of time the stage will shake and shift form. The floating rocks vanish and more tendrils of lifestream to quickmove on appear. The only platforms now aside from the large central one at the bottom are a handful of floating pillars that crumble when dashed into - and of course EX Cores will appear on top of them and vanish when you dash into the pillar trying to get to them.

This is annoying enough, the lack of solid ground, but what makes the stage terrible is the AI *loves* to quickmove along the grindbars and up the walls endlessly for no reason, forcing you to track them down. You'll see soon enough. For a fun twist though, try going into an EX Burst during the stage shift, the EX Burst background will be lit up!

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Sephiroth is overall a pushover, he has a heavy defense penalty compared to Exdeath and Zidane quickly sapped his HP away with his Tempest and Swift Attack HP links. True his own damage output is also pretty impressive, but you know how (not) aggressive the AI is, and it won't take advantage of this. As for damage output - Zidane's EX Burst did 1000 damage, plus the Bravery pool was higher due to the stage shifting.

All in all, once you get Sephiroth to half HP, as long as his Bravery isn't in Break status so you can re-Break him, an EX Burst can kill him. Beaten, Sephiroth stands up...somehow in front of Cloud again, and declares he doesn't exist...okay. Cloud rejects his taunts again and Sephiroth claims his shadow is burned into Cloud's heart and that they will meet again.

Did a yaoi fangirl write this dialogue?

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

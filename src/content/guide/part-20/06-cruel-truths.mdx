#### Cruel Truths

As we enter, the Emperor appears before us. The party is surprised to see him alive since they thought Firion beat him. How could anyone tell, the villains almost always fade away when beaten but yet they come back for more later.

The Emperor is looking to rule this world, and declares that with Chaos's victory, the Warriors of Chaos are free to pursue their own desires outside the war. He vanishes with a laughing, and the Warriors continue on.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is a Transient Witch with Knight's Blade, Knight's Arrow, Great Attractor, the Carbuncle summon, and the accessory gimmick of this gateway, it should seem familiar - a Zephyr Cloak, Heart's Ease, Iifa Dew, and 7 Near Loss, giving them a Bravery boost whenever they block or dodge an attack once you have enough Bravery to kill them.

The Dew is different from the Leaf, you don't survive with 1 HP, your Bravery drains to 0 and you get as much HP as Bravery lost. It doesn't work if they're in Break though, keep that in mind.

- Next comes a Phantasmal Harlequin with the same accessories, Havoc Wing, and Demon Wall.
- The third foe, which you don't have to fight in Round Robin, is a Capricious Reaper with Seraphic Star and those accessories again

- The second Party piece begins simply with a Capricious Reaper with Seraphic Star and nothing else.
- Second is a Transient Witch with Shockwave Pulsar.
- The last one, which again you don't have to fight if you use Round Robin, is a False Stalwart with Twin Swords, Magus Sisters, and the Iifa Dew set-up.

#### Exdeath

<BossFight>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Exdeath is a tough cookie, Level 26 with Level 30 equipment, an Earring connected to 6 Near Opponent boosters (hello Hurricane, OW THAT HURTS!), and a new accessory to us, Prayer Beads, which will sap our critical hit chances. He has a Fallacious Wanderer Assist, Tiamat as a summon, and the typical tricks for his moveset, Reverse Polarity, Black Hole, Hurricane, Turn Guard, Omni Block, Malestrom, Delta Attack and Almagest.

And we're fighting on The Rift Ω, one of the coolest Omega arenas. As you can see everything is topsy-turvy, turrets stacked on each other and spires floating in the air. Periodically the castle features will rearrange themselves in new ways, increasing the Bravery pool during the process.

Be sure to grab any EX Cores as they appear because they may vanish during the shake-up.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Because he is so predictable and you know how to bait him, this fight isn't hard, just long, he has high defenses and a lot of Bravery and HP to whittle down. Attack when he isn't nearby to charge up your Assist and perform combo attacks to up the damage output, and remember to use your summon if needed. Overall it'll take time to put him down due to his power levels exceeding any prior opponent, but you'll prevail in the end.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

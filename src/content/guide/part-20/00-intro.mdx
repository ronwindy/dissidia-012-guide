# Epilogue Part 1

Part 20 - the time to end Light to All and bring this cycle to a close is upon us!

#### Preparations

<Callout type="tip">

In the Epilogue you can use whatever party of 5 you like, and in each gateway your Assist will be randomly chosen from the five people you left out. You can use whoever you like, I took a part of Zidane, Terra, Cloud, Firion and Squall. I bought all of them two more accessory slots, for a total of 8, and that Sunrise is finally gone, we need no more Battlegen until certain gateways in Confessions of the Creator.

The line-up for them all is a Power Ring, Hyper Ring, Muscle Belt (Earring for Terra, obviously), HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, Summpon Unused, (Opp) Break, and Level 10-19. The Power Ring is back because I have no worthwhile 1.3x boosters, and a 1.2x boost to 12% is less than a flat 3% boost. I bought Zidane an extra accessory slot to give him the Rabbit's Foot so Cactuar recharges faster, just in case.

Double-check everyone's equipment, the characters from the earlier storylines may be outdated. During the storyline we'll hit Level 30 and we'll cover the equipment I know you've all been waiting for. Also check abilities in case anyone learned new ones in the final battles of their storylines, a few did for me.

When entering each gateway in this storyline, remember your Assist is random - if you get one you don't like, just exit the gateway and re-enter. Everyone save for the Warrior of Light is level 15 or 16, but the bonus line is 23, odd. Still no exp bonuses for us, I don't want to dilute the difficulty by being overleveled - not that the difficulty has been very high anyway, right?

</Callout>

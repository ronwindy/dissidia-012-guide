#### Clipping An Angel's Wings

On the world map now, we're in a new end of the Land of Discord, behind us is a long, empty beach. Moving ahead there's a chest with 20,000 gil, and to the right a moogle and a chest with a BRV Charge. The moogle sells trade accessories we don't need anymore, you found a lot of them in the storylines up to this point.

Buy a Multichain and a Straight Chain. Approach the blue gateway. Cloud senses Sephiroth inside, so let's go say hello.

A warning, once you enter the gateway, the only way out is through Sephiroth, you can exit the gateway as you can with all of them but you won't get KP.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Straight Chain at B7, of course. True there's only three but a Straight Chain is 8 KP and we'll win 9 for the three chain, so it's a slight profit, right?

The emblem is a magical critical rate up, so if you brought Terra along - and why the hell didn't you if not? - consider swapping her in to take advantage of it. Otherwise Zidane's Meo Twister, Tidus's Energy Rain, and everyone else's usual spells will appreciate the benefits. Don't worry about being hurt, there's a Potion for us before we leave and confront Sephy.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First comes an Imaginary Champion with the Moogle summon, a Hyper Ring and 9 Near Loss boosters, that's gonna suck when they go off.

He uses Shadow Flare, Fervent Blow and Godspeed.

- Next is the Strange enemy, a Delusory Dragoon, it begins with a lot of Bravery, spams Jump, and has Cactuar, an easy victory.
- The Expert piece is a False Stalwart with two Muscle Belts, six Near Opponent, an after 45 Seconds, and the Cactuar summon.

For his moveset he has Twist Drill, Twin Swords, Bardiche, Blaze, and Flare. He's fought in Dream's End Ω, the Bravery pool will skyrocket here as every attack, successful or not, will drive up the Bravery pool. He's pretty tough but you can handle him, he's oddly not as smart as I've come to expect from Expert enemies.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 90 second win, and there's another Expert enemy so let's fill that EX Gauge!

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain at C3 to get all four enemies. I swapped Terra in as party leader for this and the next gateway. The emblem is a magical critical rate up, Terra likes that of course.

Also note my party is outgrowing the Level 10-19 booster, replace it with a Power Ring when that occurs since Level 20-29 is only a 1.2 booster.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The first enemy is a Counterfeit Wraith with Tentacle of Suffering (aerial spin attack), she's fought in Crystal World Ω.

Here platforms of crystal appear in the air, and the Bravery pool rises each time. Destroying the crystal platforms causes more to appear in new formations, in turn raising the Bravery pool again.

- The second enemy is a Phantasmal Harlequin with the two Firaga attacks, Hyperdrive, Moogle summon, and a Hyper Ring with 9 Near Loss.
- Next is an Idle Sky Pirate with high initial Bravery, the Moogle summon, and Windburst.

The Expert enemy is an Imitation Despot with the usual moveset, two Earrings with six Far From Opponent, and an after 45 Seconds. He has a Phantasmal Harlequin for an Assist and Carbuncle for a summon.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

He'll use Carbuncle when low on Bravery so your Break won't give you as much as we'd like, but otherwise you know how to handle him, the Chaos Shrine is a wide open area so his traps won't work well here. Try to Break him all at once so he can't get Carbuncle off.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

With the enemies beaten, take the Polished Ore from the chest and leave.

</PanelSection>

#### Beauty Ne'er Tarnished

As we enter, Garland reveals to Golbez he was a prisoner of a time loop, but the "Great Will" freed him and brought him to this realm to orchestrate war between Chaos and Cosmos. Golbez however asks what Garland himself has wished for, since the war is now over he can pursue his own path. Garland cannot accept this "hope" and is resigned to enjoying the last final battles.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The Strange enemy is first, an Ephemeral Summoner.

She has high starting Bravery, summons Tiamat, and spams Thor's Hammer. An easy win, remember to attack through Thor's Hammer with HP attacks like Rough Divide, Straightarrow, Braver, etc.

- Next is the Expert piece, a Counterfeit Wraith with all four Tentacle attacks, Anti-Air, Fusillade, Wrath and 0-Form Particle Beams, the Ifrit summon, and unknown Assist, and some accessories of course - a Hyper Ring and Guardian Bangle with 8 HP = 100% boosters.

The stage is Ultimecia's Castle Ω. Here, regularly Time Compression will occur, randomizing the Bravery pool rapidly, even during an EX Burst, and reforming destroyed stage elements. The gears in the stage also spin rapidly, damaging characters and knocking them around.

- Now it's Party time of course.
- First, a wimpy Phantasmal Harlequin with Hyperdrive.
- Next is an Imitation Despot with Thunder Crest, Light Crest and Flare.

He's fought in Pandaemonium Ω, here the two halves of the stage (the pit and hallway separate them) will have their floors turn red and black while the other is normal, then they switch. While fighting over the red and black areas, spikes will pop out at random and stab into the air, knocking characters into the air and damaging their Bravery and adding it to the pool. Spikes can also hit and lock Assists so be cautious calling them in such areas.

The grey floors are safe, fortunately.

- The last enemy I avoided is a False Stalwart with a Hyper Ring hooked up to 4 HP = 100% and 5 BRV ≥ Base Value, and he uses Cyclone.

</PanelSection>

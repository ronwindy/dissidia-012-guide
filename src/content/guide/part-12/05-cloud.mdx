Time to talk about Terra's EX Mode now, of course. It's her Esper form, and it lets her Glide around the arena by holding X, moving much quicker than walking and with much more freedom than even Omni Air Dash+, which of course you won't get for a while yet.

But more importantly, she picks up Dualcast, which lets her cast her spells twice in a row. So Blizzara and Thundara, press Circle a second time to attack immediately, you can delay the second cast a bit, and this is smart - if the opponent dodges Blizzara, time your second cast to smack them in the face as they recover from the dodge.

- Blizzard Combo just hits more times.
- Holy and Holy Combo, which she'll learn soon, fire off three trios of orbs and the first set goes off a lot faster than normal.
- Graviga and Meltdown, also a way's off, can be cast a second time without charging up.

For her EX Burst, tap the directional pad and Circle button to charge up two power meteors to unleash Riot Blade.

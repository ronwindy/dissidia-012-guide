<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 90 second win, piece of cake with four of the enemies being Strange and thus dying in one hit. Though you already spotted that little surprise waiting on A3, didn't you? Dun dun DUN!

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Multichain on C4, use the KP Gambler.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

All four Strange enemies have the same set-up for accessories - a Hyper Ring, a Musclet Belt, five Near Opponent boosters, and an Arcane Resin, so as you should know by now, let them enter EX Mode before striking.

Enemy line-up:

- First, a Transient Gunner, it spams Ragnarok Blade. Piece of cake.
- Next an Idle Sky Pirate that uses Torrent.
- Third, Imaginary Brawler with Meteor Strike, a chargeable attack that charges her along the ground.
- Fourth is a Phantasmal Girl, she also uses a Muscle Belt and Near Opponent despite the fact that duh, it's Terra! HP attack is Meltdown, which she charges up three levels.

Her Meltdown levels:

- Little charge time fires a fireball straight forward that explodes are mid-range.
- Medium charge fires a large fireball that tracks you and explodes on contact with walls.
- Highest charge, when she calls out a second attack call, fires a very large fireball that tracks you and bounces off walls and obstacles.

Use Thundara to stop the attack. And now for the last enemy.

---

Level 22 Phantasmal Wraith. She's got all her tentacle attacks and the same moveset all higher-level Cloud of Darknesses have, Wide-Angle, Anti-Air, Long-Range, Aura Ball, 0-Form, Fusillade. And naturally she has a full set of equipment, Shiva as a summon, a Hyper Ring with 7 Near Loss boosters, and due to the purple emblem powering up both of you she'll have around 3000 Bravery, likely enough to kill you in one shot immediately.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Get a Chase and wall rush her to fully capitalize on your own initial boost, then keep your distance and rely on Thundara to avoid getting too close and whacked with an energy blast, then Flood when she holds still, the open arena makes Tornado and Blizzara unreliable in my opinion.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Your reward for all this is a smooth 75 KP, and of course that chest, with 3000 gil.

</PanelSection>

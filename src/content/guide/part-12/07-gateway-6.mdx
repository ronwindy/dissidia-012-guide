<PanelSection kind="chain">

You can get all four enemies with a Multichain at the red emblem, but there's a catch. Red emblems sap your power, and you'll be seeing lots of them from now on. This particular one is Magic Damage 0. So yeah, Terra the mage is kinda fucked. You have two sources of damage if you do this - your Assist, or Chase attacks.

Alternatively use that Multichain at C3 or a Jump Chain at D3 to get three enemies. I recommend the Jump Chain if you decide to wimp out of the first method since a 10 KP Multichain would only break even this way. But I'm doing the red emblem chain because I'm awesome.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up:

- The first enemy is a Phantasmal Harlequin with the Blizzaga attacks and Havoc Wing.
- Next, a Capricious Reaper with Seraphic Star and Snatch Blow.
- Third is a Counterfeit Youth, it has Blizzard, Blizzaga, Turbo Hit, the follow-up, Comet, a Phantasmal Harlequin Assist, and of course those EX accessories. It's fought in a new arena, the Phantom Train, a very small arena with destructible seats. No Battlegen yet for it either.
- Last, a Transient Gunner in the same arena with Machine Gun, Pummel, and Ragnarok Buster.

The small area can make dodging attacks tricky so be careful.

</PanelSection>

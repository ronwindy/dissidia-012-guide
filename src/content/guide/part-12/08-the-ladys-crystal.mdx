#### The Lady's Crystal

Head to the left, a Counterfeit Wraith to kill.

- There's a chest in the ruins above, it has a Floating Continent Gem, that unlocks the Imperial Guard in the shop but at Level 60 it is way out of our power range.
- The chest on the island has a Pink Tail, and the chest on the outcropping past the gateway has 1000 gil.
- On the path into the mountains there's a Phantasmal Harlequin to beat up, and a chest with a Destruction Incense.

Before entering the final gateway, if you want to get ready for the next storyline, go grab a Straight Chain and a Multichain. This last gateway needs a Cross Chain or Jump Chain, so buy any of the three if you lack one.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Cross Chain at E4 or Jump Chain at F4. The first gives you an initial Bravery boost to get the fights over with, the second is a KP +1, so the chain will earn 18 KP as opposed to the Cross Chain's 16, up to 19 with the KP for beating the last enemy. 3 KP isn't much but you never know when you'll be a few KP short so it can't hurt.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

With the Jump Chain, the enemy line-up:

- A Phantasmal Harlequin is first, it has Havoc Wing and the Blizzagas, of course.
- Next, a Delusory Dragoon with Lance Burst, Lance Barrage, and Jump. Jump is always a bit tricky to deal with but still not much of a problem for you unless you try using Flood, since it lasts so long you won't be able to dodge.
- Now all that's left is a Phantasmal Girl with Blizzara and Flood, as well as those EX accessories. She also has an Assist, but how often does the AI attack enough to actually use their Assists?
- The last foe not chained is the one guarding the chest, a Transient Witch with Land Worm, the EX accessories, Knight's Axe, Great Attractor, and a Phantasmal Girl Assist.

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has 1000 gil for us.

I also unlocked new shop items - trade accessories! Yes, a lot (not all but a lot) of trade accessories can be snagged in the shop by trading Elixir-type items, the Dusty Elixir, Elixir, Hi-Elixir and Megalixir. In Confessions of the Creator there's a moogle that sells the lesser trade materials but that's a bit further down the road. I have a single Dusty Elixir, maybe you have two or three.

The trade accessories needed are for various Level 1 and Level 30 equipment pieces. Let's examine what's available to us, since the items that need trade accessories are stronger than the ones only need cash. Not I'm only going to cover the Level 1 items you probably haven't unlocked yet, if I know you have then I won't mention them.

- The Zweihander needs a Bangaa Tooth, it has 24 power, making it one of the strongest weapons you have at the moment, and further boosts physical damage by 3%, but as a Greatsword some characters can't make use of it like Terra, Onion Knight's etc.
- Triton's Dagger needs Chocobo Chick Powder, 20 Attack and gives +5% EX Force at the start of each fight. Don't we have two Cyan Drops that give 10% each? Yes we do. Moving on.
- I mention the Slasher and the Figaro Gravel needed for it only for the sake of being comprehensive, it sucks.
- The Dwarven Axe has a whopping 26 power, but it needs a 3-Elixir item and can only be used by a handful of heroes so we'll skip it.
- The Full Metal staff has 22 power and boosts magic damage by 3%, but once Terra is done there's no more mages in the storyline and while some of the heroes can use staffs they're physical fighters and thus have better options.
- The Chakram has 26 power, grants an extra 3% from EX force you absorb, and can be used by a few heroes, but skip it for better options I say.
- The Six-Fluted Pole *is* good for stats, 20 attack and a nice +15 Assist charge at the start of each fight, the problem is that it needs a a Cypress Pole to trade, and thus you need a Mu Fur and Moogle Fur requiring 3 Dusty Elixirs total. Ouch. Your choice kiddies.

There's nothing too awesome for armor, and even if there was our ultimate goal now is to get enough Mythril to use three Mythril items for armor with a weapon of course choice.

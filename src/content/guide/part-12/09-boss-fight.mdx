<PanelSection kind="enemies">

As we engage the end piece, Terra refuses to join Kefka, who rolls out the Cloud of Darkness to attack them. The Onion Knight arrives, having somehow freed himself, and he and Cloud take on the Cloud of Darkness, leaving Terra to deal with Kefka.

Kefka has a nice equipment set-up and decent boosters, an Earring with 4 1.2 Far From Opponent. He's also got an Idle Sky Pirate Assist, and you have none.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

But by now you are very well-versed in how to deal with his attacks and can spam Thundara to keep him on the ropes. As you know many of his attacks have start-up lag and have him hold still for the duration, so Thundara is his bane and can halt him in his tracks most every time. Keep your distance to avoid his faster attacks like Scatter-Spray Blizzaga and Lickity-Split Thundaga, and he's a piece of cake.

The initial Break and Wall Rush for HP hit will only knock off around half his HP, let him recovery his Bravery, then blast him with an HP hit and use your EX Burst. As you land the first hit he'll summon Brynhildr, double the Bravery pool, and thus the EX Burst will Break him and do enough damage to kill him for sure.

</PanelSection>

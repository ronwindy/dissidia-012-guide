<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Three enemies in here have the Deathgaze summon, which will automatically inflict Break on you when summoned if your Bravery is a multiple of 5. It's a manually triggered summon, but of course the AI won't call it unless it'll hit, so what this means is if your Bravery hits 5 in the middle of a Tornado, don't worry, the AI, even on the highest difficulties, probably won't be quick enough to react in the middle of a combo, and I've had the AI try to do that a few times and it misses, the combo racked up more damage to change the 5 to something else but it still called Deathgaze.

But if your Bravery is a 5 or 0 for any more than 2 seconds, you're gonna get Broken for it, just watch out. The other two use Land Worm, which continuously saps your Bravery the further away from them you are, mages like Terra hate this for obvious reasons.

---

Enemy line-up:

- First up is a Delusory Knight, it has the EX accessories, Valiant Blow, Gravity Ball, and Soul Eater for the HP hit.
- Next is a False Stalwart, it uses Round Edge, Chain Cast, and Cyclone. Cyclone will deflect your spells but he has to hold still for a second or two, perfect for Flood.
- Third is an Imitation Liegeman, a Firion manikin, it uses Reel Axe, and can use his spells in the air, Fire is a slow homing projectile, Blizzard is a fat orb of ice that begins Chase, and Thunder conjures lightning around you to slam you into the ground. He's got Land Worm and those EX accessories too.
- Fourth is a Fallacious Wanderer with Reel Axe, Holy, and Land Worm and EX accessories.
- Last is the Imaginary Chamipion with Transience, Sudden Cruelty, Heaven's Light, Deathgaze and EX accessories, just watch out for those fast Bravery attacks.

</PanelSection>

The chest has a White Drop for us here, totally useless since we have two now and two combined are just as effective as a lone Pearl Necklace, except that gets booster buffs.

I guess the two Drops offer only a 10% penalty to Assist charge instead of the Necklace's 20%, but if you're so desperate for that extra EX ability with a lesser Assist penalty that you'll give up two accessory slots, just use the Necklace and one of countless accessories that gives an initial Assist charge.

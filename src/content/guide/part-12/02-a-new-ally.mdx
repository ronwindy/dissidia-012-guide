#### Exploration

On the world map, the next area should be familiar...come to think of it so was the last one, we've been here what, three times now, at least? Vaan, Tifa...yup, third time. Anyway, swap out BRV ≥ Base Value for Empty Assist Gauge now.

- Make a left to find a Capricious Reaper, level 17 with Ring Holy and Seraphic Star, he has a weapon and helmet but fortunately three Cursed Rings.
- This is a good time to try out Tornado, the close quarters and alcoves of Kefka's Tower are great, trap the manikin in a corner and go to town with Tornado. Don't worry about taking HP damage, you'll be fully healed after the battle ends as you're fighting on the world map.
- Beyond him is an Alarune Twig.
- On the main path is another Reaper, a lower-level though with Flare Star, Ring Holy and Burst Energy.
- There's a chest near him with a Cure skill.

And there's a blue gateway to check out.

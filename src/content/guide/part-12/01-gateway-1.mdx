#### Exploration

On the world map now, Terra makes a nice little nod to her original game if you talk to the Onion Knight, the devs again including a geological mythology gag.

- The chest on the island behind us has 500 gil.
- If you don't get one from the orbs of the dead, buy a Straight Chain and a Multichain from the moogle, he otherwise has nothing worth buying unless you're 100 KP richer than me, in which case buy the Flame Rod for Terra and use it over the Snowscepter, it has higher power and further boosts her magic damage a bit.
- Near the moogle is a Phantasmal Harlequin, it just uses Hyperdrive and has three Cursed Rings, no danger here.
- The chest behind it has a BRV Zero skill.
- The outcropping of land below the castle has a Pink Tail, haven't found many of those lately have we?
- Before heading into the mountains to the next gateway, make a left to find an Assist Charge, and go up the path near the gateway to find a Rosetta Stone.

<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 60-second break, easy-peasy, and the bonus line is 8, even with the blue gateway my Terra was only Level 7.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Cross Chain at C3 to trigger the Booster x5 emblem, contrary to the map the tiles connecting to the boss piece will not appear until you defeat certain enemies..

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemy line-up:

- The first enemy is a Delusory Warlock, it has Rising Wave (plume of darkness) and Float System.
- Next is a Phantasmal Harlequin, it has Scatter-Spray Blizzaga and nothing else of note.
- They get a bit tougher now, the Imaginary Soldier next has Fire, Cross-Slash, those EX accessories, and an unknown summon.
- Now it's the Strange Battle Piece, it spams Meteor Strike with a Hyper Ring, Muscle Belt, and five Bear Opponent boosters, along with a full EX Gauge. Let it enter EX Mode before you attack so it doesn't use EX Revenge.
- The last opponent is a Phantasmal Girl with Fire, Blizzara, Flood, and the EX accessories.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

As you fight charge up your EX Gauge for the boss, if you like you can swap out the BRV ≥ Base Value booster for a Cyan Drop for an extra 10% EX every fight, the blue emblem keeps your booster still astronomically high of course.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest when we finish has a Mythril for us!

- I snagged myself a Mythril Rod, with the Bangle and Hairpin Terra now has Mystic Mythril, it's EX Mode time, honey!
- I also bought a Sage's Surplice, just because I could and wanted to improve my armor.
- After this change I had to use my Cure skill to heal myself, not a problem since I never need Cure.
- I also removed Terra's Sunrise for that BRV ≥ Base Value, giving her four boosters.

</PanelSection>

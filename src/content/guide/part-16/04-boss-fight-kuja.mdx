<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Kuja is pretty well-equipped, he's a few levels weaker than the mid-chapter bosses before, but is fought a bit earlier. He has full equipment and an Earring with 3 Far from Opponent and an Aerial, Near Opponent would have been a far better choice for Kuja.

The Tiamat summon will give him a pretty significant boost if it goes off, so either Break him or use Cactuar or Leviathan to sap his Bravery so Tiamat does nothing.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

It's been a long time since you've fought Kuja, the last time was way back in Chapter 1 with Lightning. I guess he just has a knack for pissing off Gunblade users. He's quite a bit smarter and more aggressive than back then of course.

Despite their appearances not all of his moves count as magic in terms of damage priority, so sadly Heel Crush and Aerial Circle are not be-all answers to him. Snatch Blow and Burst Energy come out fast, Remote Flare is just a nuisance, and Snatch Shot can be annoying to dodge at longer range.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Ultimately though, Kuja, as bosses often are, is entirely impotent against the power of an EX Mode Squall. Speaking off, let's cover him.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Squall's EX Mode swaps out the Revolver for the Lionheart Gunblade, giving him a boost to the range of all his melee attacks.

His true power however is that when using Solid Barrel, Upper Blues or Beat Fang, he pulls the trigger of the Gunblade with each strike, hitting an extra time. This almost *doubles* the base power of the three attacks, in addition to the benefit of more critical hits. An EX Mode Beat Fang or Solid Barrel can quickly drain the Bravery of even the toughest foes, simple but effective.

His EX Burst is Renzokuken, you have to tap R as blue bars flash by a power meter, timing the button press as they enter a hit zone. Squall has the embarrassing distinction of having the weakest EX Burst in the entire game, save for Cloud and Tidus with 1 HP and Tifa with an empty EX Gauge, as those conditions negate their damage bonuses.

</PanelSection>

#### Exploration

Welcome to the desert, a big place.

- The ruined tower hides a chest with 1000 gil, and near the inactive gateway is a chest with Iifa Dew.
- There's also one of two Fallacious Trees here, an easy win.
- Far to the right of the inactivate gateway at the edge of the desert is a Rosetta Stone.
- Head to the next Tree, hard to miss given it's bright blue, and in an alcove nearby is a chest with 1999 gil.
- You can also head to an outcropping over to the sea to snag a KP Gambler, can never have too many of those...well you can, but it's still good to have more than one.
- Head back into the desert and go to the gateway, there's a chest on your right on the path with a Valor Resin.

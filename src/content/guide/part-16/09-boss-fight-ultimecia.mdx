Ultimecia came well equipped with a full moveset, and a False Stalwart for an Assist. She's back Brynhildr, though it'll probably help you more than her, and has a good accessory set. She's got on a Hyper Ring with four Far from Opponent, two Aerial, a Near Loss, and an Iifa Leaf.

The Leaf, if you never used one yourself, gives her the Last Chance ability - the attack that will normally kill her, one time will just sap her to 1 HP, even an EX Burst. It also doesn't matter how much HP she has before hand, as long as she has more than 1 HP without using Last Chance already, it'll save her.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Even without EX Mode, Ultimecia is, once again, an anti-climax boss. She's pretty stupid and just flat weak. She tends to spam Knight's Axe the entire fight, which is very easy to block uncharged and easy to dodge when charged.

Aside from a Knight's Spear counter when you wall rush her, she has no real signs of intelligence. If she used Knight's Blade or Knight's Arrow more maybe, but as is she's pathetic.

Her HP attacks are perfect chances to smash into her with a Rouge Divide - she has the ability to move during Apocalypse but the AI has her hold in place, a fatal mistake.

And for her normal blows, remember Aerial Circle and Fated Circle block magic attacks, and the only attacks of Ultimecia's that can pierce that are a charged Axe or Spear, and the Circle will detonate before she can get them off. The only complication is one of nuisance, Last Chance, all it means is that you have to sap her to 1 HP first if you plan to do the typical EX Burst finishing blow.

</PanelSection>

With the time witch faded away, Squall receives his Crystal and joins up with Zidane to go look for Bartz, who has apparently been captured.

As a final footnote, Squall hit Level 16 from the fight and got Blasting Zone. He extends a column of energy up into the air and then slams it down, dragging aerial opponents down and doing a bit of Bravery damage. He also turns on start-up to track the opponent, and its good reach and fast chop make it pretty effective as an anti-air attack.

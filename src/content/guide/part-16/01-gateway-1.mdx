#### Exploration

As we leave and appear on the world map, Zidane and Bartz are still running around.

- Turn around and approach the rock on the shoreline.

An annoying little problem Squall has is that his world map attack, Solid Barrel, has him take a step back, and the rock doesn't shatter until he does the last part of the attack, so if you aren't right up next the rock, Squall will attack twice, then step back and the third strike will miss and the rock remains intact. Get closer and try again, ugh.

- The island the beam connects to has a chest with 1000 gil, and despite being large has nothing else on the two ends.
- Back on the mainland approach the sealed gateway in view for another 1000 gil.
- Go through the mountains to find Zidane near a rock, shatter it to create a long grindbar to an island with 2000 gil. Also Zidane has a funny line if you chat with him.
- Go back to where you appeared and head to the shoreline to find a Capricous Reaper to take out, it's level 17 but a first strike and its three Cursed Rings will sap its level advantage.

---

Squall hit Level 3 from this fight and learned Solid Barrel. It has much less range than Upper Blues but does more than twice as much damage, and begins a Chase, the only one of Squall's melee blows to do so, so when the time comes to charge up that EX Gauge later on, guess what move I hope you know how to use well.

At the far end of the beach is a Maser Eye. Now just go to the next gateway, not hard to find, just watch for the dark smoke rising over the mountains. Near it is a chest with another Arcane Incense.

#### The Witch's Den

On the world map head down to the moogle you can see near the ruins. "EXECUTING MISSION RECEIVE kp GIVE MERCHANDISE", cyborg moogle! RoboMog has nothing really awesome to buy, just get a Multichain.

- There's a Shiny Metal in a chest nearby, and a False Stalwart to kill, amusingly it can break the rock for you if it's close enough to hit it with Round Edge.
- It's a long grind out to a chest with 1000 gil.
- And of course there's a blue gateway to deal with.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

In and out quickly is possible here, there's only one enemy you need to fight to get the chest and leave. Take your pick on how to chain enemies:

- Straight Chain at C2
- Multichain at B4
- Jump Chain at B3

I only say to chain them though if you have the spare skills, otherwise the winnings are minimal when you consider the cost to buy those skills.

I got a Round Chain from the orbs of the dead and used it on the purple emblem, an Initial BRV Boost one, to get two enemies, then went and manually chained the other two.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The enemy guarding the chest is a False Hero with Ascension, Crossover and Radiant Sword, the Giant of Babil summon, an unknown Assist, and a White Cape set-up.
- The other Hard foe is a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe, Great Attractor, Deathgaze, the same White Cape gear, and a Capricious Reaper Assist. As before, they're both armed with Level 30 weapons.
- The two Normal enemies are a Phantasmal Harlequin with Ultima and a Fallacious Wanderer with Solid Ascension.

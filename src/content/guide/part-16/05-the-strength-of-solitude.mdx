#### The Strength of Solitude

On the world map, down the left fork is a rock to break that leads to an island with the Cure skill. Ahead near the next gateway is another rock leading to 1000 gil, and a blue gateway to check out. This region of the world map is deceptively small.

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Note the Hard enemies here are actually quite a step up from previous ones, they're Level 19 each instead of Level 12, and have Level 30 weapons for a higher offensive boost than previous ones.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First, a Fleeting Flash with Launch and Ruin. I spammed Solid Barrel and chase attacks to fill up my EX Gauge in preparation for the Expert enemy.
- The second enemy is a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe, Typhon, and the White Cape set-up.
- Now comes the Expert, an Imitation Despot with Light Crest, Thunder Crest, Flare, a Hyper Ring and Guardian Bangle boosted by 8 HP = 100% boosters, and the Tonberry summon.

After a set of time when he's called, Tonberry does Bravery damage to you equal to however much HP the summoner has lost, and it will Break you if the HP loss is high enough.

This Expert piece is pretty easy to kill though, Aerial Circle will block his Light Crest projectiles, and with EX Mode Squall can quickly Break him, getting the stage Bravery which will surely be high enough to deal a one-hit KO. Don't use an EX Burst, save the bit of gauge you have left at the end to refill for the boss.

With him gone the foes left are:

- A False Hero with Dayflash, Blue Fang, and Crossover
- A Transient Lion with Blizzard Barret, Fusillade, Mystic Flurry, a White Cape set-up, and Typhon

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has Squall's trademark Revolver in it, but it has no special abilities and by this point you have plenty of high-end gear that do to choose from instead.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Squall learned Thunder Barret from these fights. It's his key spell, Squall creates a line of lightning in front of him, dragging opponents in front of him and out of the air. If it hits though the end lag is cancelled out, letting Squall quickly combo into another move while the opponent is helpless before him.

Thunder Barret to Solid Barrel is a brutal combo that helps compensate for the latter's crap range, and while the longer start-up makes the timing tighter, you can also combo Thunder Barret into the HP attacks Fated Circle (which you'll learn soon) or Blasting Zone (ditto).

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As we reach the end, Squall runs across the Warrior of Light. The Warrior questions his choice to travel alone, and Squall aptly points out the Warrior himself is alone, declaring he won't be dissuaded. So, now they have to fight. Why? I don't know.

</PanelSection>

Squall got to Level 6 here and learned Mystic Flurry, an aerial attack that fires ice shards at the opponent in a spread pattern, and they explode to slam them to the ground. Very easy to avoid or block, but at close-range if it connects it does a surprising amount of damage.

For a spell though its range sucks, but a new move to the arsenal is always welcome, and Squall won't get another aerial spell until Level 28 so it can't hurt to use this one.

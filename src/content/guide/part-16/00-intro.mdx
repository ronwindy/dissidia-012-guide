# Chapter 7

Chapter 7, we're finally done with the group of six and take a look at a trio now.

#### The Lion, the Mouse, and the Monkey

Well, new character time as always!

---

<CharacterIntro name="Squall" src="drake/Dissidia_Squall.png" width={175} height={261} alt="Dissidia Squall">

Squall is back from FF8, and as always is cold, quiet and professional. Unfortunately his story is one they didn't carry over well, Squall's character arc in FF8 was him being torn between his duties as a mercenary and what he knew was right which conflicted with his duties, and his coping mechanism for dealing with the world, that is complete emotional detachment, broke down as his personal universe tore at the seams, forcing him to re-evaluate his life.

Here, he's cold and impersonal for no given reason, but he still has a good heart.

- He goes off alone and leaves his friends behind, but declares they still fight together.
- He abandons them and doesn't go back to save them when he knows they're in trouble, but says he still cares about them and considers them friends...

Yeah. The morals of the next three chapters are all a bit confusing, I'll discuss this as we proceed.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Squall is another melee-focused fighter, but he has a lot of spell attacks, in fact he has five spells to four sword combos. Yup, Squall only has two melee attacks on ground or in the air. However, they're all he needs.

Squall is an absolute *beast* in Bravery damage, Solid Barrel and Beat Fang do incredible damage, and Upper Blues and Heel Crush give him a bit of versatility to save him from being completely predictable.

His spell attacks, contrary to the lines of Cloud and Tidus who have ranged moves solely so they have ranged moves, are as vital a part of his arsenal as his melee blows, setting opponents up for them and doing a fair bit of damage in their own right.

Squall starts off with:

- Upper Blues: an uppercut that has him dash forward a short distance, then he leaps up and performs a few more attacks to slam the opponent into the ground
- Blizzard Barret: a basic ice shot at the opponent

His aerial moves are:

- Beat Fang: a fast-hitting combo attack that slams the opponent into the ground
- Heel Crush: a spinning attack forward to strike once and, you guessed it, make the opponent kiss the dirt

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

However, Squall has a crushing weakness...his HP attacks are *slow* as can be, and very easy to avoid. You have to rely on Assists or his spells to set up for them, not a problem against the dumb AI in this chapter but later, it's a big hassle.

Still, his potent Bravery attacks make him so powerful you won't need to land many HP hits to finish off the enemy. And in his EX Mode, oh god...but we'll get to that when I cover his EX Mode during the chapter.

His HP attacks are:

- Rough Divide: he pauses then dashes at the opponent, rising or descending on an angle to strike
- Aerial Circle: he swings to create explosive orbs in a vertical ring around him, then detonates them

He also moves towards the opponent during the swing and always creates the ring in a way to hit the opponent based on their current position. It also has Magic Block, preventing projectiles from stopping you during the execution.

Squall is overall a very powerful character with strengths far outweighing his flaws in my opinion, but he *is* flawed and you have to learn how to make up for them. Use him well.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

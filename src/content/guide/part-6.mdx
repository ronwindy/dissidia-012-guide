# Chapter 6

Part 6, with revelations in hand let's get the full story of the other side...

#### The Dragoon's Path

Well, new chapter means new character, but not just any character!

---

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - **Kain**

<Figure side="left" src="drake/Kain_(Dissidia_012).png" width={183} height={200} alt="Kain (Dissidia 012)" />

KAIN IS HERE, KIDDIES! A strong contender for the one most popular and famous characters of the series, not to mention one of the most badass. As we now know, **Kain** isn't really a traitor, he's just going to extremes to ensure the survival of his comrades, but he is a good guy. Alone as he searches for more friends to get to safety, it's not an easy path with the chance of someone following him and finding out where the others are, but there's no one more qualified to walk it. Turn up the volume if you've had your PSP muted. Liam O'Brien's voice has been reported to induce orgasm in people from just a few seconds exposure, even straight males.

**Kain** has a comparatively small moveset, but it's an effective one with a cool gimmick, though very difficult to get right. **Kain** has, aside from his **Cyclone** which is just the token projectile all characters need, two ground **Bravery** attacks and three aerial ones. When he attacks he can press Triangle after knocking the opponent back to instantly dash after them, letting you attack again before they recover. As I said though, very hard to get right, your timing has to be precise. You can also tilt the analog stick up, down, or leave it neutral when doing the second part of his aerial combos, letting him knock the opponent in a different direction and changing if the attack does wall rush or Chase. His HP attacks consist of jumps and dives effective at many altitudes, and some mid-range spear throws.

Despite his limited moveset, like **Yuna** **Kain** has the moves he needs to be effective, unlike **Yuna** he's still fun to play as and like **Lightning** is rewarding when you learn his quirks. I must confess I never got the hang of any of his combos except for Lance Burst to **Lance Barrage**, but if you can, good for you. Jump is awesome, learn to use it effectively and you can dodge attacks and land nasty hits. Finally, Gungnir is pure win, the opponent *will* be driven across the arena by it. A fun combo, though I'm sure it can be avoided with Aerial Recovery but the AI is too dumb to do that, is to use **Cyclone** to blast them up into the air and towards **Kain**, then Jump and hit them as they're staggered. **Kain** is overall a blast to play as and a potent character, use him well.

If so you probably don't have one, but it's okay, there's a better weapon for **Kain** in this first gateway. I also bought him Iron Armor since he had no body armor, there was better stuff but I bought the Iron Armor in anticipation of later purchases of Mythril stuff, as the Iron Armor is needed for the **Mythril Armor**. Also, it hasn't been a big deal up until now, but I just wanna mention I reassigned **Crashing Dive** to forward-Circle rather than back-Circle, I'm used to my diving attacks being on the forward section and the rising ones being on the back.

A **Jump Chain** at C3 will get all four of them, I lacked one though so I have to chain them two by two. The line-up is a **Transient Gunner**, an **Imitation Despot**, a **Fleeting Flash** and a **Delusory Dragoon**, no surprises here save for one thing. The Flash has a summon, **Typhon**, when called it randomly blows away a digit of your **Bravery**, so say, 774 **Bravery** will become 770, or 074 (aka 74), and so forth. I use this example because I retried three times just to check, and every time **Typhon** blasted the hundreds digit. The AI likes using **Typhon** after you **Break** her, keep that in mind.

With the opposition dead open the chests for yet another **Pink Tail**, and a Javelin, a new weapon for **Kain**. With enough gil you can trade it with your Mythril for a Mythril Spear, but don't for the reasons I've already explained.

As we leave, **Kain** has a flashback to when he met with **Golbez**, who told him about the cycles of war and the proof of such that lies in their memories - **Kain** and **Golbez** remember their home clearly, but **Cecil**, who is newly summoned, does not, because he hasn't had time to fight and remember yet. **Kain** doesn't remember this truth because while memories of home return and remain, when you are felled in battle and revived in the next cycle, you lose your memories of the previous cycle. This leaves a gaping question to be answered - who on Team **Chaos** was so badass last cycle that they beat **Kain**? - but we'll never find out, alas. **Cecil** shows up and **Kain** confirms he has few memories, and thus realizes **Golbez** told the truth.

On the world map, behold our map theme, Suspicion, very appropriate for the story all things considered. Immediately turn around and go back through the forest. There's an **Imitation Despot** here to deal with and a chest with 500 gil in the mountains beyond him. The Despot is fought in a new arena, **Ultimecia's Castle**, with a new Battlegen to go with it, the Time Warp, though it isn't needed for anything good. Back at the start point, the island has a **Maser Eye**, a new skill that saps all enemies in a chain to 50% HP. To the right is a path with an **Assist Charge** in a chest. Continue along and ignore the bridge and the person waiting for us, there's a path up to the Gulg volcano ring with the frozen lake, and a **False Stalwart** to fight. Yay, we need to fight these guys, we need their Battlegen for that coveted **Hyper Ring**! Remember to watch for the gold star icon when you **Break** him and keep Breaking him until you get it so you know you'll get the Stalwart's Hopes when you win. Hidden behind the grass near the sealed gateway up here is a **Rosetta Stone**, you know what to do.

#### A Naive Ally

Back across the bridge, we meet up with **Firion**, who joins us as our Assist for the rest of the story. In the air he tosses our three **Fire** spells, weak slow-moving projectiles that are good for some other characters that could use distractions or stunning attacks, but **Kain** has no use for it. What he *does* have a use for is **Firion**'s ground Assist, Rope Knife. He tosses out daggers to drag the opponent close, then slams them with a superpowered punch to knock them away. It does a lot of damage and wall rushes to set up for further blows, yeah it can be a problem if **Firion** knocks the opponent too far, but a charged up Jump, which you can charge as he attacks, can get you the distance you need a lot of the time. And awesomely, **Crashing Dive**'s downward attack slams the opponent into the ground, a perfect set-up for **Firion** to come in. The area beyond him is small, shatter the remaining orbs to get your new chain skill and enter the blue gateway.

Enemy line-up is a bit more varied now, an **Imitation Despot**, **Capricious Reaper**, **Fleeting Flash** and **Phantasmal Girl**. Chain the lot of them with a **Cross Chain** at B3, a **Jump Chain** at C3, or a Multi Chain at D2 or B3. The Despot has **Thunder Crest** and the tracking ground **Flare**, use **Kain**'s Jump to reflect **Flare**. There's also some summons to look out for, the Despot has **Ifrit**, the **Sky Pirate** has **Demon Wall**, and the Girl has **Giant of Babil**. As you've noticed the game is slowly ramping up the difficulty, making opponents of higher levels more common and giving them summons and accessories. It still isn't very challenging yet but it is making a stronger effort.

The **Strange Battle Piece** is a **Phantasmal Girl** and she has a new, potentially deadly trick. **Holy Combo** is a **Bravery** attack that shoots five slow-moving orbs of light, but if one of them hits she can follow it up by firing four **Flare** fireballs at us, each fireball she turns to track us if we move. And if one of the Flares hit, she can use the HP Link **Ultima**, creating a sphere of magic around us to explode and damage our HP. Fortunately **Holy Combo** is slow and pretty easy to block. Be thankful this isn't the real **Terra**, her **EX Mode** **Holy Combo** is a lot harder to avoid and is one of the most powerful combos in the game.

During this round of fights **Kain** hit **Level 6** and picked up Jump Boost, giving his jumps greater height. Many fights here take place in the **Crystal** World, another new arena. The Battlegen is the Star's Core, which unlocks three new items, two of them are the **Dragonfly Orb** and **Bravery** Orb. The latter is largely useless but the **Dragonfly Orb** increases **EX Core** absorption, giving us more EX from the cores. It's not as good as the **Pearl Necklace** and **Attractorb** in my opinion though, I'd prefer to absorb the **EX Force** as it appears rather than let a core absorb it all and an opponent get to it first. The third item is the **Iifa Leaf**, which can be traded for one each Blackcrystal Sliver, Sealed Darkness, and Star's Core. Given how much we've fought in the **Chaos Shrine** and **World of Darkness**, the first two should be in abundance. The chests have 500 gil and a Soul of Valor.

Not much left to do on the world map except go to the next gateway.

A **Jump Chain** at C2 or a **Multichain** at C3 will chain all enemies here. I had neither so I killed the enemy at D2 and then moved there and used a **Round Chain**. The enemies here are a **Delusory Dragoon** with **Demon Wall** as a summon, **Fallacious Tree** with **Typhon**, **Fleeting Flash** with **Odin**, another **Fleeting Flash** with nothing, and the **Expert Battle Piece**, an **Imitation Despot**.

The Tree is a new enemy type, an **Exdeath** manikin. It uses **Sword Dance**, pausing for a second or two before sweeping the sword, and **Hurricane**, surrounding itself in a whirlwind. Its HP attack is **Delta Attack**, where it creates a field of energy and fires a long-range blast of light from the center. The crest deflects attacks like a block so wait for the blast to fire and die down before countering. The Tree will be encountered from now on of course, and it's Battlegen items can be used to trade for the **Heart's Ease**, which boosts your **Bravery** a little every time you block an attack, and isn't much worth it.

The **Imitation Despot** is a tough one. He has a **Hyper Ring**, Guardian Ring, and boosters that all trigger at full HP, so land an HP attack first thing to deactivate them and rob him of his considerable power boost. He has Landmine, Mine, Bombard, **Light Crest**, **Thunder Crest**, the ground and aerial versions of **Flare**, **Starfall**, good equipment, and the **Carbuncle** **Summon**. I recommend using your **Maser Eye** to knock off half his HP, which is around the 2500 area, give or take a couple hundred.

The summonstone is **Shiva**, who has an amusing scene of teasing **Firion** when she senses him. She appears when the opponent's **Bravery** hits 0 and locks it so it can't increase. The auto version is useless for the moment, the only time an opponent's **Bravery** will hit 0 is if they're in the middle of getting attacked and are gonna get Broken, and **Shiva** doesn't stop the **Bravery** regen, or they just landed an HP attack, and that shouldn't be happening since enemies are still dumb as a post so far. The chest has a Black Dragon Spear, a powerful exclusive weapon for **Kain**. Also at this point, **Kain** hit **Level 8** and learned Sky Rave. He moves forward a bit and dives down, drilling the opponent into the ground. It and its cousin move, Rising Drive, are meant to be used for combos because Sky Rave does a huge amount of **Bravery** damage the longer **Kain** drives them into the ground, so you want an Assist to get the opponent stunned at the top of the arena. But if you want to try it out, run up a pillar in the **World of Darkness**, block the opponent when they dash towards you, then let loose and watch the fun.

As we exit the gateway **Firion** asks why **Kain** is alone, and he dodges the question, musing though that they're doing well fighting the manikins so far, so it's not yet time to take out **Firion** when he can still help clear the nearby gateways.

#### The Tree's Pursuit

On the overworld map now, make a left and open the chest for a **Pink Tail**. On the shoreline to the left is a **Fallacious Tree**, it has **Delta Attack** again but is otherwise unremarkable. The Trees are easy to get the drop on on the overworld because their attack to initiate battle is **Sword Dance**, which is amazingly slow and easy to avoid. The chest beyond him has a **KP Gambler**, we're going to save it for the next chapter because we want to use it to chain as many enemies as possible and **Kain** can't get any more than four in the remaining gateways. The chest on the island has the Jump skill, which lets us leap over an enemy piece in a gateway to land behind them. We'll have a use for it too. The **Counterfeit Wraith** here has a new move, Long-Range **Particle Beam**, which creates a line of energy orbs that run along the ground firing lasers high into the air, fairly simple to dodge. On the hill behind it with the ruined castle is a chest with 1000 gil.

The enemies here are at first unremarkable, but they do have a new trick. The line-up is a **Fleeting Flash**, **Idle Sky Pirate**, **Transient Gunner** and **Fallacious Tree**. All of them except the Gunner have an accessory line-up of an **Attractorb**, **Dragonfly Orb**, **Pearl Necklace**, and six **Break** boosters. Thus once you break them, they get more than an 11x booster, greatly increasing the range at which they absorb **EX Force** and how much they get from it. As a result their gauge will charge surprisingly fast. They also have summons, **Odin** for the Flash and **Sky Pirate**, **Typhon** for the Tree. The Gunner is the weakest but has a midair Pummel that goes off pretty quick, so be ready to block it, it won't hurt much but on principle, not to mention it negates the **AP** chance. Also it may just be me, but the **Idle Sky Pirate** was a bit smarter than I expected, got in a few hits on me.

Use the Jump skill to jump over the enemy at D4 and chain all four enemies without skills, and use that **Assist Charge** we got earlier for an advantage on their haxxor EX powers, as well as to help with their high HP. The chests have 500 gil and a Pebble for us.

On the overworld now, shatter the stone and grind to the island, depending on where it is when you land the **Fallacious Tree** may attack as soon as you land so be ready to dodge if so. The arena is new, **Planet's Core**, we can Battlegen the Materia here. The Materia is the last component, along with Reaper's Dreams and Gunner's Dreams, for the **Zephyr Cloak**, we don't need it but something of note. Now just gather the orbs of the dead and approach the moogle.

The summons can mostly be passed up, though if you must buy one go for **Carbuncle**. For weapons, we can still afford the big 200 **KP** ones. I would say buy the Snowscepter, the character in the next storyline can't use weapons most of the rest can but she can use Staves, so the Snowscepter will do well for her, and continue to be handy in later storylines for spellcasters that can't use guns or swords like the rest of the cast. The Grounded and Aerial boosters are good, they can be annoying since you have to stay on the ground or in the air, but they have their uses. I'll pass them up though since we don't really need boosters urgently yet. I also bought several more **Rosetta Stones**, six, leaving me with 57 **KP**. I only used one of them for **Kain** though. Now just buy a **Multichain** and head into the gateway now.

A **Multichain** at E4 is the way to get them all here. The line-up is a **Delusory Dragoon**, **Transient Gunner**, **Phantasmal Girl** and **Idle Sky Pirate**. The Gunner and **Sky Pirate** have the same **EX Mode** accessories as the enemies in the last gateway, and the Gunner has **Odin** and the **Sky Pirate** has **Typhon**. The Girl is the **Strange Battle Piece** and has a **Bravery** boost and the **Giant of Babil** summon with **Holy Combo**-Ultima. Nothing new here, in short.

During this round of fighting **Kain** learned Celestial Shooter at **Level 13**. It's an upwards lunge aerial **Bravery** attack, and its upward follow-up slam the opponent into the ceiling. It's of limited use in my opinion, I prefer to slam opponents into the ground, knocking them higher puts them out of reach more often than not. But the initial strike is good as an upward leap so it's not useless. I also got the "Hard Habit to **Break**" accomplishment, getting me the pretty useless Desert Boots. The chest has a Yellow Drop which boosts Luck by 1, totally useless since the **Pink Tail** gives +4, among many other trade items.

Once the enemies are taken out approach the end piece and **Kain** will send **Firion** ahead, then strike him down as he walks away. I'm not sure why part of this scene is only text and the rest is a full cutscene, presumably its so you don't see **Kain** strike him down, but why, we see other people get attacked. Anyway as **Firion** lies wounded, **Exdeath** appears to taunt us, but **Kain** of course isn't about to let him find out where he's taking **Firion** and the rest of his allies. So, only one thing to do. <br />

I love **Kain**'s one-liner. "So, you offer your life in exchange for theirs?" "Of course not. I offer yours." **Exdeath** is an opponent entirely different from any other up until now due to a radically different fighting style, even the Fallacious Trees haven't tapped into his true power yet. First, he has the smartest accessory set-up of any opponent so far. He has Pre-Assist and **Assist Gauge** Empty, and since he doesn't have an Assist at all they'll be active the entire match for an instant 2.1 boost. Then, he has After **Summon**, which will tack on another 1.3 once he calls his summon for 2.7 during the rest of the match, and finally he has a booster that triggers when his **Bravery** is its base value or lower for another 1.3, leading to a potential 3.5x booster. And what is getting boosted? **Attractorb** and **Pearl Necklace** to help him charge up the **EX Gauge**, and unlike the **Manikins**, he's going to live long enough to potentially do it. And this time of course, no **Firion** Assist anymore to help you stop him. His summon, **Hecatoncheir**, is usually called when he's at low **Bravery**, it'll sap the **Bravery** pool to 0 so you get no bonus for Breaking him. Annoying, but it only works once, after the first time **Break** is inflicted the **Bravery** pool resets like normal.

Now, **Exdeath**'s manikins have had slow, easy to avoid or block attacks. This is because **Exdeath**'s true power isn't in offense, it's defense. When he blocks an attack using a Guard or Block move, he can instantly use **Sword Dance**, **Vacuum Wave** or **Hurricane**, and they execute faster, and have a higher critical hit rate since you got blocked while he attacked. **Turn Guard** is a glow red and orange barrier where **Exdeath** turns to keep defending, you need to use a guard-crushing attack, fortunately all of **Kain**'s HP attacks are such. **Omni Block** blocks *anything*, everything you throw at him, and it's a momentary instant-activation block, fortunately the AI sucks at using it. **Reverse Polarity** sends out a circle of energy that **Exdeath** can instantly teleport to when he chooses, and if he teleports on top of you you're damaged, *and* he can use an attack immediately, so you're likely to get knocked into the air and come back down into the blast from **Delta Attack**, or get caught in a **Hurricane**. And his new HP attacks, **Almagest** has him move towards you in a field of energy and explode, and he can stop it in the middle of execution to block your attack, and Grand Cross summons orbs of darkness to spin and converge on you.

All in all, **Exdeath** is surprisingly the strongest boss yet, especially if you're a new player and don't know the quirks of his playstyle and how to counter him. There is no way to stop his **Omni Block**, it comes out way too fast for you to know he's even preparing it, and when he lands it you are going to feel pain. If he uses Almgaest, he can block you if you try to attack him and you will feel pain. If he hits you with **Reverse Polarity**, you are *probably* going to feel pain. The main thing to watch for is **Reverse Polarity**, it always fires straight at you, do a neutral dodge and **Exdeath** will teleport right in front of you, you'll avoid damage and can attack him before he recovers from the teleportation. And if he does hit you with it, use Aerial Recovery quickly so you don't fall back down into his next attack.

The best opening you can ask for besides **Reverse Polarity** is **Hurricane**, make sure you have enough distance from him to get it off safety and chuck a Gungnir in his face. He *might* stop **Hurricane** by Omni Blocking and pain is soon to follow, but it's a risk you'll have to take because there's not much you can do to stop him. You can also try to hit as he fires out **Reverse Polarity**, but likely he'll teleport out of the way and laugh in your face. What stops **Exdeath** from just curb-stomping you is his AI again, the AI is good at using him, but not *too* good. Fake him out by attacking when he's nowhere near you and this will bait him into **Reverse Polarity**, dodge and counter as described. And if he Omni Blocks but mistimes it, the ending lag of it is a perfect opening. Overall just don't be too aggressive or he'll make you pay for it, be cautious and wait for your chance to strike when he gives you an opening.

I never talked about **Kain**'s **EX Mode** but I like finishing off bosses with **EX Bursts**, so this is the first time I've gotten into **EX Mode** actually. **Kain** gets a special attack called Lancet by pressing R and Square, it has a second's long charge time and causes a giant blade of energy to impale the opponent from below. The attack will not only do HP damage, but **Kain** is healed the HP he inflicted. Furthermore, Lancet will recharge to base **Bravery** very quickly after it hits, and you can press Triangle to dash at the opponent like **Kain**'s melee moves. His **EX Burst** has you rapidly tap a button to build up a power meter, and the button turns over and changes twice as he jumps, very easy to get right. Appropriately **Exdeath** is very vulnerable to Lancet when using **Delta Attack** or **Hurricane**.

In the aftermath **Exdeath** retreats, but leaves **Kain** a parting gift, a few **Delusory** **Manikins** to play with. **Kain** is unimpressed and so am I, we've killed how many so far in the game, and he only brings out three? The tree vanishes and **Kain** once again gets some badass boasting. "I'm about to teach you pathetic mockeries the difference between you, and a *real* man!"

As always we unlock a Report for this, but it's just cutscenes again. Hit the **PP Catalog** and continue to Chapter 7.

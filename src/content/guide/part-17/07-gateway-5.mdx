<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First is that lonesome lonely lone Normal enemy, the Fallacious Tree, he's got Sword Dance and Hurricane.
- Now it's a Transient Gunner with Ricochet Shot and Homing Bazooka, Typhon, and the Hyper Ring gear.
- Now comes the Expert, it's a Transient Witch. She has all of Ultimecia's Bravery attacks, two Hyper Rings with six Near Loss and two Near Death boosters, an unknown summon, a False Stalwart Assist, and of course, full equipment.

Ultimecia's Bravery attacks hit in front of her or on an angle forward, so Tempest is a pretty safe move to use, any attack she tries using will be deflected. If you're below her then go for Vortex instead. With the proper summon prepared breaking her to meet the KP Chance is easy as always.

She isn't very challenging overall, I used an early EX Burst on her to slice her HP in half, then whittled her down with Swift Attack - Meo Twister links. Sorry lady, but that's what you get when you challenge Zidane to a battle in a wide-open arena and like staying in the air using slow attacks.

Now to mop up the two last enemies:

- First is a Capricious Reaper with Ring holy, Burst Energy, Typhon, the Hyper Ring gear, and a Capricious Thief Assist.
- Finally, an Imaginary Brawler with Blizzard, the Hyper Ring, a Phantasmal Girl Assist, and an unknown summon.

With them gone that's 75 KP in the pocket.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Zidane mastered Rumble Rush during this gateway, grabbing his second Meo Twister link, and learned Stellar Circle 5 at Level 12. You know what it does but as a remind, it creates a tornado of energy around and above Zidane.

Pretty useless, there's not any reason I can think of where if I can hit with Stellar Circle I can't hit with one of Zidane's other attacks, which do more damage and may lead to HP linkage. But it can't hurt to equip if you have the CP.

</PanelSection>

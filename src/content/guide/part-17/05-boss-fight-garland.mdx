Garland is here, been a long time eh? Haven't fought him since the end of Treachery of the Gods. He's got all the attacks you remember from the Stalwarts up to now, and the Iron Giant summon to cleave your Bravery in to every time he gets an HP attack off.

His accessories are completely useless, he has a Guard Ring with six Near Opponent boosters. The Guard Ring reduces the wall rush Bravery damage Garland takes...except none of Zidane's current attacks inflict wall rush save for Vortex (I don't have it but you may) and his Chase attacks, and by nature of Ultimecia's Castle as explained further down, those attacks probably won't be able to wall rush him anyway.

Not to mention that most wall rush attacks tend to knock the opponent far away anyway so he's probably not going to be close enough to get those boosters off by the time he smacks the wall. Once again I wonder if the devs accidentally gave him the wrong equipment, maybe they meant the Guardian Bangle, the all-purpose defense bonus accessory? We'll never know.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Garland isn't really a strong enemy, and the terrain is on your side here. The problem is that here in Ultimecia's Castle, you start out the fight on the balcony, and all of Zidane's attacks outside of Scoop Art drive the opponent up and backwards, likely knocking them into a banish trap and preventing you from going into a Chase with them.

However, the banish trap cannot stop Meo Twister - smack Garland with Swift Attack and press Square to use Meo Twister like normal, Zidane will automatically teleport down to where Garland reappears to zap him. The banish trap will furthermore sap Garland's Bravery, so while he has a nice level advantage on you his Bravery won't last long.

The only real difficulty spike compared to Kefka is slightly higher HP and, unless you lure Garland to the lower levels, you probably can't use a Chase HP for wall rush. Otherwise just pummel him with Swift Attack - Meo Twister until he falls to around 800 HP, then let loose an EX Burst.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Zidane hit Level 8 from this fight and learned Vortex. It's an aerial attack that has Zidane spin upwards, then perform a combo to knock the opponent back, doing wall rush damage. Sadly, no HP link.

Vortex is alright, it has the same power as Tempest and good vertical lift, the problem is that its horizontal is non-existent. Even Cloud's Rising Fang has better horizontal range, and Cloud's Rising Fang does not have horizontal range. But it'll do to replace Scoop Art for a while until Zidane gets Storm Impulse much later.

</PanelSection>

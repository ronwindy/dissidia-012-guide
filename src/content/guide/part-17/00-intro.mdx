# Chapter 8

Part 17! Hey, my birthday was August 17th! Cosmic...no, not really actually.

#### The Thief Steals The Show

Another new character, we're running out of them eh?

---

<CharacterIntro name="Zidane" src="drake/Dissidia_Zidane.png" width={200} height={219} alt="Dissidia Zidane">

The monkey-boy thief is here! Zidane is my favorite hero in the series, just barely outranking Terra. While other heroes are bland, annoying and/or angsty, Zidane is cool, friendly, laid-back and witty, not to mention being a rampant flirt.

He does have his hesitations and insecurities, but he deals with them better than so many other character, overcompensating for them instead of being emotionally crippled. Overall he's just a really cool guy, characters like Cloud and Squall are ones you might enjoy doing a thesis on for a psychology class. Zidane is the character you'd enjoy going to a party with and just hanging out.

Apparently his English voice actor gets some flak, but I like it, totally different from the Japanese VA but I think it works. He sounds better in battle than in cutscenes, he sounds too deep in the latter, but then battles take up the majority of time you listen to the voice actors anyway.

And hey, if you want to wield a badass weapon, it doesn't get much more awesome than reverse-gripping two swords that can connect into a dual-bladed sword - just ask Gabranth and Xehanort.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Zidane is, in my humble opinion but this is my walkthrough so I'm right, the best character in the game. He excels at aerial combat, having a decent projectile attack, a diving attack, rising attack, forward spin attack, and a quick close-range keep away attack. His ground moves are limited, but they're so effective they're all he needs.

Zidane's "weakness" is that he doesn't do a lot of Bravery damage, though this is a bit of an informed attribute to me, he does plenty good damage to kick ass, and when you've got some HP attacks including links, who cares if the Bravery damage is lame, HP damage is what wins battles.

Sure he's no Squall or Cloud when it comes to his Bravery game, but he isn't as pathetic as Onion Knight so don't write his Bravery game off as weak - his Booster 8 to Storm Vortex hits just as hard as Squall's Solid Barrel.

Zidane starts out with:

- Rumble Rush on the ground: a quick rolling kick foward that launches the opponent into a chase
- Swift Attack: a quick two-hit close-range move that begins a chase
- Scoop Art: fires out three slow-moving energy bolts that track the opponent

Don't worry, he'll learn Tempest and Vortex before Level 10, giving him more options in the air.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

His HP attacks are likewise awesome, he's got a mid-range charge attack, a close-range explosion of energy, and a long-range entrapment attack of lightning and water. He also has HP links to boot.

His HP attacks are:

- Tidal Flame: sends a ring of fireballs spinning along the ground to explode in a fairly wide radius
- Free Energy: a very fast but very short range blast of energy
- Shift Break: creating a tempest of lightning strikes around the opponent before blasting them with water, very good for opponents who try to charge up attacks at range, like Starfall, Meltdown, Black Materia, etc.

As I said, I consider Zidane one of the game's best characters, he's my main, and he'll be back for the Epilogue and in Confessions of the Creator, so learn to love him. And you will, he may seem weak now, but once he learns his higher level attacks and gets his HP links, you'll be raining furry Genome death on the opposition.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

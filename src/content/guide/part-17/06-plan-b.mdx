#### Plan B

Now on Crescent Lake headed to the teleport stone we found in Firion's story, gather the nearby orbs of the dead and shatter the rock to cross to the island.

- Near the shoreline there's an Imitation Despot to whack, it has Thunder Crest and the ground Flare so be careful or it might hit you by fluke.
- The chests beyond it have Moogle Fur and a Triton's Dagger.
- Around up the hill is a Phantasmal Harlequin, on the hill over the lake is a KP Switch, and down the mountain path is a BRV Charge.

The KP Switch changes the KP Chance for the current gateway floor to a simple "Win Battle", but meeting the requirements so far hasn't been hard, eh? And if it is, moogles sell them with the other various skills.

---

Head south now to the Elven Snowfield region to find another familiar place. The moogle has little for sale, but if you need one get a Multichain. Now head into the blue gateway.

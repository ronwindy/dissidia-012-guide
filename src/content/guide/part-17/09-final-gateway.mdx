#### Final Gateway

As we enter the gateway, Garland speaks to Kuja about his fake memories and tells him none of them remember everything since being called. Kuja laughs then he has no need to feel inferior, in fact this is just more reason for him to strive to prove otherwise.

---

Now Zidane pauses to wonder what to do about his friends. Cosmos appears and after the obligatory pick-up line from Zidane, Cosmos assures him he's not powerless, he can have faith in his friends to be alright, like they have faith in him, and he shouldn't worry about them.

And she's right, I mean, so the last two chapters when the trio has split up has consisted of them getting captured and sent to enemy territory, or getting double-teamed by two villains at a time. There's no need to worry, because we have faith in each other.

Just because Zidane can't be there to help Bartz and Squall as they're assailed by Exdeath and a horde of Manikins (and spoiler alert for next chapter, they are) doesn't mean he should worry about them...seriously, why is it Zidane and Squall's chapters seem to twist the Power of Friendship idea into meaning you should worry about yourself and continue on alone not worrying if your friends are in danger in lieu of just hoping it will turn out alright for them?

Well, this is it. You've got two options, a Multichain at E4 or a Jump Chain at D4. The purple emblem is an Initial BRV Boost that affects both of you, so you may want to avoid it.

I had no Chain skills at all so I had to fight the Strange enemy first, then chain the three others manually. The blue emblem is a Booster x5, rather useful.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The Strange Enemy comes in with a Bravery boost and uses Watera, Crushing Blow, and has the Tiamat summon.
- Next, a Fallacious Tree with Delta Attack, the Hyper Ring gear, and an unknown summon and Assist.
- Third, a Delusory Knight with Nightfall and Saint's Fall.
- Last is a Capricious Reaper with Flare Star.

With these four taken care of the path to the end piece is open. The last enemy up at E1 is a Counterfeit Wraith with Tentacle of Scorn (the aerial tentacle that strikes you into the ground and fires energy orbs), Anti-Air Particle Beam, the Hyper Ring gear, and another unknown Assist and Summon.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

To leave this gateway with a lot of KP, the secret is...nothing! The purple emblem at E4 is a KP +1 emblem, giving us 18 KP alone for chaining the three enemies without any chain skills, compare to a potential 16 KP for using chain skills in a few configurations, plus 4 KP for chaining the last two enemies without skills.

I know you're itching to use that KP Gambler kiddies, don't worry, the time will come. Remember that saving it for more enemies yields exponentially more rewards - compare 48 KP to 75 for a 4 to 5 enemy chain.

I leave it up to you if you want to use either of those Charge skills yet, me though, I'm saving the Bravery Charge.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First up at the purple emblem, an Imitation Despot with Flare. The aerial version, good thing too since Zidane can't reflect the ground variant yet.
- Next is a Capricious Thief with Storm Impulse and Stellar Circle 5.
- The third enemy is the Hard one, a Capricious Reaper with Burst Energy, Flare Star, and the Hyper Ring set-up.
- The two Hard enemies near the end are a Fallacious Wanderer with Holy, Dark Flame, the Hyper Ring gear, and the Chocobo summon.
- The last enemy guarding the end piece of a Transient Lion with Mystic Flurry, Rough Divide, the Hyper Ring, and Chocobo summon.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Zidane mastered Swift Attack during these fights and learned Meo Twister, the HP link. After he sends the opponent flying, Zidane teleports in front of them and blasts them with lasers from his swords. It doesn't so much damage on itself but the damage stacks on the damage of the Bravery move, so it works out in the end.

As a note, in some arenas with weird walls and ceilings like the Chaos Shrine, Pandaemonium or Kefka's Tower, Zidane is liable to throw his daggers in such a way that an obstacle blocks one of them them, reducing the Bravery damage done and, I presume if both get blocked though it's never happened to me, not doing HP damage. Not a major problem but you're likely to see it happen a couple times.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As we open, Zidane and Bartz decide to turn the Crystal search into a treasure hunt to see who can get theirs first.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="objective">

The KP Chance is a 90 second win.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="assist">

Bartz is your Assist for the first two gateways, his aerial Assist is annoying through because it wall rushes the opponent *up*, and unless you're in a small arena like Kefka's Tower or Pandaemonium, the foe will be out of your reach.

Fortunately the attack lasts a couple seconds so you can land an HP hit during the animation if you're just using your Assist to get an opening for such.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Straight Chain on the purple emblem at C3, naturally. It's a KP +1 emblem, score!

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First, an Idle Sky Pirate with Crossbow, Shield \& Sword, and Inferno.
- Next, a Capricious Reaper with Ring Holy, Burst Energy and Flare Star.
- Third is a Capricious Thief with Rumble Rush, Storm Impulse, a forward spinning attack in the air, and Stellar Circle 5, a spinning tornado of energy on the ground.
- The last foe is a Fallacious Wanderer with Reel Impulse, Holy, and Dark Flame.

He also has the accessory gimmick of Zidane's chapter, a Hyper Ring with 9 BRV ≥ Base Value boosters. It gives them a pretty good damage boost at normal Bravery, but one hit and that 10.5x booster level drops to 1, so not very effective in practice.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Until Zidane picks up Tempest, I recommend relying on Rumble Rush for offense. It won't take long to hit Level 4, and Rumble Rush just flat does more damage.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

With the opposition cleaned up and 33 KP in your pocket, grab the chest for Destruction Resin and 1500 gil, then head to the exit.

</PanelSection>

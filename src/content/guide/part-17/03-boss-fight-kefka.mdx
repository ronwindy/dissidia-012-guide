<PanelSection kind="enemies">

This is a pitifully easy battle, Kefka's got a Cursed Ring that really cripples him. He's got the typical attacks and the Lich summon to begin a 15 second countdown to sapping your Bravery.

The catch is that the AI only summons Lich once you Break them, and you can easily land the HP strike to avoid losing your boost. Time it so you land the HP hit with 4 or 3 seconds on the countdown, then when it goes off and drains you, you're already in the process of regenerating your Bravery and Lich will effectively do nothing.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Because the fight always begins in the same location, here's a step-by-step process to how to fight.

- As the battle starts, Kefka will usually, but not always, hop onto the railing on your right and grind past you.
- Move right and block him to stagger him, then enter EX Mode and hit him with Tempest.
- This will Break Kefka and put you close enough to a wall that the Chase HP attack will Wall Rush him, putting him at less than 1000 HP.
- Now just land another Bravery combo on him or let Zidane's Bravery regenerate, then him him against and EX Burst.

My Level 5 Zidane (that first HP strike put him up another level) did over 800 damage with his Burst, so once you recover his base Bravery or use another combo as I said, Kefka shouldn't have enough HP to survive.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

So let's cover Zidane's EX Burst. As awesome heroes are want to do (see also: Terra), Zidane enters Trance to transform into a pink and purple superpowered being.

He gains the ability to jump an additional 10 times, on top of his normal jumps and any boosts to how many jumps he can do based on his abilities. He also becomes invincible while jumping, and after dodging his jumps "recharge", since Dissidia vets probably know or have realized that once a character dodges in D012, they can't jump anymore until they land or use a Quickmove indicator.

Admittedly Zidane's is one of the lamest EX Modes, his ability isn't useless, but it isn't really a stand-out help, the large open arenas where it should be awesome like Planet's Core, The Rift, etc have Quickmove options for quickly moving around the stage faster than jumping, and in other stages it isn't much needed.

Zidane's EX Burst is pretty easy, just tap Circle over and over as he bombards the opponent with attacks, the power bar is segmented but I'm not sure why, it stops for a moment at each increment but you have to keep tapping Circle so ignore this. Zidane has one of the weakest EX Bursts, Squall is the worst but above him it's the Onion Knight, then Kuja, and then Zidane.

</PanelSection>

Kuja has a pretty good set-up, two Earrings and an Attractorb with an Aerial booster and 5 Far From Opponent. He's got the Calcabrina summon to halt your Bravery regen if he Breaks you, a full set of attacks, and a Capriacious Thief Assist.

And if there's one character who can challenge Zidane's prowess over combat in the skies, it's the Graceful Glider. True he may not have any HP links, but he has a wider pool of Bravery attacks more effective over range, and his HP attacks are good just on their own. But then you already know how tricky Kuja can be.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Fittingly for a duel of aerial masters, the tide of the battle here can be kept in your favor by holding the high ground - metaphorically speaking - over Kuja. His attacks are most effective on enemies in front and below him, and even with Vortex, you do not want to be below Kuja, and if you forget why here's a Force Symphony to remind you.

If you can stay over his head then many of his attacks can't hit you, though there's still Strike Energy and Flare Star to watch for. Kuja tends to favor Snatch Blow, where he fires out his two Flare orbs, easily avoided if you have an altitude variation, then swoop down to strike with Tempest or spin up with Vortex.

And that, as usually, is his downfall, the dumb AI will keep using Snatch Blow when in the air even if you're out of its reach, and thus you can easily pummel Kuja to a bloody pulp. Go go EX Burst finish!

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

With Zidane proving his badassery, he obtains his Crystal, which is a better reason to get it than whatever half-assed backwards moral the game's story insists on feeding us. Kuja fades away as the two have some more painful banter, and through the power of deus ex machina, Zidane is teleported to Bartz and Squall.

---

Final note, Zidane hit Level 16 from this fight and learned Booster 8. It's an awesome move and Zidane's second worthwhile ground attack. Zidane takes a moment to coil his feet before launching forward and into the air.

It can't be blocked for one thing, the opponent will stagger instead of taking damage, but they're a sitting duck because of what Booster 8 is meant for - combos. After hitting, much like Squall's Thunder Barret, Zidane can quickly combo into another attack:

- Booster 8 into Free Energy for quick HP damage
- Booster 8 into Swift Attack for a Chase or Meo Twister
- Booster 8 into Storm Impulse for massive Bravery pain and wall rush

It has some recovery time to handle, but with proper usage it effectively lets Zidane combo any aerial attack from a ground attack and tack on extra damage and range.

#### The Trap

On the world map, you're in familiar territory, near the desert where we began Squall's tale - in fact, the gateway we just left is the same one Squall began in. So as we did there turn around and go back and to the right to find a False Stalwart that needs killing urgently.

- Zidane's Rumble Rush is his world map attack, it has decent range and lasts a second or so, so it's pretty effective to get first strike.
- The sealed gateway nearby is the one Squall took, but as I said we're going in a different direction.
- There's an EX Charge beyond the Stalwart.
- Going ahead now there's a familiar rock to smash to get to a familiar strip of land. The left end from where you grind over has a chest with a Pink Tail.
- On the mainland go through the mountains to find a stronger Stalwart to beat.
- The grass nearby conceals a Rosetta Stone, and the island far over the rock grindbar has a KP Gambler.

Now there's just the next gateway to tackle.

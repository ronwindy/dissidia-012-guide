#### Final Gateway

<Gateway>

<PanelSection>

I won't provide a map for this last gateway, the path is entirely linear and there's only four weak enemies, don't bother using any skills. There's also small conversations along the way, I'll let you enjoy them rather than sum them up.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The first enemy up is a Phantasmal Harlequin, he has a Hyper Ring and 8 HP = 100% boosters, so user an HP attack early to turn them off. He also has the Bahamut summon and Idle Sky Pirate Assist.

- The Phantasmal Girl is next, she has the same accessories and calls Hecatoncheir, but has no Assist.
- On the next floor is another Idle Sky Pirate with the same boosters, fought with Brynhildr and a Counterfeit Wraith Assist.
- And then finally a Counterfeit Wraith with a Transient Gunner Assist and the Leviathan summon.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chest has 1000 gil for us.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Lightning hit Level 27 here and learned Army of One, a Ravager attack with long start-up but very flashy and cool graphics, and a good range once it goes off.

As we move on there's a short scene where Vaan thinks back about Terra and how he never got her like he promised. Except he *can't remember her friggin' name and wonders if she's Tidus or Tifa before he gets it right*. God I want this kid to die.

Final line-up as we head to the third floor: Lightning Lv 27, Yuna Lv 20, Laguna Lv 23, Vaan Lv 21, and Tifa Lv 24. Your party is automatically fully healed when you get here. The path behind us vanishes, and now only the Chaos piece is here. I used an EX Charge before starting this, you can use whatever skills you have, they'll be active in all of these last battles.

<PanelSection>

There's a new type of enemy here, the Party Battle Piece. You fight them in one of two ways - Tournament or Round Robin, by pressing L to choose before you fight them.

- In Tournament, the two party leaders fight, whoever loses is replaced by the next member in-line and the winner fights them with however much HP they had left from the first fight. The winner fights the next person in line on the loser's team, and so forth until one team is out of members.
- Round Robin, the first two people fight, then second people in line fight, and so forth. Whoever wins enough fight for a majority win, for example 2 victories against a gang of 3 opponents, wins the overall conflict, and the remaining enemies on the losing team are beaten without a fight and KP rewarded for them instantly.

In both ways, you get a KP multiplier for enemy parties as though you chained them like normal enemies, and they in turn count towards the chain count when chained with normal enemies.

---

Now, naturally and obviously Tournament and Round Robin have their share of problems, soloing an entire enemy party with one person versus splitting the load among the entire group and possibly being given a poor match-up.

However, I tend to go with Tournament for a simple reason - when you fight in Round Robin, only the people who are going to fight get exp. Tournament, all five members get exp for the fight. Round Robin where you fight two opponents here for example, only the first two people get exp.

And in addition, soling all the enemy parties in this tale with Lightning, or whoever you're using up front, isn't difficult at all, in later storylines (read: Confessions of the Creator) the decision isn't so cut and dry, but here it's just more practical, especially since at the end of this tale you have to use all five people to fight and don't want them underleveled because you used Round Robin.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

As for the actual opponents, the lone enemy is an Imaginary Brawler, while the two Parties are:

- a Delusory Dragoon and False Stalwart
- and a Fleeting Flash and a Stalwart

The Stalwarts have a Hyper Ring and a series of timed boosters to give them more and more power as the fight wears off, so try and defeat them quickly.

For summons, one has Tiamat, which triples its Bravery after 15 seconds, and the other has Lich, which saps your Bravery to 0 after 15 seconds. None of these guys have any new surprises, but the enemy level in general here is a bit higher than in previous storylines, in the level 13-19 range with a few going higher. Still not very dangerous but be aware they aren't as big pushovers as you've gotten used to.

</PanelSection>

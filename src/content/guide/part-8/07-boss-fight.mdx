#### Boss Fight

You have to use everyone in turn to fight their designated opponent, and despite the similarities this isn't a party battle, you have to win all five fights. You'll notice everyone has a Level 60 armor piece on for an extra 2000 HP over you, save for Garland who is Level 39 and has that much without high-level armor because he's got 15 or so levels on us.

Throughout these matches be sure to shatter the Crystals around the arena, as the item Battlegened from this arena is what we need for the Muscle Belt we want to use in Cycle 013. If you need a refresher course on everyone's attacks, their name in the boss box links to their article so you can read about their attacks.

You won't have too much trouble with the first four, which is disappointing because you can tell the developers thought outside the box in order to try and make them a proper challenge, they just didn't think hard enough.

<PanelSection title="Kefka">

Kefka is up first, facing Vaan. He's got a Dragonfly Orb, Attractorb, Pearl Necklace, and 4 After 90 Seconds boosters for a whopping 81x multiplier when that time passes, allowing him to enter EX Mode and stay in it for the rest of the round. Thus it's vital you don't let the fight last that long.

He's got all his old tricks and Forsaken Null, which creates a field of energy that follows you around the arena, dropping exploding orbs on you. Dodge them and dash up to him because he's highly vulnerable at this point.

Aside from his higher HP and smarter AI, this Kefka is no different than previous ones so he shouldn't last that minute and a half he needs. If he does, well, you kinda deserve the intense pain his incredibly powerful EX Mode is going to inflict on you. By this time you kiddies should be good enough to win quicker.

He has the Tiamat summon and if he Breaks you he'll use it, so don't let him.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Ultimecia">

Next, Ultimecia and Tifa. She's a lot stronger this time around, though I'm not sure if she could be weaker than the first time. She has an Earring and a Muscle Belt with four Far From Opponent for a 81x booster when they're active, and for some reason held on to a Cursed Ring. She's got the Giant of Babil summon.

The problem is as always, the AI screws her over, it won't use those boosters well and will stay too close for them to activate, good thing too because her damage output is obscene when they're on.

Use the same tricks as last time, that is Beat Rush to Break and Chase HP for the Wall Rush to open the fight, then wear her down little by little. Just remember her charged Knight's Blade can't be blocked, so if you hear here call out "flash" and see her curl up and form a dark portal, get above her because it can't angle up to hit you. She's the weakest of this final round.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="The Emperor">

Third, the Emperor is back. He's got the same EX Mode accessories as Kefka, and if you're careless around his traps he may live long enough to get them off.

His summon is Brynhildr, it'll help you more than him by boosting the Bravery pool. His only new move is Dreary Cell, it creates a spark of light in the air, and when you get close it expands into a field of mines and explodes. Don't confuse it with his normal Mines.

Otherwise as always just watch out for his traps, keep two steps ahead of Flare and be careful. If he gets into EX Mode, *do not let him hit you*, his EX Mode bonus heals him every time he damages you, making killing him a lot harder.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Cloud of Darkness">

Fourth opponent, the Cloud of Darkness. She has Hecatoncheir and will use it to drain the Bravery pool and deny you a boost. She has a similar set-up to Ultimecia except with Close to Opponent instead of Far, and it's far more effective and gives her attacks ridiculous power.

This in tandem with the wide variety of attacks at her disposal probably makes the most difficult opponent of the run, though that Cursed Ring still cripples her.

- Use Laguna's Satellite beam to take advantage of the fact she's not able to dodge or move at proper speed when using an attack to blast her into the ground when she's defenseless, though be aware the giant crystal in the center of the arena will block the beam if she's beneath it.
- Remember to toss out Sticky Bombs and Missile Barrages to distract her as you go for the HP hits, and watch for the tentacle whips as warning of a Bravery attack coming so you can block it and counter.
- Pummel in midair is a quick hitting move and a good option if she charges up 0-Form Particle Beam and you're too close to get away, though you still have to be close enough to hit.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

And now the final boss, Garland. He's got a lot of tricks you haven't seen before, his high level gives him a ridiculous Bravery bonus on you, and in tandem with his innately high skills in spite of his lousy equipment, he is a worthy opponent.

However, the devs could have made him a lot more difficult, he *is* the final boss after all, give him a weapon and a real shield. Without a weapon and with the Cracked Shield, Garland's attack and defense are only about equal to the first four enemies, so the only real thing to set him apart from them in terms of difficulty is his high base Bravery due to his level advantage.

After 90 seconds pass he gets a 27x booster, raising the Wall Rush Bravery damage he inflicts by about 10 times its normal amount. And unfortunately, a lot of Garland's attacks do Wall Rush damage.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

And in spite of his slow movement speed, he has a lot of charging attacks where he suddenly moves quick, so be ready to dodge them. Blocking will only work on Round Edge if you're close, if he spins the second time he'll crush your guard, and Lance Charge can't be blocked at all.

His summon is Ultima Weapon. He'll probably use it once one of you is Broken, and it'll charge energy and 60 seconds later inflicts Break on you instantly. Nothing you can do to stop it and it's about impossible to tell when it'll fire.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Overall though, Garland is pretty simple to take down, as I said his only advantage is higher Bravery, which in practice means it takes another Smite or two to Break him.

I told you to give Lightning Cactuar and it will sap 1000 Bravery from him, draining him to 0 no matter how high his boost is, unless he's Broken you. Pummel him and use the EX Burst finish to knock Garland down and end this final gauntlet of disappointing battles.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

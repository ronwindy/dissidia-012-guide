#### Exploration

Back on the world map, approach the final gateway. The moogle has followed us here and hopes we come back safe. So do we, friend, so do we.

- If Lightning is under Level 28 in your game, buy Level Gap ≥ 10 from him, and if she's somehow Level 19 or lower, buy Level Gap ≥ 20 instead.
- Summons are pretty meaningless still, you may want to buy a few Rosetta Stones and give the party at least 7 accessory slots each, but aim for more, even 10 if you can.

---

Then equip everyone with the following: Power Ring, Hyper Ring, Pearl Necklace, Sunrise, BRV ≥ Base Value, After Summon, and After 30 Seconds if you didn't buy Empty Assist Gauge back when you could, if you did buy it use that.

- For Lightning, use Level Gap ≥ 10 instead of After Summon, or even better, get her an extra slot and use both.
- If people have more slots, use Attacking Bravery and Bravery ≤ Base Value, but don't feel you have to overdo it, save some Stones and KP for the next story.
- Equip Lightning with Cactuar, then the others with either Carbuncle or Ifrit, one other person can use Cactuar but not two, as it only has two charges and Lightning will need it.
- If you want you can also give everyone except Lightning a White Cape, if you have the extra slot.
- If you want a little bit of insurance, buy a Cure skill.

#### The Last Stand

Back on the world map, for the rest of the story we'll be without an Assist.

- If you bought it before, equip the Empty Assist Gauge Booster on your characters over Level 19 and thus can't use Level 10 - 19 anymore. I didn't have it though so I had to make due with the lousy 1.2 booster Attacking Bravery.
- Also ditch the Cyan Drop for a Pearl Necklace.
- Don't bother equipping EX Critical Boost with anyone, not worth the CP for the limited use you'll get at the moment.

---

During that last gateway Lightning picked up Ruinga, a decent projectile on the ground that explodes and is pretty good as far as mandatory projectile attacks go. Laguna got Electroshield, a good attack that creates a spherical forcefield, it can damage opponents on contact and deflect normal projectiles. Know the Emperor's aerial Flare? Think that, but as a Bravery attack.

As you can see all the enemies here are Strange, which means fun hijinks will ensue, being able to kill them all in one hit but the possibility of it happening to you being present. There's an Ephemeral Summoner, Transient Gunner, Phantasmal Girl, Idle Sky Pirate, and Delusory Dragoon.

- The Gunner has the Tiamat summon and a huge initial Bravery boost, and it uses Machine Gun and Ragnarok Blade.
- The Dragoon has the same Bravery boost with Jump and Bahamut.
- The Phantasmal Girl also has that Bravery set-up with Holy Combo, Ultima, and Cactuar.
- The Ephemeral Summon has those accessories for Bravery along with an Arcane Resin to begin with a full EX Gauge, an Iifa Leaf, and uses Thor's Hammer with the Mandragora summon. Be thankful manikins can't use EX Bursts.
- The Idle Sky Pirate has the same accessories as the first three with Odin for a summon, and uses Windburst, remember what can reflect it - Crushing Blow, Ragnarok Blade, Inferno, Rolling Blaze or Meteor Crusher. Yuna is still unable to reflect it except if it's close to the ground, in which case Hellfire can work, but it's too risky.

This gateway has a new KP Chance, Break the enemy within 60 seconds. It's pretty simple, but if you're having trouble then use Carbuncle or Cactuar to lower their Bravery, or if you still have a BRV 0 like I do, even better.

Cross Chain at E4.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemies are the lone Transient Witch and Fleeting Flash, the latter has those timer accessories and Lich.

The Party Piece consists of a Counterfeit Wraith with Fusillade Particle Beam, a False Stalwart, and an Imitation Despot with Thunder Crest, Dynamite, Flare, those accessories, and the Marilith summon.

---

The enemies with the summons and accessories have another new trick - Assists of their own now.

- The Flash has an Idle Sky Pirate and the Despot has the Transient Gunner.
- The Wraith uses a long-range tentacle thrust for its ground Assist and its whirling tentacle attack for its aerial Assist.
- The Gunner's aerial Assist is Shotgun and easy to avoid, but the ground Assist is Machine Gun, and if you get stuck on a Thunder Crest or Dynamite it will hurt a lot.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Marilith enacts a 5 second timer to freezing your Bravery, build up as much as you can before then so you can spam HP attacks without losing your Bravery.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Remember to block the Wraith's attacks rather than dodge them if you can, as this will Assist Lock the opponent and you'll instantly absorb all EX Force in the arena.

The Gunner's attacks are ranged though and don't lock it if blocked, so dodge and attack it directly, press L to switch target to the Assist.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

There's an old arena here you've been to, but only for the Kefka boss in Chapter 2 so it's sort of new, the Sky Fortress Bahamut. The Battlegen for it, the Sephira, unlocks the Earring in the shop with Summoner and Girl Battlegens.

The chest has 2000 gil.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

At Level 21, which of course I hit at this point, Lightning and I believe the others as well will learn EX Critical Boost. This is an ability that you'll learn many of, it costs a lot of CP to equip but when used, it further increases the chances of landing critical hits in EX Mode.

You'll learn many attacks like this as characters continue to gain in level, including:

- Sneak Attack (boost when attacking from behind)
- Riposte (boost when attacking staggering opponent)
- Counterattack (boost when attacking just after opponent has attacked)
- and Gambler's Spirit (boost when attacking while in Break)

Just to name a few. You can check this page to note the abilities and the boost to critical chance they give. With Lightning outgrown from the Level 10-19 booster,

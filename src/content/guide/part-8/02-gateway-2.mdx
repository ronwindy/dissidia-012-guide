#### The Final Path

On the world map, you'll quickly notice the Land of Discord is not as pretty to look at as the southern regions.

- Immediately backtrack to another area, a narrow land strip. There's an orb of the dead here and a grindbar to an island with another piece of Mythril. Sadly still not enough for the three Mythril equipment pieces we need to unlock their true power, but we're getting there.
- Head back to the Land of Discord and go left to find a chest with a Maser Eye, and a nearby moogle.

---

- He has several summons available but the best, if you want one, is Tiamat, Kraken and Marilith are useless and Lich is good but at this point in the game Cactuar is superior.
- He also sells some minor trade accessories, if you want to buy any then grab the Fish Scale, Zombie Rag or Ghost Rag to unlock various shop equipment.
- The boosters he sells are very expensive and not worth the price for their limited usefulness at this point of the game.
- Also buy one each of a Straight Chain, Multichain and Cross Chain, if you already have one of them then of course don't buy.

---

The gateway on the bridge is locked but we can see a chain of floating islands beyond leading to a gateway set into a volcano. Hmmmm....head back to the entrance of the Land of Discord and go the other way to the next gateway.

Kraken is a fun summon to counter, it triggers a countdown at the end of which you and your opponent swap Bravery, assuming neither of you has Break. Inflict an HP attack just before the counter hits 0 to steal your opponent's Bravery and leave them sitting at 0.

</PanelSection>

The feather is a new sight, the Phoenix Down. If a party member dies in a gateway, the Phoenix Down will revive them. But if your characters are dying, you're doing it wrong.

</PanelSection>

This comes to one problem I have with party battles, though it only matters in terms of wiki-work like this - they don't display a new move or ability when learned as a party member, they just mention you learned *an* ability, so if you chain enemies and you gain three or so levels at a time and learn more than one ability during that, well how do you tell what level you learned what at and record it on a page? Anyway.

- Vaan got Axe \& Shield, which...I don't know what it does, I told you I don't use Vaan.
- No one else has picked up anything aside from the typical support skills like Boost abilities, new Dashes, etc.

# Epilogue

Epilogue time!

#### Launch

For the entirety of this chapter, you'll command a party of five characters, Kain will be our Assist with the other five being the party. I used the party in order of Lightning-Yuna-Laguna-Vaan-Tifa.

When fighting in a party, at any time when fighting a foe if the first person is defeated, you can go to the next person in line to continue the fight with them, though the first member is still KO'd.

---

<Callout type="tip">

Before we depart, let's buy some new equipment.

- I bought a Silken Shirt, as all five can use it, then sold off my Pink Tails, the Metal Knuckles, a Chocobo Wing, and a few of the Battlegen items I had more than 10 of.
- I then bought a Mythril Bangle and a Wizard's Hat.
- Finally I bought two more accessory slots for Lightning, Yuna and Laguna. I put them first because at no time during this will you need to use more than three people until the end.

I gave Lightning a Capella, Mythril Bangle, Wizard's Hat and Silken Shirt, and for accessories a Power Ring, Hyper Ring, Sunrise, HP = 100%, BRV \> Base Value, and Level 10-19 for boosters, and the Cyan Drop. Sadly the Level 10-19 will quickly become useless.

Yuna, Laguna and Vaan got the same set-up, Tifa doesn't matter because we won't be using her until the end as I said. Now launch the chapter.

</Callout>

---

Lightning and crew are lost, thanks to Laguna, of course. Tifa and Kain show up and the four explain the situation. They know it's probably a suicide mission, but they have to try. Tifa and Kain naturally sign up to help, though Lightning, being Lightning, isn't enthusiastic about it.

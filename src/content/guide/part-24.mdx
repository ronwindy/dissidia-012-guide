# Chasm of Earth and Flying Castle

Part 24, finally taking on our main objective!

#### The Cavern of Earth

Bring along a **Multichain**, two **Jump Chains**, a **Cross Chain**, and a **KP Gambler**. If you have slots taken up by **KP Bonuses**, swing by the **Frozen Continent** Gateway and use them up **KP** grinding. Those five listed skills will be most useful, in that with them we can chain the floors that matter in terms of maximizing **KP** winnings. I also got **Inferno** Claws for **Prishe**, which **Level 90** weapons you can get depend on how lucky you got with the chests in the two gateways. If you got a Darksteel Ingot or two you can get the Brave Blade and Save the Queen, which together with Eureka Metal get **Excalibur**, a **Level 100** sword that, as a consequence of being a sword, most characters can equip. I mostly though still have my party with their **Level 60** stuff. Either way, also leave on the Diamond gear. Head to the Cavern of Earth and dive in.

I'm not going to cover the first two floors since the layouts are so simple, don't waste chain skills here. You'll need to move about a bit and wait for the enemies to appear. The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win, and the bonus line is 70. Don't worry, from now on "Bonus line" is just a suggestion, the upcoming gateways have **Level 97** and higher enemies, but the bonus line is 80. As we descend we hear the mured moogle deep in the gateway calling out to us to save him.

The first floor has a **Level 72** **Warrior of Antiquity** with **Zalera** and an **Idle Sky Pirate**, and a **Level 39** **Fleeting Flash**.

The second floor has a **Level 39** **Delusory Dragoon**, a **Level 74** Fallacious Giant with the **Gilgamesh** summon and **Fallacious Tree** Assist, and a Horror of Antiquity with **Hecatoncheir** and **Lady of Antiquity**. The normal chest has Destruction Resin, and the blue and gold chest has a **Level 60** trade accessory.

Behold **Feral Chaos**, a grotesquely deformed form of **Chaos** and a preview of the game's true final boss.

His **Bravery** attacks are Snarl, a quick two-hit slash on the ground, Spew, where he jumps up and slams the ground to create plumes of fire beneath you, **Brute Force**, a diving claw combo in the air, and Raid, where he descends rapidly to unleash a shockwave when he stops. All of **Feral Chaos**'s **Bravery** attacks wall rush or chase, depending on if you hit the wall too soon or not. Snarl and Spew can be blocked, but **Brute Force** and Raid cannot. **Brute Force** is his most used attack, it crushes your guards, has a good range for it, and does a lot of damage.

His HP attacks are Deus Iratus, where on the ground he blocks attacks and creates a ring of fire around him, **Ventus Ire**, where he angles up or down and then flaps his wings towards you to send out a gust of air, and Lux Magnus, where he shoots a beam of fire straight down in the air and can move to track you down. Deus Iratus can be avoided by simply not attacking him on the ground, while Lux Magnus, just keep dodging away. **Ventus Ire** is a bit trickier, dodge away but if you don't do it fast enough he'll hit you, since **Feral Chaos** likes using **Ventus Ire** after wall rushing you. **Feral Chaos**'s summon is **Shinryu** Verus, which copies the effects of the four fiend summons - **Bravery** freeze on you, **Bravery** swap, sap **Bravery** to 0, or triple his **Bravery**. And no that is not a typo, he has almost 60,000 HP.

The true difficulty of this fight is **Feral Chaos**'s accessories. He has Door to Despair, which gives him a 5% damage and defense boost, and Tyranny of Darkness, giving him 5% EX and Assist depletion. And then he has Waking Darkness, which after 60 seconds activates for a ***90x multiplier***. Suddenly **Feral Chaos**'s attacks are obscenely strong, he takes probably no damage from your attacks, and every HP attack depletes your Assist and **EX Gauges** to 0. Thus you effectively only have 60 seconds to fight, after that he will easily **Break** you and get 9999 **Bravery** with one **Brute Force**, and the next HP attack will kill you.

Now, when it comes to rating **Feral Chaos**'s difficulty, I must admit I personally have trouble with this. Anyone who follows me on YouTube knows I (at least I like to think so) have made a reputation killing him over and over and over. So I'm familiar with how his AI works and how to avoid and counter his attacks. This **Feral Chaos** doesn't fight quite the same as the real thing I'm used to, but the lessons stick. All you really need to do is get used to dodging his attacks. **Brute Force** is actually pretty easy to dodge once you learn how to see it ocming, and Deus Iratus and **Ventus Ire**, just keep your distance, which is why I brought **Terra**. *Normally* his defenses are too high to damage, but at 9999 **Bravery** the critical hit multiplier does 8x normal damage, so your critical hits will still hurt a bit. Otherwise when that booster goes off, just rely on HP attacks and only do **Bravery** attack to charge up your Assist. This is the one time I would consider using Resin-type items, but I didn't. And **Terra**'s **Kraken** summon swapped my 0 **Bravery** (got an HP hit just before the timer) with his 9999, awesome.

You have the entire party to share the burden, the only factor is if you think the current member did enough HP damage, because the remaining people have to share the rest. Also just keep in mind boosters don't work in **EX Bursts**, so feel free to go for the **EX Burst** finish as usual. Otherwise, just good luck, learn to predict his **Brute Force** and HP attacks, dodge them and counter, and you'll be just fine.

Immediately as we enter, the seal at D2 shatters to allow us access to the rare chest with 30,000 gil. I have to wonder why the seal is there at all, but a minor detail.

A **Multichain** at E3 will get many of the enemies, but the bonus line is 80, and since you're likely **Level 100**, that's a -20 penalty and overall only a 25 **KP** score, so don't waste a chain skill. Unless, of course, you have a **KP Gambler** with you, and if you don't go get one, in fact get two, we'll use one in the next gateway as well. The **KP Chance** is a 60 second win, go to the southern isle where the moogle sells trade items and attack skills and grab a **Maser Eye** or other skill to help you out if you think you need it.

The enemies are a **Level 97** **Fleeting Flash** with the **Odin** summon and a **Delusory Warlock** Assist, a **Level 97** **Idle Sky Pirate** with **Cactuar** and a **Warrior of Antiquity**, a **Level 97** **Imaginary Brawler** with an unknown summon and an **Imaginary Champion** Assist, and a **Level 97** **Delusory Dragoon** with **Alexander** and a **Fleeting Flash**. The usual enemies have come a long way haven't they? The Party piece is much less impressive, a **Level 71** **Lady of Antiquity** with **Carbuncle** and a Horror of Antiquity, and a **Level 72** **Warrior of Antiquity** with **Zalera** and an **Idle Sky Pirate**.

The normal chest has a **Diamond Ring**, and the rare chest has a random piece of Lufenian equipment. The Lufenian equipment is a set of **Level 100** gear, there's a piece for every equipment type, the combination effects are lame but in terms of pure stats they're probably the best stuff you can get at the moment. I recommend farming equipment pieces from this gateway a few times, kill the lone enemy at C5, grab the chest and exit and re-enter. Weapons are most valuable, since you may want to keep using Diamond or Gold equipment for their effects. The Lufenian items have one of two combination abilities, Seal or Judgement. Both are 3-piece abilities, one gives **Assist Gauge** Depletion, the other gives **EX Gauge** Depletion, but the devs didn't split the distribution of the two skills well, many characters flat cannot equip three pieces of Lufenian gear with the same ability, not to mention that as I said, the effects aren't very good anyway.

Head left beyond the gateway and shatter the rock to reach an island with an **Elixir**. Continue north to a second rock, leading to an island with our good old friend, RoboMog. He has several "Recipe" items, which are needed to trade for high-level equipment and accessories, one each, and aren't acquired anywhere else. Buy one of each, if you like, but if you just want the best that would be Gemini and Aries. The Gemini Recipe unlocks Dangerously Lucky, which increases item drop rate by 75% and acts like an **Iifa Leaf** too. The Aries unlocks the Miracle Shoes, which you can get now, they give a few minor helpful effects. Further north there's a chest with a Diamond, and of course the next gateway.

**Multichain** at E3 to get all enemies, I used a **KP Gambler** because why not? The bonus line is still 80, and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win.

Beyond this gateway we've reached the Mirage Sandsea. There's a gateway to tackle here, and a bunch of False Stalwarts on the overworld. The Imp stuff is another set of pointless combination gear, it does have a good effect, +15m **EX Force** Absorption, more than an **Attractorb** with a 5x multiplier, but it's only level 60, and as a four-combination set you can't use a good weapon with it. The Tomes, of course, unlock more shop items. Among the best is the Fake Mustache, the awesome First to Victory, and a quartet of accessories - Center of the World, Close to You, Together As One, and Side By Side.

The former two, one greatly reduces the rate at which your Assist charges but lets you always absorb **EX Force**, even while attacking or being attacked, and the other freezes the Assist gauge, preventing you from using an Assist at all, but gives a nice buff to the absorption range of **EX Force** and how much you get from Force and Cores. The latter two, once reduces the amount of **EX Force** you get but gives you an initial Assist charge and lets your gauge stay charged longer without attacking, the other freezes the **EX Gauge** but charges your Assist when you land HP damage. Together As One is one of the best IMO, as its negative effects can be dealt with, and its benefits are nice, and when combined with a Badge of Trust and First to Victory, you begin every fight with a full Assist gauge.

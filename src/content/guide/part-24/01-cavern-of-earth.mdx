I'm not going to cover the first two floors since the layouts are so simple, don't waste chain skills here. You'll need to move about a bit and wait for the enemies to appear.

The KP Chance is a 90 second win, and the bonus line is 70. Don't worry, from now on "Bonus line" is just a suggestion, the upcoming gateways have Level 97 and higher enemies, but the bonus line is 80.

As we descend we hear the mured moogle deep in the gateway calling out to us to save him.

---

- a Level 72 Warrior of Antiquity with Zalera and an Idle Sky Pirate

- a Level 74 Fallacious Giant with the Gilgamesh summon and Fallacious Tree Assist

The normal chest has Destruction Resin, and the blue and gold chest has a Level 60 trade accessory.

<PanelSection>

On this floor my entire party finally hit Level 100. When yours do, they should have all their attacks mastered at this point.

Now focus on mastering:

- the three Evasion Boost skills
- Counterattack
- Sneak Attack
- First Strike
- the four "Exp to ----" abilities

They do what they stay, instead of exp your character will get some HP, Bravery, Assist or EX Charge. Note EX to Bravery though is glitched when it comes to wall rush attacks, as there's a slight delay in its activation while your Bravery takes a second to drop to 0, and as a result Exp to Bravery only grants you Bravery based on the wall rush damage, not the base damage.

For Cloud and others, this sucks, for Zidane and others, this is awesome. Just keep in mind the conversation is actually a conversion, so if you have party members below level 100 and the leader has an Exp to ---- ability equipped, the rest of the party gets no exp.

</PanelSection>

#### Release the Seals

Terra is going to lead the way now, I bought her an Empyrean Rod for a stronger weapon, and also bought her a Royal Crown, a Level 100 Hairpin, it cost me a lot and I had to resort to selling Battlegen items, but it's the principle of the thing.

I also could have bought Prishe the Ehrgeiz, a Level 100 grappling weapon, but I needed way too much cash for both. Lightning and Cloud got the Kazekiri

---

Teleport to Cornelia and head to the Gateway to Departure. The seals in the gateways will be unlocked now, and we can access the chest with 30,000 gil...hm, maybe I should have come here first.

Then teleport to the Elven Castle and head east. Also note you can find Tifa near the castle and Kain near the Gulg Volcano, they say a line and the vanish. Spooky.

Next, the sealed chests:

- Go to the Gateway of Melting Sorrows and take the unsealed chest for a Pink Tail.
- Beyond at the Gateway of the Great Will, there's another sealed chest with a Pink Tail.
- Head up into the volcano area now and down the hill into the Encounters and Treason gateway, the sealed chest, of course, has a Pink Tail.

Now teleport to Crescent Lake and go north into the Cornelia Plains. Go north, shatter the rock to get to a Diamond, and approach the gateway - this is the sealed gateway the mured moogle was directing us too.

Equip everyone with the best equipment you have, and the following accessories:

- Hyper Ring
- Muscle Belt/Earring
- HP = 100%
- Near Loss
- BRV ≥ Base Value
- Summon Used
- Pre-EX Mode
- Pre-EX Revenge
- (Opp) HP ≥ 10000
- (Opp) Empty Assist Gauge

For summons, get stuff like Bahamut, Behemoth, Brynhildr, and Tiamat on everyone - essentially whatever gives you a massive Bravery boost. Also give them all the Cat Nip ability, the three Evasion Boost abilities, and if you have the CP, Counterattack. An Exp to ---- would be nice, but unless you've mastered one it costs too much CP.

Head inside, and prepare for what is easily the most powerful enemy you've yet faced.

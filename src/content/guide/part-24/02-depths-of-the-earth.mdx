<PanelSection>

As we enter the moogle calls to us again and says once we unseal him, he wants us to go to that gateway he sealed. This is, in practice, the last floor.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

The best way to chain enemies is a Straight Chain at G5. To get there, beat the enemy at E3 normally, then get your Assist out of the way and move to the square and use the chain.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

First, fight the Level 39 Delusory Dragoon at C2. The chest it guards has a random skill and might give you a Straight Chain. I got a Multichain, just as good.

Go fight the Level 43 Capricious Thief at E3, then open the chest at F5 to get an Iifa Leaf and open the path to your Assist.

In the chain:

- first comes the Party piece, a Level 42 Fallacious Wanderer, a Level 42 Phantasmal Girl, and a Level 82 Ephemeral Phantom with Malboro and an Ephemeral Vision as Assist
- Then comes a Level 80 False Stalwart with Behemoth and a False Hero Assist
- and a Level 81 Delusory Warlock with, shock and surprise, Giant of Babil and a Delusory Knight

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Grab the final chests, another skill and a random Force of the Resolute equipment piece. It's another 4-piece set of equipment you don't care about and don't want, it grants +30% EX Force Absorption and converts EX Force and EX Cores to Bravery. And is Level 30.

On the final floor, you can head right to the moogle, or open the red chest knowing it's an enemy, a Level 80 Fallacious Tree with a Fallacious Wanderer Assist, to get the two chests for more trade accessories.

</PanelSection>

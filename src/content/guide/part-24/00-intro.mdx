# Chasm of Earth and Flying Castle

Part 24, finally taking on our main objective!

#### The Cavern of Earth

Bring along:

- a Multichain
- two Jump Chains
- a Cross Chain
- a KP Gambler

If you have slots taken up by KP Bonuses, swing by the Frozen Continent Gateway and use them up KP grinding. Those five listed skills will be most useful, in that with them we can chain the floors that matter in terms of maximizing KP winnings.

I also got Inferno Claws for Prishe, which Level 90 weapons you can get depend on how lucky you got with the chests in the two gateways. If you got a Darksteel Ingot or two you can get the Brave Blade and Save the Queen, which together with Eureka Metal get Excalibur, a Level 100 sword that, as a consequence of being a sword, most characters can equip.

I mostly though still have my party with their Level 60 stuff. Either way, also leave on the Diamond gear. Head to the Cavern of Earth and dive in.

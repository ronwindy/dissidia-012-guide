#### To the Desert

<Figure side="right" src="drake/World_B_Mirage_Sandsea.png" width={250} height={260} alt="Mirage Sandsea" title="Mirage Sandsea" />

With Feral Chaos vanquished, the gateway is automatically unsealed, and we get notification of a new Report. I recommend doing it now, along with Report 18 if you haven't already.

The two fights here unlock the ability to Battlegen from Prishe and Gilgamesh, Battlegen items from them are needed for many Level 60 and 90 equipment pieces, as well as the Level 100 Genji equipment, so when you encounter their Manikins from now on you'll want to get your hands on the spoils they cough up.

---

Now on the world map, if you want to go back and look, Yuna, Terra and Prishe can be found wandering about, if they aren't your current party leader, and they have some pretty depressing things to say to you. For this next segment, Zidane is my party leader.

Head north to find a rock to smash to reach an island with a Diamond, and a teleport stone. There are several new locations on the map we can go to, but let's go by land for now. Continue north, and after a short time, you'll come to the Forgotten Trail gateway.

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The normal chest has a Diamond Ring, and the rare chest has a random piece of Lufenian equipment. The Lufenian equipment is a set of Level 100 gear, there's a piece for every equipment type, the combination effects are lame but in terms of pure stats they're probably the best stuff you can get at the moment.

I recommend farming equipment pieces from this gateway a few times, kill the lone enemy at C5, grab the chest and exit and re-enter. Weapons are most valuable, since you may want to keep using Diamond or Gold equipment for their effects.

The Lufenian items have one of two combination abilities, Seal or Judgement. Both are 3-piece abilities, one gives Assist Gauge Depletion, the other gives EX Gauge Depletion, but the devs didn't split the distribution of the two skills well, many characters flat cannot equip three pieces of Lufenian gear with the same ability, not to mention that as I said, the effects aren't very good anyway.

</PanelSection>

</Gateway>

---

Head left beyond the gateway and shatter the rock to reach an island with an Elixir. Continue north to a second rock, leading to an island with our good old friend, RoboMog.

He has several "Recipe" items, which are needed to trade for high-level equipment and accessories, one each, and aren't acquired anywhere else. Buy one of each, if you like, but if you just want the best that would be Gemini and Aries:

- The Gemini Recipe unlocks Dangerously Lucky, which increases item drop rate by 75% and acts like an Iifa Leaf too.
- The Aries unlocks the Miracle Shoes, which you can get now, they give a few minor helpful effects.

Further north there's a chest with a Diamond, and of course the next gateway.

As we enter this gateway we see a scene of Prishe and Shantotto warning that there's a new enemy type here that's not a manikin. Meet the Mirror Piece, at D4. They can't be chained and reward no KP, and when fighting them the enemy line-up is an exact mirror of your current party in every way, equipment, accessories, moveset, abilities, etc.

An easy way to outsmart them is to de-equip your party before facing them except for the lead member, then fight under Tournament rules - once the first enemy dies, your leader will fight the other four party members and they'll have no equipment, so the battle is easy.

Beyond this gateway we've reached the Mirage Sandsea. There's a gateway to tackle here, and a bunch of False Stalwarts on the overworld. They're all Level 123 with Ultima Weapon and False Hero, and full level 100 gear.

At the edge of the desert near the gateway is a Gold Angel, and beyond the Stalwarts on a cliff just outside the desert cliffs is a chest with a KP Bonus.

Well, now let's go look at that gateway, bring a Multichain, Straight Chain, Cross Chain and two KP Gamblers.

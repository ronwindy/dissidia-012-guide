On the fourth floor, I'm not providing a map. There are four Mirror pieces, and when you step on the tile just in front of the exit, eight chests appear, two each adjacent a mirror piece.

Each chest has a piece of Lufenian equipment, so fight the four Mirror pieces to access them. However, they're all red chests, guess what that means! Yup, we've got to fight for our prizes.

Clockwise, from top-right:

- Level 100 Lady of Antiquity with Strange set-up and Cactuar
- Level 100 Strange Ephemeral Phantom with Brynhildr
- Level 120 Delusory Dragoon with Ultima Weapon
- Level 120 Idle Sky Pirate with Warrior of Antiquity
- Level 122 Counterfeit Youth
- Level 120 Fleeting Flash with Ultima Weapon and Delusory Warlock
- Level 122 False Hero with Ultima Weapon and False Stalwart
- and finally Level 100 Strange Warrior of Antiquity with Tiamat

---

The spoils are two pieces of Lufenian gear, a piece of Imp Equipment, and three random Tomes - Adventure, Orator, Men or Silence.

The Imp stuff is another set of pointless combination gear, it does have a good effect, +15m EX Force Absorption, more than an Attractorb with a 5x multiplier, but it's only level 60, and as a four-combination set you can't use a good weapon with it.

The Tomes, of course, unlock more shop items. Among the best is the Fake Mustache, the awesome First to Victory, and a quartet of accessories - Center of the World, Close to You, Together As One, and Side By Side.

The former two, one greatly reduces the rate at which your Assist charges but lets you always absorb EX Force, even while attacking or being attacked, and the other freezes the Assist gauge, preventing you from using an Assist at all, but gives a nice buff to the absorption range of EX Force and how much you get from Force and Cores.

The latter two, once reduces the amount of EX Force you get but gives you an initial Assist charge and lets your gauge stay charged longer without attacking, the other freezes the EX Gauge but charges your Assist when you land HP damage.

Together As One is one of the best IMO, as its negative effects can be dealt with, and its benefits are nice, and when combined with a Badge of Trust and First to Victory, you begin every fight with a full Assist gauge.

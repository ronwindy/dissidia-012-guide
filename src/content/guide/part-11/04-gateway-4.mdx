This fight is pretty much just like the fight you had against Terra back in Vaan's story, the only difference is that you're in a different arena, and due to the costume change her Esper form is in grape flavor instead of blueberry.

She has that Puppeteer's Wheel keeping her in EX Mode for pretty much the entire fight, filling her EX Gauge with just a few motes of EX Force, but she also has a Cursed Ring, *of course*. She'll spam Tornado the entire fight, dual-casting it naturally since she's in EX Mode, but will never use her EX Burst.

This is pretty simple, she only has the ground version of Tornado, so get close, but not too close. This will bait her into using Tornado, back off, wait for it to end, then fire off a spell or approach as she lands and hit her with a melee attack. Very easy, no danger at all here.

---

I naturally used Onion Knight's EX Mode here so let's cover that. His EX Mode transforms him into a Ninja and a Sage, he'll automatically shift into Ninja for physical attacks (including all Chase attacks), and into a Sage for magic. His EX Mode powers up his physical attacks to insane levels, doing twice as many hits.

His spells change in slightly different ways:

- Thunder fires out five orbs of lightning instead of three, all around him instead of just in front too
- Blizzard fires further and thus can hit on a stronger angle
- Comet fires out more meteorites

Though he doesn't know it yet I'll note this so you know when the time comes, in his EX Mode his Firaga HP attack fires out three fireballs in a spread pattern instead of just one fireball straight ahead.

He has two EX Bursts, depending on if it was a magic or physical attack that initiated it:

- For a physical, the Ninja uses Ninjutsu
- For a magical, he uses Spellbook

For Ninjutsu, you need to open the Throw menu and select the Shuriken. Spellbook, open the Magic menu and select Holy.

The two EX Bursts have identical power, so there's no preference to either innately, it depends on if the opponent has equipment or accessories that reduces damage from one type and not the other.

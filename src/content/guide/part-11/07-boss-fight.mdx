<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The Cloud of Darkness has the same moveset as when we faced her with Laguna way back in Chapter 3 of the 012 story, but this time she has a level advantage on us and four Blue Gems, each of them giving her another 60 base Bravery. That 543 Bravery in the table is her base Bravery *before* factoring in the boost she gets from being at a higher level.

In short, she can rapidly knock off large chunks of your HP with little effort, so knowing her HP attacks and how to avoid them is crucial. Fortunately you've faced her and the manikins of her a lot by this point so you know how to do so. That same Bravery also makes it harder to Break her, and of course when you finally do there's Hecatoncheir to rain on the parade.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

0-Form Particle Beam is the worst threat, its range outstrips that of the Onion Knight's attacks, its damage priority will deflect or cancel out all his attacks, and at close-range it's hard to dodge and inflicts wall rush. Feel like eating 900 points of HP damage from one hit? Because it's probably going to happen if she fires off 0-Form while you're close enough for her to hit you with it. Unless you can hit her as she begins the attack, find a quickmove icon and run away quickly.

Ultimately though all her attacks are dangerous due to the sheer versatility of them and her high Bravery. She's not as stupid as you might have come to expect from the AI at this point, she one blocked my attack and fires off a 0-Form right in my face. Not pleasant.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Your safest bet is to land a blow as she tries a tentacle attack, block it and hit her as she staggers, remember your HP links if you've learned any. Onion Knight's HP attacks are slow so she's very liable to damage you as you recover if she avoids you.

She's ultimately not too bad because she still isn't *that* smart, just smarter than a lot of previous enemies. She gets a 2 on my template solely for her high Bravery letting you blast you into the Void with only a few hits. Otherwise, not much harder than any previous boss.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

With her defeated she fades into darkness, and Golbez arrives to speak to us again. Onion Knight vows to protect Terra from him, and for following his heart and proving himself his Crystal manifests for him. Terra awakens, though still weak, and fall unconscious again shortly after. The Onion Knight, tired after his battle, follows suit.

However the danger isn't over, because with the Cloud of Darkness's failure, Kefka is ready to take the stage and finish what she couldn't.

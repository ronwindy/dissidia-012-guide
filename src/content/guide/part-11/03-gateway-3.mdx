Use a Jump Chain right where you enter, or move to C2 and use a Multichain, to get four enemies. I prefer the Jump Chain, since if you need to buy the skill you're using it's cheaper, and the four enemies it chains includes the ones obstructing the chest.

</PanelSection>

- First, a Counterfeit Wraith, has a tentacle attack it didn't complete so I'm not sure which it was, and she also has those accessories again.

- The last enemy we didn't chain is a Transient Gunner with Missile Barrage and Satellite Laser, and of course he too has those accessories.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The accessory gimmick enemies like for this story are seven Near Loss boosters with a Hyper Ring. If you're nervous then use a super-powered blow to kill them in one hit.

Enter the opposition:

- The Ephemeral Summoner uses Aerospark, a ground charging attack, and Impulse, and uses two HP attacks, Energy Ray and Diamond Dust. She has an Assist, shockingly since the Hard enemies didn't, it's a Phantasmal Girl.
- Finally a Counterfeit Wraith, it has the ground lash and energy ball and the spinning tentacle aerial Bravery attacks, and the Long Range Particle Beam HP attack.
- Next, the Delusory Knight has those accessories with the Chocobo summon, which randomizes the Bravery of both characters. For attacks he has Shadow Lance, Nightfall, a quick downward dive that explodes on impact, and the Dark Flame HP attack.
- Next is a Counterfeit Youth, Onion Knight's manikin, he uses his Bravery melee attacks with the Magic Pot summon.

BTW, Onion Knight has Turbo Hit and Multi Hit for his attacks and they link to Extra Hit and Extra Slice. I hate when attack names don't help me remember - or describe - what they do, so I'll just say aerial combo and ground combo.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

As you fight just get use to Onion's attacks. His spells are useless until he gets their follow-ups, but with my AP boost he had them by the end of this gateway, it might take you until the next one to get them, once he has them he can use the follow-ups to begin a Chase.

Keep in mind the Muscle Belt only boosts physical damage, his spells will get the Hyper Ring's boost but that's it. Also note his Blade Torrent has surprising range both horizontally and vertically.

</PanelSection>

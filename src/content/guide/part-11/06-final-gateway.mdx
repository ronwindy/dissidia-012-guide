<PanelSection kind="chain">

Cross Chain at C3 to get them all, use that KP Gambler now for a smooth 75 KP. The blue emblem is a Booster x5, so your 3.3x boost will jump to 16.5x. Feel the pain, manikins.

<PanelSection kind="objective">

The bonus line is 14, and the KP Chance is a 90 second win, easy when you have such a lovely booster buff and the enemies are no higher level than Hard.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

We have a boss at the end of course but no Potion, so build up your EX Gauge during these fights, of course make the KP Chance in time but use Chases and such to charge up. Feel free to take HP damage since you have Cure to heal before the boss.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First, a Counterfeit Youth, it has Blade Torrent for an HP attack and Onion Knight's melee Bravery attacks.
- Next, a False Stalwart. It has that Hyper Ring with the Near Loss boosters, uses the Carbuncle summonstone, and has Earthquake, once again just stay in the air and you'll be fine.
- Third is a Counterfeit Wraith, it uses two tentacle attacks, the spinning one on the ground and the spherical whipping one in the air, and Long Range Particle Beam for an HP attack. And of course it has those accessories, and the Magic Pot summon.
- Next is a Phantasmal Girl, she has those accessories too with Carbuncle, uses Blizzara, and has Tornado as an HP attack. Stay away from her when she's in the air, don't want to get hit by accident right?
- Finally, another Counterfeit Youth, armed with Blizzard, Thunder, and Firaga for his HP attack.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="warnings">

Firaga is only dangerous if you're at the right range for it to arc and hit you, further or closer and no problem, if you are in the butter zone though it can explode large enough to hit you even in the air, so if you see it charge up Firaga, run away or dash towards him, depending on your distance, use your best judgement. You can use Firaga yourself in the lead-up fights if you want an idea of how far it fires and thus how to avoid it yourself.

</PanelSection>

#### The Pursuit

Terra senses a strange presence, and the Knight is eager to check it out in case it's a Crystal. Terra is nervous for some reason though, but he assures her if they find something unpleasant, he can handle it.

---

On the world map, look on the beach immediately behind you for a Rosetta Stone, then go ahead and to the right. Far to the left as you emerge is a chest with 1000 gil. At the ruined castle, shatter the stone and cross to the island for an Assist Charge.

The Moogle has some useless summons for us, and also sells those five EX and Assist boosters. We want the Empty Assist Gauge booster, but I can't afford it at 100 KP, if you can take it, if not, we'll get it soon, don't worry.

We have that KP Gambler waiting for a gateway with five enemies. Buy a Round Chain, if the four orbs of the dead here didn't give you one.

- Then a Fleeting Flash, she uses Launch, Smite, Aerora, has a False Hero (Warrior of Light) Assist, the Chocobo summon, and those Near Loss accessories. Not a threat, as a note though I just got lucky here and Battlegen'd a Mythril piece from her, maybe you got lucky with her or a previous foe? Well sorry, I'll cover this quickly when we're done.
- Then the Strange Battle Piece, the False Hero. He uses Shield Strike and Rising Buckler with the HP links for both, has a big Bravery boost, uses Carbuncle to sap your Bravery when he lands a hit, and has a Phantasmal Harlequin for an Assist.
- With it dead mop up the Capricious Thief, it has Scoop Art, Solituion 9, uses Shift Break for it's HP attack (so don't use Comet just in case), and has the Magic Pot summon, those accessories again, and an unknown Assist, he never attacked enough to charge up his gauge for me to see who it was.

<PanelSection>

As we leave the gateway, the two find Exdeath. The Onion Knight is promptly intimidated and decides to play weak, telling him that they're too weak to give him a proper fight. Exdeath knows it's an act but decides to let them go, warning the Knight that cowards will never find the Crystal. He leaves, and the Knight boasts he never fights opponents he doesn't know for sure he can beat.

Terra thinks Exdeath came more to deliver that message than to fight, but the Knight is ticked and insists fighting a stronger foe isn't courage, it's reckless, and he'll prove he's not a coward by finding his Crystal. Exdeath meanwhile tells Kefka Terra is out of his grasp, but Kefka isn't listening.

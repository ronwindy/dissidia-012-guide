#### Rescue Operation

That fight with Terra has benefited us though, letting us Battlegen a Girl's Desire from her. Now we can finally trade for an Earring! With it, if you want you could take out the Hyper Ring for the Earring on Onion Knight, his physical attacks will be weaker but his magic will be stronger. I leave it to you to decide based on how you're using him.

As a note the next parts of this walkthrough were done on a different day then previously, so no more AP bonus, aw.

---

Hidden behind the grass on the right is a BRV Zero. The moogle nearby has nothing worth buying.

On the shoreline ahead is a Counterfeit Wraith, she uses Anti-Air Particle Beam but has no notable traits, no summon or Assist, etc. I Battlegen'd an Alarune Twig from her somehow, it unlocked only the Gaia Bell, useless.

The nearby chest has 1000 gil. Up ahead break the rock and cross to the island for another 1000 gil. And now, the next gateway.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

I wanted to hang onto it for the last one, but sadly kiddies, I lacked the KP for that Empty Assist Gauge booster, and I wanted it, so I had to use my KP Gambler here. Use a Straight Chain at D2 to get them, you could also use a Multichain at C4 but if you need to buy a chain, go with the Straight, with the Gambler this got me 48 KP. Don't worry kiddies, there will be other gateways, other Gamblers.

The blue emblem is a magic critical rate up, purple is Stage BRV boost.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="objective">

Bonus line is 12 and the KP Chance is a 60 second Break, not a problem if you feel like using that BRV Zero we found. Be sure to take the time to build up an EX Gauge in the first three fights, check out what's at E2.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Enemies are first:

- A False Stalwart, it uses Earthquake, easily avoidable by just staying in the air.
- Next, a Capricious Thief, it uses Shift Break so just don't use Comet to avoid getting caught in the blast unable to dodge.
- Third is a Phantasmal Girl, she uses Flood as an HP attack to create blasts of water under you, long start-up and easy to avoid. Her Bravery attacks are Fire and Blizzara, and she has the Hecatoncheir summon and those Near Loss-Hyper Ring accessories.

Last foe is of course that Expert Battle Piece, a Phantasmal Harlequin. It has proper equip, the Marilith summon to activate a countdown to freeze your Bravery, uses Kefka's two Firagas, Lickity-Split Thundaga, Scattery-Spray Blizzaga, Ultima, Meteor, and for HP attacks has Hyperdrive, Trine and Havoc Wing.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

I let him get in a Bravery hit, then used Comet so I could deal HP damage without Breaking him. The AI will use Marilith once you use an HP attack and fall to 0 Bravery, and he did, then I broke him and got my Bravery frozen with the boost he gave.

The EX Gauge was used for EX Revenge to ensure I could hit before the freeze went off, the summon timer stops while in EX Revenge. With my Bravery locked at 900 I cornered him on the turret on the end of the stage where he couldn't dodge properly and spammed Comet to kill him.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

During this round of fights the Knight learned Swordshower, his HP link for his ground Bravery attack, it called a barrage of energy blades to bombard the enemy before he leaps up and knocks them back. Sadly I couldn't equip it until after the chain was done.

He also got Firaga at Level 11, which launches a fireball forward in an arc that explodes in a large radius on impact, has long start-up but gives him a ranged option on the ground and is more reliable than Comet.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Grab the chest for 2000 gil then leave.

The only way to free her is to fight the Cloud of Darkness, but he's afraid to do it, so Cosmos pretty much tells him "stop being a wimp and follow your heart" and leaves.

And now Golbez arrives and agrees with her, he's too weak to do it so why be foolish? But he's not sure so Golbez repeats the "listen to your heart" moral.

</PanelSection>

On the world map I first ran back to the moogle and bought that Empty Assist Gauge booster, of course. It replaced BRV ≥ Base Value, giving me a 3.3 multiplier instead of 2.9. If you're having trouble avoiding HP damage though, use it in place of HP = 100% and keep BRV ≥ Base Value.

---

Continue through the gateway to find a forest with the final gateway beyond in, far to the right of the gateway is a chest with Cure. On the shoreline is a stone to break with a Counterfeit Wraith nearby, she has the Shiva summon and uses Anti-Air Particle Beam.

On the island is a chest with another KP Gambler! Yay, the universe answers my sadness at having to use the last one early! If the orbs of the dead don't give you one, go get a Cross Chain from the moogle before entering the gateway.

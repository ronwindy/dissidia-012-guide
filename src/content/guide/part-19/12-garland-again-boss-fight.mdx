Well, let's look over how much Garland has beefed up - a lot, of course. He's got the Flamberge and Shielded Armor for minor boosts to his attack and defense, and his stats are significantly higher than the Emperor's. He's packing a Hyper Ring and Muscle Belt hooked up to 7 Near Opponent boosters, giving him a pretty hefty damage bonus whenever he attacks with a melee move, which is most all he has.

He also has Hecatoncheir, the eternally annoying summon that will sap the Bravery pool to zip to make the first Break you inflict pointless. And finally he has the highest HP of any boss so far, and the highest level as well.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

That said, and I hate to say this kiddies....he's a wimp like always. The AI is just never very good at dodging or block attacks or being aggressive and taking advantage of my vulnerability when it does. All that really makes Garland a stand-out opponent is his higher HP and accessories, otherwise he's just as easy to take down as any other foe.

Don't worry about losing your first EX Mode, the fight will go on long enough you'll be able to recharge it for a Burst finish like always.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

The Warrior hit Level 19 from the fight and learned Ascension, effectively a ground version of Crossover, complete with a Rune Saber HP link once you master it, it just has slightly higher power in EX Mode due to summoning more swords of light.

---

As Garland begins to fade away, the Warrior receives his Crystal for...um....I'm not really sure, the entire point of his story has been to emphasize he has no doubts or hesitations about fighting, so what personal trial did he conquer again? Anyway, Garland also warns that there's no telling what might happen to the gods and the world when all the Crystals are gathered, and vanishes with a laugh.

Then the other Warriors of Cosmos show up, all with their Crystals now, and they set off to return to Cosmos.

As they return Cosmos has a flashback of Shantotto and Gabranth, rather pointless since we've seen the full thing. Then the Warriors approach her with the Crystals, but Cosmos suddenly collapses and the world shifts from the light of Order's Sanctuary to the hellish nightmare of the End of Madness. Chaos appears, and wastes little time incinerating Cosmos in a pillar of fire.

With the goddess slain Chaos departs.

---

So ends Chapter 10, now, only the epilogue awaits us.

#### A Friend In Need

We get a narrative screen telling of the Warrior running across Cecil and Tidus. Firion has apparently run off alone to pursue an enemy, and with the two of them injured the Warrior goes after him in their place. Go ahead and smash the rock to reach the island with a chest containing 2000 gil.

Go past the moogle and to the right to the sealed gateway to find a Maser Eye. I bought ten Rosetta Stones (preparation for the Epilogue) and two Multichains from the moogle, I got a Jump Chain from the orbs but if you didn't then buy one of those too. Then dive into the next gateway.

As we enter the gateway, we get a largely nonsensical scene of Golbez and Sephiroth. And it's not just in D012, it didn't do much for the original Dissidia either.

The bonus line is 4 and the KP Chance is a 60 second Break.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

Anyway, Jump Chain at C4 to get all enemies here. Sadly, that physical critical rate up emblem has to be overlooked.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First foe to go down, a False Stalwart, Round Edge, Twin Swords and Earthquake are the moves of the day.
- Next, a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe, Knight's Spear, and Great Attractor.
- Next, a Capricious Reaper with Snatch Shot, Burst Energy, the Tiamat summon, and the Hyper Ring timed accessories.

I found it hard to Break him, he was pretty good about blocking and evading my moves and had a decent amount of Bravery. Are the enemies perhaps finally smartening up? It'd be about time.

The Strange enemy, a Transient Lion, has a pretty evil set-up. He's got the standard 1 HP, though with 0 Bravery to go with his summon, Carbuncle, but his complimentary accessories are four HP = 1 boosters that are 4x each, and a Guardian Bangle. As a result, your Bravery damage output on him is greatly reduced.

For moves his uses Fusillade, Mystic Flurry and Rouge Divide.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

If he gets first strike and Breaks you (since he'll use Carbuncle immediately to put both of you at 0), you'll have a hard time Breaking him due to his defense boost. As a result it's vital you get the first strike. Fortunately melee attacks will just cut through his spells.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Our new summonstone is Bahamut, who in a nice throwback when taken invokes the same lines he said in FF1 when bestowing the Warriors of Light with their upgraded classes. When your Bravery falls below half its base value, Bahamut arrives to boost you back up at 75 points a second, pretty nice though the manual version is much better.

</PanelSection>

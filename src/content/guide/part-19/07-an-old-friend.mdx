#### An Old Friend

On the overworld map, go ahead and left to the shoreline to find a chest with 2000 gil. On the main path at the blue gateway you can find a moogle, he has Leviathan and Bahamut if you haven't bought them in previous storylines. His boosters are largely useless, the best is the (Opp) Break one, but it's a 1.3 and we already have a couple of those we can use.

Go ahead and buy it if you like it though, I did. Also get a Multichain if you lack one from last time and dive into the blue gateway.

#### Exploration

Back on the overworld now, 25 KP richer, we continue on to that island over the grindbar. There's a Capricious Reaper and a Phantasmal Harlequin here who need killing, oblige them. The fights mastered Crossover for me thanks to a calendar bonus for AP, and the Warrior got his first HP link, Rune Saber, linking from Crossover.

Awesomely the link occurs after the upwards stab, so Crossover's high damage isn't compromised, and Rune Saber itself tacks on more damage. An EX Mode Crossover to Rune Saber is a game end, kiddies. Even if Crossover knocks the foe into a ceiling, you can link to Rune Saber and if they don't dodge away as they recover, Rune Saber will still hit.

At the end of the island is a Black Flan Membrane.

---

Back on the mainland through the mountains is another Harlequin to beat up. The rock nearby produces a grindbar to *two* chests for a change, a Rosetta Stone and a KP Gambler. Now follow the path through a ruined temple to the next gateway.

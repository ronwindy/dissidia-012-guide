As the Warrior tries to leave, yet another Chaos Warrior with a death wish appears. Ultimecia taunts that his efforts are in vain and he should give up his fool's errand. The Warrior once again tells her to move out of the way or take a sword to the face.

Guess which option she picks. Hope the infobox doesn't spoil it for you.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Ultimecia has significantly more dangerous equipment set-up than Sephiroth or Garland. First, she's a ranged fighter, so she has that advantage even if the AI is bad at using it. She also has a much better weapon then they do, giving her almost an extra 10 attack points.

Her defense is the same though, the Poncho cancels out the boost she gets from her Bronze Bangle and level. She uses the Bomb summon, which you haven't seen in some time. When she calls it the Bomb builds up energy before exploding, sapping Ultimecia's Bravery to 0 and inflicts damage to your Bravery equal to hers, Breaking you if your Bravery is below Ultimecia's.

Her accessories are an Earring and Attractorb with six Far From Opponent. I'd toss in a Pearl Necklace or a second Earring but that set-up is workable.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Ultimately though, while Ultimecia has better gear, she still has weak defenses and is easy to Break, which coupled with her AI makes her a pushover. The only annoyance is that it's hard to wall rush her since Shield of Light is so slow and doesn't stagger her when it blocks her moves, and Crossover for a Chase puts her up into the banish trap. Fortunately you have Dayflash which begins a chase by knocking her backwards, letting you get into a Chase without knocking her into the trap.

From there do the usual pattern, Break and wall rush, let her recovery, EX Burst to finish her off.

</PanelSection>

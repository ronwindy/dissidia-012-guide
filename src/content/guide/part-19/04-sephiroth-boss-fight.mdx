As we reach the end we find Firion tired from the fight with Sephiroth. The Warrior sends Firion off and takes Sephiroth off alone, once again ignoring Sephiroth's little taunts and just saying "let's get this over with".

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

On the one hand, Sephiroth's level and equipment are on-par with Garland's last time, and we just cut down another gateway of enemies to level up a couple times. On the other hand, Sephiroth was watching those manikins and swiped some of their stuff for himself. He's got a Hyper Ring, Attractorb and Pearl necklace hooked up to one each of the five varieties of timer boosters the enemies have been using, only one each so he won't get as big as quickly, but he's got a lot more HP so he might live to get the 60 and 90 second ones off...except he also has two Cursed Rings, and no shield.

So his defenses are even worse than Garland's and thus you'll easily be able to rack up enough Bravery to wipe him out long before those boosters activate. He also has the Cactuar summon, annoying as you know from using it yourself, but it only works once, and 1000 Bravery isn't much when my EX Mode Crossover did 700 damage.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Overall Sephiroth is even easier to beat than Garland, his accessories have potential but those Cursed Rings cripple him as they always do to any opponent. Combine this with the AI's usual incompetence, and this is a boss not long for this world. When I finally did my EX Burst it did over 1200 damage without a Break, so Break him, let that pesky Cactuar do its work, then let him recovery and deal an HP hit to go into the Burst.

Piece of cake, he'll be lucky to get the 45 second one off, nevermind the stronger two.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

With Sephiroth trounced, he somehow emerges unharmed, since only Cloud can kill him, for some reason they never establish, it's just another throwback. Sephiroth asks why the Warrior fights, and he of course answers he fights to end the fighting. Sephiroth is amused by this idea and says he just enjoys it, just like Sephiroth does.

He shows the reason Firion chased him - the wild rose, of course - and takes off when he senses Cloud approaching. The Warrior wonders what Sephiroth meant, but yet we must continue on. Take it from me dude, listening to Sephiroth's ramblings can only lead to madness, just tap X until he shuts up and goes away.

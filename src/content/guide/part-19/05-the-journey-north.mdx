#### The Journey North

Now the Warrior continues on. Go ahead to the sealed gateway to grab 2000 gil, then head down to the shoreline to the path to the north. Shatter the rock and cross to the island for a Cure skill.

Now just head to the next gateway on the passage to the northern regions.

<PanelSection kind="chain">

There's no way to get all four enemies, but we can get three with a Cross Chain at C1. A Multichain at B2 also works but that doesn't quite cover the KP costs of that Multichain, and we need two Multichains in later gateways to hang onto them. If you lack a Cross Chain you can just go back to the Pravoka moogle and buy one.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First comes a Transient Witch with Knight's Axe and Great Attractor.
- Next comes a Delusory Dragoon with Cyclone, Crashing Dive, Gungnir, the Hyper Ring timed gear, and the Kraken summon.
- The second Hard enemy is an Imaginary Brawler fond of Moonsault Kick, Burning Arrow, the same timed boosters, and the Marilith summon.

Marilith is the most dangerous of the four fiend summons since it freezes your Bravery on a short 5-second timer, and the AI will call it once you land an HP hit, leaving it likely you'll be frozen at low Bravery, so quickly go on the offensive so at least you can spam decent HP attacks once the freeze kicks in.

- The last enemy the Cross Chain misses is an Ephemeral Phantom with the midair Jecht Block and Jecht Blade.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

The Warrior hit Level 8 during this round of enemies and learned White Fang. It's an excellent replacement for Blue Fang, sending out a wave of lightning bolts forward, and can set the opponent up for melee moves. It's not as awesome as Squall's Thunder Barret, but it works well enough.

There's also a nifty glitch you can try out with it while in EX Mode - use White Fang, then use a melee move as it hits the opponent. If you time it right, the swords of light from the melee move will appear to strike the opponent, and depending on the move, bring them towards you. Yes it's technically cheating as a glitch, but I say hard work should be rewarded and it's tricky to pull off.

</PanelSection>

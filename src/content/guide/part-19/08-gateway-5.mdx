<PanelSection kind="tips">

As there's no boss feel free to go all-out here with your EX Mode and EX Burst. Naturally land an HP attack early to turn off those boosters, then whittle him down, remember to use Chase attacks and be aggressive to prolong your EX Mode once you activate it, it helps to attack a lot and get lots of EX Force out there so once you enter EX Mode, a single Chase will absorb all of it at once and give you a bigger boost.

It's a touch fight but you can do it, remember to use Shield of Light to block Golbez's close-range hand swings and score a free HP hit.

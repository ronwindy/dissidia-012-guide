<CharacterIntro name="Warrior of Light" src="drake/Dissidia_Warrior_of_Light.png" width={150} height={258} alt="Dissidia Warrior of Light">

The Warrior of Light, along with the Onion Knight, is an original character created solely for Dissidia, based on the artwork for the original Final Fantasy but having no character background to base him off of. As a result he's pretty bland but this is arguably the point. He's not prone to angsting or contemplating his naval in indecisiveness like the other cast members, he's just devoted to Cosmos and will do whatever he has to to protect her.

As the later Reports in D012 will show, there's a reason his personality is so flat, and when you connect the Reports, to the main story, to the ending of the 013 cycle, you get a pretty creative character arc.

The Warrior is a pretty balanced character, he last the power, speed, and technicality of others, but makes up for it with versatility. He has short and mid-range combo attacks with decent power and several HP links, and versatile HP attacks.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

His initial attacks are Dayflash, a quick three-hit slice that begins a chase, and Blue Fang which calls a hail of ice on the opponent, good for setting up combos. His aerial attacks are Crossover, a spinning forward shield attack with an upward stab to finish and begin a chase, and Shield Strike, a downward shield toss before he dives and knocks the opponent in the direction he came from.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

HP attacks are Shield of Light, a short-range blast of light that also defends from attacks, and he automatically aims at the opponent when using it, and Shining Wave, sending a burst of light energy at the opponent and tracking them into the air and through walls. Shield of Light is the best one, the others have long-start up and AI opponents later in the game will dodge them easily, the Shield though will stagger them as it blocks their attack and leave them vulnerable.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Overall the Warrior is a good character, not great, but not bad, well-suited for the basic hero archetype. Use Blue Fang (and later White Fang) to set opponents up for combos and then dash to them and use Crossover or Dayflash, depending on your preference. When he begins to master his Bravery attacks and pick up his HP links, rely on them to whittle down the opponent's HP faster than his slow and limited HP attacks.

Overall though, as I said he's balanced, other characters are faster, or do a lot more damage, or are just more fun to play as. But if you like him, enjoy him.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

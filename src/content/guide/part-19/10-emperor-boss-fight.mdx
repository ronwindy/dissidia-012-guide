His highness doesn't have much higher attack power than Ultimecia, but has a higher level and much higher defense. He's got an Imitation Liegeman Assist, potentially tossing out three Fires you need to worry about in addition to the usual traps. He's also got the Malboro summon to sap your Bravery, it gets stronger the closer you are, not something a melee fighter likes.

For accessories he's got a good line-up, an Earring, Attractorb and Pearl Necklace connected to an Aerial, two HP = 1, Near Death and a Near Loss, giving him a pretty hefty boost at 1 HP. And he will get it because he also has an Iifa Leaf. His moveset is the usual tricks he always has.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

The Emperor is pretty tricky as always, he has a good accessory build and gets very tough when weak. That EX Gauge of his will fill pretty quickly when he's got a 5x booster powering up the Attractorb and Pearl Necklace, and once in EX Mode sure he'll probably lose those boosters, but it's because he'll be recovering HP, and if he hits you with an EX Burst, he's liable to Break you and get a lot of health back.

And as with Ultimecia's Castle, Pandaemonium isn't exactly the best arena to make proper use of Rune Saber. You can still use it, the Emperor just may recover and dodge away before it hits. All in all, he isn't too tough, the AI is stupid as always and while his near-death power boost is nice, he's still near death.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

#### Exploration

On the world map, head forward and take the right path at the split, there's a Counterfeit Wraith to take out. Beyond it is a chest with a Cure skill. Head back and take the path we know from Bartz and Zidane's tales that leads to the floating islands.

Along the way is a chest with a KP Gambler, a treasured rarity now because there are none to be found in the Epilogue, so we're going to hang onto it for deep in that storyline. Cross the bridge and head right to find a chest with a Delicious Fish Scale and an Ephemeral Phantom. Once it's dead ascend the floating islands to reach the last gateway.

They don't waste any time at all, eh? Garland isn't some wimp at Level 10, even if you're only at Level 3 he's not holding back...well he is somewhat, but he's still pretty tough. He has no summon or Assist, fortunately.

His accessories consist of a Hyper Ring with 4 HP = 100% boosters. Landing an HP attack to deactivate them should be your first priority of this conflict. Otherwise he has the typical moveset, and good Bravery due to his level advantage, though fortunately his equipment isn't as awesome as usual and he has no shield.

As a result, once you get those boosters turned off, he isn't too tough, Break him, wall rush and then whittle him down.

---

I used the Warrior's EX Mode here so might as well cover it to fill space. The Warrior's EX Mode is a class change to a Knight, recovering his shiny knightly armor with red and yellow highlights and tilting the horns down to point forward for some reason. As a Knight the Warrior has Protect cast on him, giving him an extra 10 defense points, at this stage of the game that's as good as an extra shield equipped, and even in the late game is effective.

Second, he gets the Mirror Attack ability, automatically protecting him from low-priority attacks while attacking himself. Mostly this just works out to reflecting magic since melee moves will bounce off each other like normal, but it's appreciated. His third bonus is the Light's Blessing ability, which summons blades of light energy whenever he uses a melee Bravery attack to strike the opponent and do more damage.

The individual swords have low power but he summons about a dozen or more of them each time, and while the resulting boost isn't as awesome as Terra, Cloud or Squall, he still sees a noticeable spike in his damage output aside from the critical hit rate boost. The Warrior's EX Burst is quite easy, press each directional command as it appears on the screen to blast the opponent with slashes infused with light energy.

---

Warrior hit Level 4 from this fight and learned Sword Thrust. It's a mid-ranged attack that begins with a shield throw, then he charges in for a sword combo. It has slight reach into the air and can't be blocked, blocking it staggers the opponent and leaves them vulnerable to the follow-up sword attack.

---

As Garland kneels and fades, he and the Warrior exchange some generic banter, the hope of Cosmos is just an illusion and it's foolish to chase it. This is one thing that helps make the Warrior's badassery shine through, so many villains appear to the heroes and taunt and demoralize them, but the Warrior always replies to the effect of "bitch please, you're evil and I'm awesome, I'm too busy thinking of what angle to jam my sword up your ass once you shut up to care what you're actually saying".

That said, Garland fades and the Warrior's journey continues.

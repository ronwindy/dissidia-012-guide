# Chapter 6

**Cloud**, **Cecil**, **Tidus**, one left before we turn our attention to the four.

#### The Rebel

---

<CharacterIntro name="Firion" src="drake/Dissidia_Firion.png" width={200} height={290} alt="Dissidia Firion">

**Firion** is just barely a step up from the **Warrior of Light** and **Onion Knight**, but in such is more a step down. Unlike them he *did* exist in his original game, but that also carries the handicap that they have less flexibility with who he is, since the other two are pretty much original characters. And they did alright, **Firion** is a naive dreamer who just wants to end the war, and realizes that in comparison to his comrades, his goals are much simpler, but he'll come to realize that his dream isn't his alone.

**Firion** is a ground-focused character with a lousy pool of air moves, he has his spells but they aren't his niche, and he has Weaponsmaster, but **Firion** wants to be on the ground. So to make that a viable option, he's an anti-air fighter, he snags opponents out of the air and brings them down to him. His other gimmick is when using his three core ground **Bravery** attacks, he can do one of three follow-ups. Press forward and Circle to knock the opponent into the air and begin a chase, neutral Circle to slam them far away with a punch to wall rush them, or back and Circle to use a lance combo that slams them to the ground. He can also chain three aerial spells together, changing up which he uses with the same controller input. Combined with a decent pool of mid-ranged attacks and **Firion** is a fairly versatile mid-range fighter and has a lot of power, especially once he gets his HP links. However, as opposed to most other characters thus far, you really want to keep him on the ground. This means arenas like **Planet's Core** and **Ultimecia's Castle** are his worst nightmare.

**Firion** starts off with Rope Knife, a mid-ranged knife toss that drags the opponent closer, and Reel Axe, which is slow to execute but throws a guard-crushing axe a fair distance. His aerial spells are Frost and Bolt, Frost fires a blast of ice that begins a Chase, while Bolt creates lightning around the opponent to wall rush them into the ground. Three chained Thunders are usually enough to catch a dodging opponent on the third cast, if you chain them quickly, and even though he doesn't know Flame yet he can still throw out **Fire** spells as a second or third spell chained onto Frost or Bolt. His HP attack is Straightarrow, a fast but pretty easy to dodge arrow shot, hold the Square button to delay the firing and he'll turn to aim at opponents.

**Firion** is overall a pretty good character, but he won't reach his full potential until much later when he gets his other three HP attacks and learns his HP links, at which point he's a beast you don't want to get anywhere near on the ground or in the air.

</CharacterIntro>

**Jump Chain** at C3 to get all four enemies, taking advantage of the **Booster** x5 emblem in the process. The bonus line as always is 2, and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. As a blanket note for **Firion**, he has a hard time generation **EX Force**. Rope Knife and a forward follow-up with the arrow shot generates the most, use that when trying to fill the **EX Gauge**.

First is an **Imitation Liegeman**, **Firion**'s manikin, he's got Rope Knife and Straightarrow. Next comes a **Fleeting Flash** with Launch, **Smite** and **Aerora**. Now comes the Hard enemy, packing the accessory gimmick for **Firion**'s story, and it's the most dangerous one yet. They come into battle with an **Arcane Resin** for a full **EX Gauge** - already bad, now it goes to worse - and they're got a **Muscle Belt**, an **Earring**, and 7 **EX Mode** boosters. That's a pretty significant damage buff, along with the **EX Mode** benefits of the boosted critical hit rate and Regen. An Assist attack will knock them out of **EX Mode** and give you the stage bravery pool, but by the time you've charged up your Assist their **EX Mode** is probably done, and later in the story you have no Assist at all. Be wary if these guys. Anyway, this one has Rope Knife, Reel Axe and Straightarrow. The last enemy is a **False Hero** with **Rising Buckler**.

With the opposition down, the two chests both have 2000 gil.

An interlude skips us ahead a bit to when the two split up, so now **Firion** is alone on the world map. Immediately turn around, there's a chest in plain sight to the right on the outcropping of land with 1000 gil. There's also the moogle of course, grab a few **Multichains** if you like, **Firion**'s story is big on **Multichains** to get all enemies. Now move forward to **Crescent Lake**, the orbs of the dead lie along the path. There's a **Delusory Warlock**, use Rope Knife's range to your advantage when engaging enemies on the world map since most other foes use melee attacks. In battle it has **Nightglow**, watch out for that. On the island is a **Rosetta Stone** and on the ground nearby are two Ephemeral Phantoms, the first is fond of **Jecht**'s Blade and the second prefers Triumphant Grasp. And in the ruins on the hill you fought them to get to? **KP Gambler** GET! Just the gateway now.

**Multichain** at C1 or C3, save that **KP Gambler** for a 5-enemy gateway. It's **EX Gauge** charging time, they're already throwing a boss at us. Remember, Rope Knife and forward follow-up generates the most **EX Force**. The **KP Chance** is a 60 second **Break** and the bonus line is 6.

First up, a humble **Imaginary Soldier** with **Fire**, **Sonic Break**, **Slashing Blow** and **Cross-Slash**, not a moveset for the typical Normal enemy eh? Now comes an **Ephemeral Phantom** with **Jecht Rush**, midair **Jecht** Block, Triumphant Grasp, those **EX Mode** accessories, and an unknown summon. Next, an **Ephemeral Summoner** with the **EX Mode** accessories, and she has **Meteor Strike** and **Heavenly Strike**. She has a summon too but oddly these guys never use their summons. I assume that like the accessories they all have the same one, maybe two, so if a later one calls its summon I'll bring it up and you keep in mind most probably use it even if I don't see it. The last foe is a **Fallacious Wanderer** who spams **Ragnarok Blade**, it uses **Pupu** and has a **Hyper Ring** with 3 No Damage boosters, but **Ragnarok Blade** does no **Bravery** damage so it's just a waste of accessories.

**Firion** gets **Lance Combo** at **Level 4**, it's the final of the three core **Bravery** attacks he has, a lance sweep around him that sucks in the opponent. It only executes marginally faster than Rope Knife but hits all around him. Also note with all three, the backwards follow up, the overhead lance attack, can be done even if the attack misses, the other two are only usable on a hit.

The chest has Yaschas Massif Gravel before you go to the end. **Firion** runs into **Jecht**, still ticked that the **Emperor** stepped in between him and **Tidus** and looking for an outlet to vent. **Firion** is just in the wrong place at the wrong time.

**Jecht** only has two boosters on his **Muscle Belt**, one each Near Opponent and Attacking **Bravery**. It's fine, he doesn't need his **Bravery** attacks. Look at his summon - **Bahamut**. "Wait" I hear you cry out in horror, "the summon that gives him 99 **Bravery** every two seconds?" Yup, that's the one. **Jecht** will eventually get enough **Bravery** to deal a one-hit knockout to **Firion**, hope you've gotten the hang of his attacks and how to avoid them...of course there's still **Jecht** Block, so you're very likely to get blocked on your **Lance Combo** or Rope Knife and then eat a 2000 point damage **Jecht** Beam in the balls.

Not much to say on this fight, you just finished taking out **Jecht**, this one just has lower stats but a much deadlier summon. Fortunately, if you have **Cactuar**, there's a simple way to stop **Bahamut** in his tracks. **Firion**'s punch follow-up does a lot of damage. Nail **Jecht** with a Rope Knife or Reel Axe and them punch him, this should do enough **Bravery** damage that he'll call **Bahamut**. Now snag him again and as you do the follow-up, summon **Cactuar**. The faithful little cactoid will sap **Jecht**'s **Bravery** to 0, just in time for you to **Break** him, and **Bahamut**'s boosts suddenly don't mean a thing since the boosts don't work while Broken. Overall if you do this, **Jecht** is pretty easy, wall rush him after that, then let him recovery his **Bravery** and use an **EX Burst** to blast him. Just watch for **Jecht** Block, he usually isn't smart enough to do it but he can use it to deflect even your Reel Axe and Straightarrow

Let's talk about **Firion**'s **EX Mode** while we're here. It's pretty lame, sadly. **Firion** gets Blood Weapons, so every time he deals HP damage, he heals the same amount of damage. Useless unless you're about to die, and that won't happen if you play smart. His **EX Burst** is one of my favorites for badassery. Fervid Blazer is a sequence of five commands, three directional buttons and two of the command buttons, and you have to press them in the shown order within a time limit for **Firion** to bombard the opponent with all his weapons and magic, finishing by firing his arsenal at them with his bow. And he still heals all the damage he does with it.

After the fight, **Jecht** takes off, deciding to have a "chat" with the **Emperor** about butting in, and you know already how it goes. **Tidus** rejoins him a second after **Jecht** leaves, and the two comrades forge ahead together again.

A small area of the world map to explore now. Go right through the grass to find a **Cure** skill, then head into the blue gateway.

You *could* use a **Multichain** at D3, but if you only have one save it for the next gateway. Instead do a **Jump Chain** on either of the two purple emblems, the lower-left one is **Initial BRV** 0 for both you and the enemy, but I prefer the upper-right which is an **Initial BRV Boost**. If you lack a **Jump Chain**, break the orbs of the dead outside and cross your fingers. The **KP Chance** is a 90 second win.

First is the lone normal enemy, **Delusory Dragoon** with **Cyclone**, **Crashing Dive** and Gungnir. Next, an **Imitation Despot** with **Thunder Crest**, Dynamite, **Flare**, and those **EX Mode** accessories. Sadly **Firion** has nothing that can reflect **Flare** here, but his Straightarrow will shoot through **Flare** safely, no deflection. Next, a **Transient Witch** with Knight's Axe and the same accessories. Last is an **Idle Sky Pirate** with Katana, Rifle and **Windburst**, again, nothing to reflect it yet. He has the EX accessories too, naturally.

The last foe, the Expert, is an **Imaginary Champion** with full equipment, two **Earrings** with 6 Far From Opponent and an After 45 Seconds (he's **Sephiroth** so oh no, **Shadow Flare** gets a slight boost sometimes), and the **Magic Pot** summon. Attacks are **Fervent Blow**, Reaper, **Shadow Flare**, **Oblivion** and **Godspeed** for **Bravery**, and **Octaslash**, **Hell's Gate** and **Heaven's Light** for HP. Be very careful when you're below him because **Sephiroth**'s AI loves to use **Hell's Gate** whenever it can, and a single dodge on the ground won't clear the danger zone most of the time. It's a tough fight and you probably won't make the **KP Chance**, but that's fine, it's just 1 **KP**. Save **Cactuar** for after **Magic Pot**.

**Firion** hit **Level 8** from this fight and learned Weaponsmaster. It throws all his weapons a medium distance forward to whirl and attack for a couple seconds, and he can move at slow speed for the duration but can't turn. It's pretty useless in the open areas, but in the small ones like Pandamonium or **Kefka's Tower**, it's a nightmare, the attack effective creates a cone of hitzone in front of **Firion**, and if you're near a wall or in some area where you can't dodge to the sides or above properly, you're gonna be in for pain.

The chest has a Full Metal Staff, inferior to **Firion**'s Zweihander but good for other magic users.

On to the next gateway.

**Multichain** on the blue emblem, there's five enemies so go go **KP Gambler**! The emblem is sadly a Magic Critical Rate Up, not much help for **Firion**. The **KP Chance** is a 60 second **Break**, giving you lots of room after meeting it to draw out the fights and fill that **EX Gauge** for the boss. You'll need it. The bonus line is 8, but even if you're over it by a level or two, take the penalty for -1 or -2 starting **KP**. See, if you set **Firion**'s level to 8, even if it's just one level back, as he earns Exp his base level will rise but he'll still be stuck at level 8 and can't change in the gateway, and this is a boss where you do *not* want to be a measly level 8 because it's one of the hardest bosses in the base ten storylines. You *need* that full **EX Gauge** and those few extra levels so you can have every advantage possible.

First is a **Phantasmal Girl** with **Fire** and **Blizzara**. Next is a **Fallacious Tree** with **Hurricane** and **Sword Dance**, Straightarrow and Reel Axe can pierce **Hurricane**. She activates when you use your summon and swaps your **Bravery** with the opponent's, too pad for **Cactuar**, not that you need it for these weaklings. Last is another **Phantasmal Girl** with **Fire**, **Blizzara**, and the **EX Mode** accessories again.

With a cool 75 **KP** in the bank (though nothing to spend it on yet, the moogles have nothing good), open the chest for 1988 gil, then approach the end piece. Just like in **Tidus**'s story, he runs off to find **Jecht**, and the **Emperor** appears to **Firion**, miraculously healed and fine after brawling with **Jecht**. **Firion** declares he will end the war, and the **Emperor**, for no real reason except, um, evil? Sics his girlfriend on us.

Oh kiddies, prepare for a long, hard, frustrating battle. The problem is obvious just by considering the set-up - **Firion**, the slow, melee-focused ground character against **Ultimecia**, the annoyingly evasive, range-focused aerial character. And oh yay, she has like, 10 levels on you, and **Tidus** is gone so no Assist for you. And to add more difficulty, she has the **Leviathan** summonstone, an **Earring** with six Far from Opponent and two Aerial boosters, and an **Imaginary Brawler** Assist. I took the **Behemoth** summonstone into the fight because I wanted that **Bravery** boost when it counted. **Ultimecia** is very hard to pin down and hurt, and you're fighting in the **World of Darkness**, definitely not my first or even fifth choice for an arena **Firion** can excel in.

**Ultimecia** will spend most of the fight bombarding you with Knight's Axe and Knight's Arrow from range in the air, too high probably for your **Bravery** attacks to reach. When she begins an HP attack, that's the time to fire off a Straightarrow or Reel Axe since she'll be defenseless. Don't be too aggressive, she can do a lot of damage, especially if she gets off those two Aerial boosters, and she can **Assist Change** out of any combo you hit her with. The AI's idiocy is once again your friend, but she's still very annoying to face and very hard to get a good hit on.

**Behemoth** single-handedly won the day for me - I broke her and went into a Chase, and **Behemoth** doubled my **Bravery** to around 2500, and the attack was close enough to the arena border to wall rush. No matter how slow **Firion** is or how pesky the dual **Bravery** drain from **Behemoth** and **Leviathan** is, one HP attack can lead into the **EX Burst** to end it, or you just wait for the drain to wear off and nail a single Straightarrow.

After the fight **Ultimecia** reappears unharmed ans the two give **Firion** some generic taunts about how he only fights for **Cosmos** with no desire or motivations of his own, and he's too weak to end a war between gods.

Another interlude tells us **Firion** is on his way to the enemy stronghold, but he's still feeling conflicted. On the world map on yet another familiar place, but to an unfamiliar one, the moogle has Grounded and Aerial for sale, buy them if you don't already have one of each, and grab a **Jump Chain** and **Multichain** if you lack one of either, but do it after getting the orbs of the dead in case they give you one. I also bought an Icebrand, we'll be using it shortly but **Firion** doesn't need it, but you can use it if you like the Initial **Bravery** boost it gives. I also bought a Sakura and just some new gear for **Firion**, in hindsight the Mystic Mythril benefits haven't proven too useful have they? Nah. Head into the gateway.

**Cosmos** appears to **Firion** and, as she usually does, gives a pep talk that consists of reminding the character about things they already valued. **Firion** laments he has no personal dream to chase like his three friends, but **Cosmos** reminds him of his dream of a world full of wild roses and peace, and assures him that the dream is enough on its own.

Down the path beyond is a **KP Gambler** for the next chapter. Finally head into the gateway.

Take out the Strange enemy with no chain, then head to the purple emblem on C4 and use a **Multichain** to get the last four foes. The bonus line is 16 and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win, the purple emblem is **Initial BRV Boost** so it's easy to meet.

Now go Chain them as I said. **Delusory Warlock** is up first with **Float System** and **Genesis Rock**. Next is the **False Stalwart** with **Round Edge**, **Chain Cast** and **Cyclone**, Weaponsmaster can disperse the winds of **Cyclone**. Now comes a stronger Stalwart with the same attacks, but naturally a higher level, some equipment, and those EX accessories. He also has an unknown Assist. And then as with the Stalwarts, the fifth and final opponent is a **Delusory Warlock**, stronger than the first of course, EX accessories, some equipment, **Counterfeit Wraith** Assist, and the same moveset as the first Warlock.

After dragging the opponent in, **Double Trouble** drives them up into the air, then slams them down and away, likely doing wall rush. It's very effective and is definitely worth the **AP** grinding to get.

The two chests both have **Pink Tails**.

Approach the end piece, **Firion** and the **Emperor** exchange the same banter as the last two times they met, then the **Emperor** tries to blow **Firion** up, but his cutscene power seems to have all been spent on that earlier **Light Crest** because **Firion** endures.

The **Emperor** one of the most dangerous end bosses so far. He's got a **False Stalwart** as Assist, it uses **Round Edge** and Bardiche when called, and a **Land Worm** summon to burn through your **Bravery** like kindling. For accessories, an **Earring** and **Hyper Ring** with 7 Near Opponent boosters, and they suit him better than Far from Opponent, actually. His **Thunder Crest** will be deadly if he catches you in it. He has the usual moveset, but Dreary Cell is a new move I don't believe you've seen from him yet. He creates a spark of light, similar to but distinct from his mines, and he can have up to three out at a time. When you approach that spark, it expends into a rune of energy with orbs of lightning around it, sucking you in as it does so, then explodes to do HP damage. Keep a keen eye on the field so you don't confuse the Cell sparks with his Mines.

And on top of it all, it may just be me but the **Emperor** seemed to be smarter than previous fights, he kept **Thunder** Crests and Flares out and was good with his **Light Crest** to interrupt my attacks, and he was aggressive enough to charge up and use that Assist. Try to keep to the open areas to avoid getting caught in **Thunder Crest**, as if you do get trapped he'll fire off a **Flare** to hit you in the face, and after landing an HP hit watch out for his counter **Light Crest**, as the projectiles are likely to bounce off the walls behind you and hit from your blind spot. Grab **EX Cores** fast because you don't want the **Emperor** getting that **EX Gauge** filled, you never want an opponent in **EX Mode** of course but his **EX Mode** bonus is the same HP draining power that **Firion** has, and with his Flares he's liable to heal himself a significant amount, especially if he uses his Burst. He's a tougher opponent than other end bosses, but is still no match for you. Know that Weaponsmaster can strike through walls, use that to your advantage here.

As the **Emperor** fades, **Firion** receives his **Crystal**, bringing Chapter 6 to a close. You also unlock **Report 11**, and though it's battle-free it's still funny to watch, it shows how **Firion** found his wild rose, except then he lost it and **Lightning** found it...and when **Cecil** notices **Firion** watching her, he gets the idea to play matchmaker. Ah, the fun of Ship Tease.

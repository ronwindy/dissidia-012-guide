Confront the end piece to find Ultimecia, the only other cast member with a character and personality as bland and unexplored as Tifa's.

Okay, others are bland, but their confrontations have purpose, Cloud of Darkness has an awesome VA and shows some interesting insights on her thoughts about manikins at least. Ultimecia just says "I'm bored, was looking for someone to fight, you'll do". How exciting, the game doesn't even pretend to have a reason for this fight, it just needs a boss to throw at us.

---

Ultimecia is a boss that's difficult only on paper. The "difficult on paper" is that she's an long-range aerial character, fighting in an aerial arena, and Tifa is a melee fighter with crappy aerial attacks at this point of the game.

The Calcabrina summon removes Break status from us, sounds meh, until you realize it also stops the normal Bravery regen that occurs when we're Broken, so we're stuck at 7 Bravery and can be Broken again easily. She also has an Attractorb, Pearl Necklace, and a Far from Opponent and EX Mode boosters. They don't give much though.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

The reason the difficulty is only on paper is because she has *three Cursed Rings*. Yeah, those accessories that sap defense by 50%, usually worn by manikins? She has three.

I started off with a Beat Rush, Broke her, got a Bravery boost from Ifrit, entered EX Mode as I entered Chase, and an HP attack wall rushed her and put her at 200 HP as I began the EX Burst.

What an anti-climax this fight is, Ultimecia is a total pushover, you should have no trouble crushing her. I'm almost wondering if this is a glitch, only two boosters and not even good ones, maybe they meant to give her three more boosters and accidentally gave her Cursed Rings? Who knows.

</PanelSection>

</BossFight>

---

After the battle Tifa runs up to kick Ultimecia, but she turns out to be a manikin. Hm, guess that explains the Cursed Rings. The real Ultimecia prepares to strike Tifa from behind.

Suddenly Kain arrives and saves the day. Ultimecia taunts that she'd worry about Kain as an attacker than a savior, and points out a fallen Zidane who has appeared from nowhere, and says Kain attacked him. Tifa believes her for some reason and goes after Kain when he grabs Zidane and takes off.

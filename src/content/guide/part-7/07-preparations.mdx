#### Preparations

Before we begin the last chapter of the 012 cycle, a couple things to take care of.

- First, go buy Shantotto in the PP Catalog, along with the usual purchases, then select Sephiroth in Quick Battle and fight Shantotto, Battlegen an item from her.
- Why? Because once we've Battlegen'd an item from Shantotto we can Battlegen items from the Empyreal Paradox, which happens to be one of the items needed for the Muscle Belt and thus soon when we get to the Paradox we can get the items we need to use the Muscle Belt in the 013 story.
- Chapter 1 will pit us against the Sephiroth Manikins we need to face for the rest of them.

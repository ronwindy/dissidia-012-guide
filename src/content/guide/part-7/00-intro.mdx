# Chapter 7

Part 7, last chapter before the finale!

#### The Brawler's Quest

Well, last character to introduce in this cycle.

---

<CharacterIntro name="Tifa" src="drake/Tifa-d012ff-artwork.png" width={111} height={250}>

Tifa...I don't dislike her, but I am not a fan. My problem with Tifa is she's bland and uninteresting, all she's ever done in all her appearances is act as an accessory to Cloud's storyline, having no personality aside from "she's Cloud's childhood friend and love interest and she supports him".

I was actually looking forward to her appearing in this game to see if they developed her as her own character and how they explored her in a new light since Cloud was a villain in the 12th cycle. And they didn't explore her at all. Tifa's storyline is outright *boring*, she just bops around until Kain shows up and then she helps wrap up Kain's story, and as you'll see in a Report, she's attacked by Sephiroth and saved by Cloud, who changes sides to try and save her.

So once again, Tifa is just an accessory, that and shameless fanservice. Don't believe me, just watch her victory pose, jiggle physics hard at work. I almost feel sorry for her because there *is* potential to develop her beyond being Cloud's support and a source of fanservice, but they don't.

Also, I think Rachel Leigh Cook is good in her other VA roles as Tifa, but in this one it's subpar, she constantly changes the tone of voice and sounds uninterested in her dialogue.

---

Tifa's fighting style, I never really got into, she's along with Vaan as one of the four I dislike playing as. Her gimmick is she can cancel out attacks by pressing X, teleporting around the opponent and attacking from behind. I guess this gives her good potential against human opponents, faking out their dodge and blocks attempts.

But against the AI I never needed this skill because they never block or dodge, and without the gimmick Tifa's attacks are pretty bland and basic. She has an advantage to them in being able to combo them without hitting, but I'm not sure why, unlike Jecht who needs that ability in EX Mode to continue his charged-up combos, if you see Tifa begin to attack you know it's coming and can block. I guess it's so she can use her feints of course, but as I said I never used them.

Her HP attacks consists of charges and dives and are pretty good, though she's ground-focused with them and unlike Firion isn't an anti-air character so that hurts her.

<PanelSection title="Bravery attacks">

Tifa is alright to start out, Beat Rush is a solid three-hit combo with fast execution and it begins a chase, and for now is her strongest attack, and also generates significantly more EX Force than her other attacks, and this chapter wants us to focus on EX Mode, which in tandem with chase makes Beat Rush your bread and butter for Tifa's story.

Otherwise her opening moveset is lame:

- Waterkick and Elbow Smash are slow two-hit combos that knock the opponent away and cause Wall Rush, except we have no Assist so that's pointless.
- Blizzard is the crappy long-range attack all characters get at least one of so they have one for the sake of having one.
- Meteodrive is a mid-range kick that slams the opponent down.
- Meteor Crusher, the only attack of Tifa's I bothered to learn the feint for, is a series of kicks and punches driving the opponent up.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="HP attacks">

She learns Burning Arrow as an HP attack at Level 4, a quick straight-ahead kick that turns to track on start-up and has a moment to do so.

She also get's Blizzara at Level 2, a Blizzard that leaves an ice chuck to still hit the opponent when blocked, and Falcon's Dive at Level 8, slamming the opponent into the ground.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

I didn't go in expecting to hate her *or* Vaan, kiddies, I really didn't. I just didn't care enough to learn her feints because I never needed them, aside from that she wasn't really all that interesting to play as, and her story was bland.

</PanelSection>

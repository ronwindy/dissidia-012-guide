The enemies here are an Ephemeral Summoner, Idle Sky Pirate, and two Imaginary Brawlers, Tifa manikins of course.

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Throughout this storyline, if you haven't up until now, use Chase attacks frequently, Chases generate a lot of EX Force and when you begin a Chase and land a hit in one, you absorb all nearby EX Force instantly, so with our Pearl Necklace on we can build up our EX Gauge quickly by using Chases more often than previously.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Equip Tifa with the usual accessories and armor, that Snowscepter I bought last time gave her a good weapon.

As we enter her first gateway we get a chest out in the open, a Pearl Necklace, which gives an EX Force absorption boost but lowers the rate the Assist Gauge charges, not a problem since we have no Assist. I used two Rosetta Stones on Tifa because I do want to put in an effort to use her the way the game says I should so she got another booster and the Necklace.

The summonstone is Magic Pot, which is useless. It summons itself automatically when the opponent has more than three times their base Bravery, and copies it. Sounds good, but think - when at any time has an opponent you've faced, short of Strange Battle Pieces and such, gotten three times their starting Bravery? Exactly.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

I got my EX Gauge charged up on the third fight so let's discuss Tifa's EX Mode. She gets a boost to her Bravery damage, the higher her EX Gauge is, the more damage she does. This is probably the dumbest idea for an EX Mode bonus they came up with.

She equips the Premium Heart, which has the same effect with the Limit gauge in FF7, but here it makes no sense because its effect is only active in EX Mode, so the gauge will constantly be depleting and Tifa's EX Mode bonus is constantly getting weaker. Combine with the fact she has no Assist to combo her attacks and I didn't get a lot of usage out of her. Seriously, with her slow attacks and wall rushing, Tifa is precisely the type of fighter who should have an Assist to help her, but she doesn't.

Her EX Burst is a lot easier than it looks, but it takes time to get right. Seven slot reels spin and you press X to stop them, Miss and no damage is done, Hit and you attack, Yeah! and she uses a better attack.

There's actually a precise rhythm to it, my advice is to ignore the six reels after the first and stop the first one on a Yeah!, then work on learning the pattern and pressing X in the proper rhythm to get Hit or Yeah on the other six. It's hard to explain but if you listen to the sound of the reels and practice, you'll understand what I mean and will soon be getting Perfect with ease.

</PanelSection>

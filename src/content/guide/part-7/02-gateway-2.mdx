<PanelSection kind="tips">

If you haven't used EX Revenge yet, let's chat about that since we're building up our Gauge effectively in this chapter.

When you get hit with a full EX Gauge and aren't in EX Mode, pressing Square and R activates EX Revenge, stopping the attack and slowing time for several seconds, during which your opponent cannot attack, is entirely defenseless, and flies back from your attacks less so you can wail on them more.

You fight the Ephemeral Vision last, so if you're having trouble hitting it, let it use Energy Rain, then activate EX Revenge and get it while it's reeling. And if you run out of time, just retry, KP Gambler gives no penalties for starting a fight over.

</PanelSection>

---

Yeah, I know I've said a lot that the new accessories you unlock suck, but they do, only about a dozen of them are good for affecting your damage output, EX or Assist, the rest are just good in very specific characters builds I never use.

- The Battle Gem for example increases the time your Assist Gauge stays charged before decreasing but lowers the charge you get from absorbing EX Force, a heavy handicap, and really how often do you have an Assist Gauge charge and don't use it and don't attack for so long the gauge begins to deplete?
- And Regen +5%? Here's some math - let's say you Regen, at this level, around 68 HP while in EX Mode. Even with a 10x booster, that's only an extra 34 HP each time, and if you're so heavily damaged you need that boost to your HP recovery rate, you're doing it wrong.

Besides once you hit Level 100 you'll have other ways to recover HP, like Exp to HP and the Succubus Soul set.

The chest in the corner of the gateway has 1000 gil, grab it and head out.

#### Exploration

Wow, I did a lot of ranting huh? Sorry kiddies.

On the overworld continue on, there's a Transient Witch and a False Stalwart.

- The Witch is easy to get the drop on because she attacks with Knight's Axe, which arcs up and to the sides, going nowhere near you when you stand right in front of her. She uses Shockwave Pulsay, which fires an orb that sticks to obstacles and draws you in before exploding.
- The fight with the False Stalwart gave me the last Stalwart's Hopes I needed for the Hyper Ring, which replaced my Power Ring of course.

---

Buy another one though since you have to trade a Power Ring to get the Hyper Ring. Quick math, the two rings give a total 8% boost, while the Hyper Ring and a 1.5x booster give 7.5%, so in the next chapter, use the two rings. For now stick with just the Hyper Ring, since the Pearl Necklace and Sunrise are also benefiting from our boosters.

I also got notification of new shop goods, but nothing too great that we need right now, there is a good Parrying bit but we'll get one for free later in the storyline, and it's expensive.

#### The Chase

Now for some reason we're at the other end of the snowfield, chasing Kain back the way we came. I'm not sure how that happened.

- Buy a Multichain from the moogle and cross the island to the chest to find a BRV Charge.
- On the low ground below the castle, a Transient Witch guards a Rosetta Stone.
- And as the map should indicate, a blue gateway.

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

The enemies here are nothing new, a Transient Gunner, Fleeting Flash, Ephemeral Summoner, Counterfeit Wraith and Imitation Despot.

- The Despot has the aerial Flare and the Wraith has Fusillade Particle Beam, the one that sends out the barrage of orbs to hit from all directions, but as you fight it in Kefka's Tower most of the orbs will hit an obstacle and disperse.
- The Flash and Despot have Bahamut while the Summoner has Leviathan.
- The three also have the same accessory set-up from Kain's tale, that is accessories to boost their EX absorpion when they're inflicted with Break.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The chests here have a Wakizashi and a Cyan Drop.

The Wakizashi is a new Parrying weapon so equip it for a minor attack boost and possibly better defenses.

The Cyan Drop is actually a good item, it lowers Assist Gauge Duration (meh) and gives +10 Initial EX Force. This means when we enter battle, the EX Gauge is filled 10%. This stacks over multiple fights though, so say you enter a gateway and equip the Cyan Drop. When you finish the battle you keep the 10% EX you got and get another 10% in the next fight.

This makes is an effective accessory at this point in the game since EX is hard to charge up, the Pearl Necklace is good for that but later is drastically lowers the rate our Assist charges up at, and duration loss is fine because we use them quickly anyway, but loss of charge speed is bad, so we'll be using the Cyan Drop in the final chapter. Later in the game the Cyan Gem and First To Victory are superior, but we have to make do with what we have now, of course.

</PanelSection>

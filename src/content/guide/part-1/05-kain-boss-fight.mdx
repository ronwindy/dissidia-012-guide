By now you should know what to expect from Kain due to fighting several of his Manikins up to this point. Kain's pool of attacks isn't as big as Kuja's and he has a bit less HP, but he's got no Cursed Rings or Cracked Shield giving him defense penalties and is more aggressive (at least in my game), so he's a much tougher nut to crack and will probably be the first opponent so far who isn't Broken in one combo.

His attacks come out pretty quick, and unless you plan to spend the fight tossing out Ruin and Fire spells, you're going to be relying on melee blows. No two ways about it, you're gonna get a beating this time.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Being that you can't rack up Bravery as easily, when you go for the HP strike use Razor Gale or a Chase HP to land Wall Rush and do a bit more damage.

Overall Kain is a more difficult opponent than Kuja and your first real fight, though not a challenging one. Take your lumps with his Bravery attacks as needed, as long as you know the timing to dodge Dragon's Fang you'll live.

</PanelSection>

---

When the fight is over Kain realizes Lightning's badassery is more formidable than he suspected, though of course she's still no match for him (but hey, unless the subject has green hair, who is?). Garland and Exdeath arrive and Kain retreats with Bartz, Exdeath in pursuit. Well Kain has a two second head-start, with Exdeath's speed he should be perfectly fine.

Garland meanwhile taunts Lightning a bit before departing, warning a Manikin horde is on its way and he can just sit back and let them beat us up. I guess he hasn't been paying attention because we've already mowed down a dozen with no trouble, but maybe a few thousand can do the trick.

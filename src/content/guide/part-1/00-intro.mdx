# Prologue and Chapter 1

Part 1, let us begin our epic tale!

#### The Adventure Begins

We open the prologue on the main heroes of D012 discussing the fact that Laguna, big shock, lead them the wrong way. Thank god Lightning and Kain are around because otherwise this group would be a joke. Speaking of.

---

<CharacterIntro name="Lightning" src="drake/Lightning_(Dissidia_012).png" width={150} height={250} alt="Lightning (Dissidia 012)">

Lightning is the de facto main character of the 012 storyline. Well, consider her competition. Hey, Kain is awesome, but I think he's better as a supporting role, and the rest, well, you saw them.

I like Lightning, she's snarky and stoic but also heroic and selfless. And hey, much like Squall in FF8, when you're surrounded by clowns like Vaan and Laguna all the time, that can make anyone a little bitchy. I'm sure the amnesia doesn't help her mood either, so much like her base game, chalk her attitude up to a couple of really crappy days.

Lightning is my fav character to use of the 012 six, and in my opinion the strongest of them, as she's very rewarding when you begin to learn how to use her to her full potential.

<PanelSection title="Paradigms">

Lightning uses Paradigms in battle to switch attacks by pressing L and R.

- Commando focuses on physical blows
- Ravager on magic
- Medic on Cure spells to boost Lightning's Bravery without attacking.

I mostly only ever use the Commando role because her Ravager spells are not that great aside from Watera, but as I'm sure YouTube videos of players more skilled at such than me will attest, they combo well with her Commando attacks.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection title="Starting set-up">

Lightning starts out with a decent set-up, Watera is a slow-moving spell that works excellent as a distraction, if the opponent blocks it they'll stagger, letting you use Blitz or Crushing Blow to deflect Watera back at them and probably hit them before they recover, and if they dodge it then you'll hit them as they finish the dodge.

She'll quickly learn Launch at Level 3, a much more reliable attack for ground options over Blitz, and gets Thunder at Level 8, a fast-hitting Ravager attack that begins a Chase, giving her a good measure of defense in Ravager mode.

All of Lightning's Commando attacks except for Ruin Wall Rush the enemy, perfect for setting up Assist combos. I also recommend de-equipping her Cure spells so when you shift Paradigms from Ravager, she goes back to Commando.

</PanelSection>

</CharacterIntro>

So once the group gets their bearings and the scene ends, we find ourselves on the world map. Throughout D012 you'll find glowing orbs of light on the overworld, collect four of them to get a random chain skill.

Most of the time you'll get Jump, Round or Cross chains, but more rarely get Straight or even Multichains. Hope you get lucky with them kiddies.

- Cross and Straight are decently useful, and Multichain is the best.
- Jump chains are occasionally useful
- Round Chains are a waste of time and should always be tossed out for a Cross Chain if you can get one.

This first one though will always be a Straight Chain.

---

If you imported Prologus, open the menu now and equip a Chocobo Cologne and the Midgar Flower to give Lightning a Luck boost, might as well since we have no use for either yet until the Shop becomes available.

Go forward through the mountains to where Kain is, and grab the chest for 50 gil. The red Gateway ahead is our goal, head there once you get the chest and the four Orbs, there's nothing else out here to find.

The gateway is pretty simple, open the chest to grab our first equipment piece, a Broadsword. You kiddies can figure out how to equip it, right? Right. Do so and approach the Stigma of Chaos to end the stage and the storyline.

---

Yup, that's it. I'm not even sure why this is separate from Chapter 1, but meh.

Enter the menu before beginning Chapter 1 to get an Accomplishment completed and receive your first Booster, Level 1-9. Boosters enhance the power of Basic accessories by the multiplier stated, when certain conditions are filled.

This is probably one of the best for the game's story, it's a 1.5x multiplier, the best you can hope for without handicapping your character in some way to activate better ones, and will always be active until your character hits Level 10. We have no Basic accessories yet though so it's useless at the moment.

Now on to Chapter 1.

<PanelSection>

There's a couple ways to chain the enemies here.

- If you have a Jump Chain, use it at E3 to chain all enemies except the one at G3
- If you have a Straight Chain, use it at C3 to chain all but the one at D1.
- If you were lucky and got a Multi Chain, use it at B3 to chain all enemies in one go.

Once again the KP Chance is simply winning the fight. Feel free to use the EX Charge skill before activating your Chain Skill, it'll begin all the fights with a full EX Gauge, while in EX Mode you have a boost to your critical hit rate and can really inflict some pain. You can also check out Lightning's EX Burst, but for badassery's sake I save them for bosses and tougher enemies.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Your Assist this time is Tifa, her ground Assist isn't as good as Laguna's but is still effective, it begins a Chase so press X when it appears to teleport to the opponent instantly, then unleash whichever attack you like while they're reeling.

Her midair assist is far better than Laguna's slamming the opponent into the ground to set up for another attack. Just make sure there's ground for her to slam them into, of course.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- First up, there are two Delusory Dragoons, these ones use Crashing Dive, a spinning slam attack into the ground which hurts a lot, and Gungnir, an HP attack with no vertical range but pretty good horizontal range, and if it hits it'll do a *lot* of Bravery damage. If you get hurt too much feel free to retry.
- There's also two Fleeting Flashes, they've got Crushing Blow for an HP attack and Launch, stay in the air against them to avoid Launch, Crushing Blow is pretty easy to avoid or even stop if you're close enough to hit them before they charge.
- Next is an Idle Sky Pirate, in midair he has an attack that could trip you up, Sword \& Shield, where he blocks your attack and counters. For safety only strike with Bravery attacks when he's grounded. When he's grounded he has the rolling attack Axe \& Shield, and Cataclysm, he turns to keep facing you as he charges it up so wait a moment before dodging to avoid it.

</PanelSection>

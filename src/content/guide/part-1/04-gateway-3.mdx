#### The Traitor

Now on the world map, the game instantly becomes 2.6 times better - Moogles are here! Open the chest by the shoreline for a Rosetta Stone, these invaluable items can be traded for in the Shop (the menu Shop, not Moogles) for give the current character an extra Accessory slot, do so now.

Now that the shop is open, let's chat about Prologus.

- You've probably got over 50 Chocobo Colognes, which as you can see can be traded for a free level up with 1000 gil. A waste of money, instead, sell off the Colognes for 100 gil each, giving us a few thousand bucks to throw around to buy equipment.
- If you've Battlegen'd at least three Flash's Desire from the Lightning Manikins, grab Lightning the Axis Blade, only available with the Midgar Flower from Prologus.
- You can also buy her a Kunai for a further boost to her Attack, and your choice of a helmet or armor for her. I recommend Leather Clothing and Leather Hat myself.

---

With that done, head through the gateway to Cornelia Plains. There's two more orbs here, with the other two extras we got before the last gateway that's an extra four and we get another Chain Skill. I've still got Straight Chains, how about you kiddies?

You'll also meet Manikins out here on the world map, two Capricious Reapers. When you approach they attack, dodge to the side to avoid it and attack after, or attack before they can make their move first, striking them triggers a pre-emptive strike, giving them 0 Bravery to start.

They're the same enemy each time, using Ring Holy, Snatch Blow and Seraphic Star, after the real deal the Manikins shouldn't be much of a problem. Don't worry about HP damage, when fighting on the overworld you're fully healed after the fight.

My Lightning reached Level 6 at this point and learned Ruin, giving her Commando role a ranged option and a way to begin Chase. I also got Speed Boost, equip it too, might as well since you have plenty of spare CP.

There's two chests in this area, a Pink Tail and Cure. Cure is a skill useful to healing in a gateway, while the Pink Tail can be traded for a Ribbon when you have a couple dozen, save it. It sells for a nice price but those Chocobo Colognes have given us plenty of gil for now.

At the Moogle Shop, check out the stock. The equipment is good but way out of our current KP total. The Summons aren't worth the price except for Ifrit and Brynhildr which we can't afford. Buy an HP = 100% Booster at least, and a Rosetta Stone if you have enough KP left for another accessory slot. Then head to the Gateway.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

First up, another Fleeting Flash. This is an effect you'll see throughout the game, tiles and other enemies that only appear when a certain enemy is slain. It has the Lightning Strike attack, which creates a wave of electricity horizontally in front of it, just dodge backwards to get out of the way.

When it's dead three more enemies appear, use a Straight Chain at C4 to get all three of them at once. They're two Idle Sky Pirates and a Delusory Dragoon.

- The Dragoon has Jump, a ground HP attack where it leaps into the air to dodge attacks and comes crashing down. Jump can be charged to increase the pause between the jump and landing, so learn to anticipate when the hit is coming and dodge it.
- The Sky Pirates have the HP attack Windburst, which creates a cyclone of wind in the air to pull you in. Use Crushing Blow to deflect the storm, though you probably won't notice, and the Sky Pirates will get hit with their own attack.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Lightning will learn Air Dash at Level 7, it's a faster dash than your current Free Air Dash, but can only be used while Quickmoving.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

The two chests have 500 gil and a Red Drop. The Drop is a Special Accessory that gives +3 damage like the Power Ring, but is unaffected by boosters. Might as well use it, ditch whichever trade accessories you have equipped for luck benefits.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Approach the end piece for a cutscene. Lightning finds Bartz wounded, and as he mutters Kain's name she narrowly dodges a sneak attack from the Dragoon. I guess Kain believes there's only room for one badass on this team. Challenge accepted.

</PanelSection>

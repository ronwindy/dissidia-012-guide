<PanelSection>

Enjoy the pretty FMV of Cosmos granting the warriors her powers, and sends them back our to fight until it manifests for them in the form of a Crystal that will grant them greater strength. But there's trouble afoot as a strange new enemy approaches.

Approach the Fleeting Flash at B3 on the map to begin your first proper battle. Being such, it's pretty easy, the enemy has equipment that lowers its stats heavily.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="assist">

Laguna is our Assist for this Gateway, slam the opponent into the ground with Smite or a Chase attack and call Laguna to bombard them with Machine Gun, giving us time to use an HP attack while he piles on heavy damage. His aerial assist is Shotgun, and it really sucks, don't waste an Assist charge calling him for it.

You shouldn't even need him though, these enemies are the lowest rank possible and are pretty dumb. Wall Rush them with Smite, then use Crushing Blow and odds are they'll dodge as they recover, Crushing Blow executing and hitting as they come out of the dodge.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection>

Now let's chat about KP. The shops on the overworld are run by Moogles, who use KP instead of gil, and you earn KP by winning battles in certain ways in Gateways. The KP chance here is just winning.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="chain">

I got a Straight Chain from the Orbs of the Dead in the Prologue, if you did move to C5 to trigger the enemy below to appear, then move to C4 and use the Straight Chain. The more enemies you chain the more KP you earn, and this will net you 9 KP, 10 total with your first victory done.

If you didn't get a Straight Chain, chain the two adjacent enemies without a chain skill, then fight the last one at the bottom separately.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

- The first enemy up is the Capricious Reaper, a Kuja Manikin, it has Burst Energy, a quick close-range blast that's hard to anticipate, so keep a small distance away because it's short range. His other attacks are Snatch Shot and the HP attack Flare Star, both are telegraphed on start-up and aren't too hard to dodge.
- The second enemy is another Fleeting Flash, it's one level higher but is otherwise just like the first.
- The last opponent is a little tougher, a Delusory Dragoon, Level 6 and with a bit more HP. He has Spiral Blow and Lance Barrage, two quick-executing attacks, so be quick on the R button to block. Dragon's Fang is his HP attack and it hurts, but it has a slow start-up and shouldn't be too hard to dodge.

If you want to be safe, wait for him to attack as you keep your distance, then attack before he recovers, back away and repeat.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="treasure">

Open the chest for a Buckler, equip it, and exit the Gateway.

</PanelSection>

On the world map, the group is confused by the appearance of this new type of foe and decide to return to Cosmose. Shatter the orb nearby, then break the stone on the shoreline to create a beam of light connecting to the island, the chest has a Power Ring equip it with the Booster we got earlier, it doesn't matter which of the two Trade accessories you de-equip, they have the same effects.

Go left from the closed gateway nearby to get the second Orb and find a chest with 120 gil. Go up the hill on the other side to get the last two orbs, then pass through the open gate on the left to an area with a fifth and sixth orb, as well as a chest with an EX Charge. With these goodies in hand, head to the next gateway.

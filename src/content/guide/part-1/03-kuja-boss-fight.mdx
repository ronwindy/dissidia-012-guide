<PanelSection>

In the aftermath, Lightning should definitely be Level 3 by now, and she'll have picked up Launch, equip it of course, when you master it you'll learn the HP link Flourish of Steel.

Open the two chests for 200 gil and a Bronze Helm, equip the latter. The last chest has Soul of Destruction, it boosts ATK to 55, but breaks after a single fight. You could use it for the boss but I don't, breakable accessories are cheap and for wimps, and my walkthrough is not for wimps.

If you didn't use EX Charge before, use it before approaching the Chaos Piece. We now get a private show from Kefka and Kuja! These guys always have the best scenes of the game, they're practically a sitcom odd couple. After Kefka annoys Kuja for lulz, the rest of the group runs off leaving Lightning to fight Kuja.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="enemies">

Alright, time to run down Kuja's list of attacks.

- Burst Energy is back, and Strike Energy is a similar attack with short range, and both hurt a lot if they hit.
- Snatch Blow and Snatch Shot fire Holy and Flare at you and are easier to block.
- Ring Holy creates three slow moving rings of magic, deflect them by dashing into them if you can, blocking leaves you vulnerable.
- For his HP attacks, Seraphic Star creates a field of energy that last several seconds and is very hard to avoid because it sucks you closer, while Force Symphony rains down explosive orbs from above and is a nightmare if Kuja is high in the air and you're stuck on the ground.

And to compound your joy, you have no Assist for this fight. Fortunately those Cursed Rings Kuja has on give him a heavy defense penalty.

</PanelSection>

<PanelSection kind="tips">

Smite should be your main offence along with Watera, being on the ground is a dangerous game to play when fighting Kuja at any time. When you see the screen darken for his HP attacks register which one it is and react appropriately because if they catch you off-guard you're doomed.

His Bravery attacks aren't too much of a hassle, they can hurt but the defense penalty makes it easy to get back what you lose. Feel free to use your EX Gauge for EX Revenge if Kuja catches you in an HP attack, especially if he inflicts Break on you.

Otherwise whittle him down to a few hundred HP, then unleash the EX Burst to finish him off. Lightning's EX Burst is pretty simple to get right, and you'll get an Exp bonus for killing Kuja with a Burst. Mine did around 700 damage, plus the bonus of 300 or so for Breaking him.

</PanelSection>

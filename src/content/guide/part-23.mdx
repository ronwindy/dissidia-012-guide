# Shantotto and Gabranth

Part 23, the Lady and the **Warrior** await our challenge!

#### Sanctuary

You can do either of these two gateways in any order, but I'm going to do this one first since the other has a higher bonus line, so the game seems to say this one should be first. These two gateways are good warm-ups for the Chasm in the Rotting Land. Teleport to the Cavern of Earth teleport stone, this is where the Chasm in the Rotting Land is, but we're bypassing it. Follow the beach to a chest with a **Crystal**, around the other end we can see our destination gateway. Shatter the rock and grind to another **Crystal**, and to the left and behind the gateway are yet more **Crystals**. Finally, head inside.

As we enter we meet **Shantotto**, that bossy little mage we've met in reports. She says we have no business here, but since we can't find the way out, she invites us to fight our way to find the path. This is another hold-over from the original Dissidia, when this gateway was its own storyline. The Official Quests also explain this gateway is a path to another dimension, and despite the fact we can leave any time, in the story, we're stuck in this alternate dimension now.

**Multichain** at D2 to chain all enemies, letting us voice the annoying red emblems on the two pieces guarding that chest. This gateway has five floors, actually four when it comes to exploration and chaining, so even only two **Multichains** is plenty to get us through the way. The bonus line is 75, and the **KP Chance** on all floors is a 90 second win. The blue emblem is an accessories protected, but I don't use resins and was already at a pretty high level and needed no help from the **Chocobo** stuff. so this was meaningless to me.

First is a **Level 60** **Lady of Antiquity**, the **Shantotto** manikin which you've probably fought in a Report, you're perhaps a bit unfamiliar with them but the huge level advantage will take care of any disadvantage that might give you. Second comes a **Level 59** **Idle Sky Pirate**. Third, a **Level 59** **Delusory Dragoon**. Last, a **Level 60** **Ephemeral Summoner**.

**Prishe** took the lead from now on. **Cloud**, **Zidane** and **Terra** are **Level 79**, **Lightning** is 77, and **Prishe** is 76. As for the gold and blue chest, it contains the same items all such chests in this gateway have - a random trade component for **Level 90** equipment.

As we leave, we overhear **Shantotto** musing that she's in trouble somehow and might need some help. <br />

**Shantotto** needs more proof of our power, let's give it to her. The best way to chain enemies is a **Cross Chain** at F4 to get four, but our Assist is in the way, fight it if you want to clear it out. Be warned though, if you only have a **Multichain** left, save it for the next floor. I did, so I had to fight enemies one by one. If you do, be warned that the two emblems are a Physical Damage 0 and Magic Damage 0, swap party members accordingly.

The enemy guarding the chest is a **Level 59** **Delusory Knight**. The enemy on H2 above is a **Level 60** **Transient Gunner**. Third, a **Level 62** **Fallacious Wanderer**. The last Hard enemy is a **Level 63** **Capricious Thief**. The Expert piece is a **Level 50** Horror of Antiquity, **Prishe**'s manikin. She's pretty tough, a **Hyper Ring** with No BRV Damage and 8 HP = 100% boosters, and the **Hecatoncheir** summon. Once you turn off those boosters though she isn't too hard, though **Prishe**'s fast attacks are annoying, her slow HP attacks and the Horror's lack of an Assist make it easy to avoid HP damage. Remember to use the crystal of the arena to help drain the enemy's **Bravery**. Defeating the Expert reveals the tile at B1 so we can reach the third chest.

The far right chest has a random chain skill - by pure luck, I got a **Multichain**. The cheat at A1 that the Expert guarded is a lame **Valor Resin**. <br />

As we enter, **Shantotto** decides we're just who she needs. An option box pops up but we can't change the option and have to agree to help her. There's more **Manikins** down here that are running amok, and she needs us to take care of them.

Multhchain at C4 to get all the enemies present here. The blue emblem is awesome a **Booster** x5 emblem. There's eight enemies here with the Party piece, so if you brought along a **KP Gambler**, this is the time and place to use it.

First foe on the chopping block, a **Level 62** **Fallacious Wanderer**. Next is a **Level 61** Horror of Antiquity, naturally from the level it's much weaker than the Expert one from before. Then comes the Party - **Level 63** **Transient Lion**, **Level 63** **Capricious Thief**, **Level 63** **Ephemeral Vision**, and finally a **Level 79** **Lady of Antiquity**.

Then comes one of the Expert enemies, a new one, a Fallacious Giant, **Gilgamesh**'s **Manikin**. He has Cross Slash, a low-ranged chop attack on the ground, Whirlwind Slash, a slow long-ranged dive forward, Wind Slash, which fires a blast of wind, **Double Trouble**, a very quick but very short-ranged attack, Tsubamegeshi, a diving chop forward, and Electrocute, which summons a lightning bolt around you. His HP attacks are Death Claw, a two-hit charge attack forward similar to **Cloud**'s **Cross-Slash** but with one less hit and more horizontal range, Jump, which is just like **Kain**'s but with longer lag on impact and no charging, **Hurricane**, a close-range aerial spin attack, and Missile, which fires a large homing missile at you that explodes. His summon, appropriately, is **Gilgamesh**, which randomly triples his **Bravery** or reduces it to 1. His Assist is a **Fallacious Tree**. He's not too dangerous as long as you don't get close, aside from **Double Trouble** and **Hurricane** his attacks are easy to anticipate and dodge or block, once you get the hang of him of course, feel free to retry.

The last enemy is a **Level 89** **Counterfeit Wraith**, not sure what her summon is, she never used it.

The two normal chests have a **Gold Angel** and a **Diamond Ring**. Save this, it increases the **AP** we earn x4, you can use it at the gateway I mentioned last chapter, with the accessories protected emblem. The silver chalice is a **Moogle** Chalice, it will instantly double your earned **KP**, like a **KP Bonus**.

As we enter we someone hear **Shantotto** think it was she who let the manikins in, to experiment on, and she wonders who made them. **Multichain** right at C3 where we begin to get all four enemies.

First, a **Level 63** **Imaginary Soldier**. Second, a **Level 63** **Capricious Thief**. The Strange enemy is a **Level 100** **False Hero**, he comes into battle with several thousand **Bravery**, uses **Dayflash** and **Shield of Light**, and has a **Cactuar** summon. Pretty simple to beat. The last enemy, the Expert, is a **Level 89** **False Stalwart** with the **Behemoth** summon and a **False Hero** Assist. He's pretty annoying to tango with, at least for me with **Prishe** he was. Thank you **Chaos Shrine** Ω for helping me sap his **Bravery**!

The chest has an **Iifa Dew**, but the red chest is a new feature - it's actually an enemy in disguise. Our playmate is a **Level 90** **Delusory Warlock** with the **Giant of Babil** summon and **Delusory Knight** Assist. Have fun! Victory yields a random **Level 90** trade accessory.

On the final floor, the layout is simple and not needing a map. **Shantotto** thanks us for our help, in spite of how much of a loser we are, and invites us to come receive our reward. There's a final enemy to face, as you can see, a **Level 62** Horror of Antiquity.

The blue and gold chest has more level 90 trade stuff, the normal chest has a random piece of the "Allure of Honey" set - the Sexy Cologne, Member's Card, Blonde Wig and Silk Dress. When all four are worn, the Battlegen and item drop rates increase by 50%. It's pretty useless though, Mognet gives us an endless supply of Superslick and Mog's Amulet for those effects, and for Battlegen there's the **Sunrise**. Not to mention the equipment is a lame **Level 30** set. Now, you can approach the end piece, or you can fight your Assist and get them out of the way to get access to the chests their guarding, three more trade accessories. The choice is yours, but I say do it.

**Shantotto** thanks us for our help and scores us, and now for our reward - a private lesson from the Minister of Magic herself. The class is Pain 101.

**Shantotto**'s main problem should be obvious right off the bat - **Level 70**. As a result you're going to benefit from a pretty large **Bravery** boost. Of course, she has the same - she's got nine Blue Gems equipped, which is why she has that whopping 1,391 base **Bravery**, and thus her HP attacks are going to hurt a lot. She's also got the Bonecrusher to give her another **Bravery** boost when near death. She has the **Ultima Weapon** summon and a Horror of Antiquity Assist.

This fight was over before **Ultima Weapon** fired, in spite of **Shantotto** calling it right at the start of the fight. The reason is simple - I had the **Bahamut** summon, the perfect summon for **Order's Sanctuary** Ω. When I broke **Shantotto** the stage gimmick doubled my **Bravery**, which was over 4000 between the damage I was doing and **Bahamut**'s boosts. Then it was just the formality of an **EX Burst** finish and the Chainspeller was felled.

Even if you don't use this strategy, **Shantotto** won't be too much trouble, her massive level disadvantage will work against her, as long as you got the hang of her attack patterns when facing her manikins, you'll be fine. Just watch out for Stun and remember to block her ground staff combo, you can block her aerial staff throw but it'll stagger you and you might get hit by something else.

**Shantotto** laughs we did alright, for someone who could barely handle her magic. Yeah, sure. Now she has an appointment to attend with an opponent. Gabri, Gabro, Gabrand? Whatever, she's off to find him, and has some more shout-outs to FF11 stuff as she departs.

Well, that's done, the gateway is unsealed. Now, how's about we go find this "Gabrand" fellow she was talking about?

#### Highway to Hell

Restock your chain skills and *definitely* bring a **Multichain** and **KP Gambler** this time. Otherwise, bring a **Jump Chain** or two. Use the teleport stone to teleport to the Marsh Cave, this is the gateway down in the snowfields we couldn't reach before. Head down, open the chest for a **Crystal**, then dive into the gateway. This time **Lightning** will lead the charge.

As we enter, we find ourselves approached by **Gabranth**. He leaves quickly, telling us to choose our path and head down it, not thaty it matters. This is another alternate dimension like the one **Shantotto** dwells in, but this one is where those who refuse to fight in the cycle are banished until they submit. **Gabranth** was one such warrior and now guards this place in case anyone stumbles into it, like we have.

**Jump Chain** at D3 to get all enemies, the purple emblem is a **KP** +1. You could also do it at B3, but that purple emblem is an **Initial BRV** 0 so we win less **KP**. Also only do this if you have more than one **Jump Chain**, if not hold onto it. The bonus line is 80, and the **KP Chance** is a 90 second win. Enemies here are much stronger than in **Shantotto**'s gateway, the weakest you'll see is Level 70s, most though reach into the 80s and 90s.

First up is a **Level 79** **Lady of Antiquity** with **Carbuncle**. Second is a **Level 80** Horror of Antiquity with **Hecatoncheir**. Third, a **Level 89** **False Stalwart** with **Behemoth** and a **False Hero** Assist.

The last is a **Warrior of Antiquity** **Level 79**, **Gabranth**'s **Manikin**. Don't be deceived by how the fight in the Reports against **Gabranth** went, these guys are equipped for war. As I covered in his profile, **Gabranth**'s gimmick is to charge that **EX Gauge** and stay in **EX Mode** where he's a powerhouse of mayhem. Well, the **Warrior** has an **Arcane Resin** to begin with a full **EX Gauge**, then he has a **Pearl Necklace**, **Dragonfly Orb**, **Attractorb**, and 6 **Break** boosters. I'd have given him **EX Mode** boosters, but that is a good build. He's armed with Enrage, a long and powerful ground combo that has him dash forward on start-up, Relentless Lunge, a series of three sharp dashes forward, Aero, which creates a blockable tornado, Rupture, a dive up or down at you to slam you in the same direction, Dual Rend, a quick two-hit blast backwards, and **Vortex** of Judgment, a trio of spin attacks. His HP attacks are Execution, a charge forward to consume you in an explosion, Innocence, sending four beams of energy in an arc downwards, and Hatred, creating a sphere of energy around him that explodes. Have fun fighting the Judge Magister in his most powerful form and seeing just how strong he is, and get used to it because the real thing is even worse as I'll cover when the time comes.

When you're done, the normal chest has a random skill. The blue and gold chests again have level 90 trade components throughout the gateway.

As we reach the exit, **Gabranth** understands we still wish to return, so now there's one final test. Time for the Judge Magister to do what he does best - dispense judgement upon us and carry out sentencing.

**Gabranth** is a *much* more competent opponent than **Shantotto**. You've probably got a level advantage on him, but a much weaker one than **Shantotto**, since **Gabranth** is **Level 90** to **Shantotto**'s 70. And they gave him what is, honestly, one of the most brilliant accessory builds of any enemy - a Puppeteer's Wheel. Yes, the same one **Terra** had in her boss fights that let her stay in **EX Mode** the entire fight. Ho boy. **Gabranth** also has an **Attractorb** and **Hyper Ring** hooked up to five **EX Mode** boosters, giving him a 3.7x booster as long as he's in **EX Mode**, and after 30 seconds pass in the fight, off go two more boosters, taking him to 8.3x. And to finish up, he's got an **Idle Sky Pirate** Assist and the **Omega** **Summon**. About the only way he could be worse is if they gave him an **Iifa Dew** or Leaf, or a **Muscle Belt** instead of the **Hyper Ring**. Fortunately he doesn't have EX Critical Boost or else most all of his attacks would be critical hits.

I hope you came into the fight with a full **EX Gauge**, because with that pumped up **Attractorb** he won't be leaving any **EX Force** for you. Between that and the Puppeteer's Wheel, **Gabranth** is going to be in **EX Mode** the entire fight unless you knock him out of it with an Assist **Break**...unless you killed your Assist to get access to those chests. Oops. As well, the fight is in Edge of Madness Ω, where the **Bravery** pool is constantly in flux from several thousand to a few hundred **Bravery**. Time your **Break** well or else you'll have nothing to show for your effort in wearing him down.

You know how **Gabranth**'s attacks work from his manikins, he's just a much bigger, beefier version of them, and is fought in a very small arena so you have nowhere to run or hide from him. His accessories are going to give him a big damage buff so I hope you can block on a whim because if he connects he's going to hurt, and know which of his attacks can't be blocked. And of course, don't forget he's going to be recovering HP the entire fight thanks to Regen. He's a very powerful, very *smart* opponent, and he can thrash you easily. If you didn't kill your Assist, charge it up and get in an Assist attack to EX **Break** him and end his **EX Mode**, then don't let him sit still long enough to charge it back up. If not, then it's just a battle of pure brawn. Good luck kiddies!

After he's beaten, **Gabranth** shows us the exit, but warns that we will face despair and loss, but we can't let it break our spirits and have to hold onto hope.

With the two optional gateways completed, it's time to head to our actual objective. As before, go restock your chain skills and prepare to enter the Chasm in the Rotting Land.

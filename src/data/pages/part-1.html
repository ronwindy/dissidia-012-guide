Prologue and Chapter 1

Part 1, let us begin our epic tale!

The Adventure Begins

We open the prologue on the main heroes of D012 discussing the fact that Laguna, big shock, lead them the wrong way. Thank god Lightning and Kain are around because otherwise this group would be a joke. Speaking of.

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Lightning

Lightning is the de facto main character of the 012 storyline. Well, consider her competition. Hey, Kain is awesome, but I think he's better as a supporting role, and the rest, well, you saw them. I like Lightning, she's snarky and stoic but also heroic and selfless. And hey, much like Squall in FF8, when you're surrounded by clowns like Vaan and Laguna all the time, that can make anyone a little bitchy. I'm sure the amnesia doesn't help her mood either, so much like her base game, chalk her attitude up to a couple of really crappy days.

Lightning is my fav character to use of the 012 six, and in my opinion the strongest of them, as she's very rewarding when you begin to learn how to use her to her full potential. Lightning uses Paradigms in battle to switch attacks by pressing L and R. Commando focuses on physical blows, Ravager on magic, and Medic on Cure spells to boost Lightning's Bravery without attacking. I mostly only ever use the Commando role because her Ravager spells are not that great aside from Watera, but as I'm sure YouTube videos of players more skilled at such than me will attest, they combo well with her Commando attacks.

Lightning starts out with a decent set-up, Watera is a slow-moving spell that works excellent as a distraction, if the opponent blocks it they'll stagger, letting you use Blitz or Crushing Blow to deflect Watera back at them and probably hit them before they recover, and if they dodge it then you'll hit them as they finish the dodge. She'll quickly learn Launch at Level 3, a much more reliable attack for ground options over Blitz, and gets Thunder at Level 8, a fast-hitting Ravager attack that begins a Chase, giving her a good measure of defense in Ravager mode. All of Lightning's Commando attacks except for Ruin Wall Rush the enemy, perfect for setting up Assist combos. I also recommend de-equipping her Cure spells so when you shift Paradigms from Ravager, she goes back to Commando.

So once the group gets their bearings and the scene ends, we find ourselves on the world map. Throughout D012 you'll find glowing orbs of light on the overworld, collect four of them to get a random chain skill. Most of the time you'll get Jump, Round or Cross chains, but more rarely get Straight or even Multichains. Hope you get lucky with them kiddies. Cross and Straight are decently useful, and Multichain is the best. Jump chains are occasionally useful, and Round Chains are a waste of time and should always be tossed out for a Cross Chain if you can get one. This first one though will always be a Straight Chain.

If you imported Prologus, open the menu now and equip a Chocobo Cologne and the Midgar Flower to give Lightning a Luck boost, might as well since we have no use for either yet until the Shop becomes available. Go forward through the mountains to where Kain is, and grab the chest for 50 gil. The red Gateway ahead is our goal, head there once you get the chest and the four Orbs, there's nothing else out here to find. The gateway is pretty simple, open the chest to grab our first equipment piece, a Broadsword. You kiddies can figure out how to equip it, right? Right. Do so and approach the Stigma of Chaos to end the stage and the storyline.

Yup, that's it. I'm not even sure why this is separate from Chapter 1, but meh. Enter the menu before beginning Chapter 1 to get an Accomplishment completed and receive your first Booster, Level 1-9. Boosters enhance the power of Basic accessories by the multiplier stated, when certain conditions are filled. This is probably one of the best for the game's story, it's a 1.5x multiplier, the best you can hope for without handicapping your character in some way to activate better ones, and will always be active until your character hits Level 10. We have no Basic accessories yet though so it's useless at the moment.

Now on to Chapter 1.

Enjoy the pretty FMV of Cosmos granting the warriors her powers, and sends them back our to fight until it manifests for them in the form of a Crystal that will grant them greater strength. But there's trouble afoot as a strange new enemy approaches. Approach the Fleeting Flash at B3 on the map to begin your first proper battle. Being such, it's pretty easy, the enemy has equipment that lowers its stats heavily. Laguna is our Assist for this Gateway, slam the opponent into the ground with Smite or a Chase attack and call Laguna to bombard them with Machine Gun, giving us time to use an HP attack while he piles on heavy damage. His aerial assist is Shotgun, and it really sucks, don't waste an Assist charge calling him for it. You shouldn't even need him though, these enemies are the lowest rank possible and are pretty dumb. Wall Rush them with Smite, then use Crushing Blow and odds are they'll dodge as they recover, Crushing Blow executing and hitting as they come out of the dodge.

Now let's chat about KP. The shops on the overworld are run by Moogles, who use KP instead of gil, and you earn KP by winning battles in certain ways in Gateways. The KP chance here is just winning. I got a Straight Chain from the Orbs of the Dead in the Prologue, if you did move to C5 to trigger the enemy below to appear, then move to C4 and use the Straight Chain. The more enemies you chain the more KP you earn, and this will net you 9 KP, 10 total with your first victory done. If you didn't get a Straight Chain, chain the two adjacent enemies without a chain skill, then fight the last one at the bottom separately.

The first enemy up is the Capricious Reaper, a Kuja Manikin, it has Burst Energy, a quick close-range blast that's hard to anticipate, so keep a small distance away because it's short range. His other attacks are Snatch Shot and the HP attack Flare Star, both are telegraphed on start-up and aren't too hard to dodge. The second enemy is another Fleeting Flash, it's one level higher but is otherwise just like the first. The last opponent is a little tougher, a Delusory Dragoon, Level 6 and with a bit more HP. He has Spiral Blow and Lance Barrage, two quick-executing attacks, so be quick on the R button to block. Dragon's Fang is his HP attack and it hurts, but it has a slow start-up and shouldn't be too hard to dodge. If you want to be safe, wait for him to attack as you keep your distance, then attack before he recovers, back away and repeat.

Open the chest for a Buckler, equip it, and exit the Gateway. On the world map, the group is confused by the appearance of this new type of foe and decide to return to Cosmose. Shatter the orb nearby, then break the stone on the shoreline to create a beam of light connecting to the island, the chest has a Power Ring equip it with the Booster we got earlier, it doesn't matter which of the two Trade accessories you de-equip, they have the same effects. Go left from the closed gateway nearby to get the second Orb and find a chest with 120 gil. Go up the hill on the other side to get the last two orbs, then pass through the open gate on the left to an area with a fifth and sixth orb, as well as a chest with an EX Charge. With these goodies in hand, head to the next gateway.

There's a couple ways to chain the enemies here. If you have a Jump Chain, use it at E3 to chain all enemies except the one at G3, and if you have a Straight Chain, use it at C3 to chain all but the one at D1. If you were lucky and got a Multi Chain, use it at B3 to chain all enemies in one go. Once again the KP Chance is simply winning the fight. Feel free to use the EX Charge skill before activating your Chain Skill, it'll begin all the fights with a full EX Gauge, while in EX Mode you have a boost to your critical hit rate and can really inflict some pain. You can also check out Lightning's EX Burst, but for badassery's sake I save them for bosses and tougher enemies. Your Assist this time is Tifa, her ground Assist isn't as good as Laguna's but is still effective, it begins a Chase so press X when it appears to teleport to the opponent instantly, then unleash whichever attack you like while they're reeling. Her midair assist is far better than Laguna's slamming the opponent into the ground to set up for another attack. Just make sure there's ground for her to slam them into, of course.

First up, there are two Delusory Dragoons, these ones use Crashing Dive, a spinning slam attack into the ground which hurts a lot, and Gungnir, an HP attack with no vertical range but pretty good horizontal range, and if it hits it'll do a lot of Bravery damage. If you get hurt too much feel free to retry. There's also two Fleeting Flashes, they've got Crushing Blow for an HP attack and Launch, stay in the air against them to avoid Launch, Crushing Blow is pretty easy to avoid or even stop if you're close enough to hit them before they charge. Next is an Idle Sky Pirate, in midair he has an attack that could trip you up, Sword & Shield, where he blocks your attack and counters. For safety only strike with Bravery attacks when he's grounded. When he's grounded he has the rolling attack Axe & Shield, and Cataclysm, he turns to keep facing you as he charges it up so wait a moment before dodging to avoid it.

In the aftermath, Lightning should definitely be Level 3 by now, and she'll have picked up Launch, equip it of course, when you master it you'll learn the HP link Flourish of Steel. Open the two chests for 200 gil and a Bronze Helm, equip the latter. The last chest has Soul of Destruction, it boosts ATK to 55, but breaks after a single fight. You could use it for the boss but I don't, breakable accessories are cheap and for wimps, and my walkthrough is not for wimps. If you didn't use EX Charge before, use it before approaching the Chaos Piece. We now get a private show from Kefka and Kuja! These guys always have the best scenes of the game, they're practically a sitcom odd couple. After Kefka annoys Kuja for lulz, the rest of the group runs off leaving Lightning to fight Kuja.

Alright, time to run down Kuja's list of attacks. Burst Energy is back, and Strike Energy is a similar attack with short range, and both hurt a lot if they hit. Snatch Blow and Snatch Shot fire Holy and Flare at you and are easier to block. Ring Holy creates three slow moving rings of magic, deflect them by dashing into them if you can, blocking leaves you vulnerable. For his HP attacks, Seraphic Star creates a field of energy that last several seconds and is very hard to avoid because it sucks you closer, while Force Symphony rains down explosive orbs from above and is a nightmare if Kuja is high in the air and you're stuck on the ground. And to compound your joy, you have no Assist for this fight. Fortunately those Cursed Rings Kuja has on give him a heavy defense penalty.

Smite should be your main offence along with Watera, being on the ground is a dangerous game to play when fighting Kuja at any time. When you see the screen darken for his HP attacks register which one it is and react appropriately because if they catch you off-guard you're doomed. His Bravery attacks aren't too much of a hassle, they can hurt but the defense penalty makes it easy to get back what you lose. Feel free to use your EX Gauge for EX Revenge if Kuja catches you in an HP attack, especially if he inflicts Break on you. Otherwise whittle him down to a few hundred HP, then unleash the EX Burst to finish him off. Lightning's EX Burst is pretty simple to get right, and you'll get an Exp bonus for killing Kuja with a Burst. Mine did around 700 damage, plus the bonus of 300 or so for Breaking him.

With Kuja fading away, enjoy more hilarity from Kefka before he leaves and we continue on alone.

The Traitor

Now on the world map, the game instantly becomes 2.6 times better - Moogles are here! Open the chest by the shoreline for a Rosetta Stone, these invaluable items can be traded for in the Shop (the menu Shop, not Moogles) for give the current character an extra Accessory slot, do so now. Now that the shop is open, let's chat about Prologus. You've probably got over 50 Chocobo Colognes, which as you can see can be traded for a free level up with 1000 gil. A waste of money, instead, sell off the Colognes for 100 gil each, giving us a few thousand bucks to throw around to buy equipment. If you've Battlegen'd at least three Flash's Desire from the Lightning Manikins, grab Lightning the Axis Blade, only available with the Midgar Flower from Prologus. You can also buy her a Kunai for a further boost to her Attack, and your choice of a helmet or armor for her. I recommend Leather Clothing and Leather Hat myself.

With that done, head through the gateway to Cornelia Plains. There's two more orbs here, with the other two extras we got before the last gateway that's an extra four and we get another Chain Skill. I've still got Straight Chains, how about you kiddies? You'll also meet Manikins out here on the world map, two Capricious Reapers. When you approach they attack, dodge to the side to avoid it and attack after, or attack before they can make their move first, striking them triggers a pre-emptive strike, giving them 0 Bravery to start. They're the same enemy each time, using Ring Holy, Snatch Blow and Seraphic Star, after the real deal the Manikins shouldn't be much of a problem. Don't worry about HP damage, when fighting on the overworld you're fully healed after the fight. My Lightning reached Level 6 at this point and learned Ruin, giving her Commando role a ranged option and a way to begin Chase. I also got Speed Boost, equip it too, might as well since you have plenty of spare CP. There's two chests in this area, a Pink Tail and Cure. Cure is a skill useful to healing in a gateway, while the Pink Tail can be traded for a Ribbon when you have a couple dozen, save it. It sells for a nice price but those Chocobo Colognes have given us plenty of gil for now.

At the Moogle Shop, check out the stock. The equipment is good but way out of our current KP total. The Summons aren't worth the price except for Ifrit and Brynhildr which we can't afford. Buy an HP = 100% Booster at least, and a Rosetta Stone if you have enough KP left for another accessory slot. Then head to the Gateway.

First up, another Fleeting Flash. This is an effect you'll see throughout the game, tiles and other enemies that only appear when a certain enemy is slain. It has the Lightning Strike attack, which creates a wave of electricity horizontally in front of it, just dodge backwards to get out of the way. When it's dead three more enemies appear, use a Straight Chain at C4 to get all three of them at once. They're two Idle Sky Pirates and a Delusory Dragoon. The Dragoon has Jump, a ground HP attack where it leaps into the air to dodge attacks and comes crashing down. Jump can be charged to increase the pause between the jump and landing, so learn to anticipate when the hit is coming and dodge it. The Sky Pirates have the HP attack Windburst, which creates a cyclone of wind in the air to pull you in. Use Crushing Blow to deflect the storm, though you probably won't notice, and the Sky Pirates will get hit with their own attack.

Lightning will learn Air Dash at Level 7, it's a faster dash than your current Free Air Dash, but can only be used while Quickmoving. The two chests have 500 gil and a Red Drop. The Drop is a Special Accessory that gives +3 damage like the Power Ring, but is unaffected by boosters. Might as well use it, ditch whichever trade accessories you have equipped for luck benefits. Approach the end piece for a cutscene. Lightning finds Bartz wounded, and as he mutters Kain's name she narrowly dodges a sneak attack from the Dragoon. I guess Kain believes there's only room for one badass on this team. Challenge accepted.

By now you should know what to expect from Kain due to fighting several of his Manikins up to this point. Kain's pool of attacks isn't as big as Kuja's and he has a bit less HP, but he's got no Cursed Rings or Cracked Shield giving him defense penalties and is more aggressive (at least in my game), so he's a much tougher nut to crack and will probably be the first opponent so far who isn't Broken in one combo. His attacks come out pretty quick, and unless you plan to spend the fight tossing out Ruin and Fire spells, you're going to be relying on melee blows. No two ways about it, you're gonna get a beating this time. Being that you can't rack up Bravery as easily, when you go for the HP strike use Razor Gale or a Chase HP to land Wall Rush and do a bit more damage. Overall Kain is a more difficult opponent than Kuja and your first real fight, though not a challenging one. Take your lumps with his Bravery attacks as needed, as long as you know the timing to dodge Dragon's Fang you'll live.

When the fight is over Kain realizes Lightning's badassery is more formidable than he suspected, though of course she's still no match for him (but hey, unless the subject has green hair, who is?). Garland and Exdeath arrive and Kain retreats with Bartz, Exdeath in pursuit. Well Kain has a two second head-start, with Exdeath's speed he should be perfectly fine. Garland meanwhile taunts Lightning a bit before departing, warning a Manikin horde is on its way and he can just sit back and let them beat us up. I guess he hasn't been paying attention because we've already mowed down a dozen with no trouble, but maybe a few thousand can do the trick.

On that note, we end Chapter 1.

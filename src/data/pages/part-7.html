The Brawler's Quest

Well, last character to introduce in this cycle.

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Tifa

Tifa...I don't dislike her, but I am not a fan. My problem with Tifa is she's bland and uninteresting, all she's ever done in all her appearances is act as an accessory to Cloud's storyline, having no personality aside from "she's Cloud's childhood friend and love interest and she supports him". I was actually looking forward to her appearing in this game to see if they developed her as her own character and how they explored her in a new light since Cloud was a villain in the 12th cycle. And they didn't explore her at all. Tifa's storyline is outright boring, she just bops around until Kain shows up and then she helps wrap up Kain's story, and as you'll see in a Report, she's attacked by Sephiroth and saved by Cloud, who changes sides to try and save her. So once again, Tifa is just an accessory, that and shameless fanservice. Don't believe me, just watch her victory pose, jiggle physics hard at work. I almost feel sorry for her because there is potential to develop her beyond being Cloud's support and a source of fanservice, but they don't. Also, I think Rachel Leigh Cook is good in her other VA roles as Tifa, but in this one it's subpar, she constantly changes the tone of voice and sounds uninterested in her dialogue.

Tifa's fighting style, I never really got into, she's along with Vaan as one of the four I dislike playing as. Her gimmick is she can cancel out attacks by pressing X, teleporting around the opponent and attacking from behind. I guess this gives her good potential against human opponents, faking out their dodge and blocks attempts. But against the AI I never needed this skill because they never block or dodge, and without the gimmick Tifa's attacks are pretty bland and basic. She has an advantage to them in being able to combo them without hitting, but I'm not sure why, unlike Jecht who needs that ability in EX Mode to continue his charged-up combos, if you see Tifa begin to attack you know it's coming and can block. I guess it's so she can use her feints of course, but as I said I never used them. Her HP attacks consists of charges and dives and are pretty good, though she's ground-focused with them and unlike Firion isn't an anti-air character so that hurts her.

Tifa is alright to start out, Beat Rush is a solid three-hit combo with fast execution and it begins a chase, and for now is her strongest attack, and also generates significantly more EX Force than her other attacks, and this chapter wants us to focus on EX Mode, which in tandem with chase makes Beat Rush your bread and butter for Tifa's story. Otherwise her opening moveset is lame, Waterkick and Elbow Smash are slow two-hit combos that knock the opponent away and cause Wall Rush, except we have no Assist so that's pointless, and Blizzard is the crappy long-range attack all characters get at least one of so they have one for the sake of having one. Meteodrive is a mid-range kick that slams the opponent down, and Meteor Crusher, the only attack of Tifa's I bothered to learn the feint for, is a series of kicks and punches driving the opponent up. She learns Burning Arrow as an HP attack at Level 4, a quick straight-ahead kick that turns to track on start-up and has a moment to do so. She also get's Blizzara at Level 2, a Blizzard that leaves an ice chuck to still hit the opponent when blocked, and Falcon's Dive at Level 8, slamming the opponent into the ground.

I didn't go in expecting to hate her or Vaan, kiddies, I really didn't. I just didn't care enough to learn her feints because I never needed them, aside from that she wasn't really all that interesting to play as, and her story was bland.

Equip Tifa with the usual accessories and armor, that Snowscepter I bought last time gave her a good weapon. As we enter her first gateway we get a chest out in the open, a Pearl Necklace, which gives an EX Force absorption boost but lowers the rate the Assist Gauge charges, not a problem since we have no Assist. I used two Rosetta Stones on Tifa because I do want to put in an effort to use her the way the game says I should so she got another booster and the Necklace. A Straight Chain where you opened the chest will chain all four enemies, but I didn't have one so I used a Round Chain at D4 to get three of them. Throughout this storyline, if you haven't up until now, use Chase attacks frequently, Chases generate a lot of EX Force and when you begin a Chase and land a hit in one, you absorb all nearby EX Force instantly, so with our Pearl Necklace on we can build up our EX Gauge quickly by using Chases more often than previously.

The enemies here are an Ephemeral Summoner, Idle Sky Pirate, and two Imaginary Brawlers, Tifa manikins of course. Nothing of note here, though the Brawler guarding the summonstone has Ultros to stop you from seeing your Bravery. I got my EX Gauge charged up on the third fight so let's discuss Tifa's EX Mode. She gets a boost to her Bravery damage, the higher her EX Gauge is, the more damage she does. This is probably the dumbest idea for an EX Mode bonus they came up with. She equips the Premium Heart, which has the same effect with the Limit gauge in FF7, but here it makes no sense because its effect is only active in EX Mode, so the gauge will constantly be depleting and Tifa's EX Mode bonus is constantly getting weaker. Combine with the fact she has no Assist to combo her attacks and I didn't get a lot of usage out of her. Seriously, with her slow attacks and wall rushing, Tifa is precisely the type of fighter who should have an Assist to help her, but she doesn't. Her EX Burst is a lot easier than it looks, but it takes time to get right. Seven slot reels spin and you press X to stop them, Miss and no damage is done, Hit and you attack, Yeah! and she uses a better attack. There's actually a precise rhythm to it, my advice is to ignore the six reels after the first and stop the first one on a Yeah!, then work on learning the pattern and pressing X in the proper rhythm to get Hit or Yeah on the other six. It's hard to explain but if you listen to the sound of the reels and practice, you'll understand what I mean and will soon be getting Perfect with ease.

The summonstone is Magic Pot, which is useless. It summons itself automatically when the opponent has more than three times their base Bravery, and copies it. Sounds good, but think - when at any time has an opponent you've faced, short of Strange Battle Pieces and such, gotten three times their starting Bravery? Exactly. As we leave we get a funny line, Tifa wants an accessory but she doesn't have enough KP, and the game tells us about KP Chances. I find it funny because you would think the game might want to explain KP Chances to us a little earlier than the second-to-last chapter.

Outside on the world map you're in a familiar place, the moogle has the same inventory as last time. Buy a Multichain from him, and once you break the orbs of the dead if you lack a Straight Chain, come back and get one. The chest on the island behind us has a Pink Tail. Head forward to the blue gateway and duck inside. This is where we use that KP Gambler we picked up back in Kain's tale, use it with a Multichain at D3 to get all five enemies and 75 KP. The KP Chance is to win in 90 seconds, don't screw it up or the Gambler will backfire and rob you of your multiplier.

Enemies are an Imitation Despot, Delusory Dragoon, Fleeting Flash, Counterfeit Wraith and Ephemeral Vision. No new surprises, though the enemy line-ups are getting more varied which is nice. The Vision (Tidus, Jecht is the Phantom, I note this because I got them mixed up so maybe you did too) is the Strange Battle Piece, has several thousand Bravery, and Energy Rain. Its summon is Odin. The Wraith has Leviathan, which saps you of 50 Bravery every other second or so once you Break her, and the Flash has Odin. If you haven't used EX Revenge yet, let's chat about that since we're building up our Gauge effectively in this chapter. When you get hit with a full EX Gauge and aren't in EX Mode, pressing Square and R activates EX Revenge, stopping the attack and slowing time for several seconds, during which your opponent cannot attack, is entirely defenseless, and flies back from your attacks less so you can wail on them more. You fight the Ephemeral Vision last, so if you're having trouble hitting it, let it use Energy Rain, then activate EX Revenge and get it while it's reeling. And if you run out of time, just retry, KP Gambler gives no penalties for starting a fight over.

A new arena (I think? I'm trying to note the arenas as they get included but maybe I'm forgetting) is Pandemonium, a very closed-in arena with walls and low ceilings, certain attacks that explode over a large area can hit between these walls, like the Despot's Flare, or like the Sky Pirate's Cataclysm, they just ignore the wall entirely. The Battlegen item is the Demon's Soul, but it doesn't unlock anything new, it just lets you buy old stuff, like the useless Angel Brooch and largely useless Pendant. Yeah, I know I've said a lot that the new accessories you unlock suck, but they do, only about a dozen of them are good for affecting your damage output, EX or Assist, the rest are just good in very specific characters builds I never use. The Battle Gem for example increases the time your Assist Gauge stays charged before decreasing but lowers the charge you get from absorbing EX Force, a heavy handicap, and really how often do you have an Assist Gauge charge and don't use it and don't attack for so long the gauge begins to deplete? And Regen +5%? Here's some math - let's say you Regen, at this level, around 68 HP while in EX Mode. Even with a 10x booster, that's only an extra 34 HP each time, and if you're so heavily damaged you need that boost to your HP recovery rate, you're doing it wrong. Besides once you hit Level 100 you'll have other ways to recover HP, like Exp to HP and the Succubus Soul set.

I'm also gonna mention at this point Tifa gave me a pantyshot as I took out the Dragoon with Meteor Crusher. Why do I note this? Because it's pretty much the only reason she's here, well that and two other reasons. The chest in the corner of the gateway has 1000 gil, grab it and head out.

Wow, I did a lot of ranting huh? Sorry kiddies. On the overworld continue on, there's a Transient Witch and a False Stalwart, the Witch is easy to get the drop on because she attacks with Knight's Axe, which arcs up and to the sides, going nowhere near you when you stand right in front of her. She uses Shockwave Pulsay, which fires an orb that sticks to obstacles and draws you in before exploding. The fight with the False Stalwart gave me the last Stalwart's Hopes I needed for the Hyper Ring, which replaced my Power Ring of course. Buy another one though since you have to trade a Power Ring to get the Hyper Ring. Quick math, the two rings give a total 8% boost, while the Hyper Ring and a 1.5x booster give 7.5%, so in the next chapter, use the two rings. For now stick with just the Hyper Ring, since the Pearl Necklace and Sunrise are also benefiting from our boosters. I also got notification of new shop goods, but nothing too great that we need right now, there is a good Parrying bit but we'll get one for free later in the storyline, and it's expensive.

Exdeath only has one physical attack, Sword Dance, but that is one of his potential counters so if you get blocked, you are going to experience a lot of pain with those boosters pumping up the Sword Dance counters, and again, since you're staggering from being blocked, he has a higher critical hit ratio during the counters. During this round of fights Tifa learned Falcon's Dive and Rolling Blaze. So Jecht has Falcon Punch, Prishe has Falcon Kick, I guess Tifa got the leftovers. Confront the end piece to find Ultimecia, the only other cast member with a character and personality as bland and unexplored as Tifa's. Okay, others are bland, but their confrontations have purpose, Cloud of Darkness has an awesome VA and shows some interesting insights on her thoughts about manikins at least. Ultimecia just says "I'm bored, was looking for someone to fight, you'll do". How exciting, the game doesn't even pretend to have a reason for this fight, it just needs a boss to throw at us.

Ultimecia is a boss that's difficult only on paper. The "difficult on paper" is that she's an long-range aerial character, fighting in an aerial arena, and Tifa is a melee fighter with crappy aerial attacks at this point of the game. The Calcabrina summon removes Break status from us, sounds meh, until you realize it also stops the normal Bravery regen that occurs when we're Broken, so we're stuck at 7 Bravery and can be Broken again easily. She also has an Attractorb, Pearl Necklace, and a Far from Opponent and EX Mode boosters. They don't give much though.

The reason the difficulty is only on paper is because she has three Cursed Rings. Yeah, those accessories that sap defense by 50%, usually worn by manikins? She has three. I started off with a Beat Rush, Broke her, got a Bravery boost from Ifrit, entered EX Mode as I entered Chase, and an HP attack wall rushed her and put her at 200 HP as I began the EX Burst. What an anti-climax this fight is, Ultimecia is a total pushover, you should have no trouble crushing her. I'm almost wondering if this is a glitch, only two boosters and not even good ones, maybe they meant to give her three more boosters and accidentally gave her Cursed Rings? Who knows.

After the battle Tifa runs up to kick Ultimecia, but she turns out to be a manikin. Hm, guess that explains the Cursed Rings. The real Ultimecia prepares to strike Tifa from behind. Suddenly Kain arrives and saves the day. Ultimecia taunts that she'd worry about Kain as an attacker than a savior, and points out a fallen Zidane who has appeared from nowhere, and says Kain attacked him. Tifa believes her for some reason and goes after Kain when he grabs Zidane and takes off.

Now for some reason we're at the other end of the snowfield, chasing Kain back the way we came. I'm not sure how that happened. Buy a Multichain from the moogle and cross the island to the chest to find a BRV Charge. On the low ground below the castle, a Transient Witch guards a Rosetta Stone. And as the map should indicate, a blue gateway.

The enemies here are nothing new, a Transient Gunner, Fleeting Flash, Ephemeral Summoner, Counterfeit Wraith and Imitation Despot. The Despot has the aerial Flare and the Wraith has Fusillade Particle Beam, the one that sends out the barrage of orbs to hit from all directions, but as you fight it in Kefka's Tower most of the orbs will hit an obstacle and disperse. The Flash and Despot have Bahamut while the Summoner has Leviathan, and the three also have the same accessory set-up from Kain's tale, that is accessories to boost their EX absorpion when they're inflicted with Break. Use a Multichain right where you enter the gateway to chain them all.

The chests here have a Wakizashi and a Cyan Drop. The Wakizashi is a new Parrying weapon so equip it for a minor attack boost and possibly better defenses. The Cyan Drop is actually a good item, it lowers Assist Gauge Duration (meh) and gives +10 Initial EX Force. This means when we enter battle, the EX Gauge is filled 10%. This stacks over multiple fights though, so say you enter a gateway and equip the Cyan Drop. When you finish the battle you keep the 10% EX you got and get another 10% in the next fight. This makes is an effective accessory at this point in the game since EX is hard to charge up, the Pearl Necklace is good for that but later is drastically lowers the rate our Assist charges up at, and duration loss is fine because we use them quickly anyway, but loss of charge speed is bad, so we'll be using the Cyan Drop in the final chapter. Later in the game the Cyan Gem and First To Victory are superior, but we have to make do with what we have now, of course.

Head back to where we began the story, there's nothing else along the way except for a Pink Tail up a hill to the left halfway there, and some orbs of the dead of course. Kain enters the gateway and we follow, de-equip the Pearl Necklace for the Cyan Drop before entering and buy a Straight Chain from the Moogle.

Tifa approaches Kain and he raises his spear against her, and Tifa uses a Potion on him when she notices he's weak. Tifa refuses to believe he's evil, after all he saved her and has been loyal all this time. Kain still won't explain what's going on and Tifa decides to invite herself to follow him since she's used her Potion and plays helpless and tells Kain they're friends to make him agree. Yawnfest 2012 over here. To recap, this chapter serves to establish Kain's motives for his apparent betrayal, and what he's been doing with the other heroes and in general since he's been gone...just like the last two chapters did. From a narrative standpoint, should Tifa's chapter have come before Kain and Light's?

Straight Chain at D4 of course, we can't get all four and don't need to anyway. Line-up is Ephemeral Summoner, Fleeting Flash, Imaginary Brawler and Delusory Dragoon. The Summoner and Flash have that EX accessory set-up again, and the Leviathan and Bahamut summons, respectively. But no surprises once again. Kain is our Assist now, his ground Assist is Lance Burst, a dashing impaling attack that kicks the enemy into the air and lets us Chase them, while his aerial Assist is Crashing Dive and you know what that does and thus know it is awesome for comboing. However we don't really need Assists since the last round of enemies are pansies. At Level 16 Tifa will pick up Dolphin Kick, basically an aerial uppercut. The chest has a White Drop, it's basically a weaker version of the Pearl Necklace and therefore is useless.

After the first fight Tifa talks to Kain and tosses the "friend" word around again. Ya know, in Kingdom Hearts and Yugioh, it was somewhat forgivable because it's the main themes of the franchises and everyone abuses those words. Here, it's just making Tifa look bland and forgettable, devoid of her own character because they have to define her through her relationship with the rest of the group. And guess what, it's working.

As we end the stage, Kain finally comes clean about what's going on, deciding for no real reason all of a sudden Tifa is a summoned warrior and deserves the truth. I guess the four or five other people he's attacked by this point didn't. Tifa is upset of course that all their fighting is pointless, but Kain consoles her that when she awakens the next cycle she won't remember any of this. So Tifa says it's time to go find the others then, she doesn't want people to get hurt so she's going to help Kain save them. I should feel something here, the revelation that Tifa agrees with Kain, but the scripting and voice acting on Tifa is so atrocious I really don't care, and it's not like it amounts to anything anyway because spoiler alert, next chapter they decide to help Lightning and crew stop the manikins instead. Again, doesn't it feel like Tifa's chapter should have come earlier in the story, when these plot revelations and her decision might have been relevant?

Preparations

Before we begin the last chapter of the 012 cycle, a couple things to take care of. First, go buy Shantotto in the PP Catalog, along with the usual purchases, then select Sephiroth in Quick Battle and fight Shantotto, Battlegen an item from her. Why? Because once we've Battlegen'd an item from Shantotto we can Battlegen items from the Empyreal Paradox, which happens to be one of the items needed for the Muscle Belt and thus soon when we get to the Paradox we can get the items we need to use the Muscle Belt in the 013 story. Chapter 1 will pit us against the Sephiroth Manikins we need to face for the rest of them.

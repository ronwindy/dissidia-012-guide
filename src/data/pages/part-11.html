Onion Powah!

I like that D012 made the fairly simple alteration of putting the storylines in a sensical, somewhat chronological order, rather than just leave you to figure it out yourself. Anyway, character time.

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Onion Knight

The Onion Knight, much like the Warrior of Light, has the problem of being an original character. They made him more memorable though, he's a cheeky wimp who is actually quite cowardly and foolish, and puts up an act of being brave and intelligent to hide it. He's given a pretty weak character arc as a result, but even if his development isn't all that interesting, he's still amusing and memorable, in part to a good VA performance from Aaron Spaan.

The Onion Knight is a fast and agile character, armed with fast-hitting low-range sword attacks and slow long-range magic attacks. His attacks don't do much damage though, but he makes up for it with HP links. Uniquely, while other characters learn their HP links by mastering appropriate attacks, the Onion Knight learns BRV to BRV attacks, then once he masters the BRV link, he learns the HP link. This means mastery of his attacks costs less AP, though totalled with the BRV link it's about the same as other characters, and his attacks can all link to Bravery or HP attacks, like Blizzard to Blizzaga or Quake. This goes for all his Bravery attacks too. His HP attacks are slow to start but are similarly divided into sword and magic. He later learns "booster" abilities to give him temporary boosts to his attack, defense and critical hit rate for either magic or physical attacks. There's not much to talk about when it comes to what his attacks do, you start out with all four of them and what you see is what you get, they're pretty straightforward.

The Onion Knight's problem is that he's incredibly predictable, because aside from his link attacks, he has only 8 attacks, 4 Bravery, two in the air one each for melee and magic, two on the ground for melee and magic, and 4 HP attacks, same distribution. This means at most times he only has four attacks available to him, and three of them - the spell and HPs - are slow to start up while the melee one is easily blocked. This unfortunately makes the Onion Knight one of the weakest characters in the game. He's not one of my #4 that I never use, but he is certainly one of my last choices aside from them for a fight. Against the AI they're of course too stupid to realize his flaws, but I'll reckon human opponents are no threat. But if you're really good at faking out the opponent and playing mind games, I guess give him a shot. As I've said before though, this walkthrough is only concerned with story mode, not human opposition. In that regard the Onion Knight is perfectly capable, though still low on the ladder and outclassed by many other characters.

Time to equip the Onion Knight of course. He can't use the Mythril Helm stuff, so I gave him the Snowscepter, Wakizashi, Wizard's Har and Silken Shirt. I bought him three more accessory slots and gave him a Hyper Ring, Muscle Belt, HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, and Level 1 - 9. I'll be relying on his melee attacks in this story since they hit faster and do more damage. Also note I had an AP calendar bonus, helpful indeed since I'll automatically get 3 more AP per fight and will master Onion's attacks quicker.

As we approach the end piece, Terra says they've reached the power source she sensed, when suddenly she begins to moan and clutch her head. This fight is pretty much just like the fight you had against Terra back in Vaan's story, the only difference is that you're in a different arena, and due to the costume change her Esper form is in grape flavor instead of blueberry. She has that Puppeteer's Wheel keeping her in EX Mode for pretty much the entire fight, filling her EX Gauge with just a few motes of EX Force, but she also has a Cursed Ring, of course. She'll spam Tornado the entire fight, dual-casting it naturally since she's in EX Mode, but will never use her EX Burst. This is pretty simple, she only has the ground version of Tornado, so get close, but not too close. This will bait her into using Tornado, back off, wait for it to end, then fire off a spell or approach as she lands and hit her with a melee attack. Very easy, no danger at all here.

I naturally used Onion Knight's EX Mode here so let's cover that. His EX Mode transforms him into a Ninja and a Sage, he'll automatically shift into Ninja for physical attacks (including all Chase attacks), and into a Sage for magic. His EX Mode powers up his physical attacks to insane levels, doing twice as many hits. His spells change in slightly different ways, his Thunder fires out five orbs of lightning instead of three, all around him instead of just in front too, Blizzard fires further and thus can hit on a stronger angle, and Comet fires out more meteorites. Though he doesn't know it yet I'll note this so you know when the time comes, in his EX Mode his Firaga HP attack fires out three fireballs in a spread pattern instead of just one fireball straight ahead. He has two EX Bursts, depending on if it was a magic or physical attack that initiated it. For a physical, the Ninja uses Ninjutsu, and for a magical, he uses Spellbook. For Ninjutsu, you need to open the Throw menu and select the Shuriken. Spellbook, open the Magic menu and select Holy. The two EX Bursts have identical power, so there's no preference to either innately, it depends on if the opponent has equipment or accessories that reduces damage from one type and not the other.

With Terra tired after Onion's thrashing, she suddenly vanishes, teleported away by the Cloud of Darkness. The time has come to awaken Terra's true power, and all he did was help them, guiding her along with false wisdom even as she knew something wasn't right, then beating her into submission so they could kidnap her. The Cloud vanishes, leaving poor little Onion Knight alone.

The Cloud of Darkness has the same moveset as when we faced her with Laguna way back in Chapter 3 of the 012 story, but this time she has a level advantage on us and four Blue Gems, each of them giving her another 60 base Bravery. That 543 Bravery in the table is her base Bravery before factoring in the boost she gets from being at a higher level. In short, she can rapidly knock off large chunks of your HP with little effort, so knowing her HP attacks and how to avoid them is crucial. Fortunately you've faced her and the manikins of her a lot by this point so you know how to do so. That same Bravery also makes it harder to Break her, and of course when you finally do there's Hecatoncheir to rain on the parade.

0-Form Particle Beam is the worst threat, its range outstrips that of the Onion Knight's attacks, its damage priority will deflect or cancel out all his attacks, and at close-range it's hard to dodge and inflicts wall rush. Feel like eating 900 points of HP damage from one hit? Because it's probably going to happen if she fires off 0-Form while you're close enough for her to hit you with it. Unless you can hit her as she begins the attack, find a quickmove icon and run away quickly. Ultimately though all her attacks are dangerous due to the sheer versatility of them and her high Bravery. She's not as stupid as you might have come to expect from the AI at this point, she one blocked my attack and fires off a 0-Form right in my face. Not pleasant.

Your safest bet is to land a blow as she tries a tentacle attack, block it and hit her as she staggers, remember your HP links if you've learned any. Onion Knight's HP attacks are slow so she's very liable to damage you as you recover if she avoids you. She's ultimately not too bad because she still isn't that smart, just smarter than a lot of previous enemies. She gets a 2 on my template solely for her high Bravery letting you blast you into the Void with only a few hits. Otherwise, not much harder than any previous boss.

With her defeated she fades into darkness, and Golbez arrives to speak to us again. Onion Knight vows to protect Terra from him, and for following his heart and proving himself his Crystal manifests for him. Terra awakens, though still weak, and fall unconscious again shortly after. The Onion Knight, tired after his battle, follows suit. However the danger isn't over, because with the Cloud of Darkness's failure, Kefka is ready to take the stage and finish what she couldn't.

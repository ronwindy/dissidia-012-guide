Twilight Knights

Well, guess who now?

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Cecil

Cecil has the unfortunately distinction of being the most dry and bland of the core 10 heroes. Sure, he wasn't exactly an interesting guy back in IV, but they did more for the Warrior of Light and Onion Knight than they did for Cecil, and they didn't exist at all until this game. Cecil's character arc in the 13th cycle largely focuses on him wondering if he can trust Golbez or not, since he's Cecil's brother but also an enemy. I do appreciate that they explore their relationship deeper than The After Years did, but mostly it boils down to Cecil running after Golbez crying "Brother!" and then angsting when Golbez talks down to him. Beyond that, not much character to talk about here.

Cecil fights somewhat similar to Lightning, he switches forms automatically between Dark Knight and Paladin when attacking. The Dark Knight specializes in ground attacks and aerial moves that bring himself or his opponent to the ground, while the Paladin relies on aerial combo attacks and ground moves that take the fight to the skies. Cecil can only change form now by using an HP attack, shifting into Dark Knight mode for ground HP attacks and Paladin for aerial ones. He can change freely in his EX Mode, which I'll explain later, and he'll get Bravery attacks that let him shift forms later at around Level 30. Cecil has reliable moves, his projectile attacks are good and his melee combos have a good amount of range on them, but I find that aside from his Searchlight set-ups, he isn't really a stand-out character. There could be a lot of potential for creative combos if they gave him more freedom over his form shifting, but they don't, aside from relying on a sustained EX Mode build.

Cecil's Dark Knight begins with Valiant Blow, a quick three-hit combo with shocking range to it, Dark Cannon which sends out orbs of darkness that spiral back to him and drag the opponent closer, and Gravity Ball which in the air sends an orb of darkness out to drag the opponent into the dirt. Dark Cannon to Valiant Blow or Soul Eater will be our bread-and-butter combo. As a Paladin he has Slash, which has him spin forward and up to knock the opponent away, and in the air Radiant Wings, a spinning combo forward that then has him slam the opponent down, and Sacred Cross, where he dashes under the opponent and spins up into the air to begin a Chase. A good combo is to use Slash and then immediately follow it up with Radiant Wings, but this only works if you're near a wall, otherwise the opponent flies too far away form Slash and Radiant Wings won't connect. His HP attacks are Soul Eater, a forward stab that does Bravery damage before slamming the opponent backwards, and Saint's Fall, a rising swoop that has him rise up and them slam the opponent into the ground.

Cecil is perfectly capable in his own story, but he's not one of my favs.

As always, grab Cecil three more accessories slots, and give him a Hyper Ring, Muscle Belt, Sunrise, HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, and Level 1-9. He can't use the Mythril Bangle so he lacks the Mystic Mythril set, but that's fine, Cecil has his own custom set of gear sprinkled throughout his story that we'll be using. During this part I had an Exp calendar day, but I pointedly avoided buying upgrades to my Exp calendar bonuses so that I don't end up overleveled and get KP penalties, and I don't mislead you kiddies into what I say are easy fights when I'm 4 levels higher than you. As a result the bonus is only 1.1x the normal Exp won, 10% is not a huge amount, if your levels are any more than 2 levels lower than what I mention my levels are as I cover the storyline, then equip a Chocobo Wing or Chocobo Powder for a boost in a few fights and you'll catch up just fine. As Cecil takes a moment to wonder how to find the Crystal, Golbez appears and warns him that hesitation and doubt will hinder the path. Firion approaches and Golbez vanishes, ominously telling Cecil to follow him if he wants to find the Crystal.

Straight Chain at E2, of course, gets all four with the Initial BRV Boost blue emblem. The bonus line as usual for first gateways is 2, and the KP Chance is a 90 second win. From this storyline on, it may be my imaginary or faulty memory, but there is a slight difficulty spike, you're going to be seeing several Expert Battle Pieces in this story, and for the next five Expert and Strange enemies seem more common. First up, a Delusory Knight with Dark Lance, Slash, Searchlight, which sends a disc of light at you to stop and fire projectiles, and Saint's Fall as his HP hitter. Next is a Transient Gunner with Homing Bazooka and Satellite Laser for the HP attack. Now it's the two Hard enemies, the Capricious Thief has Scoop Art, Storm Impulse, an aerial spinning attack with decent range but easy to block, and for the HP attack, Tidal Flame, which either of Cecil's HP attacks can reflect, the build is wrapped up with the Pupu summon. The Thief also has the accessory gimmick for Cecil's story, a Zephyr Cloak and Heart's Ease with 8 Near Loss boosters, giving them a pretty impressive boost to their Bravery every time they block or dodge an attack. Of course, this assumes the enemy is smart enough to block or dodge your attacks in the first place, and most of the time they aren't. The last foe is a Fleeting Flash with the same accessories, Smite, Aerora, Lightning Strike, and Pupu.

The chest, when you're done, has a Dark Sword. This is one of Cecil's four exclusive equipment pieces found in his story, when three are equipped at once he gets a powerful Combination, the same way as Mythril gives you an ability when three pieces are worn, but the Dark equipment pieces have a different effect. The Dark Sword is pretty potent so use it if you like, but as with Mythril we need three pieces to get real use of them, so I kept Cecil with a Nodachi.

A Divergent Path

On the world map immediately turn around and attack the Fallacious Tree. As a nice nod to continuity, the trio is in the same region they were checking out when Cloud went off alone. Go to the sealed gateway behind the Tree when you've chopped it into kindling to find a BRV Charge. The Moogle has nothing you desperately need, but if you have 200 KP and want a new weapon, I leave it to you. Beyond it at the other sealed gate is a chest with Mythril, save it, we now want to to get Mythril Armor or Mythril Vest. The stone on the outcropping opposite leads to a chest with 1991 gil. Now returning to the main path (your companions are visible to help you figure out the way), there's another Fallacious Tree on the shoreline to chop down, on the island beyond it is the Dark Helm, and by the gateway now there's an EX Charge. Remember this gateway, it's important much, much later. Now only the next gateway awaits.

Multichain at B4 to get them all, sadly we can't use the emblems here. The KP Chance is a 60 second Bravery Break, and the bonus line is 5. Go ahead and use the BRV Charge or EX Charge if you like, I didn't though.

First is a Phantasmal Girl, she uses Blizzard Combo on the ground, Blizzara in the air, Meltdown for HP, has those Near Loss accessories, and the Asura summon. Asura does one of three random effects when called, she either shatters your summonstone to prevent you from using it, forces it to activate, or seals it like Ramuh. Not a problem, we don't much use our summons for much except bosses anymore. Well I don't, I just stick with Cactuar now. Next is a Delusory Knight with Shadow Lance, Nightfall, Slash and Searchlight for Bravery attacks, Nightfall is a quick downward slam attack that creates a short burst of darkness on impact and begins a Chase, be careful if he's above you in Dark Knight form. Otherwise, those accessories, Asura, and Saint's Fall as an HP attack. Cecil hit Level 4 at this point and learned Nightfall for his own uses. Now it's the Counterfeit Youth's turn, he has the melee Bravery attacks, Blade Torrent, and those same accessories.

Last but not least, it's Strange time. The enemy is an Imaginary Soldier with Climhazzard, Rising Fang, and Braver. He's got a Hyper Ring and Muscle Belt with 5 Near Opponent boosters, the enemy 2x versions, and an Arcane Resin for a full EX Gauge. And finally, the Giant of Babil summon. If he lands Climhazzard, you will feel great pain, he's got a big damage buff and a good chance to score critical hits since he'll likely be in EX Mode. Be sure to listen and watch for when Climhazzard begins so you can block him, and don't get above him, stay on the ground so Rising Fang can't be used properly. He can do a lot of damage very fast to be careful, he's a surprisingly tough opponent. His summon is pretty worthless fortunately, the Giant takes away a percentage of your Bravery, but even with EX Mode's Regen he's going to be weak enough to kill in one blow.

As Cecil continues to wonder how to handle Golbez, Tidus tells him to head off on his own and get the answer. Cecil takes off to find Golbez at the volcano.

Pricey, but what else are we saving up for much anymore, the moogle equipment is now being equalled by our shop equipment right? So I bought Brynhildr, and if you need one get a Multichain. Go the blue gateway on the left, there's a Pink Tail nearby, and then head inside. Fun note, this area is where Lightning met the moogles, that moogle is the first moogle shop you ever visited way back in Chapter 1 of the 012 story.

Multichain at C2 to get them all, there's an Expert Battle Piece here to deal with. KP Chance is a Break in 60 seconds.

The first foe is a False Stalwart, easy prey. Next is a harder Phantasmal Harlequin, it has Pupu, those Bravery accessories, and uses Ultima, Meteor and Hyperdrive. Breaking him in 60 seconds is hard with Pupu. A few ways to do it, build up EX Force against the Stalwart and go EX Mode, let the Harlequin block and then hit after because when an enemy mistimes a block the next attack is all critical hits, or just be all-out aggressive, if you go Valiant Blow on him over and over, as long as he doesn't dodge too often, you can just do more damage than Pupu gives and you can get him after several seconds of battering down his Bravery. Now comes an Imaginary Champion, he has Fervent Blow (the ground sword beam), Oblivion, Hell's Gate, and those Bravery accessories.

And the last one is the Ephemeral Phantom, he has full equipment, a Hyper Ring and Guardian Bangle with 8 HP = 100% boosters, and the Phoenix Summon. Phoenix grants him Reraise, so for a period of time, whenever you reduce his Bravery enough to Break him, his Bravery restores to its base value and he isn't Broken. And he has pretty much all of Jecht's attacks, here's Jecht's page to read up on them since we haven't fought him or his manikins lately, and he has Jecht Block both on the ground and in the air so he can block anything you through at him, and is smart enough to do it his share of times.

Base line, keep your distance until you're ready to attack since his Bravery combos come out fast, and wait for him to let loose if you see him charge an attack and get him as he attacks and misses. Remember, both his Bravery attacks combo right into HP attacks, so it is vital you not get hit. To get the KP Chance get him to use Phoenix before the 30 second mark, then wear him down to where a combo will Break him, and wait for Phoenix to wear off a few seconds before time is up. And of course as always, HP attack first thing to get rid of those nasty boosters. This is one of the toughest fights I've had, he got a lot of HP hits in, even Broke me. I used Cecil's EX Burst to finish the fight, which I'll cover in the next gateway. The chest when you're done has a Shell Shield, an effective new armor piece.

First is a Delusory Dragoon, he has Cyclone, Lance Barrage, and Jump, piece of cake, though he seemed to be good about hitting me after I wall rushed him so maybe he's a bit smarter? Or just luckier, who knows? Next is an Ephemeral Summoner with Aerospark, Impulse, Energy Ray, those Bravery accessories, and Pupu. Last is a Delusory Knight with Valiant Blow, Gravity Ball, and Saint's Fall.

The Expert enemy is a Counterfeit Wraith, she has the same moveset that all the higher-level Cloud of Darknesses have, the Tiamat summon, the same accessories as the Ephemeral Phantom higher up, and of course full equipment. She seemed to favor the Bravery attacks on my game, block them and punish her. I used Cecil's EX Burst here so let's cover that now. Cecil's EX Mode swaps out his generic spear-type swords for the Mythgraven Blade and Deathbringer. He does 150% normal Bravery damage, in addition to the critical hit rate bonus, and by pressing R and Square. It's mostly a convenience, like say you want to use Valiant Blow without having to do Soul Eater to swing or vice-versa with a Paladin attack. Now, it could potentially open up combo possibilities, like dragging them close with Dark Cannon, switching to Paladin and hitting them with Slash, but I find that the change takes too long to reliably set up those types of moves, maybe against a dumber AI who won't dodge away but not against the smarter AI. Cecil's EX Burst has you input two sets of commands, you must press the indicated directional button at the same time as the indicated X-Circle-Triangle-Square button, three times. It's pretty simple once you try it once or twice.

The summon is the Magus Sisters, they're automatically called when you get Broken and instantly sap half the opponent's Bravery, also halving their base Bravery for a while. They're useless, there are a lot of summons in Dissidia to sap Bravery more reliably, right now we have Cactuar, and later there's Leviathan, Lich, Omega, even Hecatoncheir will sap the Bravery pool and so sap your opponent's boost if they're about to Break you. Anyway, approach the end piece. Golbez is pleased Cecil has followed him and begins to speak to him about what he meant earlier, but a familiar face arrives. Exdeath laughs that Golbez is a traitor and is here to dispense justice appropriately. Cecil, of course, does not agree with this plan.

Exdeath is back from Kain's story, and he's got all the same tricks as before. In fact he's almost the same opponent as last time, he's the same level, has a few hundred more HP, mostly the same attacks. The major difference? In Kain's story Exdeath had several good boosters buffing up an Attractorb and Pearl Necklace. This Exdeath has...a Cursed Ring. Right there, his good arm is crippled. He also swapped out Hecatoncheir for Brynhildr, bad move on his part, because that summon so easily backfires if your opponent gets the Break.

Ultimately though, aside from your attacks doing more Bravery damage, Exdeath is a very similar opponent to before. He's got the same sneaky tricks like Almagest and then cancelling into an Omni Block, he's still got the Reverse Polarity to Hurricane combo. Do not use Gravity Ball on him, it moves way too slowly and is just an invitation for him to Omni Block it and fire off a counterattack. You could try Dark Cannon, the AI I found tries to block it on the initial firing and not the return trip, but it's Planet's Core so good luck lining it up to get Dark Cannon to hit. Sacred Cross is a good move to spam here, since Radiant Wings is a bit slower and easier for him to block, Sacred Cross is harder to predict I find, though it's mostly due to Radiant Wing's spinning motion taking longer. It's Planet's Core so ground attacks are out. As before, fake out Exdeath by attacking and baiting him into using Reverse Polarity, neutral dodge to have him teleport in front of him, and smash Cecil's spear into his face.

Exdeath vanishes, leaving some rather meaningless yin-yang-esque mocking words in Cecil's ear. Golbez is confused though, he fought Exdeath alone to defend what he believes in and won, but the Crystal didn't appear. He thought Cecil would be strong enough to get it. Cecil asks Golbez to come with him to tell his friends about his advice, and then they can look for the Crystal together. Golbez refuses, unsuiting to accompany him, and is disappointed in Cecil's attitude. He leaves, and Cecil is alone again on the world map.

Cecil hit Level 11 and learned Dark Flame here, it sends a plume of dark fire at the energy similar to Shining Wave, it's pretty good, and you can send it out then jump up and use Saint's Fall, doubling your opponent's threat of getting hit. Something quick I want to note I miss, in the original Dissidia the storyline fights had the characters begin the fight in the same locations as they were in the preceding cutscene, in this game they begin in the same starting positions as normal fights. I just miss that little touch, helped blend the cutscenes and fights together.

Anyway, Golbez time. He has all four Cosmic Ray chains, so aside from Attack Force and Gravity Force, all his Bravery attacks chain into HP attacks, and he has Nightglow and Genesis rock. He's got good equipment and an Earring with four Far From Opponent. Not sure why because Golbez is a mid-range to melee fighter, Close to Opponent would be far better. He's also got a Delusory Dragoon as an Assist. I like that he has Kain as an Assist and Giant of Babil as a summon, so fitting for Golbez, yes?

He's not a very complex opponent, or really all that difficult. Exdeath was a far more challenging opponent to me based solely on the vast level difference, Golbez has only three levels on him, but you've gained far more than that by this point so the power gap between Cecil and Golbez is much smaller. As well, Golbez has lower Defense than Exdeath, so overall you'll easily do a lot of hurt to him. Attack Force is useless, Gravity Force is easy to count, and his other attacks, just don't get close to him, or do if you know what cues to watch for and know how to counter or block them. A Break and Wall Rush sapped half his HP for me, and then an EX Burst to Break him again finished him off.

As Golbez kneels, weakened, the Crystal appears to Cecil as proof he kept his faith in the things he already had faith in despite his brother challenging this faith. I get that the Crystals appear when they prove their will and determination, but for a lot of these guys, it isn't until the villain shows up that those traits are in doubt, or the revelation they reveal at the end of their storyline is just a reaffirmation of what they already believed. Just underscores how poor the original Dissidia's story was. Anyway, Golbez departs, leaving Cecil to return to his friends.

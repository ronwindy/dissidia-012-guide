Land of Discord and Feral Chaos

The time has come kiddies! Land of Discord and the end of the game's storyline and this walkthrough are imminent!

Return to Where It Began

You don't have to worry about Chain skills if you don't want to for the next few gateways, I'll still note how to chain most enemies but earning KP from them isn't a concern. First, you should already have several thousand, and second, by the time you need more you'll have access to the most efficient gateway for earning KP. Besides that, the KP Chance is hard to meet, because in this final region, all enemies are Level 120+. Good luck getting that 60 second win/Break every single time. Far easier (and less madening) to just not worry about KP for these gateways.

Also, if you don't yet have Level 100 equipment for everyone, go farm some Lufenian items at an earlier gateway. Try and use matching pieces if you can, since you probably can't access Level 100 equipment in the shop on a major basis yet, you're going to have to make due with Lufenian stuff, so you might as well access their combination skills. For me, Cloud and Lightning are using Excalibur, Prishe has the Level 100 Ehrgeiz grappling weapon, and Zidane and Terra have their exclusive Level 100 weapons. If you have the trade accessory needed for a Level 100 weapon, you should have several Electrums, while Scarletites for the Level 30s are sold by the moogle near the Gulg Volcano, accessible by the Pravoka or Elven Castle teleport stones. Easy to spot and get to, if you're unsure it's a white ring on your map.

Head into the Land of Discord from the Citadel of Trials teleport stone and make a white. You'll come to the Inherited Memories gateway.

First, beat the enemy at C4, then move to G5 and use a Multichain to get the rest of them, avoiding all those pesky red emblems. The KP Chance is a 60 second win.

First, a Level 125 Ephemeral Vision with Omega and an Ephemeral Summoner, and you're forced to fight on a Physical Damage 0 emblem. Then, chain and Party time! Enter the opposition - a Level 128 Ephemeral Phantom with Omega, a Level 128 Lady of Antiquity with Ultima Weapon, and a Level 129 Warrior of Antiquity with Omega and an Idle Sky Pirate. Beyond the gateway is a dead end with a Moogle. He sells the Adamantite and Samurai Spirit accessories, needed to trade for the top-grade Level 100 Adamant and Genji sets. The two sets are both three-set combinations, though they have pieces in all four equipment types. Adamant Chains boosts your Bravery every time you dodge an attack and increases the distance you move when you dodge in midair, making it easier to dodge attacks, while the Genji set's Blessing of Yamato gives you +3 luck, +20% HP Regen, converts extra HP from any regeneration into Bravery, and raises the rate at which EX Cores appear. The moogle also sells the four pieces of the Snowpetal set, there's no weapon of the set but two hand pieces, its combination doubles the power of deflected magic attacks and also gives you a nice boost to your Bravery when you block an attack, equal to 30% of your base Bravery. All three are excellent equipment pieces, some of the best in the game, go nuts if you're tired of the Lufenian stuff. The moogle also sells the Omega, Hecatoncheir and Ultima Weapon summons, buy all three.

Well, that was worth the trip huh kiddies? Time to get to the proper quest now, head back to the entrance of the Land of Discord.

Multichain at A4 to get them all, with a KP Chance of a 60 second win. Four of the six enemies here are Strange, the rest, only Level 100s. I think they meant to make them all Strange but forgot or something, or maybe they forgot to make the two normal enemies 120s, who knows.

Then a Level 100 Counterfeit Wraith with Tiamat and 0-Form Particle Beam. Then the Party piece, a Strange Level 100 Delusory Dragoon with Bahamut and a full move-set, a Level 100 Transient Lion with the Magus Sisters and a Transient Gunner, and a Level 100 Imaginary Soldier with Bomb and an Imaginary Brawler. When you're done, the chest has 30,000 gil.

What makes this gateway so significant is that this is the best one for earning KP. Come here with a Level 1 character with no equipment, and for accessories the four "No equipment" boosters, Level 1-9, Level Gap ≥ 90, and any 1.4 or 1.5 booster you like, Pre-EX Mode will do nicely. Then equip the Smiting Soul accessory. You'll get 99 KP for going under the bonus line, and in battle all opponents will enter with 9999 Bravery, until you hit them and Iai Strike has a 99.9% of instantly Breaking them, transferring that 9999 Bravery to you and making the battle go quite quickly. Clear out the enemies one by one without chain skills, and grab the moogle chalice for 240 total KP.

Now, this isn't much more than the Frozen Continent gateway. However, because you can get to 240 KP without any skills, you can bring in 5 KP Bonuses with you, increasing that 240 KP to 7,680. Strictly speaking one of the Multifloor gateways gives much more, into the tens of thousands, head into the Forsaken Land to find out. In the long run, you'll find this is the best gateway for KP even without KP Bonuses, since Frozen Continent takes longer to clear, bring along Multichains and a KP Gambler for even more winnings, include a KP Switch if you think the KP Chance is too difficult.

With the gateway open, the moogle tells us he senses a portal to the Rift, and freedom, has opened, but first we have to defeat Chaos. The last teleport stone, Onrac, is here. Go left from it to find an Elixir, then head right and grind up onto the floating islands to the last three gateways.

God in Fire

Kiddies, this part of the walkthrough, I won't be playing through the game as I walkthrough it here. The reason is, frankly, I've done this part of the game so often I could play through it in my sleep. Welcome to the final gateway, it's five floors but don't worry, we're just going to skip to the end fighting as few enemies as possible, after all we want to conserve our strength. Feel free to mix up party members and draw out fights to charge up EX Gauges.

The first enemy in our path is a Level 120 Delusory Dragoon with Ultima Weapon, defeat him and continue to the next floor. The chest has a Gold Angel, if you want it the enemy guarding it is a Level 120 Fleeting Flash. On the second floor, the enemy is a Level 121 Transient Gunner with Ultima Weapon and a Transient Witch, beat him and continue on, the chests only have Friendship and Valor Resin, if you want them then go to a moogle and don't be cheap. On the third floor, the enemy is a Level 122 Imitation Liegeman with Omega, the blue emblem before it restores your HP before the fight. On the fourth floor there's two enemies, a Level 127 Imaginary Champion with Ultima Weapon and an Imaginary Soldier, and a Level 125 False Stalwart with Ultima Weapon and a False Hero. Fight your Assist after if you want the Potion and just flee to make them vanish.

On the fifth floor, as you enter your Assist is chosen at random. I recommend Gabranth, Kuja, Jecht or Gilgamesh, preferably Jecht. To get this, use the home button on your PSP to quit the game, when you reload you'll load the game's auto-save to appear back on the fourth floor next to the end piece, and get a new random Assist when you proceed to the fifth, rinse and repeat until you like who they give you. The mured moogle restores our HP, and the path to the boss is clear. On all characters, equip Level Gap ≥ 30, Large Gap in HP, (Opp) HP ≥ 10000, (Opp) Summon Unused, and whatever other boosters you like. Also consider an Iifa Leaf for everyone. For summons I would recommend Alexander, Bahamut, Omega, Demon Wall, and even Tiamat, Kraken or Carbuncle. Then approach the piece.

Enjoy the amazing boss music, Cantata Mortis & God in Fire. This is the ultimate challenge, Feral Chaos. He's Level 130 with over 120,000 HP, and due to his level innately has a base Bravery of over 3000. Let's run down his attacks. Vicious has him leap in place for a moment before lunging forward, it crushes guards. Spew has him slam the ground to summon columns of flame below you, it can be blocked or dodged easily. Erupt is an upwards charge attack straight up that has him spin when he decides to stop, not seen too much, I find Feral Chaos prefers to jump up to my level. Destroy is his basic aerial Bravery attack and is his main offense with Vicious, a quick combo attack with a spinning tail horizontally, it can be blocked. Splinter launches four large waves of fire at you, each once staggers you and tracks individually. Raid is the opposite of Erupt, he rushes down to spin when he stops or to slam into the ground and create a shockwave on impact, for this reason stay out from under him if he's high in the air.

For HP attacks, Quo Vadis has him pause for a moment then warp towards you and uppercut into the air, then he performs a series of attacks and slams you into a wall. Deus Iratus has him block and summon a shockwave around him. Via Dolorosa has him send out two waves of fire, but he can delay the firing of the second wave, it's arguably his worst attack because he is very patient and will wait for you to dodge and sends it out to catch you on the recovery, or he waits for you to land from a jump and sends it out during the brief moment you're recovering and can't dodge. In the air he has Ventus Ire, he angles up or down at you and flaps his wings to generation wind at short-range, Lux Magnus has him fire a pillar of fire straight down in the ground and he can move to chase you with it, and Flagro Maximus has him charge up a large fireball he launches forward. Also, all of Feral Chaos's attacks Wall Rush, and his Bravery ones all begin Chase as well, if you don't hit a wall first.

Now to round out that arsenal with an Assist and summon. Garland is his Assist, attacking with Round Edge or Bardiche. Round Edge can only be blocked on the first swing, remember. Garland is one of the two factors that makes this battle so annoying, Feral Chaos is very good at coordinating attacks with him, comboing a wall rush with an Assist and an HP attack as we often have, and when he calls out Garland while charging Via Dolorosa, well guess what, 9 times out of ten you're going to get hit, even if you dodge Garland, here comes the fire wave. Next, Shinryu Verus, his summon, is drastically depowered from the original. He copies the effects of the Four Fiend summons - 20 second timer to a Bravery swap, triple Bravery, freezing your Bravery, or sapping it to 0. Pretty easy to handle, the Bravery swap is easily to manipulate, let Feral Chaos beat you up then land an HP attack to swipe his Bravery and leave him at 0, triple Bravery, whittle him down, Bravery freeze, rack up as much as you can and spam HP attacks, sap to 0, land an HP attack just before it goes off and its effect is wasted.

Here's where things go from bad to worse - Feral Chaos has three unique accessories, one to just boost his HP. Endless Despair gives him a 400% damage boost in EX Mode, and has his Assist charge up 50% faster. and Ruthless Atrocity...any time he lands an HP attack, even if it does no damage, your Assist and EX Gauges are completely drained. Yeah, that is by far the most annoying part of this fight, for this purpose Assist Change and EX Revenge out of an HP hit if you can, you'll lose them anyway so you might as well avoid the damage part. BUT, there is a mercy! Feral Chaos has piddling Level 1 equipment. As a result, while his attack is only slighter lower than the Level 120s we've fought so far, his defense is only 167, significantly lower. Thus, with those four boosters I mentioned which will constantly be giving you a 3.8x boost, with a few other boosters you'll actually do quite a lot of Bravery damage, much more than the 120s took.

And well kiddies, good luck. The best advice I have now is, honestly, video footage - this playlist shows all 43 fights I've done against Feral Chaos using one character. Don't mistake me, hundreds, possibly even thousands of retries went into them, especially the No Damage ones, but they will help you out. Watch them, take note of his attack patterns, how his moves work, when he likes using them, how to anticipate them and deal with them, when's a safe time to counter-attack, etc. And Feral Chaos is highly predictable, he can fake you out decently enough when he tries but largely he is very predictable, and once you get the hang of his attacks it's just a matter of dodging and countering as you whittle down that monstrous HP.

As my vids demonstrate Alexander is awesome and almost single-handedly breaks the difficulty of this fight in two. HP wall rush the opponent, call Assist (Jecht), HP attack in the middle of the Assist attack, they're wall rushed by Jecht, third HP attack, knocks off a large chunk of HP. Exp to Assist is my recommended Exp ability of choice, the Assist gauge is a critical lifeline, letting you get in guaranteed hits and avoid a fatal HP attack. You probably will have to use the entire party for this fight, I did on my first time. Feral Chaos is tough, but with my vids for help, your own experience and proper preparation, you can handle him and take him out. Just remember to use Assist Change and EX Revenge to avoid damage, and really, watch the vids, you'll see how Feral Chaos fights and will know what to expect even after just a couple of them, and can also see how I use each character to fight him as I use all but Tifa, Vaan, Tidus and Shantotto. Good luck kiddies, you'll need it, but you can do it!

With Feral Chaos vanquished, five chests appear. Four contain the equipment he was wearing, now yours because defeating him unlocks Feral Chaos for purchase in the PP Catalog as a playable character. I won't give him a profile because Feral Chaos is crippled as a character by very high CP costs, you can only equip three or four attacks with him and he's really a novelty than a character. The four equipment pieces in turn are needed to trade for his exclusive Level 100 equipment. The fifth chest will appear on subsequent trips through the gateway after you beat Feral Chaos on said revisits, and contains a Megalixir.

As we exit, Chaos, having regained his senses, calls out to us - he tells us some intriguing info, and thanks us for saving the mured moogle, and if you didn't know who he was before, well this should make it obvious. The final 20th Report is unlocked, containing the game's secret ending. Go watch it and enjoy it, you've earned it. Well kiddies, what's left? The Labyrinth, which I won't cover, Arcade, also not covered, Feral Chaos solos like mine, and just grinding out trade items to get the best stuff in the game like the exclusive weapons and the combination sets. Otherwise, just go to Quick Battle and have fun slugging it out with your fav characters.

We love you, kiddies! Good night!

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Vaan

I fucking hate Vaan. Yeah I know, everyone does, but I actually didn't mind him in FF12, he was a supporting player and he was fine in that capacity, he was a bit of a goof but he had his moments of insight and wisdom and was ultimately tolerable. Here though he's an outright clueless idiot, incapable of remembering simple names, and even the other party members joke about how stupid and naive he is. My outlook on this shift of character is accompanied by the problem of the two characters his story focuses on aside from him. We're supposed to believe he's Terra's protector? My lovely Esperkin could roast him alive with a snap of the fingers. And for that matter so could Kefka. Sorry kiddies, I'm getting this all out of my system now so this part of the walkthrough isn't just a rant.

As for his fighting style, there are four characters with annoying fighting gimmicks that I decided I didn't care enough to master - Vaan is one of them. Yes I suck with Vaan, I admit that, sue me. I suck by choice though. His gimmick is that when he attacks using a different Bravery attack than the one he just used, he switches weapons, triggering a Switch attack that has different effects. So using Spear when he just used Axe & Shield will have a different effect than the second use of Spear after the first. HP attacks disarm him entirely. I never got the hang of this myself, but if you can, good for you.

Vaan's starting attacks are good enough, Spear is a close-range spin attack that does high damage, Greatsword is a charging attack you can charge up for range, and he'll get Crossbow at Level 2, which fires a barrage of shots as he turns to track enemies. Vaan will learn aerial variants of his attacks a few levels after the ground version. I find these three base attacks enough for Vaan's story and never use him beyond that. His HP attacks are great though. Cataclysm has a good reach into the air and turns on start-up, and Inferno is a basic and simple charging attack. At Level 8 Vaan gets Torrent, which calls a flurry of watery orbs to drive the opponent into the ground from above, and combos well with the Assist that joins him later.

Start off equipping Vaan, we should have gear for him already, but the shop has new weapons and I recommend getting him an Spear for more power than what we already have. Give him the same Power Ring-Level 1-9-HP 100% accessories we've been using before. I won't say this from now on but this is going to be my starting accessory set for all characters, it works well enough for what we have so do the same.

Begin by heading onto the island to get EX Charge from the chest, then shatter the two orbs. On the peniunsula on the right ahead is a chest with a BRV Charge skill, it gives you 5x your base Bravery when you begin the fight. The Manikins ahead are Phantasmal Harlequins, their world map attacks are slow and you can easily strike them before they hit you. Both of them just spam Havoc Wing to try and stab you, but its long start-up makes it easy to dodge and you should be able to Break and kill them pretty quickly. The path to the right, where Lightning's story took us, is empty so go through the empty gateway into the mountains. The other two orbs are here, and in the grass on the right is a chest with 200 gil. The Blue Gateway here is a new sight, these gateways are optional and can be exited any time or skipped entirely, and can be re-entered if you like. For purposes of this walkthrough they are non-optional.

Inside the Onion Knight is waiting for us, he's only in this gateway but he'll be our Assist for now. He's a good one too, his ground Assist begins a Chase while the midair one wall rushes the opponent up into the air. If you have a Jump Chain use it at C3, or use a Multi Chain at D3. You can use that BRV Charge if you want to make the battles easier. Note you can only have five Skills at one time and have to discard one when you find a sixth, so no point hoarding them. The opponents are nothing new, the same three enemy types Lightning faced and another Phantasmal Harlequin. They aren't too hard to beat up. The Harlequin's offense is Meteor, which calls five fireballs to hit the ground around you and bounce toward you, and Hyperdrive, sending a wave of explosions along the ground. Both have long start-up and are easy to dodge. However, you'll notice the last enemy in the right corner is a new type.

Strange Battle Pieces are always risky to face. They begin battle with low HP and can probably be killed in one hit, but they use Summons to sap your Bravery or increase their own, then spam HP attacks. This one is a Fleeting Flash that has Bahamut, giving it regular boosts of 99 Bravery for a period of time, and then it'll use Crushing Blow repeatedly. Keep your distance to avoid the blast and hit it on the recovery. If you're having trouble getting in the HP hit to kill it, Wall Rush it and call your Assist, giving you a clear shot. The two chests have 200 gil and a Blue Drop, which gives a decent +20 base Bravery.

With that done head to the next gateway.

If you have a Multichain, activate it at D3 to chain all five enemies for a cool 25 KP. Otherwise the enemies in the upper-right can be avoided, it's the same trio of Dragoon-Flash-Sky Pirate Manikins we've seen before, I won't cover them because I won't be fighting them and because you should know how to handle them by now. The two enemies we need to fight to get to the end piece are another Phantasmal Harlequin and a new foe, the Phantasmal Girl. The Harlequin has Meteor and Hyperdrive and a new move called Ultima, it's a little harder to anticipate at close-range, and if the start-up hits us it'll blow up in our face. The girl is a tricky one, in the air she has Blizzara, a long-range but medium speed ice projectile we can block, and on the ground she has Blizzard Combo, a fast-executing but very low range combo attack. Her HP attack is Tornado, it lasts several seconds and protects her from attacks, so when she uses it keep your distance and hit her as she recovers.

Open the chest for an Orange Drop and approach the end piece, use EX Charge if you want the benefit. Vaan is suddenly ambushed by a mystery opponent! I won't give this boss a template because it's fairly simple, she spams Tornado, which you likely just saw, but can use it twice in a row. That's all she does, Tornado spam. Let her, then swat her with a Crossbow or charged Greatsword as she recovers. Don't expect her EX Gauge to deplete, a unique accessory all but ensures she'll be in EX Mode the entire fight. This is as good a time as any to talk about Vaan's EX Burst. Press R to shift commands and use White Whorl, then shift to use Pyroclasm. Odds are you'll only have enough time to get the command to come up once so try not to shuffle past it. With Vaan's opponent deciding he isn't worth killing (because I doubt he actually beat her, I mean really) she runs off and Vaan continues on. He should be Level 4 now and will learn the aerial variant of Crossbow.

We have two paths now, the plains to the right or the mountain to the left. Shatter the orb and go right first. The chest on the island has a Cure command for us. The Phantasmal Harlequin up ahead has two new Bravery attacks, a bouncing Blizzaga attack on the ground and in the air, a Blizzaga that shoots forward, stops, then shatters and spreads out. The shattering Blizzaga comes out fast and can deflect Crossbow blasts so be aware of that. Beyond it are two chests with a Leather Chestplate and a Green Drop. Return to the mountain entrance and head up, there's a chest here with another EX Charge, and the other orbs we need for a new chain skill. There's a new enemy two, a False Stalwart, dodging its attack is difficult to be aggressive for the pre-emptive strike. In battle it uses Earthquake, creating spikes of earth in an area in front of it, but its only usable on the ground and has little vertical reach, so stay in the air to crush it with ease.

Guess the four enemies here. If you guessed "Dragoon, Sky Pirate, Flash, Harlequin", have an e-cookie. The Dragoon has Dragon's Fang, the Harlequin has Havoc Wing, the Flash has Lightning Strike and the Sky Pirate has Windburst. As with Lightning's Crushing Blow, use Vaan's Inferno to deflect Windburst.

The new foe at B4 is a lot more dangerous though, a Capricious Reaper at a higher level of power. He's level 15, has the Demon Wall summon to protect his Bravery from damage, and has a massive boost to his attack and defense while he's at full HP. Focus on landing an HP attack quickly to drop him of his buffs, then fight him, you should know how to dodge the Reaper's attacks by now. Use a Straight Chain at D4 with an EX Charge to give yourself an advantage when fighting the Reaper. Vaan's EX Mode effect is quite good, he gets variably special effects to each attack as described on his page, Spear for example spins twice, and of his three base Bravery attacks so far that's the best benefit. He can also put away his weapon with R and Square, letting him prepare for a Switch attack without having to use another attack. The KP Chance this time isn't just to win, but to win in 90 seconds, so retry if you run out of time. Focus on HP attacks as Demon Wall will halt your attempts to Break him and wastes your time if you keep trying such.

Chain all five enemies for 25 KP. Inspect the red orb to get our first summon, Ifrit. Equip him in the summons menu. Ifrit will automatically trigger when you Break an opponent and gives you a 50% boost to Bravery. However, he only has three charges, after which he has to recharge by sitting out for three fights. Open the chest for a powerful new weapon, the Slasher. Equip it to Vaan now, it has the highest power boost of any weapon thus far and gives a slight boost to the Wall Rush HP damage he inflicts. The shop also has an assortment of new inventory for us, the Wakizashi is an upgraded Kunai, giving a higher defense benefit. You can also grab an Iron Shield for higher defense but no attack boost, or the Power Armlet for a slight HP boost, take what you like. The helmets are similar, the Plumed Hat has a higher BRV benefit, Iron Helm boosts defense and Headband boosts HP. Armor is more clear, the Chainmail is where it's at.

While we're here, let's talk about accessories and Battlegen, since you're probably curious about all those greyed-out accessories you can't buy. A lot of shop items cost more than gil, you also need to trade other equipment pieces or trade accessories for them, and Battlegen trade accessories are won from enemies, specific enemies or arenas rewarding different items. If you have three Flash's Desire you can buy another Power Ring, but you can only use one at a time. The superior Hyper Ring needs three Blackcrystal Slivers and three Stalwart's Hopes, and we get neither yet. The Guard Ring needs three Dragoon's Desires, we could get it but we don't need it really. Everything else we can't get yet because we don't meet enemies that leave the Battlegen items we need for trade, so I'll mention what's new as we progress from now on.

At the Chaos Piece, Vaan sees Kefka mocking Terra, sporting her new alt outfit for this game which rather gratuitously gives an unobstructed view of the bottom part of her leotard, front and back. There's your fanservice, Terra fans! Kefka tortures her into submission when she refuses to fight, and Vaan intervenes to stop him. My money is on Kefka, but since we're fighting on Terra's behalf I guess I might as well root for Vaan.

You know how several of these attacks work already. The two Firagas are new, one of them throws out a single fireball that weaves through the air, stunning you and doing nasty damage when it hits. The other one is telegraphed by Kefka making some hand motions in the air, then he shoots out three fireballs that zigzag towards you, easy to anticipate and block. His HP attack Trine has him tap the air to create a spark of light, then three triangles of energy surround you and quickly converge, watch for the flash of light on Kefka's hand to anticipate and dodge them. This stage also has a new feature, Banish Traps. The pits in many stages will suck Bravery from a character if they fall into one, or are knocked in, and teleports them back to solid ground with a 20% Bravery loss. If you're having trouble wearing Kefka down, use your normal Spear attack (not Switch) to knock him down into the traps and let them help you out.

If you've got a full or nearly full EX Gauge, the battle is a snap, bombard Kefka with Crossbow from range and use your double Switch Spear when close. With Ifrit on your side, Breaking Kefka will put you at 1300 Bravery or so, a Wall Rush later he'll be weak enough to kill with your EX Burst. His Firagas and Scatter-Spray Blizzaga are tricky if you're a new player, but if you played the first game you know how to handle Kefka well enough and shouldn't have any trouble. You may be caught off-guard a few times, but that's what Kefka does, in the end you'll prevail. In the aftermath Vaan realizes he's no match for the Mad God and runs off with Terra, leaving Kefka to once again demonstrate his comedy prowess to us. Oh, and enjoy some creepy stalker subtext between him and Terra while you're at it.

Character Derailment FTL

So as you'll soon realize, Vaan is just the viewpoint character for a chapter that focuses on Terra changing sides and escaping Kefka, much like his original game. We head down into the Elven Snowfields now, my favorite part of the D012 world map. The path out of the mountains and onto the field is linear, grab two orbs of the dead as you head to the blue gateway. Before you head in get the chest on the island for a Rosetta Stone, you know what to do. I recommend adding in the Red Drop or Blue Drop. The Near Opponent booster you'll have for completing Chapter 1 is tempting, but it's only a 1.2 booster, which raises our 2.2 boost from our other Boosters to a 2.7 boost. Do the math, that's a 50% boost to an accessory that raises damage by 3%...or you can just use the Red Drop which has a full 3% boost. Now enter the blue gateway.

Terra will be our assist for the rest of the storyline. When you're on the ground she'll use Graviga, charging up for a couple seconds to slam an orb of darkness on the opponent's head, it does a lot of damage but is hard to hit with. The easiest way if you're determined is to block an attack, call her out, then attack the opponent with Spear and Wall Rush them as she charges. Her aerial Assist is Blizzara, which is easier to use, Wall Rush an opponent then call out Terra as you drop to the ground to use Torrent or dash closer to use Inferno. If you lack the CP to use Torrent, de-equip one of Vaan's ground attacks (I recommend Spear) to make room.

The easiest way to chain enemies here is a Jump Chain at B3. If you lack one there's a moogle right outside that will sell one for 6 KP. But we'll win 16 KP for chaining all four enemies so why not. I had 55 KP before buying the Jump Chain, so we'll come back to the Moogle after the gateway is done because there's stuff to buy. The KP Chance is once again to win within 90 seconds. The enemies are two Idle Sky Pirates, a Fleeting Flash and a Phantasmal Harlequin. I know, I hate being so brief about this but D012 reuses a lot of enemy equipment and move set-ups over the course of a story, so if you've seen one Idle Sky Pirate odds are you've seen all of them for the next chapter or two. These ones are a few levels higher but are just as incompetent as their predecessors, and now you've got a bigger accessory pool, Ifrit, and Terra. Just watch out for the Flash's Watera, it'll deflect your Crossbow shots but otherwise can be blocked or avoided.

As a note, it was at this point Vaan mastered several support skills. Depending on how your AP Chances have gone you may have mastered them before or will soon. This should free up enough CP to use all of his Bravery attacks and Torrent without having to give up other skills. And I also had enough left over to give him Speed Boost, yay! Vaan is at Level 10 now on my game so the Level 1-9 Booster is now useless, I replaced it with a Blue Drop for a minor Bravery boost. The chests have an Iifa Leaf and a Delicious Fish Scale. The Leaf gives us the Second Chance ability - when you're hit with an HP attack that would kill you, you live with 1 HP left over. We'll save it for later because there is a powerful enemy in a future storyline we could use it for. The scale unlocks two new Shop items, the Linen Cuirass and the Capella. The Cuirass can be passed up, you could get it but it needs a lot of gil to trade. The Capella we want, it's one of the best weapons we'll be able to access for the time being, but we need an Altair to trade for it along with almost 15,000 gil, so we'll have to buy it later.

Back outside we can fight a Phantasmal Harlequin to get access to a chest with a second Pink Tail. The fight got me to Level 11 and Vaan learned the ground variant of Katana, it's a charge attack past the opponent to chop then into the air and begin a chase. It's pretty good with damage to rival Spear and generates a lot of EX Force, and its switch attack is two chops. I would say get rid of Greatsword for it if you want it, so you've got melee, mid-range and long-range all covered. Now, I've got 65 KP so there's some stuff we can buy at the moogle. Pass up the equipment, the 200 ones are where it's at. The boosters are of questionable usage since they rely on the opponent's HP. For summons, Mandragora is limited in usefulness and Tonberry can be passed up. Cactuar is pricey but worth it, buy it. When called it instantly does 1000 Bravery damage to the opponent. However it only has two charges. Call it whenever you like by pressing R and Circle in battle. Set your reserves now since you have two summons, Ifrit first than Cactuar second. Now whenever a summon is depleted, the next highest-numbered summon is automatically equipped in its place. Get the last two orbs on the world map and head to the gateway for the end of the story.

As we enter we see the worst cutscene of the game, where in a moment of weakness, Terra asks Vaan to kill her, and he has to talk her down, assuring her the side she's on doesn't matter, he saved her because it was right. Now it's time for the gateway. The opposition consists of a Phantasmal Girl, Phantasmal Harlequin, two Idle Sky Pirates, and the Strange Battle Piece, the Fleeting Flash. This time around the Flash has Iron Giant, which will cleave your Bravery in half every time she lands an HP attack. Not a huge problem though since she has no Bravery attacks to take advantage of your weakness. Enemies are Level 15, a bit tougher than before, but not a huge problem. A Cross Chain at C4 will chain all five of them for 25 KP. The two chests here hold a Soul of Destruction and our third Pink Tail.

Now we see another scene, the mind control Kefka had Terra on is broken entirely, as you can tell from her eyes now. Vaan tells Terra to find somewhere to hide until Chaos is gone, then he'll take her to see Cosmos and find out how to go home. Terra finally introduces herself to him as he departs, and we unlock another Report to be covered on that page.

Head to the main menu, check your Mognet and buy some calendar upgrades from the PP Catalog, then prepare for Chapter 3.

Southern Areas

Part 22 kiddies, time to dive into a living nightmare!

Beyond Discord

Before launching Scenario 000, let's cover a few things. First, the world is entirely wide-open, you can go anywhere you want in the southern regions and can go right for the first objective. This is a bad idea though, since the enemies in the objective gateway are quite high level, 90 in fact, and you're likely no higher than 50 after the main storyline, if even that. So I'm going to go around the world visiting other gateways and powering up the party before we get to the main mission.

000 lets you use a party of any five people you like, I will be using Zidane, Terra, Cloud, Lightning and Prishe. So, let's do it!

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Prishe

Prishe is here, where's the grub? I've never played FF11, but as I understand it in her original game she's an immortal due to a crystal embedded in her chest that marks her as the mortal vessel for the day the twilight god Promathia will descend to the world, but the god was vanquished and Prishe made mortal. Naturally none of this comes up in D012. Prishe is a foul-mouthed, rowdy tomboy who loves a good brawl, and as seen in report flashbacks was one of the earlier fighters in the war with Shantotto. I quite like Prishe, she's a spunky lass and rather funny to listen to, especially once she hits Level 100 and you can unlock her hidden voice clips to randomly appear in battle. I won't spoil the fun, kiddies, level her up and find them for yourselves.

Prishe has a unique fighting style. She has eight Bravery combo attacks aside from the obligatory ranged ones. Each Bravery attack though can set one of the eight, including itself, as a follow-up attack, and much like an HP link, or more specifically Onion Knight's Bravery links, Prishe can then use two attacks in a row to pile up more damage. Combining certain skills like this together can create a Skillchain for even more damage. Prishe's attacks are Combo, a three-hit combo attack, Dragon Kick, a leg sweep that can be charged to crush guards and fire a projectile into the air, Howling Fist, a wind-up punch that crushes guard when charged, Should Tackle, a leaping shoulder charge, Spinning Attack, an aerial uppercut, Backhand Blow, a drop-down punch attack, Raging Fists, a five-hit combo, and One-inch Punch, a wind-elemental punch that crushes guards when charged. Phew!

Prishe's Skillchains do pretty massive damage, and she has quite a bit of tactical diversity with them too. As she levels up and learns them I'll be using the set-up I preferred in another file on this game - Combo-Combo, Howling Fist-Shoulder Tackle, Dragon Kick-Backhand Blow, One-Inch Punch-Howling Fist, Backhand Blow-One-Inch Punch, and Spinning Attack-Spinning Attack. You can use whichever set-ups you like. In her EX Mode, Prishe gets a movement speed boost and performs her second attacks twice in a row to do even more damage. Her EX Burst is pretty simple, a button command appears and then you quickly press them in sequence clockwise starting at that button.

I know I'm going in-depth on Prishe's Bravery game, and it's because that's her real strength - her HP attacks suck, oh how they suck! They aren't bad compared to each other, Auroral Uppercut is well, an uppercut, Banishga sends out a shockwave around her, Asuran Fists is a rapidfire punch barrage that reaches forward and into the air, and Nullifying Dropkick is a long-range homing dropkick. The problem with them is they all have massive start-up, making them ridiculously easy to avoid since the opponents in 000 are competent now, and forcing Prishe to rely on blocking and punishing, Assists, or Chases to deal HP damage.

However, in spite of her flaws Prishe is still one of my favorite D012 characters, her awesome Bravery game makes her a blast to play as, and I like using my Assists anyway so relying on them isn't too big a concern for me.

Also, screw honor now - go to the PP Catalog and buy out all the Exp calendar stuff! Suit up the party, if there are any new characters you're bringing along, put them in the back and let them get Exp without fighting. And now we're off!

Cornelia

As we begin, a voice calls out to us and asks us to come rescue it near Melmond, in the Chasm in the Rotting Land. We wake up on the beach near Cornelia, and a moogle is surprised to see us! Buy some Pebbles from it. The accessory line-up is up to you, I recommend a Hyper Ring with a Muscle Belt or Earring and whatever boosters you like, the Sunrise is not needed. Also as a note, at any time if you save and quit the storyline, when you re-launch it you can change your party freely.

Buy whatever limited items the moogle has for sale and go north and left, Final Fantasy fans will recognize this place as the ruins of the Chaos Shrine. The wiki has maps of this scenario so I'll provide them for you. Take the chest, chat with the moogle if you like, then return to Cornelia and enter the Gateway to Departure.

As we enter, spirits of fallen warriors call to us and offer their assistance, but warn they're not here to help you in your quest, they'll stay within the gateways and will attack if you approach them. Be warned - in all gateways, talking to your Assist will trigger a fight with their character, and they will always be level 100 with full equipment and strong accessories. Furthermore, flee or victory, they will vanish and you'll have to complete the gateway without an Assist. It's best to just avoid them unless they deliberately block your path, and I'll mention such circumstances.

Gulg Volcano Ring

This region also covers the Crescent Lake area down to that island where the teleport stone was in the 013 story. There's only one gateway in this region, but lots of treasures and our first map enemies, as you can see from the map. Follow the path to the waterfront, the gateway to the left is sealed so smash the rock and grind over. The chest has a Diamond, which naturally trades for Diamond equipment, but it's Level 60 gear and a few levels out of our range now. Go along the path to a split, you can go up into the volcano, or to a gateway.

Up in the volcano is a chest with a KP Bonus, and our first map enemy, a Delusory Warlock Level 88, ouch. He has Demon Wall, a Delusory Knight Assist, Level 60 equipment with Level 100 Armor, and a Hyper Ring with 9 Near Loss bostters. He is easily the most challenging opponent in the game so far, have fun. Remember and feel free to use the entire party to fight him, fortunately when fighting on the overworld, party members are automatically healed after a fight, so it's five-on-one with no penalties.

Now just head into the gateway you passed, Encounters and Treason.

Multichain at D4 to get all enemies. The enemies here and in the next area are about 10 levels above previous enemies, fortunately you're more than 10 levels stronger by now. The bonus line is 60 and the KP Chance is a 30 second Break. The blue emblem you're on refills your HP before each battle so feel free to get beaten up a bit.

First is an Level 63 Ephemeral Vision. Then comes the first Party piece, Level 52 Transient Witch with Deathgaze, a Level 52 Capricious Reaper with an unknown summon, and a Level 52 Ephemeral Phantom with Chocobo. Yes, even now gateways reuse enemy set-ups - not that they aren't working, right?

The two chests each randomly have a Tome of the Farplane and a Tome of Lies. They give access to the Dismay Shock and Battle Hammer, basic accessories that sap a bit of the opponent's EX or Assist Gauge when you deal HP damage. They allow access to more accessories, like Aggressor and Avenger, which increase the damage done during EX Mode or EX Revenge but with a penalty, the largely useless Great Gospel to boost Regen rate and Bravery recovery rate, or the awesome Beckoning Cat, which doubles the AP won in battle, the decent Sturm und Drang and Soul of Thamasa, which boost physical or magic damage with benefits to wall rush and chase damage, and finally the pathetic Battle Ring, which gives you a 5% chance to not lose the charge when you call an Assist.

On the other side, the moogle sells trade accessories, specific ones. There's Pink Tails, the Gear Collection, needed for the Machine equipment which has high stats but needs an ability to be used, and Scarletite, which as I said is needed for exclusive weapons. Nothing we need yet, but if you're short on Pink Tails you'll want to come back here later. The Ribbon is level 60 armor and well out of our range, but it gives a -20% chance for breakable accessories to break, letting us use the Exp-boosting Chocobo accessories with only a 10% break rate, and of course at level 100 we can trade it for the Super Ribbon with a -30% chance. But that's not for yet some time, just remember this place for future reference. Grab a Multichain and KP Gambler for the road. Follow the path into the mountains, there's a moogle to chat with and a Crystal in a chest, but it's otherwise a dead end.

Return to the volcano and descend into the Elvan Snowfields. Watch out on the journey, the Warlock may have respawned. Fun discovery too - if you get a sneak attack on them, the 0 initial Bravery carries between battles! Send out your first party member, easy Break and HP damage, then fight as normal and if you lose and go to the next person, they still have 0 Bravery and are easy pickings for the next person in line. Times fight party members and yeah, slightly unfair.

This is the best place in the game at the moment to grind for KP. Multichain at A3 with a KP Gambler to chain all three enemies and get 243 KP. Add in a KP Bonus or two to spice it up even more. The bonus line is 60, and the KP Chance is a 30 second Break.

First up are a Level 60 Imaginary Brawler and Level 60 Transient Gunner. Then comes a Level 51 Phantasmal Harlequin with Ultros and Level 51 Imaginary Champion with auto Bahamut. Then comes a Level 50 Imitation Despot with an unknown summon, probably Brynhildr since the last Despot had that, and Level 50 Counterfeit Wraith with Hecatoncheir. The last party is a Level 60 Ephemeral Summoner, Level 61 False Hero, and Level 61 Imitation Liegeman.

The two chests have a random Tome (I think) and a Pink Tail. The Tome I got was a Tome of Lies. This gave me to the two I needed for a Beckoning Cat, but I was also short a Lucky Charm. Getting more requires Wanderer's Desire and Despot's Desire, which we have plenty of, and Ghost, Battlegen'd from the Phantom Train. Go Battlegen some now, I did. The Lucky Charm and the Rabbit's Foot it trades for trade for Hero's Essense and Hero's Spirit, which give us more CP so we can equip more abilities at a time and master them faster.

Also note at this point I gave leadership to Prishe to level her up faster, she was level 33 by this point so she could take the enemies on with a bit of help from the right summons like Bahamut, Kraken, Behemoth, etc. I bought her an Iron Fan for a weapon which begins the fight with a 20% Assist charge, helpful since as I said above she needs Assists to land her slow HP attacks.

Beyond the gateway is a moogle, this is the second awesome thing, that gateway not only has 9 enemies to chain, but it's about two feet from a moogle who sells Multichains and KP Gamblers, so KP grinding is quick and easy! Sure you get a penalty if you're over level 60, but if you do the math, you're getting 240 KP a run, so even at level 100 you're just losing 40 KP, but the level advantage makes the fights go much quicker, so it evens out. The moogle sells the Warlord and Heike equipment, the Warlord stuff is level 30 armor with a Combiation, but unlike the Mythril and Gold stuff you need all four pieces to activate it, lamely the effect is just faster Bravery recovery. The Heike equipment is level 60 and gives you a full EX Gauge at the start of each fight, but in return your EX Mode only lasts half as long. Quite useful I imagine for someone like Cloud or Terra, with EX Mode damage buffs so awesome they don't need a lot of time in EX Mode to batter the opponent and end the fight.

Near the moogle is a Level 88 Imaginary Champion to play with, he has the normal Bahamut. Beyond it is a chest with a KP Bonus. I bolded that because if you don't need KP Bonuses, or have no room for them in the skill list, leave the chests with them alone. The only way to get more aside from the chests is the Cornelia moogle, which only sells one a day on a specific two days or so, so leave the chests be and come back for them later when you need them, just scroll up and down the page looking for the bolded KP Bonus word to find them when you need them. Further down the main path is a Diamond, and further down is the Gateway of Melting Sorrows.

Melmond Fens

There's actually very little to do here, no gateways to unlock as you can see save for two, and they're special ones. Go out and to the right to find a Level 89 Transient Witch with Kraken to fight, and she's got an Iifa Dew, yay. The island has a Diamond on it. On the other path you can see two distant gateways over the see, the moogle says that one of them is the Chasm in the Rotting Land, where the mured moogle is sealed, but the only way there is by teleport stone. Head further into the region and grind over to an island with a Crystal.

After heading through an empty gateway in the mountains, turn left, it will begin to rain here. Soon you're come to a teleport stone in a ruined town, smash the rock and grind over to a chest with a Diamond. Cross the swamplands and you'll find another Transient Witch to make friends with. On the beach behind it is a KP Bonus. Return to the previously mentioned gateway and go right this time. Go to the right this time and smash the rock, the island has yet another Crystal. The gateway here leads back to the Cornelia region.

Well, we've explore the entire world now. Go visit the various moogles and buy whatever needed, grab some of the listed KP Bonuses and grind for KP as I told you if you're poor. We can head to the mured moogle's resting place, but first, there's two optional gateways to explore. Grab a couple Multichains any maybe a KP Gambler, then head to a teleport stone.

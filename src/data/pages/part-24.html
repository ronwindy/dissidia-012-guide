Behold Feral Chaos, a grotesquely deformed form of Chaos and a preview of the game's true final boss.

His Bravery attacks are Snarl, a quick two-hit slash on the ground, Spew, where he jumps up and slams the ground to create plumes of fire beneath you, Brute Force, a diving claw combo in the air, and Raid, where he descends rapidly to unleash a shockwave when he stops. All of Feral Chaos's Bravery attacks wall rush or chase, depending on if you hit the wall too soon or not. Snarl and Spew can be blocked, but Brute Force and Raid cannot. Brute Force is his most used attack, it crushes your guards, has a good range for it, and does a lot of damage.

His HP attacks are Deus Iratus, where on the ground he blocks attacks and creates a ring of fire around him, Ventus Ire, where he angles up or down and then flaps his wings towards you to send out a gust of air, and Lux Magnus, where he shoots a beam of fire straight down in the air and can move to track you down. Deus Iratus can be avoided by simply not attacking him on the ground, while Lux Magnus, just keep dodging away. Ventus Ire is a bit trickier, dodge away but if you don't do it fast enough he'll hit you, since Feral Chaos likes using Ventus Ire after wall rushing you. Feral Chaos's summon is Shinryu Verus, which copies the effects of the four fiend summons - Bravery freeze on you, Bravery swap, sap Bravery to 0, or triple his Bravery. And no that is not a typo, he has almost 60,000 HP.

The true difficulty of this fight is Feral Chaos's accessories. He has Door to Despair, which gives him a 5% damage and defense boost, and Tyranny of Darkness, giving him 5% EX and Assist depletion. And then he has Waking Darkness, which after 60 seconds activates for a 90x multiplier. Suddenly Feral Chaos's attacks are obscenely strong, he takes probably no damage from your attacks, and every HP attack depletes your Assist and EX Gauges to 0. Thus you effectively only have 60 seconds to fight, after that he will easily Break you and get 9999 Bravery with one Brute Force, and the next HP attack will kill you.

Now, when it comes to rating Feral Chaos's difficulty, I must admit I personally have trouble with this. Anyone who follows me on YouTube knows I (at least I like to think so) have made a reputation killing him over and over and over. So I'm familiar with how his AI works and how to avoid and counter his attacks. This Feral Chaos doesn't fight quite the same as the real thing I'm used to, but the lessons stick. All you really need to do is get used to dodging his attacks. Brute Force is actually pretty easy to dodge once you learn how to see it ocming, and Deus Iratus and Ventus Ire, just keep your distance, which is why I brought Terra. Normally his defenses are too high to damage, but at 9999 Bravery the critical hit multiplier does 8x normal damage, so your critical hits will still hurt a bit. Otherwise when that booster goes off, just rely on HP attacks and only do Bravery attack to charge up your Assist. This is the one time I would consider using Resin-type items, but I didn't. And Terra's Kraken summon swapped my 0 Bravery (got an HP hit just before the timer) with his 9999, awesome.

You have the entire party to share the burden, the only factor is if you think the current member did enough HP damage, because the remaining people have to share the rest. Also just keep in mind boosters don't work in EX Bursts, so feel free to go for the EX Burst finish as usual. Otherwise, just good luck, learn to predict his Brute Force and HP attacks, dodge them and counter, and you'll be just fine.

The former two, one greatly reduces the rate at which your Assist charges but lets you always absorb EX Force, even while attacking or being attacked, and the other freezes the Assist gauge, preventing you from using an Assist at all, but gives a nice buff to the absorption range of EX Force and how much you get from Force and Cores. The latter two, once reduces the amount of EX Force you get but gives you an initial Assist charge and lets your gauge stay charged longer without attacking, the other freezes the EX Gauge but charges your Assist when you land HP damage. Together As One is one of the best IMO, as its negative effects can be dealt with, and its benefits are nice, and when combined with a Badge of Trust and First to Victory, you begin every fight with a full Assist gauge.

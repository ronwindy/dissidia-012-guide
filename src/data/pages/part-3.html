Chapter 3

The Greatest Attack Call Ever

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Laguna

  • Hardest of the new six. His Bravery attacks hurt but are easy to block, so trap the opponent between two attacks.
  • HP attacks are slow. Combo them: Sticky Bomb then Satellite Beam as it explodes, or Homing Bazooka then Ragnarok Blade.
  • Starts with Machine Gun (high damage) and Missile Barrage (tracking missiles).
  • Your Assist covers his slow HP attacks.

Equip a Power Ring with a 1.5 booster and a Red Drop. No weapon needed, you get one free in the first gateway.

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Laguna

Ah, Laguna. I wasn't too big on him in FF8, he was a goof and a half-insane loon, like most of the cast of that game come to think of it. Dissidia however has made him one hell of a badass, giving him an unrivaled arsenal of weaponry and tweaking his quirks to be genuinely funny. Laguna is a well-meaning do-gooder who just has a habit of misusing words and getting confused when it comes to directions. Also give a big round of applause for his VA, A.V. Kennedy, who does an awesome job, especially since it's his first time with SE voicing a character who has never been voiced by anyone before. He turns in what I consider one of the more memorable and character-fitting performances alongside Kefka, Kain and The Emperor.

Laguna is far and away the hardest of the new six to use, relying on tactical fighting and pressuring the opponent to catch them when they slip or or trap them between two attacks to inflict pain. His Bravery attacks hurt a lot but are easy to block, but that's where the trickery on your part comes in. Laguna's HP attacks are slow to use but combo well with his Bravery attacks. For example, throw a Sticky Bomb at the opponent, then call down a Satellite Beam as it explodes so they can't dodge, or fire out a Homing Bazooka missile and smack the opponent in the face with Ragnarok Blade as they try to dodge the missile. Or, flip it around. You get the point. This makes Laguna very hard to use and he'll require practice, fortunately you have an Assist the entire storyline as a crutch if his slow HP attacks are giving you trouble.

He starts off with some of his key attacks, Machine Gun and Missile Barrage. Machine Gun is just a solid attack option that does a lot of damage, and Missile Barrage fires out six missiles that turn and weave to track enemies, and should be used to combo with Satellite Beam or Ragnarok Blade as mentioned above. He'll quickly learn the Ricochet Shot attack, letting him shoot a bullet off of obstacles to bounce the shrapnel into an enemy, and he picks up Ragnarok Blaster as an HP attack, blasting the enemy on the ground at close-range. Once you master him Laguna is quite effective and hurts a lot, but it will require effort. And of course, his EX Burst makes it worthwhile too.

As you suit Laguna up before beginning the story, I did some quick math and found out the Power Ring with a 1.5 booster and a Red Drop is stronger than the Ring with two boosters, so yeah, let's go with that build from now on. He won't have a weapon unless you wanna buy him one, but don't, he'll be tough enough to handle the enemies we meet as we begin and before we exit the starting gateway we'll find a weapon for him for free. And alas, while the shop may have new wares, none of them are the Altair. The Capella remains out of our reach for a while longer...

We'll quickly see that as goofy as he acts, Laguna knows a strategy when it hits him in the face. The first chest gives us a Cross Chain, and Laguna lets us know precisely where to use it - C4, of course, chains all five enemies for a cool 25 KP. The enemies of this stage are NEW! Yes, gone are the Idle Sky Pirates and Delusory Dragoons, my comrades! We instead have two Fleeting Flashs, a Counterfeit Wraith, a Transient Gunner, and an Ephemeral Vision.

The Wraith has an Anti-Air Particle Beam attack that fires four lasers high in the air in a square formation around her, and she moves towards you on start-up, it has weak horizontal range for a back-dodge will be enough to avoid it. She also has two Tentacle Moves with varying effects, the ground one has her use a spin attack you can't block, and at full charge fires a large energy orb you can reflect to damage her. The aerial tentacle blow fires several energy orbs at us that bounce along the ground and are easily avoided by being airborne. The Gunner has the same moves you have and will be fought last so you'll know how to handle it by that point. The Vision has Hop Step on the ground, a quick and hard-to-anticipate move, fortunately it has low range, and in the air uses Dart & Weave, where he dodges our attack and jumps up to come down spinning. Fortunately if he uses it when we aren't attacking it's ridiculously easy to block and punish. The HP attack is Energy Rain, when you see the notice appear run away because he'll bounce off you, even stopping attacks, and bombards you with magic blasts.

Being that the enemies are dumb, don't worry about comboing Laguna's moves too much, just Machine Gun or Shotgun them and then slam them with a Ragnarok Blade or Satellite Beam. Open the chest when you're done mopping up the opposition to find a Tanegashima, a gun-type weapon that of course Laguna can use, so let him. It also unlocks the Valiant in the shop, not as badass as the Capella but still nice, but we lack a Fish Scale and won't have one for a while so too bad. That's the main problem with D012's shop, by the time you can access trade accessories consistently the equipment you need them to trade for are underpowered compared to the point in the story you'll be at, but you have to take them anyway to unlock higher-level equipment, which you'll also lack the trade accessories to get, and so on until you get to the top Level 100 equipment. Yup, you'll find that a good half or so of the cost of most equipment pieces you'll be buying is actually spent paying for lower-level equipment pieces you need so you can trade up to what you actually wanted in the first place - like how we want an Altair right now just so we can get the Capella. Squiggly-mouth emoticon, :/.

Exploration

Laguna is one the world map now, the music is a remix of Eyes on Me, insufferable in FF8 but here as a world map theme with no lyrics, actually quite nice. Immediately turn around to explore the region behind us, there's a blue gateway between a ring of grass, and a chest by the closed gateway near it with a KP Gambler. Save this item, it is very useful later and will make big bucks for us. The Phantasmal Harlequin outside the gateway has no new tricks to watch for. To the left of the gateway is a stone that produces a grind bar leading to an island with our fourth Pink Tail. Head for the gateway now.

Squall is here and he'll be our Assist. He is an excellent combo to call after landing a Satellite Beam or when the opponent is tumbling down from Machine Gun, he'll use Solid Barrel which hurts a lot - for direct comparison, a full-blast from Machine Gun has 32 power, while Solid Barrel has 55. And for more fun it begins a Chase teleport so we can follow up with a Shotgun blast or Ragnarok Blade. His aerial Assist...well Squall used so much awesome for his ground version he has little left for the aerial one, he uses Heel Crush, which is slow to execute and does about a third the damage Solid Barrel will do. So yeah, stick to the ground Assist, doesn't much matter through since he's only in one gateway.

Enemies are another Gunner and Vision with a Fleeting Flash and a Delusory Dragoon, no new tricks here except for the Dragoon, being a Strange Battle Piece and all. He begins the fight with a big Bravery boost and uses Bahamut to get more, then spams Jump the whole fight. Jump ironically leaves him wide open for Squall's assist, just be sure to call Squall after the landing so you don't get Assist Locked - that is, if you're unsure, when your Assist gets hit or blocked, and your gauge is locked and can't be used or charged for a period of time. The treasures here are a Fish Scale and a Soul of Valor, hey, I spoke too soon! Go pick up a new Valiant, if you're lacking in gil for it sell off those "Soul" accessories, or some of the Chocobo stuff your play plan might have given you. If you really need a lot, go ahead and sell the Pink Tails.

Outside again, go back to where we began and follow the path onward to find Vaan, who will be our Assist for the rest of the storyline. On the island beyond the breakable stone is an Assist Charge, it'll fill our Assist Gauge in a chain. There's a Capricious Reaper around here, as you may have noticed earlier Laguna's Machine Gun is a great attack to use to get the drop on enemies in the field, long range and duration for the win. Continue on to a mountain area with a Moogle, if you don't have one buy a Cross Chain, and a few Rosetta Stones if you like, there's another one in the chest nearby. For fun here, corner Vaan in a mountain and bombard him with Machine Gun. So much fun, I wish there was a gif of this. Head into the gateway now.

The KP Chance is to win in 90 seconds, we'll be using the Assist Charge for massive advantage so it'll be simple. We'll also be winning a huge cache of KP. That KP Gambler you got triples the KP you win in a chain, but only if you win all battles in the chain. Use the Gambler and the Assist Charge, then use a Cross Chain at D2 to chain all enemies, getting a whopping 108 KP when the dust settles. The enemies are two Fleeting Flashes, two Transient Gunners, a Counterfeit Wraith, and a Delusory Dragoon as the Strange Battle Piece. The Dragoon still spams Jump but this time has Mandragora as a summon, restoring it's Bravery to its default value almost instantly whenever it falls below that value, including after landing an HP attack. The Wraith has an annoying HP attack called Fusillade Particle Beam, which fires several energy orbs into the air that turn and converge on you. When she uses it use Quickmove to dash away, it's hard to dodge at close range.

With Laguna now having a ton of KP, open the chest for a Power Armlet. A new sight is the Potion, which restores all our HP and fills the EX Gauge...kinda useless now that all the enemies are dead but a nice thought. And now outside again, go forward to find a Counterfeit Wraith, deal with it and take the EX Charge behind it. Go to the left to a marsh area, there's a Soul of Unrivaled Valor in the chest, and a False Stalwart to fight. We're in a new arena for this fight, break the Esper capsules and hope you Battlegen a Magicite Shard. The Stalwart can be tough if you're unfamiliar with them from the first game, it has Chain Cast for a long-range aerial attack, Round Edge for a spinning ground attack, and its HP Attack Cyclone will deflect all of Laguna's current Bravery attacks and stays out for several seconds, use Ragnarok Blade or Buster to disperse the winds.

Now, that Magicite Shard Battlegen is needed for the Sunrise accessory, which gives a 50% boost to Battlegen rate, helpful since we need them to trade for so much. Kefka's Tower will be encountered more in the next gateway, aim for two more Shards. All we need from there is three each Dragoon's Desire and Harlequin's Desire, and we've fought a lot of those two enemies so far so you may have them, if so get the Sunrise and equip it over your Booster. If you lack them, and I did sadly, continue on for now. Beyond the Stalwart's position is yet another Pink Tail. Back on the main path there's a BRV Zero on an island over a grind bar from the stone. Now head into the blue gateway.

Use a Cross Chain at C3 to get all the enemies, good time for that EX Charge. Let's chat about Laguna's EX Mode while I bring it up. His special bonus is to instantly cancel out the lag from a Bravery attack by using another one, letting him launch even more firepower than ever. His EX Burst is actually pretty challenging to get right, you have to hold R and move the cursor to center the opponent between a crosshair as they move around, and if you land enough hits to fill up the power bar Laguna summons his hyperspace arsenal together to charge power and fire a giant laser cannon...which he rides. Badass? Very. And it comes complete with the best attack call of any video game ever. I'll let you hear for yourself so as to not ruin the fun.

The enemy line-up is two each Sky Pirates, Wraiths and Gunners. This time around the Wraith is the Strange Battle Piece and is packing Mandragora. Her HP attack is pretty nasty, 0-Form Particle Beam, she charges for a second and then fires a big laser beam a short distance, turning to track you if you dodge. It's very difficult to avoid at close-range, when she begins to charge it up run away and then dodge backwards when it fires. You can slam her with an HP attack as she recovers, so it's not hard to win. Otherwise, nothing new here to watch for. The Cross Chain at C3 will miss the Sky Pirate at E4, you'll have to fight him after. As I fought Laguna got to Level 15 and learned Pummel and Sticky Bomb. Pummel is an effective two-hit keep-away attack and is handy in his aerial arsenal when he learns the midair version, but for now his current line-up of ground attacks is fine. Sticky Bomb throws an explosive that sticks to the opponent and explodes several seconds later, great for comboing with other attacks but it's easy to block so it's hard to use, but you might as well if you've got the CP.

The new Summonstone is Carbuncle, it summons when our Bravery hits 0 - probably after landing an HP hit - and mirrors the opponent's Bravery to 0. For now it's useless, oh it'll be effective down the road, but at this point by the time you're going for an HP hit, it's probably after the opponent is already Broken so Carbuncle will do nothing, and we also have Cactuar which is just as effective for sapping the opponent's power, and can be called whenever we like. When you approach the path to the end piece, Vaan vanishes, so we'll be fighting the boss alone. Laguna finds himself alone in the World of Darkness, when suddenly, something stripperific this way comes.

The Cloud of Darkness has all four tentacle attacks, whose effects including to recap - ground variants which create dark electricity around you, send a large orb of darkness forward, spin attack, or just lash out to strike you, and aerial variants which fire two dark energy balls, fire a swarm of smaller ones, whirl to attack in a sphere, or lash out. She has two new HP attacks. Wide-Angle Particle Beam has a second of charge before a wave of dark energy appears with a wide horizontal range and sweeps forward quickly, not too hard to dodge if you see it coming. Aura Ball creates several large spheres of darkness one by one that spread out and move towards you, then explode when she wills it. Overall a pretty versatile pool of HP attacks to trip you up with, and her tentacles are hard to predict too.

What breaks this fight through and stops a potential 2 or even 3 on the scale is one problem for her - Ifrit. I Broke her with a single Ricochet Shot, and Ifrit then boosted me to 1600 Bravery, Wall Rush her and a good 2000 HP of hers vanishes in the first 15 seconds of the fight. After that mopping her up was pretty simple, and while she can be a difficult fight if you're a beginner, the ability to take off 2000 HP so easily will swing the fight your way regardless of skill level, if you've made it this far there's no reason you can't handle her.

In the aftermath of the scene, Laguna suddenly notices the Cloud of Darkness is hot, and suddenly gets a leg cramp. The Cloud of Darkness, understandably dumbfounded by this, has a chat with him when he mentions he's fighting not for Cosmos but just to go home. She tells him where the Manikins came from and how to stop them from increasing their numbers, by closing the portal they're using to emerge from the Rift. And why tell him this? Because she wants to see how he handles it, it would be a suicide mission to seek out the portal, so what will Laguna do? She overestimated his dumb heroicness. Laguna heads off and that ends the chapter. Unlike the last two, no Reports this time.

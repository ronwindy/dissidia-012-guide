Chapter 5

Chapter 5, the plot thickens!

The Fabled Gun Appears

We're back with Lightning now, on her way to Sanctuary. As we suit up, you'll learn there's new equipment in the shop, better stuff in general, including an Altair! We can finally get the Capella we've been dreaming of! Well I have very nerdy dreams, excuse me. There's just for one problem, the Altair is 3800 gil and the Capella is another another 15000 on top of that. Well, fortunately we have something expensive to sell off. Yup, we're gonna ditch the Pink Tails. You always knew they'd come in handy some day, didn't you? I know you did, I told you so, this is just a different use than what I said back then. The six tails net us 10000 gil, getting us halfway there about. To get the rest of the way I sold off the Chocobo Wings I had from my Play Plan, that Slasher axe, and the "Soul" breaking accessories.

As for other new goodies, the Earring is a very nice accessory to boost magic damage, but I haven't got the goods for it, it needs 3 Sephira, 3 Girl's Dream and 3 Summoner's Dream, so if my memory serves you could have these items, and if so buy it if you have it, it's a great accessory. The Muscle Belt boosts physical damage but we won't be getting it for a long time because the arena associated with the Promyvion Shard is not encountered until way later, as for storyline purposes it has a special usage.

With our glorious new weapon equipped, begin the storyline.

Immediately turn to the right and you'll see a blue gateway, head over naturally. There's a chest near it with a BRV Charge skill. The enemies inside aren't too tough, go ahead and use the BRV Charge if you like. As for the enemy types....sigh, they're back. Delusory Dragoon, Fleeting Flash, Idle Sky Pirate, and they haven't changed a bit. The Strange Battle Piece is the Sky Pirate, he's got a huge Bravery boost and Torrent, along with the Cactuar summon. The False Hero at A2 is new-ish, it has Ascension, a short-range shield throw that reaches into the air and knocks you into the sky to begin a chase, and Crossover, essentially a midair version of Ascension. Both have high power.

At Level 9 Lightning learns Reverse Paradigm, which lets her cycle through them in reverse order. But we're not using the Medic role so she only has two, and thus the ability is useless. At Level 11 she gets Lightning Strike, an effective mid-range HP attack that is a welcome addition to our ground arsenal when the alternative has been Razor Gale.

The chests have an Iifa Leaf and a Soul of Patience, and then a Zombie Rag. It unlocks a single equipment piece, the Bard's Tunic, which is way out of our price range.

Back outside follow the main path, grabbing two orbs of the dead along the way, to a ruined castle with a moogle and our next gateway. Turn right to another area with a Capricious Reaper, he won't last too long with three Cursed Rings on. Behind it is a chest with 1500 gil. Approach the ruined castle and the moogle calls out to us as the "Thunder Lady" (I love that line, moogles are so adorable). Apparently he's the same moogle who greeted Lightning back in Chapter 1 and followed us around that little region, and now he's got his own shop, but not much for inventory. All he has is Pebbles. They're useless as bought, but if you equip them, after a fight they have a random chance to break and become either another Pebble, or a Growth Seed, which is one of several trade accessories needed for the Growth Egg, along with some Chocobo accessories from the Play Plan. We'll worry about this later, no point giving up accessory slots for stuff we can't use yet.

In the gateway, open the chest for 2000 gil. The two enemies you can skip but you won't, because you're not a wimp, are an Ephemeral Summoner and a False Hero. The Summoner has a new Bravery attack, Aerospark, a ground-based mid-range charge attack. Given that both are pushovers, draw out the fights to charge your EX Gauge. As I fought Lightning learned Ground Dash. Ground Dash only works when you're on the ground and won't let you dash into the air, but when dashing you count as being on the ground, letting you quickly stop and use ground attacks. Take the summonstone and Lightning unites with an old friend, Odin. He summons himself automatically when the opponent has enough Bravery to kill you in one hit, and tries to Break them. Now, time for the last enemy. Warning - he's the most powerful enemy in the pre-Epilogue storyline.

The Ephemeral Phantom is Level 23 so he has a huge boost to his starting Bravery, and has Bahamut to boot. He has all of Jecht's attacks except for Ultimate Jecht Shot - that includes Jecht Block which again can block almost anything. And as the highest level of battle piece possible, he's very good with them, and will chain his combos into HP attacks if he gets in a hit on you. And when he gets weak his boosters trigger for a 25x multiplier to his two Hyper Rings, giving him an insanely high damage buff. You need to be in top form to beat this guy. Begin by entering EX Mode and pummeling him what-good, he'll activate Bahamut. You've got a few choices for a summon of your own, I took Cactuar but I imagine Carbuncle is a better choice really, land an HP attack after Bahamut wears off to sap him of all his hard-boosted Bravery and Break him. He's not too bad really, but he is easily the most competent opponent yet easily above the bosses, and if you're a new player he'll be quite a challenge I imagine.

The rewards for beating him are of course the two chests, which have a Blazefire Saber and a piece of Mythril. The Saber has power nowhere near that of the Capella, while the Mythril unlocks numerous items in the ship requiring it, none of which we'll buy because the ones I think worth buying, like the armor or the hand armor, either require two pieces or are too expensive. It's okay kiddies, Mythril equipment is only worth using in threes, I'll explain why and cover buying it when we can get three of them. Now chug the Potion to top off your HP and EX gauge after the fight, and approach the Cosmos piece. Lightning tells the Warrior of Light and Cosmos about the hell out there with the manikins running amock, Kain being a traitor and the rest of the warriors scattered to who-knows what fate. Cosmos then senses Jecht's defeat and insists she can sense the rest of the group is alive in spite of what Lightning thinks of Kain's actions, but Lightning cuts her off to keep bitching. The Warrior decides he's had enough and raises his sword.

The Warrior has mostly attacks you've seen from his Manikins - Dayflash is a quick ground combo, Blue Fang summons ice shards to shoot at you from above, Ascension is a short-range shield throw into the air, Crossover is basically the same in the air, Shield Strike is an aerial downward shield throw, Rising Buckler is an upward one. His HP attacks are where this gets deadly. Ultimate Shield has him pause for a moment, then throw his shield at you to a surprising range, and drags you closer to combo you. Shining Wave sends a pillar of light at you, tracking you into the air, and Bitter End and Rune Saber are a new type of trick - HP Links.

Some Bravery attacks can link right into HP attacks without pause, and the Warrior has four of them here, Ascension, Crossover, Shield Strike and Rising Buckler. This makes avoiding his Bravery attacks more important than previous enemies, taking Bravery hits has been fine before since they can't actually damage HP, here, he can link them to attacks that can. Fortunately all four of those attacks are a bit slow on the start-up so you can block them. And if he Breaks you, well you're in for a real rough time of it because he has Alexander, which freezes his Bravery at its current value, preventing him from increasing it yes, but also preventing you from damaging it, and if he lands an HP hit, guess what, he doesn't reset to 0. Do not let him Break you whatever you do. And to wrap this all up, he's got six boosters and a Power Ring to give him a decent buff when he attacks with a Bravery attack. Be thankful it's not a Hyper Ring or Muscle Belt.

Similar to the Emperor, the Warrior has a pretty good set-up. What saves us and dooms him is once again the AI's incompetence, he can't use his moveset effective. He has HP Links mostly in the hypothetical sense, the AI doesn't much use them when it should be using them every chance it has because it has. Smack the Warrior around with EX Mode Lightning's simpler but more effective Smite and Launch to Break him, and I had Ifrit here to boost me up to 1600 Bravery or so. Then I let him recover his Bravery and whipped out my EX Burst, Breaking him again and killing him. Not a very difficult battle, especially compared to the Ephemeral Phantom and even then he wasn't too hard.

Cosmos breaks up the brawl and the Warrior confesses to what's going on. Kain told him that the wars are a cycle that will be repeated, and if they can incapacitate their allies and keep them safe from the manikins until the next cycle begins, they'll be able to survive the manikin horde and awaken to fight again. However, if they keep fighting now the manikins will eventually overrun them through sheer numbers. Thus Kain set out to find the others before the manikins do, put them to sleep, and hide them away to save them. Lightning is promptly pissed at this plan, and sees the pretty obvious flaw of it - who's to say when they wake up next time the manikins won't still be around, and what's it matter either way if the war is indeed endless? Laguna, Vaan and Yuna arrive in time to hear what's going on, and when the Warrior tells them that there's nothing to doubt accept defeat and let the cycle run its course, Lightning is again unimpressed and says that before the current cycle ends they might as well go down fighting and killing as many as they can. However, Laguna has a third option to let us know about and tells them about the portal they're coming from. Well, there's our path then. The four set out with the Warrior staying to guard Cosmos.

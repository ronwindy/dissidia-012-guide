PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Warrior of Light

The Warrior of Light, along with the Onion Knight, is an original character created solely for Dissidia, based on the artwork for the original Final Fantasy but having no character background to base him off of. As a result he's pretty bland but this is arguably the point. He's not prone to angsting or contemplating his naval in indecisiveness like the other cast members, he's just devoted to Cosmos and will do whatever he has to to protect her. As the later Reports in D012 will show, there's a reason his personality is so flat, and when you connect the Reports, to the main story, to the ending of the 013 cycle, you get a pretty creative character arc.

The Warrior is a pretty balanced character, he last the power, speed, and technicality of others, but makes up for it with versatility. He has short and mid-range combo attacks with decent power and several HP links, and versatile HP attacks. His initial attacks are Dayflash, a quick three-hit slice that begins a chase, and Blue Fang which calls a hail of ice on the opponent, good for setting up combos. His aerial attacks are Crossover, a spinning forward shield attack with an upward stab to finish and begin a chase, and Shield Strike, a downward shield toss before he dives and knocks the opponent in the direction he came from. HP attacks are Shield of Light, a short-range blast of light that also defends from attacks, and he automatically aims at the opponent when using it, and Shining Wave, sending a burst of light energy at the opponent and tracking them into the air and through walls. Shield of Light is the best one, the others have long-start up and AI opponents later in the game will dodge them easily, the Shield though will stagger them as it blocks their attack and leave them vulnerable.

Overall the Warrior is a good character, not great, but not bad, well-suited for the basic hero archetype. Use Blue Fang (and later White Fang) to set opponents up for combos and then dash to them and use Crossover or Dayflash, depending on your preference. When he begins to master his Bravery attacks and pick up his HP links, rely on them to whittle down the opponent's HP faster than his slow and limited HP attacks. Overall though, as I said he's balanced, other characters are faster, or do a lot more damage, or are just more fun to play as. But if you like him, enjoy him.

I gave the Warrior a Zweihander, Shell Shield, Healing Helm, Mythril Vest, and the typical accessories. He never has an Assist so yay for Empty Assist Gauge!

They don't waste any time at all, eh? Garland isn't some wimp at Level 10, even if you're only at Level 3 he's not holding back...well he is somewhat, but he's still pretty tough. He has no summon or Assist, fortunately. His accessories consist of a Hyper Ring with 4 HP = 100% boosters. Landing an HP attack to deactivate them should be your first priority of this conflict. I used the Warrior's EX Mode here so might as well cover it to fill space. The Warrior's EX Mode is a class change to a Knight, recovering his shiny knightly armor with red and yellow highlights and tilting the horns down to point forward for some reason. As a Knight the Warrior has Protect cast on him, giving him an extra 10 defense points, at this stage of the game that's as good as an extra shield equipped, and even in the late game is effective. Second, he gets the Mirror Attack ability, automatically protecting him from low-priority attacks while attacking himself. Mostly this just works out to reflecting magic since melee moves will bounce off each other like normal, but it's appreciated. His third bonus is the Light's Blessing ability, which summons blades of light energy whenever he uses a melee Bravery attack to strike the opponent and do more damage. The individual swords have low power but he summons about a dozen or more of them each time, and while the resulting boost isn't as awesome as Terra, Cloud or Squall, he still sees a noticeable spike in his damage output aside from the critical hit rate boost. The Warrior's EX Burst is quite easy, press each directional command as it appears on the screen to blast the opponent with slashes infused with light energy.

Warrior hit Level 4 from this fight and learned Sword Thrust. It's a mid-ranged attack that begins with a shield throw, then he charges in for a sword combo. It has slight reach into the air and can't be blocked, blocking it staggers the opponent and leaves them vulnerable to the follow-up sword attack.

Then the Warriors approach her with the Crystals, but Cosmos suddenly collapses and the world shifts from the light of Order's Sanctuary to the hellish nightmare of the End of Madness. Chaos appears, and wastes little time incinerating Cosmos in a pillar of fire. With the goddess slain Chaos departs.

So ends Chapter 10, now, only the epilogue awaits us.

The chest has a Titan's Tear, a unique trade accessory. It unlocks the Giant's equipment - Giant's Axe, Giant's Gloves, Giant's Helm, and Giant's Harness. This set isn't entirely useless and in fact is quite useful. When three pieces are worn together they trigger the Pride of the Titans combination, giving you an extra 100 base Bravery and 750 more HP. Though the pieces are only level 90, this boost is more than what you'd get if you traded out the Helm and Harness for Level 100 gear. Thus the only penalty you get for opting for Giant's gear over Level 100 stuff is slightly lower defenses. And as a three-piece combination, you only need the three armor pieces and can still use a Level 100 weapon for no power loss.

Grab the Phoenix Down if anyone has died and head down.

When you're done, grab the KP Chalice to triple your winnings, the five chests for three more Level 100 trade accessories, a Friendship Resin, and a Dragon's Tear. The Tear is part of the Whisper of the Wyrm combination, when three pieces are worn together they give you a 10% bonus to physical and magical defense. The pieces are all Level 60 except for the Wyvern Lance, which is Level 90. That 10% bonus to defense may make you wonder about the lost defense in Level 60 armor, but let's do some quick math on that - my level 100 Zidane, with no armor, has 110 defense. With the Dragon Armlet he gets 170 defense, and a 10% boost of that is an additional 17 point, whereas the top-level hand equipment in the same category as the Armlet gives 72 defense.

So the math, if I understand Dissidia's stat system properly, says go ahead. Sure you'll have a slight penalty to Bravery and HP, but you're perfectly functional regardless of both, if you play properly HP is no concern, and base Bravery, meh. The only catch is of course, doing the dungeon over to get two more Tears. Up to you kiddies, but me, I'm done. Let's blow this popsicle stand and get to the final objective - the Land of Discord, where the true final boss of the game awaits.

The Thief Steals The Show

Another new character, we're running out of them eh?

PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Zidane

The monkey-boy thief is here! Zidane is my favorite hero in the series, just barely outranking Terra. While other heroes are bland, annoying and/or angsty, Zidane is cool, friendly, laid-back and witty, not to mention being a rampant flirt. He does have his hesitations and insecurities, but he deals with them better than so many other character, overcompensating for them instead of being emotionally crippled. Overall he's just a really cool guy, characters like Cloud and Squall are ones you might enjoy doing a thesis on for a psychology class. Zidane is the character you'd enjoy going to a party with and just hanging out. Apparently his English voice actor gets some flak, but I like it, totally different from the Japanese VA but I think it works. He sounds better in battle than in cutscenes, he sounds too deep in the latter, but then battles take up the majority of time you listen to the voice actors anyway. And hey, if you want to wield a badass weapon, it doesn't get much more awesome than reverse-gripping two swords that can connect into a dual-bladed sword - just ask Gabranth and Xehanort.

Zidane is, in my humble opinion but this is my walkthrough so I'm right, the best character in the game. He excels at aerial combat, having a decent projectile attack, a diving attack, rising attack, forward spin attack, and a quick close-range keep away attack. His ground moves are limited, but they're so effective they're all he needs, His HP attacks are likewise awesome, he's got a mid-range charge attack, a close-range explosion of energy, and a long-range entrapment attack of lightning and water. He also has HP links to boot. Zidane's "weakness" is that he doesn't do a lot of Bravery damage, though this is a bit of an informed attribute to me, he does plenty good damage to kick ass, and when you've got some HP attacks including links, who cares if the Bravery damage is lame, HP damage is what wins battles. Sure he's no Squall or Cloud when it comes to his Bravery game, but he isn't as pathetic as Onion Knight so don't write his Bravery game off as weak - his Booster 8 to Storm Vortex hits just as hard as Squall's Solid Barrel.

Zidane starts out with Rumble Rush on the ground, a quick rolling kick foward that launches the opponent into a chase, Swift Attack which is a quick two-hit close-range move that begins a chase, and Scoop Art, which fires out three slow-moving energy bolts that track the opponent. Don't worry, he'll learn Tempest and Vortex before Level 10, giving him more options in the air. His HP attacks are Tidal Flame, which sends a ring of fireballs spinning along the ground to explode in a fairly wide radius, Free Energy, a very fast but very short range blast of energy, and Shift Break, creating a tempest of lightning strikes around the opponent before blasting them with water, very good for opponents who try to charge up attacks at range, like Starfall, Meltdown, Black Materia, etc.

As I said, I consider Zidane one of the game's best characters, he's my main, and he'll be back for the Epilogue and in Confessions of the Creator, so learn to love him. And you will, he may seem weak now, but once he learns his higher level attacks and gets his HP links, you'll be raining furry Genome death on the opposition.

I gave Zidane a Sakura, Green Beret, Silken Shirt, and bought him a Chakram. For accessories, Hyper Ring, Muscle Belt, Sunrise, HP = 100%, BRV ≥ Base Value, and Level 1-9. I'm also playing on an AP calendar bonus so I'm getting bonus APs, awesome because Zidane really wants to master Rumble Rush and Swift Attack quickly.

This is a pitifully easy battle, Kefka's got a Cursed Ring that really cripples him. He's got the typical attacks and the Lich summon to begin a 15 second countdown to sapping your Bravery. The catch is that the AI only summons Lich once you Break them, and you can easily land the HP strike to avoid losing your boost. Time it so you land the HP hit with 4 or 3 seconds on the countdown, then when it goes off and drains you, you're already in the process of regenerating your Bravery and Lich will effectively do nothing.

Because the fight always begins in the same location, here's a step-by-step process to how to fight. As the battle starts, Kefka will usually, but not always, hop onto the railing on your right and grind past you. Move right and block him to stagger him, then enter EX Mode and hit him with Tempest. This will Break Kefka and put you close enough to a wall that the Chase HP attack will Wall Rush him, putting him at less than 1000 HP. Now just land another Bravery combo on him or let Zidane's Bravery regenerate, then him him against and EX Burst. My Level 5 Zidane (that first HP strike put him up another level) did over 800 damage with his Burst, so once you recover his base Bravery or use another combo as I said, Kefka shouldn't have enough HP to survive.

So let's cover Zidane's EX Burst. As awesome heroes are want to do (see also: Terra), Zidane enters Trance to transform into a pink and purple superpowered being. He gains the ability to jump an additional 10 times, on top of his normal jumps and any boosts to how many jumps he can do based on his abilities. He also becomes invincible while jumping, and after dodging his jumps "recharge", since Dissidia vets probably know or have realized that once a character dodges in D012, they can't jump anymore until they land or use a Quickmove indicator. Admittedly Zidane's is one of the lamest EX Modes, his ability isn't useless, but it isn't really a stand-out help, the large open arenas where it should be awesome like Planet's Core, The Rift, etc have Quickmove options for quickly moving around the stage faster than jumping, and in other stages it isn't much needed. Zidane's EX Burst is pretty easy, just tap Circle over and over as he bombards the opponent with attacks, the power bar is segmented but I'm not sure why, it stops for a moment at each increment but you have to keep tapping Circle so ignore this. Zidane has one of the weakest EX Bursts, Squall is the worst but above him it's the Onion Knight, then Kuja, and then Zidane.

Final note, Zidane hit Level 16 from this fight and learned Booster 8. It's an awesome move and Zidane's second worthwhile ground attack. Zidane takes a moment to coil his feet before launching forward and into the air. It can't be blocked for one thing, the opponent will stagger instead of taking damage, but they're a sitting duck because of what Booster 8 is meant for - combos. After hitting, much like Squall's Thunder Barret, Zidane can quickly combo into another attack. Booster 8 into Free Energy for quick HP damage, Booster 8 into Swift Attack for a Chase or Meo Twister, Booster 8 into Storm Impulse for massive Bravery pain and wall rush. It has some recovery time to handle, but with proper usage it effectively lets Zidane combo any aerial attack from a ground attack and tack on extra damage and range.

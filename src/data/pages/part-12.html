PLAYABLE CHARACTER - Terra

Oh my lovely Terra, good to see you at last! Terra is, for those of you not familiar with me, one of my all-time favorite Final Fantasy characters, narrowly edged out of the #1 slot by a hero we'll get to later in the game. A beautiful, powerful, determined, heroic warrior-mage, Terra is all kinds of awesome...in Final Fantasy VI. Unfortunately she kinda got shafted in the Dissidia games, her determination and protectiveness are toned down and her self-doubt and insecurities are played up, to the point of, as you saw in Vaan's chapter, temporarily wishing for death to avoid being used by Kefka anymore. They cast Onion Knight as her protector, when to keep to the spirit of VI, she should have been vowing to protect him. Dissidia 012 tweaks the dialogue in both their stories to have more emphasis on a mutual friendship between them, as well as to show her heroic spirit and determination more, but the 12th cycle more than made up for that. So between games her character arc has a higher end point, but a lower start. At least they tried, didn't quite work but they did try.

That misrepresentation of her carries over into gameplay, but that is not to say she's weak and helpless, it's just that she focuses on magic while in VI she was also a strong physical fighter too. Terra is one of my favorite characters in the game to play as, she was one of the strongest in the original Dissidia but got heavily nerfed in this game, her three bread-and-butter attacks (Blizzara, Blizzard Combo, Holy Combo) getting reduced range, tracking and/or power. She's still perfectly powerful though, possessing powerful magic combos that combo very well with Assists, and she has a very well-rounded moveset, preferring to fight from long range in the air but having a few good options for close-quarters and ground combat. Blizzard Combo is a fairly quick melee attack with good power, it's Terra's core "back off, I'm a mage and like keeping my distance" attack. Blizzara is a very powerful ranged attack that inflicts Wall Rush, it's range kinda sucks though. Fire sends a burst of flame along the ground that explodes over a small radius to begin a Chase, it packs a surprising punch, generates the most EX Force of her attacks, and isn't slow to execute, but isn't very fast either. Finally Thundaga summons four converging lightning bolts around the opponent, easy to dodge but like Shift Break is good to punish opponents trying to charge up an attack since they can't avoid it.

Her initial HP attacks are Flood, which summons a blast of water below the opponent, also good for charging opponents if you're on the ground at the time but useless otherwise since it's slow to activate, and Tornado, a close-range attack that summons a trio of whirlwinds to cycle Terra before combining into one to slamming the opponent into the air, sucking them in for the duration and deflecting projectiles - it has melee damage priority so even the likes of Cloud's Braver and Lightning's Crushing Blow just causes both Terra and them to stagger rather than have them pierce it to hit her. It's slow to start up and has a very long end-lag that makes it easy to punish her, as I've noted in the instances where you've fought her or her manikins, but in close quarters in small areas like Pandaemonium or Kefka's Tower, it's a nightmare. She levels up she diversifies her moveset with more utility attacks, including what was, in the original Dissidia, one of the most powerful combos in the entire game and is still lethal, but we'll get to those when she learns them.

To repeat, Terra is one of my favorite characters to fight as and one of my most favorite actual characters. Enjoy using her to rain magical chaos on your enemies.

Kefka has a nice equipment set-up and decent boosters, an Earring with 4 1.2 Far From Opponent. He's also got an Idle Sky Pirate Assist, and you have none.

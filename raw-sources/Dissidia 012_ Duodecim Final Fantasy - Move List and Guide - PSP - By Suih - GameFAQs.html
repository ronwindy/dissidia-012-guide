If you want your Quest added here or removed then message me at Gamefaqs or see Contact Info. While it is not needed, you can include all the information as all the other cards do to save me a bit of time but providing a version is mandatory. Do not worry too much about formatting (such as bullets and bold) because the FAQ write up is different from simple text so I can't copy and paste "proper" formatting anyway. This saves you a bit of a hassle anyway I would assume, hm?

As long as your Quest has proper grammar and spelling (more of a preference rather than a requirement) as well doesn't contain anything suggestive or has vulgar language, it'll probably be accepted. This mostly relies on the honor system however. You are responsible for maintaining the link of your Quest. The author names here are their Gamefaqs handle unless stated otherwise.

If you want to present your personal rating and/or review for any of these quest (even for own) contact me for that too. Honesty is a great thing but keep it civil and contructive.

To use these quests, follow these instructions:

  1. Download the quest at the listed link.
  2. Extract the resulting file by right clicking and finding the appropriate option.
    1. If you can't then it's probably because the file is in a format that you don't support yet (most likely .rar). Download Winrar in that case--you can find it by googling.
  3. Move the ULUS10566Quest## folder right into your SAVADATA folder located on your PSP.
  4. Load up Dissidia 012 and play!

If it doesn't work then you most likely put the files in the wrong location or your region is different from the quest in question.

Friend Cards

If you want your friend card added here then message me at Gamefaqs or see Contact Info. While it is not needed, you can include all the information as all the other cards do to save me a bit of time but providing a version is mandatory. As long as your friend card doesn't contain anything suggestive or has vulgar language, it'll probably be acceped. You are responsible for maintaining the link of your card.

To use these friend cards, follow these instructions:

  1. Download the friend card at the listed link.
  2. Extract the resulting file by right clicking and finding the appropriate option.
    1. If you can't then it's probably because the file is in a format that you don't support yet (most likely .rar). Download Winrar in that case--you can find it by googling.
  3. Move the ULUS10566Quest## folder (yes, the entire folder not just the files) right into your SAVADATA folder located on your PSP.
  4. Load up Dissidia 012.
  5. Go to Communications Mode then to Communications.
  6. Select, Import a Friend Card and import the cards you want.
  7. Exit out then go to Friend Cards. Enjoy.

If it doesn't work then you most likely put the files in the wrong location or your region is different from the friend card in question.

Arguably the most important thing, however, is that you cannot farm items from a ghost until you buy the option to do so in the shop.

000: Confessions of the Creator

Foreword

This is the main meat of Dissidia 012's story mode and is unlocked after you finish 012 and buy it in the PP shop for 2000 PP or you finish 013. Personally I recommend that you buy this and then go through 013. When you have a full skill list when you're in 013, save and exit out and advance a bit in 000, emptying out that list and going back to playing 013 to fill it up again.

Before starting this part of the story I recommend that you create custom ruleset(s) that'll benefit you and make up for any differences in level/equipment/skill such as a ruleset that'll spawn EX Cores every second, et cetera.

This walkthrough will assume that you actually want to do everything so I'll be covering every gateway possible but note that you don't actually have to go through every gateway to "finish" it and unlock Feral Chaos. If you want to go to the fastest route then follow my guide until you get a Moogle to follow you then talk to him and follow his instructions.

Additionally, I'll write this walkthrough a bit different than the other sections. I won't mention what the assists are in this scenario mostly because they're random this time around. The same goes for the maps shift a lot whether this be moving to certain panels to reveal enemies or to kill manikins to reveal paths. Additionally, you can also attack them for EXP and what not if you would so like. Pick your five most comfortable characters and don't fret--you can change them later on. Your first character will be the leading man and will be the one appearing in any cutscenes that involve you and will be your avatar on the world map. You shouldn't really worry about this too much unless you're these characters:

Squall: Your field BRV attack is extremely hard to hit manikins with. Breaking rocks is impossible unless you hit it at a certain angle.
Exdeath: He has the slowest moving speed out of the whole cast.

With that out of the way, lets get started shall we?

New Characters

If there is no tips or recommended assists section for a character it is because there is practically none that I can give.

If I don't offer any explanation as to why an assist is recommended it is because I can't possibly cover the numerous reasons as to why or it's a obvious fact.

Lightning

Attributes
Pros
    1. Commando has many attacks that stagger guards.
    2. Ravanger creates combines pressure with speed.
    3. Medic: has the ability to pile BRV and pressure without even touching the opponent.
    4. Lightning HP attacks have a large range and punish those who dodge too soon.
    5. Most of her moves are unblockable or impractical to block in the first place.
    6. Has one of the best EX Modes: She can't be wall rushed which means most combos become unusable against her.
Cons
    1. Height differences will screw up her Commando attacks.
    2. Slow start up times for her HP attacks.
    3. Once any opponent gets the hang of a Lightning player's playstyle, she is very dependant on a limited selection from her moveset.
Role as an Assist
Lightning's ground BRV is greatly outclassed by other fighters. Her air BRV attack is powerful but again, outclassed by other assists that do the same thing such as Jecht or Cloud. The true brilliance of her Assist is in her HP attacks: they have a huge area of effect and tracking along with the fact that it punishes players who dodge on reaction.
Preferences
Best Stages: None
Worst Stages: None
Tips
    1. Medic is NOT useless. It can create similar pressure as The Emperor's Flare or Exdeath's Maelstorm can and prevents you from being broken if you use it right.
    2. Use Blizzara often to keep the enemy on their toes.
    3. When she hits with Aerora you can use Lightning Strike to execute a link glitch to hit the enemy afterwards. Lightning Strike will not always work though because of range. The first part of the attack has much less range than the second part though. The glitch sometimes doesn't work though since there is a small window of time for the enemy to dodge.
    4. Lightning can also follow through with the whole Army of One (ground) attack if she hits with Aerora. This will execute 100% of the time but may not always reach depending on your range. It is however a great strategy to use for amazing pressure. Satsukun suggests that you send out an assist attack that the enemy will be forced to dodge when you do this so you catch them in the act.
Recommended Assists
    1. Jecht - Like Firion you can use Jecht's assist as a crutch to use an HP attack while Jecht is sending an enemy up or down.
    2. Squall - "I've found Squall to be an excellent Assist for Lightning. If you smack the enemy down with Smite and call Squall as he hits the ground, he'll use Solid Barret and launch the enemy back into the air. Then you can warp with Lightning and then use either Smite again or Blitz to gain more Bravery, or you could hit him with Thunderfall or Crushing Blow while they're helpless."

Player Strategies

Don't ever try to throw yourself into a head on battle with Feral Chaos because he will always win. You can sneak your attacks carefully between the risky spots in his attacks but even doing this is risky since he has speed that is above the majority of the cast. Since his HP attacks often puts enemies astray at close range, playing a zoning game is pretty smart. Be mindful of spacing when facing him. Don't ever try to block him unless Feral Chaos uses Spew. It's the move where he erupts flame under you.

If you're facing Via spam then just calm down. You can avoid the first hit by just running on the ground (Speed Boost helps if your walk speed is too slow), jumping (Jump Boost is good here), or by just dodging a bit ahead of time and then dodge for the second hit. It has a very distinct animation so just watch carefully and you can predict it coming.

Against his EX Burst you'll just have to focus on depleting Chaos' Bravery to a point where it won't really matter if he hits you with the second part or not. You could also use a summon to achieve this effect too such as using Alexander to seal his BRV at a reasonable level or Malboro to deplete his Bravery. If the Feral Chaos player executes the EX Burst manually then there's not really much you can do against it to be honest.

Additionally you can boost your chances if you learn how to play Firion or Golbez. Both of the aforementioned are Feral Chaos' worst enemies and can kill Feral Chaos with the most insane amount of ease.

    3. High combo potential.
Cons
    1. Brave attacks are easily blocked.
    2. Her Brave moveset suffers from a painful lack of priority.
    3. She lacks variety in her Brave moveset.
Role as an Assist
Cloud of Darkness' place in battle is in the air. Her aerial BRV attack has a large radius of effect and will pressure a lot of enemies because of it. She's also kind of like The Emperor/Gabranth/Laguna of the air in that she keeps her enemy in one place for awhile. Her attack is easily blocked like her normal appearance which is a trait that can be used to trick enemies or create unblockable/unavoidable situations.
Tips
    1. You should be using her HP attacks a lot; throw the notion of them being unsafe out of the window and learn what each one does and use them carefully according to the situation at hand.
Preferences
Best Stages: None
Worst Stages: None

Golbez

Attributes
Pros
    1. Intimidating.
    2. Deflects all close range attackers on the ground.
    3. When he's in the right enviroment he can snatch enemies up and keep them entrapped for a long time.
    4. Sector Ray can pickle enemies well.
    5. Extremely high combo potential.
    6. Genesis Rock has nearly infinite vertical range.
    7. Golbez can fight enemies from the other side of the wall. He's usually even better than Firion in this regard too.
    8. His block animation is subtle which hides his vulnerability very well.
    9. Teleports when he jumps; dodging by Jump Boost is extremely viable and is one of his greatest assets.
Cons
    1. When Golbez is forced to ascend into the air he can be tripped up easily. All of his aerial attacks can be punished even by a complete amateur including his Genesis Rock.
    2. Certain ranges are inaccessible to him. Moving to fit that range becomes troublesome for him. Exdeath can have a field day with inexperienced Golbez' this way.
    3. Has no attacks to counter aggressive mages with as they either will not reach the opponent or require point blank range to execute which is often not possible against such foes.
    4. Poking projectiles (like Zidane's) will screw with him like guard geese on a burglar.
    5. Can be assist punished easily.
    6. Can be pressured easily.
    7. Black Fang has horrid recovery time when cancelled. When used, Golbez will have to follow through with it.
    8. Genesis Rock's infinite vertical range is more or less useless against a player that already knows about it. Also the hitbox for this infinite vertical range is extremely tight so you have to get a feel for it or you'll miss and be punished harshly.
    9. People who are patient can give him a real run for his money.
Role as an Assist
Attack System lacks a lot of tracking which is useless for an assist character. If you wanted to use him for his Glare Hand then you could've just gone with a Cloud assist. Nightglow is often used as a shield attack which is totally outclassed by Aerith's Planet Protector and Genesis Rock, well, is awesome but to use him just because of that would be a real waste. I honestly don't think there is any use for his assist.
Preferences
Best Stages: Pandaemonium, Kefka's Tower, Phantom Train, Edge of Madness (in some cases)
Worst Stages: Dream's End, Sky Fortress Bahamut (Omega), Edge of Madness (in some cases)
Tips
    1. Pay attention to the range between you and your opponent, Golbez' attacks need precise spacing to work.
    2. You must use the proper tool for the proper occassion. Practice with his attacks and get a feel of them.
    3. As long as any of his other BRV attacks hit and linger long enough he can use Cosmic Ray in link glitch fashion.
Recommended Assists
    1. Jecht - There are countless amounts of stylish combos that you can do with him.
    2. Yuna - Also the same and opens up even more combo options.
    3. Cloud of Darkness - Another assist with a great number of stylish combos.
    4. Aerith - You get the idea.

Exdeath

Attributes
Pros
    1. Blocks everything.
    2. Is undoubtedly the best character at playing mind games.
    3. Pressures enemies better than any other character.
    4. Comptent Exdeaths leave nearly zero openings.
    5. Builds Assist fast.
Cons
    1. Doesn't have that much combo potential.
Role as an Assist
He keeps the enemy in place and lets you get some free hits before he launches them which he gives you an Assist Chase to let you attack the flinged enemy. He requires some timing to use though.
Preferences
Best Stages: None
Worst Stages: None
Tips
    1. You don't have to use his Omniblock, in fact, it will probably be easier to not use it but you'll be missing out on a lot.
Recommended Assists
    1. Firion - Use Firion in the air so you can eventually block his Fire when they're sent back to you. Also you could use his ground BRV attack for a quick wake up call for the enemy.

Kefka

Attributes
Pros
    1. Unorthodox.
    2. Powerful.
    3. Attacks pressures enemies well.
    4. Large moveset pool.
    5. One of the best, if not the best, EX Mode.
Cons

    2. Kefka can't deal with enemies that actually manage to get close.

I would never recommend him as an assist.
Preferences
Best Stages: None

Sephiroth

Attributes

    3. Fast attacks that keep an enemy off guard.

Fast character with a lot of range that can assist change as an added bonus.
Preferences
Best Stages: None
Worst Stages: None

Kind of useless.

Kuja

Attributes
Pros

    2. His physical attacks are hard to avoid.

Cons
    1. His ranged attacks are insignificant. They can be brushed off with ease.
Role as an Assist
Kuja is the most commonly used assist and has great reason to be so. High power, fast, and you can set up easy combos with him. If you attack during his BRV moves he will usually not drop the enemy.

Worst Stages: None

    2. Never be below your opponent, optimally you'll always want to be above your opponent.
Recommended Assists
    1. Cecil - You can do a lot of advanced combos which require a bit of creative thinking but are all too damaging.     2. Zidane
    3. Onion Knight

Help/Crafting

Duplicating

  1. Put the items you want to duplicate onto your Ghost, or in other other words, your character that's associated with your Friend Card.
  2. Save your Friend Card (Export).
  3. Import your Friend Card.
  4. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  5. Battle your Friend Card until you drop said item.

This also works with gaining Artifacts.

KP

Method 1 (Zahlzeit)

  1. Get any chain skills you want.
  2. In 000, reduce the levels of all of your party to 1.
  3. Teleport/Travel to Onrac. Go to the first gateway leading to Feral Chaos (final boss) called Pervasive Sorrow.
  4. Clear the gateway. Repeat.

Method 2 (Max Raschke)

  1. Get KP Switch, KP Gamble, chain abilities, and 1-2 KP Bonus(es).
    1. KP Gamble can be bought from any Moogle Shop in 000.
  2. Go to Frozen Continent.
  3. Use KP Switch and KP Gamble upon entry.
  4. Use your chain abilities and take out as many enemies as you can with them.
  5. Use all of your KP Bonus(es) just as you're going to exit the gateway.

Method 3 (Franklin Jingga)

  1. Make all of your characters in your party level one.
  2. Equip Smiting Soul, Destroyer(if needed), Sniper Eye(if needed) and some booster accessories according to the situation.
  3. Set the rule to the in-game BRV-Centric.
  4. Get Chain Skills, KP Switch, Gambler, and Bonus (if needed) according to the situation.
  5. Go to any gateway. Rinse and repeat.

Method 4 (TerraBunny95)

  1. Travel to the Onrac region.
  2. Go to the gateway (Confinement and Flight) before the grind rail, leading to the chain of gateways.
  3. Use KP Gambler, Multichain, and KP+1 Emblem.
  4. Clear as much battles as you can.
    1. If your character fails the KP Chance, make sure your opponent is at low HP before you kill yourself.

AP

  1. Travel to the Gateway of Melting Snows.
  2. When you enter, head for the emblem that'll protect your accessories from breaking.
  3. Equip a Diamond Ring, Beckoning Cat, et cetera. Have as many AP boosts as you can.
  4. Equip any other breakable accessories to speed through the battles as they won't break here.
  5. Use a Multichain.

Rosetta Stones

Method 1 (not repeatable)

  1. Collect them from chests in the 012 story.

Method 2

  1. Buy them from Moogles in 012, 013, and 000.

Method 3

  1. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  2. Download the Exulus- card from the Friend Card section.
  3. Fight repeatively against Exulus-'s card for Rosetta Stones.

Megalixers

Method 1

  1. Kill Feral Chaos (final boss version) in 000 repeatedly. A chest will spawn afterwards containing a Megalixer.

Method 2

  1. Purchase Time Attack in the PP Catalog.
  2. Finish Time Attack in under 15 minutes for a Megalixer.

Method 3 (only once per day)

  1. Accumulate a large amount of KP.
  2. In 000, teleport/travel to Castle Cornelia.
  3. Talk to the Moogle shopkeeper and buy a Megalixer.

Method 4

  1. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  2. Download the Exulus- card from the Friend Card section.
  3. Fight repeatively against Exulus-'s card for Megalixers.

Method 5

  1. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  2. Download the Edgar friend card from the Friend Card section.
  3. Fight repeatively against the Edgar friend card for Megalixers.

Smiting Soul

  1. Finish 013 if you haven't already.
  2. Buy multiple Battlegen Rate Up in the PP Catalog to speed the next step up.
  3. Get into a battle with Chaos and grind for Discord's Dream x3 and Discord's Hopes x2.
  4. In 000, teleport/travel to Mirage Tower.
  5. Head for, Dreams of a Flying Castle located in the middle of the desert with False Stalwarts in the background.
  6. Descend to floor 5. Grab all chests.
  7. Repeat step 6 until you get a Tome of the Orator.
  8. Craft Smiting Soul.

Lufenian Set

  1. In 000, teleport/travel to Mirage Tower.
  2. Head for, Dreams of a Flying Castle located in the middle of the desert with False Stalwarts in the background.
  3. Descend to floor 4.
  4. Defeat all Mirror Pieces.
  5. Step into the panel where the left mirror piece was. Red Chests should spawn everywhere.
  6. Defeat every Red Chest piece, they all contain a random piece of Lufenian Armor.
  7. Rinse and repeat steps 2-5 until you're satisfied.

Genji Set

Method 1

  1. In 000, teleport/travel to the Elven Castle.
  2. Head east. Past the Frozen Continent you should see a Moogle shopkeeper.
  3. Buy Heike armor.
  4. Travel back to the teleport stone. Teleport/Travel to Onrac. Travel to the west to get into the huge desert on the leftside of the map. Travel downwards to find a Moogle shopkeeper.
  5. Buy four Samurai Spirits.
  6. Exit out of 000, go to your Reports. Defeat Bartz in Report 19 if you haven't already.
  7. Buy multiple Battlegen Rate Up in the PP Catalog to speed the next step up.
  8. Get into a battle with Gilgamesh and grind for five each of the following: Giant's Hopes, Giant's Dream, Giant's Desire, and Giant's Hopes.
  9. Craft the equipment.

Method 2

  1. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  2. Download the Exulus- card from the Friend Card section.
  3. Fight repeatively against Exulus-'s card until you acquire all pieces.

Machine Set

Note: No matter what method you pick you must buy a Chemist Lore in the normal shop then buy the skill in the etc tab. Finally, you must equip the skill to actually use this set in the first place.

Method 1

  1. Teleport to Pravoka. Head South to the Ancient Volcano Ring - Gulg. Continue along to see a Moogle Shop--it's right next to "Encounters and Treason" Gateway.
  2. Buy six Gear Collections.
  3. Buy multiple Battlegen Rate Up in the PP Catalog to speed the next step up.
  4. Get into a battle with Laguna in the M.S. Prima. Grind for Gunner's Hopes (x10), Gunner's Dream (x5) and Gunner's Desire (x5) as well as Mallet (x16).
  5. Craft the equipment.

Method 2

  1. Buy multiple Item Drop Rate Up from the PP Catalog.
  2. Download the Edgar friend card from the Friend Card section.
  3. Fight repeatively against the Edgar friend card until you acquire all pieces.

Scarletites

  1. Teleport to Pravoka. Head South to the Ancient Volcano Ring - Gulg. Continue along to see a Moogle Shop--it's right next to "Encounters and Treason" Gateway.
  2. Buy as many as you want.

Pink Tails

  1. Teleport to Pravoka. Head South to the Ancient Volcano Ring - Gulg. Continue along to see a Moogle Shop--it's right next to "Encounters and Treason" Gateway.
  2. Buy as many as you want.

Dragon's Tear

  1. Access a teleport stone in 000. Teleport to Dragon Cave.
  2. Descend into The Dragon King's Gateway.
  3. On floor 10, if I remember correctly, there is a chest that contains the Dragon's Tear.
  4. Rinse and repeat.

Shell Armor

Play Chapter 10 of Main Scenario 013. There is a chest near the end that contains it.

Allure of Honey Set

Run through Shantotto's dungeon in Main Scenario 000. There is a chest in it that contains a random piece of the armor.

Electrum

In Main Scenario 000, teleport to Gaia. There should be only one gateway near you which is called The Forsaken Lands. There are chests (notice the pluralization) in it that have the Electrum in them every time you play through the dungeon.

Excalibur II

You can only get this weapon by farming it off of friend cards that have it. If you exchange letters with the Moogles you'll eventually get an in-game Friend Card called No. 1 Moogle which can drop it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is Main Scenario 013?

A. A complete remake of the first Dissidia. It doesn't have Shantotto or Gabranth's dungeons from that game--instead they are gateways in 000.

Q. Should I buy the first Dissidia?

A. Unless you're low on money, no. See the previous question.

Q. How do I unlock Prishe and Gilgamesh?

A. Finish the first battle in Report 8 for Prishe, finish the last battle in the report for Gilgamesh.

Q. How do I unlock Feral Chaos?

A. "Finish" Main Scenario 000.

Q. How do I unlocked the locked areas in 000?

A. Finish Chasm of the Rotting Land.

Q. What is a "Bravery Attack Finish"?

A. It just means using a bravery attack on an enemy and well, finishing the attack not interrupted.

Q. What are booster accessories?

A. They're accessories that, as the name suggests, boosts other accessories (most notably the white colored ones) when you fill out the condition it lists in battle and keep it filled.

Q. Is it possible to use alternate costumes in Main Scenario 000, 012, 013, and the reports?

A. No.

Q. Is Zack, Angeal, Genesis, and any other characters not listed in the New Characters section in this game?

A. No.

Q. Why is it that when I dodge it counts as a block?

A. You're doing it the instant the enemy is about to attack you. At the early frames of a dodge, it is counted as a block.

Q. Do have links to the friend cards or quests on this FAQ?

A. No.
